# Advisor · Cómo responde Mimo

> Parte de [Advisor](./advisor.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): modo resolutivo, una ronda de corrección, «Hecho» solo con confirmación, cifras del menú, nunca «sin gluten» sin ingredientes, notas, invitaciones, avisos, la pregunta de las sobras y la encuesta.

## Assistant Skills: cómo responde

**Decisiveness (resolutive mode).**
- The prompt tells the model to do everything a message asks in the same turn, with parallel tool calls and up to `MAX_TOOL_ROUNDS = 6`.
- It doesn't ask permission for reversible changes; it confirms only destructive or bulk ones.
- **Acts instead of asking, reads before it says, no tail-ends (PRO-1297, 2026-11-24).** The exam of Mimo's whole history (6-oct to 23-nov) found 20 turns where Mimo asked when it had to act or did half after a «sí», 4 where it said things about the menu or the catalogue it hadn't read (on 23-nov, «casi no hay pasta» in the catalogue, and there was), offers, surveys or links after a «no» or a «gracias», two cooking doubts turned down as off-topic and a joking thank-you answered with the off-topic sentence. Now `ACT_RULE` (`systemPrompt.ts`, every mode, in «Reglas criticas»): (a) it asks only when the message has two readings with different consequences or before deleting something (a recipe, the existing menu, leaving the household); everything reversible, also on a meal already gone, is done without asking; after a «sí» it does everything it proposed; (b) a taste or a house rule is always saved (where the tool lines say) as well as applied, and on saving it Mimo itself reviews the rest of the week and changes the dishes that break it (allergies, intolerances and health keep their own rule); (c) nothing about the account (menu, recipes, list, pantry, nutrition) or the catalogue that wasn't read this turn or in the context (`search_recipes` before saying there are or aren't recipes of something), and a «similar» dish is never presented as the one asked for; (d) after «gracias», «no» or a goodbye, one kind line and nothing else (no offers, surveys, questions or links); anywhere, one tail-end at most and never a link nobody asked for. (e) On WhatsApp, a cooking doubt or a question about their own recipe (even one not in Mimoia) is never off-topic, and `WHATSAPP_OFF_TOPIC_REPLY` never answers a thank-you, a greeting or a joke: those get one kind line. It replaced the lines it overlapped (WhatsApp's «haz TODO», «no pidas permiso», «solo agradece», «solo pregunta si es imprescindible»; «hazlas todas», «comprueba antes que existe», «no inventes datos de SU cuenta», «sugiere generar mas menus»), so the prompt is no longer than before in any mode (all tools, no «Sobre Mimoia»: web 24 544 characters, was 24 551; WhatsApp 32 938, was 33 431; voice 26 217, was 26 224). The offers the code appends (`appendReplyOffers`: invites, leftovers, Sean Ellis) keep their own rules. Battery `CONDUCT_EVAL_CASES` (`mimoConductEvalCases.ts`, graded by `conductReplyProblems`, in `scripts/evalHealthAnswers.ts`): 13 cases, two or more per part — `actua` (asks permission with nothing done fails, `pregunta-en-vez-de-actuar`; live, fewer calls than proposed, `a-medias`), `guarda-norma` (live, no save `no-guarda`, no swap `a-medias`), `lee-antes` («casi no hay pasta», a similar dish as the one asked for), `sin-coletilla` (a question, an offer or a link after a closing message, `coletilla` / `enlace`; two tail-ends, `coletillas`), `duda-cocina` (the off-topic sentence, `fuera-de-tema`). **Tests:** `tests/mimoConduct.test.ts` (the rule in every mode, the replaced lines gone, every bad reply fails and a good one passes) and `tests/mimoToolGroups.test.ts` (each case gets its tools).
- An explicit request beats a stored like or dislike, but **never an allergy, intolerance or health restriction**. For those, the model warns in one line and asks for confirmation. (Fixed 2026-10-07: the first version said "restricción" too.) Since 2026-10-07 the tools enforce it too: `swap_meal` with a named recipe that breaks an allergy/diet changes nothing and returns the conflict, and it only places the recipe on a second call with `confirmRestriction: true`. `suggest_recipes` and the random swap never return such recipes (see [Menus → Restrictions & allergies](./menus-restricciones.md)). The memory digest now reads "Le disgustan (evítalos al proponer; si el usuario pide algo explícitamente, hazlo)".
- If a week has no menu yet, the model generates one and then applies the changes.
- **One corrective round for an unbacked claim (PRO-239, 2026-10-19).** From 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-19 there were no backstop rounds (with the small model the regex guards produced the oddest replies, «Tienes razón, ya estaba hecho desde hace un turno») and a claim was only flagged when **no** tool ran, so «Ya está apuntado como cocinado…» after only `get_shopping_list`, or «Los pañales ya salían como…», went out untouched. Now, if the final reply claims a change (`claimsAction`, `assistant/claims.ts`: «he cambiado / apuntado / corregido…», «hecho.», «ya está apuntado / bien / corregido…», «ya salían como…») and no tool that changes something ran this turn (`toolMadeChange`: not in `READ_ONLY_TOOLS`, no error, no «no he encontrado…»), the engine sends the reply back with a hidden note (`UNBACKED_CLAIM_NOTE`: do it now with the tool, or say clearly you haven't / it was already so, without claiming you just did it, no apologies or talk of tools) and gives the model **one** more round (`corrections: ['claim_retry']`). If the second answer still claims without a changing tool, it goes out flagged `unverified_claim` for the daily review. Not on the forced-text last round. **Tests:** `tests/assistantToolLoop.test.ts` (the Dani y Sara and Marta y Jorge cases).
- **«Hecho» only for what a tool confirmed (PRO-349, 2026-10-24).** Something changing was not enough: Laura got «Hecho» for «quita el cilantro del butter chicken» while the recipe stayed as it was, because another tool of the turn had changed something. Now the same one extra round also goes when a tool that changes things failed or changed nothing and no later call of the **same** tool fixed it (`unconfirmedCalls`), with a note naming what wasn't confirmed (`partialClaimNote`); a reply that already says what wasn't done («…la receta no la he podido cambiar», `admitsNotDone`) goes out as it is. A second half-true claim is flagged `partial_claim`. A tool summary that opens with «Hecho» / «Cambiado» counts as a change even when it adds «No hay una receta que se llame igual…»; «No encontré…» and «no estaba en…» count as no change. Evaluation cases, one per part of PRO-349: `CLAIM_EVAL_CASES` in `assistant/mimoClaimEvalCases.ts` (`arroz-dia-sin-cocinar`, `cilantro-butter-chicken`, `pan-receta-de-hoy`), graded in CI (`tests/mimoClaimEval.test.ts`) and live (`scripts/evalHealthAnswers.ts`).
- The user context now includes **today's date and time in Madrid** with its dayIndex, plus **this week's menu** read from `dishes[]`. Before, it read the legacy `slot.recipeName` and was always empty.
- **Mimo cites the menu's and the recipe's numbers (PRO-183, 2026-10-19).** The households got minutes, servings and amounts from Mimo's memory («unos 15 minutos» for a 25-min dish, «600 g para 4» for a recipe of 300 g for 2, «unas 4 raciones» when the app said 5). Now the menu tools give the servings per meal and the minutes per dish (above), `get_recipe_details` takes `servings` (the meal's, from the menu tools) and returns the amounts already scaled (`scaleRound`, same bands as `scale_recipe`) with «cantidades para 4 raciones, la receta base es para 2» and a line saying the steps may quote the base amounts, and a line of `MENU_STATE_RULE` tells the model to cite those numbers, never estimate them, and say so when one is missing. The «Menu de esta semana» context block is unchanged (no minutes or servings): Mimo reads them with the tools. **Tests:** `tests/menuFacts.test.ts`, `tests/assistant-skills.test.ts` («menu tools cite the app's servings and minutes»).
- **Never «sin gluten» without the ingredients; the app's notices in the context (PRO-297, 2026-10-21).** Dani y Sara (coeliac) got «esa pizza y ese ramen deberían ser versiones sin gluten» (they had gluten), and both test households, after the PRO-205 notice said «díselo a Mimo», got «Desde aquí no veo ese aviso… escribid a hola@mimoia.com» and «Esos "Me apetece" no los puedo deshacer desde el chat». Now: (1) `ALLERGEN_CHECK_RULE` in every mode's instructions: Mimo never says a dish is free of gluten (or lactose, egg, nuts, shellfish, «apto para celiacos»…), nor that it «should be» a free-from version, without reading its ingredients with `get_recipe_details` in that turn; if one has it, it says so; if it can't look, it says it doesn't know. (2) The user context ends with «Avisos que la app y tu habeis mandado a este hogar (ultimos 14 dias)»: each `household_notices` row of the household with its type, the date (Madrid) and channel it went out (WhatsApp from Mimo, the card in the app from when it shows, closed) and the text it said (`noticesContextBlock`, `services/afinaNotice.ts`); a WhatsApp still waiting for the 24 h window isn't there yet. (3) `NOTICE_TASTE_RULE`: explain a notice from that block, never «no lo veo» or the email; remove «Me apetece» with the tools above. **Tests:** `tests/mimoAfina.test.ts`; battery cases `pizza-sin-gluten` (check `alergeno-sin-mirar`: fails on a free-from claim, `dice-sin-alergeno`, and, live, without `get_recipe_details`, `no-mira-receta`) and `aviso-afina-quitar` (check `aviso-afina`: fails on «no veo ese aviso», «no los puedo deshacer», hola@, `no-ve-aviso`, and, live, without `forget_taste_verdicts`, `no-quita`).
- **«Hoy cenamos otra cosa» is a note, not a recipe (PRO-1293, 2026-11-24).** Mimo's history (2026-11-23) showed that when a household said it had eaten, or would eat, something other than the menu's dish, Mimo created one or two estimated recipes and swapped the dish: 16–60 s and 0,22–0,33 € a turn (5 rounds); the one time it wrote a note, 9 s and 0,035 €. Now `IMPROVISED_MEAL_RULE` (`systemPrompt.ts`, a tool line that goes in when `mark_meal_eaten` or `set_meal_note` are in the turn) says: what was eaten or improvised for today goes in that meal as text with their words — `mark_meal_eaten` with `dish` if already eaten, `set_meal_note` if it's for later — never a recipe (`create_recipe`, or `newRecipe` in `swap_meal` / `add_dish`) nor a catalogue lookup; a recipe only when they ask to keep it («guárdamela») or dictate it; if the menu's dish stays unmade, one question offering another day. The descriptions of `create_recipe`, `newRecipe`, `set_meal_note` and `mark_meal_eaten`, and the WhatsApp lines for notes and for dishes not in the catalogue, say the same. Battery (check `comida-improvisada`, grader `improvisedMealProblems`): `cenamos-tortilla-nota` and `pedimos-pizza-nota` fail on a recipe created (live: `create_recipe` or `newRecipe`; CI: the reply saying so, `crea-receta`), nothing written in the meal (`no-apunta`), no offer of another day or more than one question; `lentejas-guardamela` (`keepsRecipe`) fails without `create_recipe` (`no-guarda-receta`). **Tests:** `tests/mimoImprovisedMeal.test.ts`.
- **The saved menu says what is done, not Mimo's memory (PRO-101, 2026-10-18).** A fixed block in every mode's prompt (`MENU_STATE_RULE`, `systemPrompt.ts`): before confirming, denying or correcting a change (this turn or an earlier one), Mimo looks at the saved menu — the «Menu de esta semana» block of the context, re-read on every message, or `get_todays_menu` / `get_weekly_menu`. If the menu shows it, it's done: no apology, no redo, no «no lo hice / no lo comprobé / me lo inventé». The context block is the current week, plus «Menu de la semana que viene» when next week already has a menu (PRO-1039); a day of next week is still looked up with the tools. The list and the pantry aren't in the context: Mimo checks them with their tool before saying something is marked. The menu Mimo reads (context and menu tools, `mealLinesForDay`) shows diners set by hand as `comida [solo hoy: 4]: …` («Solo hoy: 4» in the app). It also names every dish as the card does (PRO-296): a táper or an added dish saved without its name (before PRO-240) takes its recipe's (`fillRecipeNames`, `services/menuNames.ts`) instead of «receta sin nombre (del congelador)»; tests `tests/mimoTaperNames.test.ts` and `tests/assistant-skills.test.ts`. Why: the test households got «Antes te dije que lo había ajustado, pero este turno no veo que lo guardara», «No hice ningún cambio… me lo inventé», «el menú no me muestra cuántos comensales hay» and a Sunday reply that «corrected» a good next-week menu by reading this week's. **Tests:** `tests/mimoMenuState.test.ts` (rule in every mode's cached instructions, `[solo hoy: N]`, and the battery cases below fail on the real replies).
- **A link to the app it announces, the code writes it (PRO-1301, 2026-11-24).** «¿Me mandas el enlace de la app?» got «te paso el enlace» with no link, and the household had to ask again. Now, when the final reply announces a link («te paso / dejo / mando… el enlace», «aquí tienes el enlace», «en este enlace», «enlace:» at the end) and has none written (full or short form), the engine adds the app's link (`withPromisedAppLink`, `assistant/promisedLink.ts`, in `buildResponse`, web chat and WhatsApp): the page its announcing sentence talks about — the list (`/shopping`), Avisos, Ajustes, Perfil, recetas, the recipe the turn read when it says «receta» — and the week's menu otherwise; short form (`mimoia.com/menu`), right after a final «:» or on its own line, flagged `app_link_added`. A link that isn't to the app (the original video, an invitation, a blog) is left to the reply. WhatsApp's own app link line isn't added twice (`renderAssistantReply` skips one already in the text). **Tests:** `tests/promisedLink.test.ts`.
- **Mimo answers the last message (PRO-348, 2026-10-24).** After a change to the list or the menu, the test households' next question («lo mínimo para la semana del 26, en euros», «como se lo adapto a leo?») got «No he cambiado nada en el menú… Dime qué cambio quieres», «no he tocado el menú… sigue igual» or «En este mensaje no he cambiado nada: solo he mirado tu lista…». Now `NEW_QUESTION_RULE` (`systemPrompt.ts`, every mode): answer the newest message; what earlier turns did (⟦hecho: …⟧) was already told, so no summary again, no «no he cambiado nada / sigue igual», no «¿qué cambio quieres?». And the engine's claim note (`UNBACKED_CLAIM_NOTE`, PRO-239) says that when the last message is a question, Mimo answers only it, without talking about changes. Battery cases `minimo-euros-tras-cambio` and `adaptar-leo-tras-lista` (check `pregunta-nueva`: talking about changes or repeating the previous «Hecho:» fails, `contesta-lo-anterior`; each expects its answer: a figure in euros, cooking help for Leo). **Tests:** `tests/mimoNewQuestion.test.ts`.
- **«Efecto bola de nieve» in the web chat (PRO-170, 2026-10-18).** When a turn generates a week (`generate_weekly_menu` or `complete_onboarding`, `generatedMenuThisTurn`) in the household's first `SNOWBALL_WEEKS` = 2 generated weeks, `POST /assistant/:userId/chat` puts the same line as WhatsApp on top of Mimo's reply: «Las primeras semanas acertaré menos con los gustos de tu casa. Dime qué cambiarías y lo apunto: así iré acertando cada vez más. Es el efecto bola de nieve ⛄» (`withSnowballLine` in `menuText.ts`; count `generatedWeeksOf` in `menuWeek.ts`, see [whatsapp.md](./whatsapp.md)). A count that fails → no line.
- **Invitar tras una semana resuelta (PRO-164, D-059).** The same in the web chat and on WhatsApp (`chat()` in `engine.ts`, text and WhatsApp modes, never voice or the sign-up chat): once the household has a **resolved week** (a menu for that week and the shopping list used that week or the weekend before — the `/admin/metrics` definition), Mimo adds once, at the end of its next reply: «Esta semana ya la tienes resuelta. ¿Conoces a alguien que siempre está con el "¿qué cenamos?"? Te dejo tu enlace para invitarle: <enlace>» (the account's `?invita=` link, PRO-162). Only in a reply, so on WhatsApp always inside the 24-hour window — never a message of its own or a template. Stamped atomically (`users.invite_offer_at`, migration 0071) so it never repeats; a «no» as the next message (the whole message: «no», «no gracias», «paso»…; not «No, cámbiame el jueves») sets `users.invite_offer_declined_at` and it stays off for good. Never the same week as the /menu WhatsApp card (PRO-107): while that card is still to be shown it goes first; the week it is shown, the offer waits for a later resolved week. `services/inviteOffer.ts` (`shouldOfferInvite`, `isInviteOfferDeclined`); tests `inviteOffer.test.ts`, `inviteOffer.smoke.ts`. A failure leaves the reply as it was.
- **Invitar a la familia tras una semana resuelta (PRO-262, D-088).** Web chat and WhatsApp (text modes, never voice or the sign-up chat): once, after the household's first resolved week, Mimo ends its reply with «Esta semana ya tienes menú y lista. ¿Se los mando también a tu pareja o a quien vive contigo? …»; a «sí» → `invite_to_household`, a «no» switches it off for good. Only one-account households with 2 adults or more; inside the weekly aviso budget. `services/familyInviteMimo.ts`; full rules in [household.md](./household.md) → «Ofrecer la invitación».
- **Avisos offered one more time in the web chat (PRO-248, 2026-10-19).** A user with WhatsApp linked who was asked about the avisos and never answered gets, once their first menu is 7+ days old and the list was opened, one extra line at the end of Mimo's reply: «Por cierto: si quieres, también te escribo yo por WhatsApp (…). Dime «sí, avísame» y lo activo; solo te lo pregunto esta vez.» (`withReofferLine`, `claimOptInReoffer` in `services/whatsapp/optInOffer.ts`, shared with WhatsApp: once across both channels; never in `onboarding` mode). See [whatsapp.md](./whatsapp.md) → Opt-in. Never in the same reply as an invite offer (PRO-262 / PRO-164: `offerAdded`); on WhatsApp the same.
- **La pregunta de las sobras (PRO-285, Miguel 2026-10-20, to decide PRO-227 / D-069).** WhatsApp only, to each beta user with WhatsApp linked: the next time they write to Mimo, while the link's last inbound message is inside Meta's 24 h window (`SERVICE_WINDOW_MS`; a reply, so no template and not counted in the weekly aviso budget), Mimo adds ONCE at the end of its reply, word for word: «Una pregunta rápida para Mimoia: ¿os sobró algo de la última cena que hicisteis en casa? Y si sobró, ¿qué hicisteis con ello: táper para el día siguiente, al congelador, se lo comió alguien o acabó tirándose? Contesta como te salga: nos ayuda a decidir si te pregunto por las sobras.» Stamped atomically in `users.leftovers_question_at` (migration `0092_leftovers_question`), so never twice. Never in the web chat, the first steps (WhatsApp sign-up) or a reply that carries another offer: `appendReplyOffers` in `engine.ts` adds at most one line (family → other household → leftovers question) and `offerAdded` holds back the avisos re-offer (PRO-248) that turn. Off by itself from 2026-11-08 (Madrid) or once each user has seen it. The user's next message is saved verbatim in `user_feedback` with `tag = 'sobras'` (channel `whatsapp`, once per user; `leave_feedback` unchanged), counted by `/admin/feedback` (`byTag`, see [Feedback](./feedback.md)); Customer Success summarises them without names. `services/leftoversQuestion.ts`, tests `leftoversQuestion.test.ts`.
- **La encuesta de Sean Ellis (PRO-334).** WhatsApp only, once per person, to beta adults with two weeks of real use who wrote to Mimo there in the last 7 days: the last line `appendReplyOffers` may add (after the leftovers question, never with another offer); the next message is filed as an opinion tagged `sean_ellis` with its option. Answered talking to Mimo anywhere, the `answer_sean_ellis` tool (group `hogar`, words «decepcionad…») files it. Who, texts and counts: [Feedback](./feedback.md) → «The Sean Ellis survey». `services/seanEllisSurvey.ts`, tests `seanEllisSurvey.test.ts`.
- The card or app link comes from the most visual skill of the turn (menu/list/recipe > nutrition > confirmation > text). An empty final text says "Hecho." (never a tool summary, which may carry notes meant for the model).
