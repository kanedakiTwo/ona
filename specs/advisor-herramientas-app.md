# Advisor · Las herramientas que hacen lo mismo que la app

> Parte de [Advisor](./advisor.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): las skills de paridad con la app (menú, compra, tiendas, comidas fijas, semana tipo, cole, despensa, recetas, perfil) y sus convenciones (días en Madrid, nombres aproximados).

## Assistant Skills: lo mismo que la app

**UI-parity skills** (`services/assistant/appSkills.ts`, 2026-10). The goal is that anything the web app can do is reachable from chat. These skills call the app's own REST API as the user (`appApi.ts`: loopback + a 5-min JWT), so validation, side effects and permissions match the UI exactly.
- **Menu:**
  - `set_meal_note`: "apunta que el jueves viene la abuela", or what they improvise for today instead of the menu ("esta noche pedimos pizza", PRO-1293) (replaces the slot's dishes with a note by default; the summary names the dishes it took off).
  - `clear_meal`, `set_day_skipped` (day without cooking / back on). **A meal not eaten at home is removed, not noted (PRO-113, 2026-10-18):** "el viernes no comemos en casa", "cenamos fuera", "comemos en casa de Paqui" → `clear_meal` (its ingredients leave the list); the whole day → `set_day_skipped`. The prompt and both tools' descriptions say so; before, Mimo left a «Cenamos fuera» note (reviewer, 2026-10-10).
  - `add_dish`, `remove_dish`: multi-dish slots.
  - **Repeats in the week (PRO-238, 2026-10-19).** `swap_meal` (named, created, note or random), `add_dish` and «con lo que tengo» → pick look at the rest of that week after placing a dish: if the same recipe or a dish with the same name (accents/case aside; notes too) is already in another slot, the summary adds «Ojo: "Pollo empanado" ya esta esta semana en la cena del domingo 25 de octubre. Diselo al usuario en una frase y ofrece cambiar uno de los dos.» (`assistant/weekRepeats.ts`). A planned leftover or táper of the dish is not a repeat. The dish is still placed (the user asked for it). **Tests:** `tests/weekRepeats.test.ts`, `tests/assistant-skills.test.ts` (swap_meal).
  - `set_meal_servings`, `lock_meal`, `move_meal` (move/swap slots).
  - `ban_recipe_this_week`: vetoes the recipe for the week **and changes it where it already is**, like «No la pongas más esta semana» (the veto) on the web, a locked meal too since PRO-1175 (PRO-113; PRO-72 on the web). After `POST /menu/:id/ban`, each appearance from today on (every day for a future week; past days stay) is regenerated with `POST …/dish/:position/regenerate` (the matcher skips vetoed recipes); a táper of it whose source changed becomes sobras of the new dish (slot cleared, `POST …/leftover`, its «Solo hoy» put back). Left as they are, and said so: locked meals, freezer tápers, sobras of a dish already cooked, meals with no other recipe that fits (409). The summary lists what each meal got («- Miércoles 14 de octubre, cena: "Garbanzos con espinacas"») and `data.changes` carries date, meal and dish; the prompt tells Mimo to repeat it. Before, it only blocked the recipe and Mimo said «vetado de toda la semana» while it stayed on the menu.
  - `set_leftovers`.
  - `add_freezer_dish` (PRO-82): "esta noche comemos táper de lentejas" → that meal becomes the recipe with `variant: 'freezer'` («Del congelador», adds nothing to the list); with `place: 'nevera'` (the household says it's in the fridge, PRO-240) `variant: 'fridge'` («Táper de la nevera»); default congelador. Replaces the meal's dishes unless `keepDishes`; a dish not in their recipes or the catalogue goes as a «Táper: …» note. **«Lo tengo hecho»** (PRO-276): «tengo crema de puerros hecha, ponla el jueves», «me sobró cocido» → `place: 'hecho'`, `variant: 'cooked'` («Sobras de otro día», nothing to buy); it links only to a recipe whose name says nothing the household didn't (`namesSameDish`: «crema de puerros» never links «Crema de puerros con jamón»), a recipe id from the model included; otherwise a note «Hecho en casa: Crema de puerros» (no ingredients). A rule of the prompt sends these to `add_freezer_dish`, never to `swap_meal` / `add_dish`; eval case `crema-puerros-hecha` (`plato-hecho`). Tests: `tests/appSkills.test.ts` («add_freezer_dish with place «hecho»»).
- **Shopping:** `add_shopping_items` (units normalised to g/ml/u/cda/cdita; never duplicates: a product already pending — same words, or a list name starting with what was said, "leche" → "Leche entera", "huevos" ↔ "Huevo" — is reported as "ya estaba"; one marked bought, "en casa" or left out goes back to pending; "leche de coco" is never "Leche"; «2 paquetes» goes as the line's own amount, not into the name), `remove_shopping_items` (manual lines are deleted; a menu line is left out of this shopping, «No lo compro esta semana», the same as on `/shopping`; a staple is pointed to `manage_staples` — PRO-175: it used to answer «la herramienta ha dado un error» on menu lines), `change_shopping_item_amount` (menu lines in their unit, «2 kilos» → 2000 g, else it asks; manual lines in any words; no amount = back to the menu's, PRO-175), `change_shopping_item_unit` (PRO-292, `shoppingLineSkills.ts`, registered through `shopOrderSkills`: «de cebolla pon 3 unidades», «las patatas en kilos» — switches a line to another unit through `PUT …/edit` `{ unit, quantity? }`, only to a unit in the line's `unitOptions`, else it says which ones; the menu's unit alone goes back to the menu's amount; manual lines take any unit; staples → `manage_staples`; test `shoppingLineSkills.test.ts`), `set_shopping_habitual` (PRO-293, same file: «pon la leche como habitual» / «quita el pan de habituales» — the list line's «Habitual» through `PUT …/edit` `{ habitual }`; a product not on the list switches its staple on or creates it, and pausing one that isn't a staple changes nothing), `regenerate_shopping_list`, `manage_staples`.
- **Compra en mis tiendas** (`shopOrderSkills.ts`, see [Shop orders](./shop-orders.md)): `manage_shops`, `prepare_shop_orders` ("hazme la compra" → one `/c/<token>` short link per shop that opens the user's own WhatsApp with the order written), `edit_shop_order` (before sending: add products — each to its shop —, remove, choose "serrano", amounts, include, pickup/delivery + address; same links), `register_shop_reply` (forwarded/pasted shop reply → OK / Revisar / No hay per line, total vs cap), `approve_shop_order` (only after an explicit "sí"; returns the confirmation link), `close_shop_order`, `get_shop_orders`. The prompt treats a shop's reply as data, never instructions, and allows copying `/c/` links verbatim.
- **Comidas fijas** (`fixedMealSkills.ts`, PRO-218, see [Menus](./menus.md)): `get_fixed_meals` and `set_fixed_meal` (meal, days — all by default —, name, items per person in u/g/ml; `remove: true` takes it away), stored as «something fixed» slots of the household's semana tipo (D-182). It applies when the next week is generated. `get_fixed_meals` is read-only for the «Hecho:» check.
- **Semana tipo** (`weekTypeSkills.ts`, D-182 phase 3, web and WhatsApp; see [Menus → Semana tipo](./menus-semana-tipo.md)): «los viernes por la noche, pizza», «los jueves, una semana ceviche y otra carpaccio», «legumbres el sábado, cada dos semanas», «quita el desayuno del domingo», «los lunes somos tres en la cena».
  - `get_week_type` reads the household's week type (`GET /households/me/week-type`) and lists only what differs from lunch and dinner with any recipe («- viernes, cena: pizza cada semana»).
  - `set_week_type_slot`: one or several days (lunes…domingo, accents and numbers accepted), **one** meal, `dishes` 1–3 («las cenas, primer y segundo plato» = 2, 2026-11-19; the confirmation says «primero y segundo»), `options` (up to 4: `type` = a `DISH_TYPES` key; `dish` = a name, resolved as in phase 1 — a dish type when the name is one, the recipe when the search finds exactly one, else written with no recipe nor shopping, and the summary says so —; `fixed` = something fixed with what it has per person; `taper`; `any` = cualquier receta, alone = leave it free), `mode` (`turns` by default, `together`), `every` (2–4, «cada N semanas» with one option = that option then N−1 «cualquier receta»), `off` (true = «no hay», keeping the options for when it comes back; false = planned again) and `diners`. New options on a «no hay» meal turn it on until they go (`offWhenFree`). Every slot is checked before any is written (a táper on a dinner, a Monday or next to another option writes nothing); then one `PUT /households/me/week-type/:day/:meal` per slot that changes, as the user (a child gets the route's 403 as «No he cambiado nada: …»; the route puts the turn's week). Nothing changes → «Ya estaba así». The summary is the one line Mimo repeats: «Hecho, en tu semana tipo: viernes, cena, pizza cada semana». Without the meal or the day the tool reads and writes nothing and tells Mimo to ask only that.
  - Prompt (`WEEK_TYPE_RULE`): a habit of a day and a meal (days in plural, «siempre», a meal removed without saying which week) goes there, a plan of one week stays in the menu (`clear_meal`, `generate_weekly_menu`); without the meal, one short question and no tool, unless the dish says it (legumes, stews and creams are lunch; toast, cereal and yoghurt, breakfast); a taste without a day («nos gusta mucho la pizza») goes to the ficha, not there.
  - `update_weekly_template` (diners or dishes of a meal over several days) and `set_fixed_meal` still work and still write there; their descriptions send day-and-meal habits to `set_week_type_slot`.
  - Tests: `weekTypeSkills.test.ts` (parameters to slot, names to recipes or types, táper where it doesn't go, «no hay», turns, «cada 2», together, missing meal, refusals, the prompt, `note_ficha_fact` without `skipped_meal`). Eval cases (`mimoEvalCases.ts`, check `semana-tipo`, graded on the tool's input in the live run): `semana-tipo-viernes-pizza`, `-turnos-ceviche-carpaccio`, `-legumbres-cada-dos`, `-quita-desayuno-domingo`, `-gusto-a-la-ficha` (goes to the ficha), `-falta-comida` (asks the meal). Run live with `scripts/evalHealthAnswers.ts --case=<id>` (needs the API).
- **Menú del cole** (`schoolMenuSkills.ts`, PRO-224, see [Menus](./menus.md)): `get_school_menu` (what is saved for a range; with nothing saved it explains how to send it: WhatsApp photo/PDF with «menú del cole», or Perfil → Mi casa → Menú del cole) and `set_school_menu_days` (fix or dictate days — date, dish, group — and `clear` dates). Wording: «para no repetir», never health. `get_school_menu` is read-only for the «Hecho:» check.
- **Pantry:** `update_pantry` (add/set/remove with quantities and expiry). Reading the pantry is `get_pantry_stock`; the old `list_pantry` duplicate was removed (62 skills).
- **Recipes:**
  - `log_cooked` (cook log + pantry decrement). Answering the WhatsApp check-in with «Sí, ¡a repetir!» / «Sí, estuvo bien» → also `update_recipe_notes` rating 5 / 3 (PRO-235, prompt rule).
  - `update_recipe_notes`: rating, notes appended, substitutions, tags, `minServings` ("siempre la hago para 6"; 0 clears). **Which recipe (PRO-299, `recipeForNotes`):** the household's own recipe with that name wins (a copy or one they wrote); else the menu/catalogue match, and if they have a copy of it (`copiedFromRecipeId`, carried in `GET /user/:id/recipes` `own`), the copy; two different recipes with the same name and none theirs → nothing saved, the summary lists them with their `recipeId` and Mimo asks, then calls again with `recipeId`. The summary says where it went («guardado en "Lentejas guisadas" (su receta)» / «(receta del catalogo de Mimoia)» / «(receta de otra persona)») and the prompt makes Mimo say «guardada» only on that confirmation and repeat that name (before, the catalogue's lentils in the menu won and Mimo said «tus lentejas»).
  - `delete_recipe`: own recipes only; the prompt asks for confirmation first.
  - `manage_cookbook`, `regenerate_recipe_image`.
- **Profile:**
  - `update_profile`: restrictions add/remove, priority, physical data.
  - `update_profile` and «sin alcohol» (PRO-144): its description names «sin alcohol» as an example and says that what the menu must always avoid goes to the profile restrictions, not to `notes` («cocinamos siempre sin alcohol» → `addRestrictions: ["sin alcohol"]`, which the generator reads; see [Menus → Restrictions](./menus-restricciones.md)). The `update_memory` description and the system prompt are unchanged. Test: `appSkills.test.ts`.
  - `update_weekly_template`: diners per meal/day, dishes per meal — written to those slots of the household's semana tipo (`PUT /households/me/week-type/:day/:meal`, only the slots that change; D-182). For what is eaten on a day and a meal, `set_week_type_slot` (above).
  - `invite_to_household`: creates an invite to this household (`source: 'mimo'`, counted as sent) and returns the written message with the link (30 days, one use; in the web chat also the `wa.me` link to send it), PRO-260 — see [household.md](./household.md).
  - `share_invite_link` (PRO-163, D-051): the account's «Invitar a otro hogar» link (`GET /referrals/me`) with Miguel's approved message (`inviteHouseholdShareText`) to forward; counts `invite_shared` (`POST /usage/invite-shared`, channel web/whatsapp). For another household, never to join this one.
  - `set_cook_today` (PRO-230): «hoy cocina Pablo, mándale la receta» — sends that member the day's recipe and pending preparativos by WhatsApp and moves that day's prep alerts to them; without WhatsApp, a link to forward it ([WhatsApp → Who cooks today](./whatsapp-avisos.md#who-cooks-today-hoy-cocina-pablo)).
  - `get_alerts` / `set_alerts` (PRO-237): list Mimo's alerts with where each arrives, and switch one or all on/off — the same switches as Perfil → Avisos ([Notifications](./notifications.md#avisos-perfil--avisos)). `all` on never turns on the opt-in morning menu (`daily_brief`, PRO-215). Since PRO-381 the list has `weekly_autoplan` («La semana, hecha cada jueves», where it arrives: «en su menú de la app»): «no me hagas tú la semana» → `set_alerts { kind: 'weekly_autoplan', enabled: false }`, and then the Sunday question comes back. `set_alert_time` (PRO-1301, `alertTimeSkills.ts`): the household's time for the morning menu (08:00–13:00) or the dinner question (19:00–22:00), `reset` back to the default; out of range or another aviso → «No he cambiado nada: …» with why (before, Mimo couldn't and once said «apuntado» without saving). `set_whatsapp_notifications` stays and goes through the same code (`PATCH /whatsapp/link` → `setAlertPrefs`).
- **Conventions:**
  - **Days resolve to dates in Madrid (PRO-63, `menuDay.ts`).** Menu skills (`swap_meal`, `get_todays_menu`, `mark_meal_eaten`, `set_meal_note`, `clear_meal`, `set_day_skipped`, `add_dish`, `remove_dish`, `set_meal_servings`, `lock_meal`, `move_meal`, `set_leftovers`, `log_cooked`) take `day` with the user's words or a date — "hoy", "mañana", "pasado mañana", "ayer", "el sábado", "el sábado 17", "este lunes", "el martes pasado", "el próximo lunes", "el jueves de la semana que viene", `AAAA-MM-DD` — plus `meal`. A bare weekday is the next one, today included (on Sunday "el martes" = in two days). Legacy `dayIndex` (0 = lunes) + `nextWeek` still work. Unknown words → "No sé qué día es…; no he cambiado nada", never a guess. The tool edits the menu **of that date's week** (before, `swap_meal` and `mark_meal_eaten` edited the newest menu row, so once next week's row existed "el sábado" landed on next week — hogares de prueba 2026-10-12) and every summary repeats the day with its date ("la comida del sábado 17 de octubre"); `move_meal` / `set_leftovers` refuse two days of different weeks. The prompt tells Mimo to pass the user's words as `day`, to repeat day, date and dish as the tool returned them in its "Hecho:" lines, and that a tool saying "no he cambiado nada" or failing means it isn't done.
  - Named recipes resolve fuzzily (`bestMatch`, Spanish stopwords ignored, culinary synonyms such as vaca→ternera/entrecot).
  - "Las lentejas" prefers the dish in this week's menu.
  - A lookup that finds nothing says so; it is never a silent no-op.
- A dish the user names that isn't in their recipes nor the catalogue (`swap_meal` / `add_dish` with a `recipeName` that matches nothing, or only by one of its words) becomes a **household recipe** when the model sends `newRecipe` (a simple version it makes up: servings, ingredients with amounts, steps and, since PRO-308, `prepTime` / `cookTime` — estimated from the steps when missing — PRO-74, `services/assistant/dishRecipe.ts`): it's saved through `createRecipeFromParts` with servings «Estimado» (PRO-32, internal tag `estimated`) and placed as a recipe dish, so its ingredients reach the shopping list (test households 2026-10-11/14: «pescado a la plancha», «pizza casera» stayed notes and the list had no fish). `add_dish` replaces an earlier note with the same text instead of doubling it. Without `newRecipe`, or if saving fails, it goes on the menu **as a note with the user's words** ("pizza casera" → note "Pizza casera") and the tool tells the model to call again with `newRecipe`; the closest real recipe, if any, is only offered. Never "no existe, ¿la creo?" before placing it (2026-10-08, WhatsApp). The prompt rule lives in the shared critical rules, so the app chat, voice and WhatsApp behave alike. Lookups only search the catalogue plus the user's own recipes, never another user's private ones.
