# Advisor · Las herramientas de Mimo (menú, recetas y ficha)

> Parte de [Advisor](./advisor.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): las skills de lectura y de menú (`get_todays_menu`, `generate_weekly_menu`, `swap_meal`…), las de recetas y las de la ficha del hogar.

## Assistant Skills

The assistant can call back-end skills (function calling). Each skill has a name, description, JSON schema parameters, and an executor. Current skills:

- `get_todays_menu` — read the menu for today in Madrid time (or any `day`, see Conventions). Reads only the menu of the resolved week (no fallback to another week: "No hay menu para la semana del …") and lists every dish of each slot from `dishes[]`, recipes and notes, e.g. "comida · 2 raciones: Lentejas (40 min en total, 15 de trabajo) + Ensalada (5 min); cena: Cenamos fuera". **Since PRO-183 (2026-10-19)** each meal carries the servings the menu page shows (the slot's «Solo hoy: N», or the household's adults + kids plus its planned leftovers, mirror of the web's `batchServings`) and each cooked dish its total (`totalTime ?? prepTime`, as the page) and hands-on minutes (`activeTime`) — `assistant/menuFacts.ts`; leftovers, tápers and notes carry no minutes. If those reads fail, the bare lines (`services/menuText.ts`) go out. Fixes the pre-2026-10 bug where it read the legacy `slot.recipeName` and answered "no hay comidas planificadas" for every day.
- `get_weekly_menu` — read the whole week's menu (this week, or next with `nextWeek`), day by day with dates and «sin cocinar» days, with the same servings and minutes as `get_todays_menu` (PRO-183); data `{ menuId, weekStart, days, skippedDays }`. No menu or nothing planned → text without menu data. Every dish of each meal («Arroz blanco + Curry de berenjena»), and, read for this week, next week's too when it has a menu («La semana que viene (del …) ya tiene menu:», PRO-1039; data stays this week's). On WhatsApp the reply is the week picture with a «Tu menú de esta semana» caption, not the model's list (see [whatsapp.md](./whatsapp.md)); on the web the model writes the week and its recipes link. Added 2026-10-15; the prompt says to use it (one call) instead of reading day by day.
- `get_recipe_details` — pull a recipe by name, or **the one on the menu** with `day` + `meal` («la receta de hoy», «¿lo de hoy lleva pan?»; PRO-349): that meal's recipe, never another with a similar name; a meal with no recipe says so and reads nothing else, a meal with several asks for `meal`/`recipeName`. By name, this week's menu goes first (from today on, `menuRecipeDishes`), then their recipes and the catalogue; a recipe from the menu starts with «Es la receta del menu (comida del jueves 22 de octubre).» (Carmen y Antonio, 2026-10-22: Mimo answered from «Garbanzos con espinacas y huevo»). Returns ingredients, **numbered steps** (from `recipe_steps`), servings, total and hands-on minutes (PRO-183), tips and the original link (video or web, `source_url`), bounded to 4 000 chars (`recipeDetailsSummary`). For a coeliac household a recipe with a brand-dependent cured meat also carries «Aviso para este hogar: Elige embutido sin gluten.» (`data.dietNotices`, PRO-196, same as the web ficha). Until 2026-10-12 it read a `recipe.steps` column that doesn't exist, so the model never saw a step and couldn't walk anyone through a recipe or give its video
- `get_weekly_nutrition` — calorie and macro summary for the week
- `get_shopping_list` — reads the same rolling list `/shopping` shows (today → next Sunday: menu items, staples, manual items) and names what's still missing, sorted by aisle, with "Ya comprado: N. En casa: M." ("¿qué me falta?"). Before 2026-10-11 it re-aggregated the latest menu and only told the model a count, so manual items and bought ones were invisible **PRO-1039 (2026-11-24):** every pending line (no cap: a cap of 60 left the manual lines, which go last, out — «Pan de barra · MANUAL», «60 de las 69 cosas»), each under its week as `/shopping` labels it («Esta semana», «La que viene», «Para las dos semanas» with what each week asks, «A mano o básicos (sin semana)»), and the names of what is bought and at home («Ya comprado (23): …», not just a count). `week: esta | que_viene` gives only that week with its amounts, like the chips («44 huevos» were both weeks; next week 25). `bestMatch` takes the same words singular or plural first («tomates cherry» → Tomate cherry, not Tomate). Pure builder `shoppingListSummary` (`appSkills.ts`); tests `tests/appSkills.test.ts`.
- `suggest_recipes` — search with several conditions at once (2026-11-19, `recipeCriteria.ts`; before, the whole sentence had to be inside a name or a tag and 10 of 17 searches found nothing): «calabacín rápida menos de 30 minutos», «cena ligera sin arroz sin garbanzos». It reads the time («menos de 30 min», «rápida» = 30, «media hora»), «sin X» / «ni X» (a family counts whole; also diets and allergens), «ligera», the meal and «postre»; every other word must be in the name, tags, dish types or ingredients. Also `maxMinutes`, `exclude`, `light`. Never one that breaks the household's allergies, diet, dislikes or the ficha's, in season, no sweets for lunch or dinner; with none that has every word, the closest ones, said as such. `cook_with_what_i_have` — «tengo calabacín y huevos, ¿qué hago?» (chat, voice and WhatsApp; PRO-31): the same search as the «Con lo que tengo» filter on `/recipes` (`services/withWhatIHave.ts`) with free-text ingredient names (word match, plural-tolerant; up to 8 from Mimo, more are ignored and said so); **«¿Qué hago hoy con lo que tengo?» (PRO-222, D-069):** answers with **3 recipes**, each with its total time, what it uses and what's missing, ranked by how many of the named ingredients they use, then by how little is missing; basics (sal, aceite, agua, pimienta, harina) never counted; same allergy/diet/dislike rules as `suggest_recipes` **plus the week's menu vetoes** (`bannedRecipeIds`). From a fridge/pantry photo (WhatsApp; a photo with a question goes to Mimo, who sees it — the web chat takes no photos) Mimo passes what it sees with `fromPhoto: true` and the reply starts «En la foto he visto: … Si me he equivocado en algo, dímelo y lo cambio.» before the recipes. When the user picks one, `pickRecipeId` (+ optional `day`/`meal`; default today's next meal — lunch until 16:00 Madrid, then dinner) puts it in that meal, replacing its dishes; a week with no menu gets a menu row with only that dish; vetoed recipes and locked meals are refused. Works without a weekly menu. Tests `withWhatIHave.test.ts`
- `search_recipes` — search by name substring; a query with conditions (time, «sin X», ligera, a meal) goes to `suggest_recipes`' search
- `get_recipe_of_the_day` (PRO-1099, `recipeOfTheDaySkills.ts`) — «¿cuál es la receta del día?»: today's (or `date`'s) through `GET /recipe-of-the-day`, the endpoint of `/receta-del-dia`; on «hoy no hay» it says which day the last one is from. Adds the ficha's allergens and tells Mimo it isn't picked for the household, so it warns against their allergies or diet. Web chat and WhatsApp alike. See [Recipe of the day](./recipe-of-the-day.md); test `recipeOfTheDay.test.ts`
- `complete_onboarding` — WhatsApp-first sign-up only: saves adults, kids and how they cook (required: `cookingStyle`, the web's four answers since 2026-11-03, or `cookingFreq` only for a «poco» that fits none; `cookingFreq` is then read from the style) plus favourite dishes and priority only if the person said them (optional since PRO-245, D-064; no default priority since 2026-11-03) like the web onboarding (`saveOnboarding`, never health data), marks `onboardingDone` and generates the first week (same as `generate_weekly_menu`). Refuses (summary, no write) when a required answer is missing («como cocinan») or the account is already onboarded. The WhatsApp prompt adds `WHATSAPP_ONBOARDING_PROMPT` while the user isn't onboarded (`ChatOptions.onboarding`), plus `WHATSAPP_ONBOARDING_FAVORITES_ASK` (ask once for 2–3 favourite dishes) when the account has no «Me apetece»/«Me encanta» yet, or `WHATSAPP_ONBOARDING_FAVORITES_KNOWN` (see [WhatsApp](./whatsapp.md))
- `set_taper` (PRO-223, `taperSkills.ts`) — «mañana me llevo táper»: that day's lunch (or the `meal` given) becomes the táper of the dinner before through `POST …/taper` (`count`, `remove: true` undoes it); see [Menus](./menus.md) → «El táper de mañana»
- `generate_weekly_menu` — full menu generation for the user. `nextWeek: true` targets next Monday (used when the user says "la semana que viene" or accepts the WhatsApp Sunday nudge). Enqueues prep alerts like `POST /menu/generate`. A week that already has dishes or notes is **never remade** without `replaceExisting: true` (the user confirmed remaking the whole week): otherwise it changes nothing and points to `swap_meal` / `add_dish` / `set_meal_note` — asking for a dish never regenerates the week (PRO-63; an empty week row, which the web creates just by opening it, is generated without asking). **Acierto (PRO-155):** after generating it runs the menu evaluator ([Menus](./menus.md) → Acierto) and appends to its summary «Acierto con sus gustos: N/100 (alto|medio|bajo)», what's met, what «no está en el catálogo», what «no cabía esta semana» and, when low, the ways to add recipes this household hasn't used (photo, link, writing it); `data.fit` carries the verdict. The prompt (`systemPrompt.ts`, «Acierto del menu») gives Mimo a guide, not a fixed text: alto → sure and brief, naming one or two of their dishes; medio → says what it couldn't fit and why, and offers to put it in; bajo → humble, «con las recetas que tengo no he podido acercarme mucho a lo que os gusta (carbonara, rodaballo…)», and invites to add their recipes explaining one unused way, different each time. Without the line Mimo doesn't talk about it, and it never says only five favourites fit (there's no cap: `complete_onboarding` takes up to 30). Replay of the three levels: `scripts/replayWhatsAppTurns.ts --fit-cases=scripts/replayMenuFitCases.json --user=<uuid>` (needs the real model)
- `plan_freezer_items` (PRO-228, `freezerSkills.ts`) — «Sí, mételo» to the monthly freezer review: puts what has been 4+ weeks in the freezer (or the `names` given) in the next two weeks' menus, one per day from tomorrow, in a free dinner/lunch; summary lists what went where, what had no recipe or room, and weeks without a menu. `update_pantry` / `mark_in_stock` take `location` (nevera, congelador, despensa); see [Pantry](./pantry.md)
- `generate_weekly_menu` — full menu generation for the user. `nextWeek: true` targets next Monday (used when the user says "la semana que viene" or accepts the WhatsApp Sunday nudge). **Antes de planificar (PRO-225):** `mealsOut: [{ day, meal? }]` and `guests: [{ day, meal?, diners }]` (0 = lunes) shape only that week — the meal out is dropped, the guests' day gets that many diners — and never the plantilla; when the model passes neither, `answer` (the user's text) is read by `parseWeekPlanAnswer`. `fridge_text` (PRO-229, PRO-295) is what's left in the fridge, the whole text as said (or the photo in one sentence): read by Claude into foods for the despensa («nevera») and weighed in the menu (`fridge: string[]` is the old shape, joined). If it talks about an allergy or intolerance the tool **doesn't generate** and tells Mimo to ask whether to save it as a restriction, then call again with `fridge_health: 'save' | 'skip'` (and `fridge_restrictions` if the person says which); in the chat, saving needs the health consent already given in the profile (otherwise the consent reply); over WhatsApp health data is never saved (the WhatsApp reply), so only `skip` generates there. The prompt forbids saying the menu is done until the tool generates it. The summary adds «Solo esta semana (la plantilla no cambia): sin la cena del jueves; sábado: 6 comensales; en la nevera: medio calabacín, nata». The prompt tells Mimo to pass what the user said about meals out or guests (also when answering the Sunday nudge) and not to ask again. **Comensales de la semana (PRO-275):** `diners` (1–24) puts that many diners on every meal of that week only («toda la semana: 2 comensales» in the summary; per-day `guests` still win; the plantilla is not written). When the user asks for the menu with a number of diners different from their household («hazme el menú para dos» in a household of 4) and doesn't say for how long, the prompt makes Mimo ask once «¿Solo esta semana o siempre?» ([[opciones: Solo esta semana | Siempre]]): «solo esta semana» → `generate_weekly_menu` with `diners`; «siempre» → `update_weekly_template` (those diners on every planned meal) and then generate; if they already said it («esta semana somos dos») it doesn't ask. Eval case `menu-para-dos-hogar-de-4` (`mimoEvalCases.ts`, check `menu-comensales`: generating with the usual diners fails). **Con mis favoritas (PRO-377):** `only_favorites: true` («hazme la semana con mis favoritas») generates the week out of the household's favourites (`onlyFavorites`, [Menus](./menus.md)); the summary adds «Todos los platos salen de sus favoritas» or «Tenia pocas: N platos son nuevos (del catalogo); diselo», and `data.favoritesShort` carries N. Test: `assistant-skills.test.ts`. Enqueues prep alerts like `POST /menu/generate`. A week that already has dishes or notes is **never remade** without `replaceExisting: true` (the user confirmed remaking the whole week): otherwise it changes nothing and points to `swap_meal` / `add_dish` / `set_meal_note` — asking for a dish never regenerates the week (PRO-63; an empty week row, which the web creates just by opening it, is generated without asking). **Acierto (PRO-155):** after generating it runs the menu evaluator ([Menus](./menus.md) → Acierto) and appends to its summary «Acierto con sus gustos: N/100 (alto|medio|bajo)», what's met, what «no está en el catálogo», what «no cabía esta semana» and, when low, the ways to add recipes this household hasn't used (photo, link, writing it); `data.fit` carries the verdict. The prompt (`systemPrompt.ts`, «Acierto del menu») gives Mimo a guide, not a fixed text: alto → sure and brief, naming one or two of their dishes; medio → says what it couldn't fit and why, and offers to put it in; bajo → humble, «con las recetas que tengo no he podido acercarme mucho a lo que os gusta (carbonara, rodaballo…)», and invites to add their recipes explaining one unused way, different each time. Without the line Mimo doesn't talk about it, and it never says only five favourites fit (there's no cap: `complete_onboarding` takes up to 30). Replay of the three levels: `scripts/replayWhatsAppTurns.ts --fit-cases=scripts/replayMenuFitCases.json --user=<uuid>` (needs the real model)
- `swap_meal` — replace a single meal slot of the menu of the resolved week (never "the latest menu", see Conventions); the slot keeps its «Solo hoy» diners (`servings`, PRO-102); it confirms only after reading the dish back from the saved row ("No he podido guardar el cambio…" otherwise) and returns `data.change` = week, date, dayIndex, meal and dish. Without a `recipeId`/`recipeName` parameter, picks like the app's «Cambiar» (2026-11-19, `loadSlotPickContext` + `pickForSlot`, see [Menus → Single-Meal Regeneration](./menus-ajustar.md)): every member's allergies and diet (PRO-305), the ficha (whole families, weekly limits, light meals, what was saved as a note), no sweet, a working day's time, a main food not already in the week; if nothing fits it changes nothing and says so (Mimo asks what they fancy). When the user names a recipe ("pon la fabada de mi madre el lunes"), the model passes `recipeName` (or `recipeId` when known) and the skill pins that recipe directly without the matcher. Recipes the user owns win over system recipes when names collide
  - A dish on a «sin cocinar» day reactivates it (PRO-349, see [Menus](./menus.md)); its summary — and those of `add_dish`, `set_meal_note`, `add_freezer_dish` and the `cook_with_what_i_have` pick — adds «El sábado 24 de octubre estaba marcado sin cocinar: ya vuelve a estar activo.»
- `toggle_favorite` — favorite/unfavorite a recipe
- `mark_meal_eaten` — log that the user actually ate a meal (records `eaten` + `eatenAt` on the slot; the menu card then shows «✓ Comido», PRO-185). **What they ate instead (PRO-1293):** with `dish` («al final cenamos tortilla») the meal's dishes are replaced by a note with their words and the slot marked eaten, through the app's routes (`markEatenAsNote` in `appSkills.ts`: DELETE the slot, POST the note, PATCH `eaten`); no recipe is created and the summary names the dishes left unmade so Mimo can offer another day
- `create_recipe` — save a new user recipe. If the user asks for a basic recipe without details ("créalo tú"), the model writes a reasonable version and saves it without further questions. Since 2026-10 it persists through `createRecipeFromParts` (`services/recipeImport.ts`): the same ingredient matching (USDA auto-create) and soft-lint persist as URL/photo imports, with safe defaults (2 servings, lunch+dinner, all seasons; `normalizeRecipeParts`). **Minutes (PRO-308):** `prepTime` and `cookTime` are required in the tool (Mimo estimates them if the user didn't say); if they still come missing, they are estimated from the steps with the catalogue's rule (`estimateRecipeTimes` in `catalogRecipeTimes.ts`: each step's minutes, or those in its text; without them 5 min if hands-on, 15 if it goes by itself — oven, simmering, resting…), and the recipe is saved with total and hands-on («de trabajo», `activeTime`) time, so the menu can respect the time to cook. The summary says «Tiempo: N min en total, M de trabajo (estimado de los pasos)». Estimated minutes get the internal tag `times-estimated` (PRO-1037), and the recipe details Mimo reads say «…, estimado» next to them. Household recipes saved without minutes before PRO-308 are filled on boot with the same estimator and tag (`applyHouseholdRecipeTimes` in `householdRecipeTimes.ts`, after `db:migrate`; only empty fields, idempotent; a recipe with no steps and no times stays without). Eval case `receta-merluza-sin-minutos` (check `receta-minutos`). The old raw INSERT skipped `servings` (NOT NULL) and failed in prod; the mock-DB tests never caught it.
- `edit_recipe` — author-only field edits on a user recipe (name, prepTime, cookTime, difficulty, notes, tips). Voice cannot edit ingredients/steps inline; with `openEditor: true` it returns a hint pointing at `/recipes/<id>/edit` so the user can continue in the form. By name it looks at their own recipes, then this week's menu, then the catalogue (PRO-349): a catalogue recipe answers «No he cambiado nada: "…" es una receta del catálogo de Mimoia…» (copy it first), never «no la encuentro» while it is on their menu; every refusal says «no he cambiado nada», so it never backs a «Hecho»
- `set_cooking_style` — how the household cooks (PRO-217: `daily` «Cocino cada día», `cook_and_reuse` «Cocino unos días y aprovecho para otros», `batch` «Batch cooking…», `improvise` «Improviso…»), in the household's ficha through `PUT /household/cooking-style`, the same route as Perfil; on every channel. Triggered by «ahora hacemos batch los domingos», «voy improvisando». The context line «Hogar:» carries the answer — see [Ficha del hogar](./household-profile.md)
- **The ficha's tools** (PRO-319, `fichaSkills.ts`, every channel; see [Ficha del hogar → Mimo and the ficha](./household-profile-tu-ficha.md)): `get_ficha` («¿qué sabes de mí?»), `explain_ficha_fact` («¿por qué crees que…?»), `note_ficha_fact` («apunta que…», «quiero proteína en cada plato» → `protein_minimum` of whoever says it; since D-182 phase 3 it no longer takes `skipped_meal` — it answers «usa set_week_type_slot … off=true» — and its description sends anything with a day and a meal to the semana tipo: a `fixed_dish` or `dish_frequency` there is only a taste without a day), `forget_ficha_fact` («olvida que…»), `set_ficha_learning` («pausa lo que aprendes de mí»), `get_ficha_question` / `answer_ficha_question` (the pending question, at most one a week). They call the «Tu ficha» routes as the user; the summaries are guidance and Mimo answers in its own words (D-065). Never another person's health in what Mimo says; on WhatsApp no health at all, read or written
- `list_taste_verdicts` / `forget_taste_verdicts` — see and forget the user's «Afina tu menú» verdicts (PRO-297: «Me apetece», «Me encanta», «No para mí», «No la conozco»; `verdict` filters), through `GET /taste/verdicts` and `DELETE /taste/:recipeId`, the same routes as «Olvidar» in Perfil → «Tu ficha» → «Editar mis datos uno a uno»; on every channel. Forget takes the names as the user says them («la pizza» → «Pizza margarita»: exact name first, then contained) or `all: true`; says which it removed and which it didn't find. Only the caller's own verdicts. Tests: `tests/mimoAfina.test.ts`
- `update_household` — set the user's `adults` + `kidsCount` (children 2–10 years; <2 don't count, >10 count as adults). Drives shopping-list portion sizing immediately. Its summary speaks of people, never of the multiplier («3 adultos y 4 niños de 2 a 10 años, 7 personas. La compra sale para las 7 (cada niño de 2 a 10 años cuenta como media ración de adulto)», `householdUpdatedSummary`; 2026-10-22 a large family read «5 raciones» as a mistake). Triggered by phrases like "ahora somos 2 adultos y un niño" or "quítame el niño"
- `add_recipe_to_mine` — copy a system (or another user's) recipe into the caller's catalog so they can edit it. Lookup by `recipeId` or `recipeName` (prefers Mimoia system matches; refuses if the only matches are recipes the user already owns). Returns the new recipe id + name. Same wire as the `POST /recipes/:id/copy` endpoint surfaced in the UI as "Añadir a mis recetas"
- `import_recipe_from_url` — "guárdame esta receta: <enlace>": extracts a YouTube video, recipe article or Instagram/TikTok post caption with the same pipeline as `POST /recipes/extract-from-url` and saves it as the user's recipe (soft lint, `internalTags: ['auto-extracted','from-url']`). Returns `{ recipeId, name }` with `uiHint: 'recipe'`; non-recipe links and pages that fail to download come back as a plain-text summary the model relays; since PRO-1301 every failure comes back with **the motive in one sentence and what does work**, for the model to relay as it is (`services/sources/importFailure.ts`): a private post or one behind a login, a video or post without the recipe written in its text, a YouTube video that shows nothing (private or gone), a page with no text or that doesn't open — then «Sí puedo con vídeos de YouTube (con subtítulos o la receta en la descripción), posts públicos de Instagram o TikTok con la receta escrita en el texto y webs de recetas; y si no, mándame una foto o captura de la receta.» (`IMPORT_WORKS`). A link from a network the import doesn't read (Facebook, X/Twitter, Pinterest, Threads, Snapchat, Vimeo, Twitch, Kwai) is answered up front, without trying. The screenshot sentences («No he podido leer la receta de este vídeo / esta página; mándame una captura») stay in the summary for the photo that comes next (`whatsapp/photoSource.ts`). The tool description and the prompt line say which links it reads and not to offer pasting links from other networks (before: Mimo invited a household to paste a video link, the import failed and it asked for a screenshot without saying why). Shared persist in `services/recipeImport.ts`
- `update_memory` — «sin X», «no me sugieras X», «prescindir de X» go to `dislikes` (also whole families: legumbres, arroz, casquería…), never to `notes`, which the menu doesn't read (2026-11-19; the prompt line for `update_memory`/`note_ficha_fact` says where each menu preference goes and that «lo tendré en cuenta en los próximos menús» is only said when saved there; the tool group `ficha` also wakes on «no me sugieras», «como mucho», «ligeras»…; battery cases `preferencia-menu` in `mimoEvalCases.ts`). Persist any preference the user mentions ("recuerda que no me gusta el cilantro", "tengo freidora de aire", "los lunes no cocino más de 20 min"). Accepts `facts: [{ key, value, confidence?, remove?, replace? }]`. Lists are **merged** with what's stored (`mergeMemoryFacts`: union without duplicates; `remove` takes items out; `replace: true` rebuilds the list) and an inference never overwrites a fact the user set by hand (`source: manual`) unless the user asked for it (confidence 1). Writes through `setMemoryBatch` with `source='inferred'` and default confidence 0.8 (1.0 if the user is emphatic). Canonical keys live in `@ona/shared` `MEMORY_KEYS` — see [User Memory](./user-memory.md). The assistant's system prompt already carries a Spanish digest of every stored fact, so a write here changes the next response's grounding.
- `recipe_variation` — generate a variation of a recipe (e.g., dairy-free version). Hands Mimo the recipe's times too (preparación, cocción, total, de trabajo) and tells it to pass `prepTime` / `cookTime` if it saves the variation with `create_recipe` (PRO-308)
- `nutrition_advice` — return advisor summary based on `user_nutrient_balance`
- `get_pantry_stock` — what's at home: the real pantry (`pantry_items`, with quantities and expiry dates, what `/pantry` shows) plus items marked "ya lo tengo" on the latest shopping list, deduped by name. It used to read only the list flags, so the pantry kept in the app was invisible to the assistant
- `mark_in_stock` — "tengo X" / "se me acabó X": sets the list item's `inStock` **and** adds the item to (or removes it from) the pantry, whichever exist. With no list it still writes the pantry. With `quantity` (+ `unit`) on a menu line — «nos queda una cebolla y medio pimiento» — it records how much is at home instead (PRO-182): the list keeps only the rest to buy and the answer says how much («quedan 6 u por comprar»); a unit it can't convert to the line's is asked back. **PRO-349:** an amount also goes in the pantry (a new row with it, or the row's amount set to it: it's what's left) and the summary says what the pantry holds now («En la despensa queda apuntado: Huevos: 4 u.»); before, «nos quedan 4 huevos y medio brócoli» only reached the list or left «huevos 1 u» while Mimo said both were in the pantry (Dani y Sara, 2026-10-22). **PRO-1294:** several ingredients go in one call with `items` (up to 100; each inherits `inStock` / `location`), each with its own `quantity`/`unit`; see Pantry below
- `check_shopping_item` — set/toggle the `checked` flag of a shopping-list item (mark groceries as bought)
- `get_my_recipes` — list recipes authored by the user (`recipes.authorId = user.id`)
- `get_menu_history` — list past weeks' menus to answer "when did I last eat X"
- `scale_recipe` — return ingredient quantities scaled to a different `servings` count without mutating the recipe
- `evaluate_food_health` — frame "is X healthy?" through Mimoia's cooking principles so the model answers with criterion (not neutral), in cooking words: veg/legume/fibre, real food vs ultra-processed and added sugar, how often it fits the week, a better kitchen alternative; never body effects (PRO-67)
- `suggest_substitution` — frame ingredient substitutions through the philosophy: never propose margarine, refined vegetable oils, artificial sweeteners. With a milk allergy anywhere in the household (or named in the call) it swaps the usual «AOVE, ghee, mantequilla…» for `MILK_ALLERGY_SUBSTITUTION`: no dairy, lactose-free included, no butter or ghee (PRO-312)
- `get_variety_score` — count distinct ingredients / vegetables / proteins in the current week's menu (principle 2, «Verdura todos los días, y variada»; advice in cooking words, no microbiome since PRO-67)
- `get_eating_window` — average first/last eating hour and window length from `eatenAt` timestamps (principle 7, «Comidas a su hora, sin picoteo»; advice in cooking words, no insulin since PRO-67)
- `get_week_balance` — kitchen balance of the current week's menu (no `recipeName`) or of one recipe: how many meals carry vegetables, on how many days there are legumes, which days repeat fried dishes, which recipes are salty or carry processed ingredients (from ingredient names, step texts and `nutritionPerServing.fiberG`/`saltG`). Answers in cooking words ("Esta semana vas corto de verdura… El jueves y el viernes repiten fritos."), never a score. Replaced `get_inflammation_index` (0–100 "anti-inflammatory" score) on 2026-10-11 (PRO-09, constitution §6: no health effects or disease names; an "inflammation index" reads as a medical-device calculator). The prompt tells Mimo to talk about kitchen balance and to send questions about a disease, a symptom or health effects to a health professional (doctor or dietitian-nutritionist). Guard: `tests/weekBalance.test.ts` (no tool, description, prompt instruction or default principle says "inflam…" or names a disease; the principles block appended to the prompt and the fixed health rule are guarded by `tests/principiosMimo.test.ts` and `tests/healthAdvice.test.ts`)
- `start_cooking_mode` — resolve a recipe and emit a `cooking_navigate` hint so the client routes to `/recipes/:id/cook`
- `set_timer` — emit a `cooking_timer` hint that `CookingShell` consumes via `subscribeCookingCommands` to start a timer at the current step
- `cooking_step` — emit a `cooking_step` hint with `direction: 'next' | 'previous' | 'repeat'` to advance the cooking shell
