# Authentication · Onboarding

> Parte de [Authentication](./auth.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): las preguntas después del registro, paso a paso.

## Onboarding (post-registration)

- Onboarding is required before any in-product page (menu, recipes, shopping, Mimo) is meaningful
- The `users.onboardingDone` flag tracks completion
- **«Afina tu menú» first (PRO-146, [taste.md](./taste.md)):** right after creating the account and before the WhatsApp/web choice, 10 recipe cards («Dime qué te *apetece*.», «Saltar» always visible, never blocks); shown once (`users.taste_state.onboardingAt`), so a reload continues with what comes next
- The web onboarding collects: household composition (`adults` + `kidsCount` for children aged 2–10; under 2 doesn't count, over 10 counts as adult; until PRO-383 the step offered, with 2 adults or more, a card to prepare the invitation for the other person), **«¿Quién hace la compra?»** (PRO-383, D-158: only with 2 adults or more, right after the household: «Yo» · «Otra persona de casa» · «Nos turnamos»; one tap, required when shown; sent as `shopper` (`me` · `other` · `shared`) and saved in the household's ficha as the fact `shopper`, origin onboarding — see [Ficha del hogar](./household-profile.md) — plus the usage event `shopper_answered` with `value` and channel `web`; with 1 adult it isn't asked and a `shopper` sent anyway is not kept; the draft keeps it). With «Otra persona de casa» or «Nos turnamos» the next screen is **«Invítale y le llegará la lista cada semana»** («Entrará en tu casa de Mimoia con un enlace: verá el menú y la lista de la compra de cada semana.»): **«Mandar invitación»** creates the household invitation (`POST /households/me/invites { role: member, source: alta }`, counted in `familyInvites.bySource.alta`) and shows the message «Te he metido en nuestra casa de Mimoia: ahí tendrás el menú y la lista de la compra de cada semana. Entra aquí: <enlace>» (`shopperInviteText`) with «Enviar por WhatsApp» and «Copiar», then «Siguiente»; **«Ahora no»** under it goes on. It replaced the «Familia en el mismo hogar» card of step 1 and keeps its «once» (shown only while `GET /households/me/family-offer` says `alta`; showing it stamps `alta`) — see [Household](./household.md) → «Ofrecer la invitación». Steps 7 and 8 in `onboardingSteps.ts` (`SHOPPER_STEP`, `SHOPPER_INVITE_STEP`; funnel names `quien_compra` and `invitar`); a draft left on either resumes on the question (on how they cook if the draft says 1 adult). Tests: `apps/api/src/tests/onboardingShopper.test.ts`, `onboardingShopper.smoke.ts`, `e2e/onboarding-shopper.spec.ts` at 390×844 (2 adults: the question, «Otra persona» → the invitation with its link; «Nos turnamos» → «Ahora no»; 1 adult: no question). **«¿Cómo cocináis en casa?»** (PRO-217, D-069: «Cocino cada día» · «Cocino unos días y aprovecho para otros» · «Batch cooking: cocino una o dos veces y dejo hecha la semana» · «Improviso: decido cada día»; one tap, required; under it, optional, «¿Congeláis la carne o el pescado cuando compráis?» — «Sí, casi siempre» · «A veces» · «No» → `freezesMeatFish`, a habit in `prep_habits`, PRO-221, see [User memory](./user-memory.md); sent as `cookingStyle` and saved in the household's ficha as `cooking_style`, origin onboarding — see [Ficha del hogar](./household-profile.md); the draft keeps it; `e2e/onboarding-cooking-style.spec.ts` at 390×844), **«Entre semana, ¿cómo te gusta cocinar?»** (PRO-138: «Rápido · En media hora, a la mesa» = 30 min in total Mon–Fri, «Poco rato de trabajo · Aunque luego se haga solo» = 20 min of work Mon–Fri, «Sin prisa» = no limit; the weekend is always «sin prisa»; stored as `time_available` in the memory, see [Menus](./menus.md) → Time to cook) with an optional «¿Algo más? Cuéntanos tu caso» field (`cookingNote`, ≤ 500 chars, read by the AI into the memory and, since PRO-242, the diners of the household's semana tipo («a mediodía mi hijo come en el instituto entre semana» → one diner less at weekday lunch); the first menu waits for that read, and then for a pass of the ficha's refiner at the moment (PRO-320), at most 15 s in all — see [User memory](./user-memory.md) and [Ficha del hogar](./household-profile.md) — and kept as `users.onboarding_note` for the household ficha's daily signals, PRO-151; `users.onboarding_completed_at` records the first time the answers were saved, web or WhatsApp — see [Ficha del hogar](./household-profile.md)) and dietary restrictions. **Four questions since 2026-11-03** (Miguel: they overlapped): «¿Cuántas veces a la semana cocinas de verdad?» and «¿Qué quieres que Mimoia priorice?» are gone. The API reads `cookingFreq` from how they cook (`cookingFreqForStyle` in `@ona/shared`: cada día → `daily`; unos días y aprovecho, improviso → `3_4_times`; batch cooking → `rarely`) and leaves `priority` as it is (Perfil's «Prioridad nutricional» and Mimo can still set it). Both are optional in `onboardingSchema`, which needs `cookingStyle` or `cookingFreq`, so an app cached before the change still signs up. The step numbers stay 1, 3, 4, 5, with 7 and 8 shown after 1 since PRO-383 (drafts and the usage funnel count by them; `apps/web/src/lib/onboardingSteps.ts`). Tests: `apps/api/src/tests/onboardingHowTheyCook.test.ts`, `e2e/onboarding-cooking-style.spec.ts` (the POST carries neither field; batch cooking stores `rarely`). Before: six steps since PRO-217; five after PRO-146 removed the three favourite dishes (`favoriteDishes` is optional in `onboardingSchema`, default `[]`, up to 30 — no cap of 5 since PRO-155 — and «Me encanta» replaces it; `e2e/onboarding-cooking-time.spec.ts` at 390×844). Dietary restrictions are health data: the step shows the separate, unticked health-data consent box and only stores them when it's ticked (`healthConsent: true` in the body records the consent) — see [Privacy](./privacy.md) → Health-data consent (PRO-21)
- **«Así lo he entendido» (D-182 fase 4, 2026-11-11).** The help text of «¿Algo más? Cuéntanos tu caso» gives habits as examples, with no preset options: «P. ej. «pasta los miércoles», «pescado una semana sí y otra no», «el domingo por la noche, sándwich mixto»». The same AI read (no extra call) also returns the habits of a day and a meal — day or days, meal, dishes, by turns or together, «cada N semanas», «no hay» («la cena del sábado fuera») — and writes them into the household's semana tipo ([Menus](./menus.md)) through the same habit → slot conversion as Mimo's `set_week_type_slot` (`nextWeekTypeSlot` in `services/weekType.ts`) and with `saveWeekTypeSlots`, the function behind `PUT /households/me/week-type/:day/:meal` (a dish name is a dish type when it is one, else the one recipe of that name, else written by its name: `optionForDishName`). A habit without a day or a meal stays a note in the memory. With the text written, `POST /user/:id/onboarding` waits for that read (at most 15 s, `FIRST_MENU_WAIT_MS`, which the first menu waited anyway) and answers `weekTypeUnderstood` `{ rows, undo }`. Then, before the first menu, «Así lo he *entendido*» shows one row per slot (the same thing on several days of a meal, one row): the stroke icon of Perfil → Tu semana tipo (`DishTypeIcon`, plus «no hay» and diners; no emoji), what, «día · comida · frecuencia» («Pizza · Viernes · cena · cada semana»; diners lowered by PRO-242 too, «Para 1 persona»), with **«Está bien»** (the first menu, as before) and **«Corregir»** (every slot back as it was, through the PUT route, and back to the text; sending it again reads it again). Nothing understood, or a late or failed read → no screen. The WhatsApp sign-up has no open question like this one, so Mimo doesn't ask it there. `onboardingMemoryAnalysis.ts` (`parseWeekTypeHabits`, `weekTypeHabitChanges`, `understoodWeekType`). Tests: `apps/api/src/tests/onboardingWeekType.test.ts`, `e2e/onboarding-week-type.spec.ts` at 390×844
- Until `onboardingDone = true`, the landing page redirects authenticated users to `/onboarding`
- **Allergies first (a beta tester, 2026-10-22: «la principal preocupación de alguien con intolerancias»).** When «Dime qué te apetece» is coming in the sign-up, «Antes de nada, ¿hay algo que *no comáis*?» goes before it (same `RestrictionsPicker` and separate health-data consent, RGPD art. 9). «Siguiente» saves them at once (`POST /user/:id/health-consent` + `PUT /user/:id` `restrictions`), so the 10 dishes already leave out what the household doesn't eat. Then the web questions skip «¿Hay algo que no comas?» (three questions, «1 de 3»…) and the WhatsApp sign-up's «Tu menú está listo» doesn't ask again and opens `/menu`. A sign-up that doesn't show the 10 dishes (already shown, or resumed) keeps the question as the last one («4 de 4»). `OnboardingFlow.tsx` (`answerAllergiesFirst`), e2e `afina-tu-menu.spec.ts`.
- **Left halfway, resumed (PRO-178, 2026-10-18).** Every «Siguiente» of the web questions saves the step reached and the answers so far on the account (`PUT /user/:id/onboarding-draft`, `onboardingDraftSchema` in `@ona/shared`, column `users.onboarding_draft`, migration 0068; returned as `onboardingDraft` by `GET /user/:id`; ignored once onboarded, cleared when the sign-up is saved, web or WhatsApp). Coming back — any device, after logging out — `/onboarding` skips the WhatsApp/web choice (unless WhatsApp is already linked) and opens on that step with those answers. Allergies and restrictions are never in the draft (health data, stored only with the consent at the end), so a sign-up left on the last question resumes on «¿Hay algo que no comas?» («4 de 4»). A draft saved on a question that is gone since 2026-11-03 (how often, priority) resumes on the next one (`resumeStep`). An unfinished sign-up with no draft (left before this, or on the choice) starts at «1 de 4»
- **`/menu` without the sign-up finished goes to `/onboarding` (PRO-178).** Logging in (which lands on `/menu`), the welcome email's «Abrir Mimoia» and any link to `/menu` used to show «Tu semana está en blanco» and «Generar mi menú» — a menu without the answers. `/menu` now waits for `GET /user/:id` → `onboardingRequired` when the browser doesn't already know the account as onboarded (no extra request otherwise) and, if true, replaces itself with `/onboarding` without loading or creating the week. `onboardingRequired` is false for an account that finished the sign-up (also when it finished on WhatsApp or another device: the browser's copy is updated) and for a **member who joined someone else's household** by invitation (they read that household's menu; `needsOnboarding` in `services/onboarding.ts`; since PRO-271 they're asked their allergies and diet right after accepting, see [Household](./household.md) → «Alergias y dieta al entrar en una casa»). A failed check never blocks the menu, **nor one still unanswered after 8 s** (`API_SLOW_MS`; the installed app on Windows sat on a near-empty «Cargando...» after signing up, 2026-10-21 — a late «pending» answer still goes to `/onboarding`) (`hooks/useOnboardingGate.ts`; `e2e/desktop-windows.spec.ts`, tests `apps/api/src/tests/onboardingDraft.test.ts`, `e2e/onboarding-resume.spec.ts` at 390×844) Only the answer fetched for that visit counts: an earlier visit's cached «pending» (an account that logged in, landed on `/menu` and then finished the sign-up) used to send it back to `/onboarding` and loop `/menu` ↔ `/onboarding` for good (a beta tester, 2026-10-22); finishing the sign-up (web or WhatsApp) also writes «done» into the gate's cache (`markOnboardedInCache`; not into `/onboarding`'s, which still shows the WhatsApp sign-up's «Tu menú está listo»), and `/onboarding` shows «Cargando...» instead of the questions while an onboarded account is on its way to `/menu`.
- **With a menu already made, `/menu` shows it (PRO-1042, 2026-11-04).** An account signed up through the API (as the WhatsApp bot does) whose menu Mimo already made, with the sign-up unfinished, used to be sent from `/menu` back to the household question, starting at two adults with one already saved. Now `GET /user/:id` also says `hasMenu` (a menu of the account or of its household) and `onboardingPending` (the sign-up still to do, as `needsOnboarding`), and `onboardingRequired` is `onboardingPending && !hasMenu` (`onboardingGate` in `services/onboarding.ts`). `/menu` shows the menu and, above it, «Te quedan unas preguntas del alta» with «Terminar el alta» (→ `/onboarding`) and «Ahora no» (this visit) (`PendingSignUpCard.tsx`, `usePendingSignUp`). `/onboarding` opens with what the account already says (adults, children; a draft still wins), and with a menu already there finishing it doesn't generate another: it goes back to `/menu` with Mimo's. Without the health-data box ticked, the restrictions Mimo saved stay (tests `apps/api/src/tests/onboardingGateMenu.test.ts`, `e2e/menu-before-sign-up.spec.ts` at 390×844).
- **WhatsApp first (2026-10-10):** when WhatsApp is available for the account (`GET /whatsapp/status` → `available`), `/onboarding` opens on «¿Dónde quieres hablar principalmente con *Mimo*?» («Puedes usar Mimoia tanto desde WhatsApp como desde la web. Te haremos un par de preguntas donde elijas, y así vas viendo cómo se usa.», 2026-10-15) with **«Por WhatsApp» (recomendado)** and «Prefiero la web». WhatsApp mints the one-time code (same linking as `/whatsapp/conectar`: «Enviar desde WhatsApp» opens the chat with «Vincular Mimoia: XXXXXX» written); once the phone sends it the page shows «Mimo te está escribiendo» + «Abrir WhatsApp» and polls `GET /user/:id` until `onboardingDone`, then shows «Tu menú está listo» (allergies with the health-data consent; adding something rebuilds the week — PRO-161, [WhatsApp](./whatsapp.md)) and «Ver mi menú» goes to `/menu`. Mimo asks the first steps in the chat — four questions since 2026-11-19: who they are, which meals they cook, three dishes they make often, what doesn't go in — and builds the first menu with their dishes (`complete_onboarding`, see [WhatsApp](./whatsapp.md)); health data is never asked there. «Prefiero la web» (also reachable from either WhatsApp screen) runs the web questions, and «Atrás» on the first of them returns to the choice. Already linked and not onboarded → straight to «Mimo te está escribiendo». Not available → the classic questions, as before (`OnboardingWhatsApp.tsx`, `e2e/onboarding-whatsapp.spec.ts`)
- Finishing onboarding generates the user's **first real menu** for the current week (`POST /menu/generate`) before landing on `/menu`, built from those answers. **Friday to Sunday (Europe/Madrid wall clock) it also generates the next week**, one after the other, and lands on `/menu?week=<next Monday>` (PRO-65; `firstMenuWeeks` in `@ona/shared`, `apps/api/src/tests/firstMenuWeeks.test.ts`, `e2e/first-menu-next-week.spec.ts`). **The first menu starts on the sign-up day (PRO-244), with the next meal that hasn't gone by (2026-11-19):** the client sends `fromDate` (`firstMenuWeeks(now, { fromNextMeal: true })`: today, or tomorrow from 21:00 Madrid) and the days of the current week that already went by are not generated, nor today's meals already past (lunch from 15:00…; see [Menus](./menus.md)) — they stay without dishes (not «sin cocinar»), add nothing to the list and can still be filled by hand. `/onboarding` only redirects to `/menu` by itself when the user was already onboarded on arrival, so it doesn't race that navigation. The WhatsApp onboarding (`complete_onboarding`) follows the same rule since PRO-244: from today on, plus the whole next week Friday to Sunday (`apps/api/src/tests/firstMenuWhatsApp.test.ts`). It used to land on an auto-created empty week. The client marks the user onboarded only **after** that generate answers (button "Preparando tu menú..."): marking it first let `/onboarding`'s "already onboarded" redirect reach `/menu` before the menu existed, and `/menu` auto-created an empty week on top (fixed 2026-10-08, guarded by `e2e/registration-onboarding.spec.ts`, which now sees the "Tu semana está en blanco" card). An already-onboarded user who opens `/onboarding` goes straight to `/menu`. If generation fails, `/menu` still offers "Generar mi menú"
- **Primeros pasos on `/menu` (PRO-07, 2026-10-11).** The first time a new account reaches `/menu` (once the current or a future week has loaded — a weekend sign-up lands on next week), three steps over the real screen, each lighting up its element with the rest dimmed: «Toca «···» en un plato para cambiarlo» (the «···» options button of the day's featured dish — tapping the dish itself opens the recipe; changing it goes through «···»; PRO-68, 2026-10-13; if the day has no dish yet, the first «···» on screen — an empty slot's; read-only, the whole dish), «Aquí tienes la lista de la compra: se hace sola» (the «Compra» tab, or the sidebar link on desktop), «Este es Mimo: pídele lo que quieras» (Mimo's floating button). An account with WhatsApp linked (`GET /whatsapp/status` → `linked`) gets only the first two steps, «1 de 2» / «2 de 2», without the one about Mimo's web button (PRO-161; `firstStepsFor`). «Siguiente» / «Entendido» advance and close; «Saltar» (or Esc) closes at any step. Closing calls `POST /user/:id/first-steps` and it never comes back, on any device. Stored on the account, not the browser: `users.first_steps_pending` (migration 0044, `NOT NULL DEFAULT false`), returned as `firstStepsPending` by `GET /user/:id`. **Only `POST /register` writes `true`**, so every account created before the column existed (and any row inserted another way) counts as already seen — no backfill, no existing data touched. WhatsApp-first sign-ups see it too (in two steps) the first time they open `/menu`. The e2e helpers `registerFreshUser` / `completeOnboarding` mark it as seen so it doesn't cover other specs (`skipFirstSteps`; `{ keepFirstSteps: true }` keeps it). `components/menu/FirstStepsGuide.tsx`, `e2e/first-steps.spec.ts`, `apps/api/src/tests/firstStepsPending.test.ts`
- The sign-up doesn't ask physical data (sex, age, weight, height, activity level): the calorie calculator reads them from Perfil (the voice sign-up, `/onboarding/voz`, asks the age)
