# Billing (plans, pricing page, subscriptions)

**Status:** live since 2026-10-15: production has Miguel's live keys (Managed Payments, autónomo per D-057), staging the test keys. First real subscription (Miguel, Esencial) checked end to end: Checkout «74 días gratis… a partir del 29 de diciembre de 2026», webhook → `subscriptions`, cancel from the portal. Payments are switched on per environment by `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` + `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`; without them every pay button says payments aren't open yet. Production gets live keys only once there's a business to invoice (Todo Miguel).

Mimoia is paid per **household**, with two plans, each monthly or yearly. **Esencial is the plan we recommend** (Miguel, 2026-10-15): it goes first and carries «Recomendado» everywhere. Stripe Checkout with **Managed Payments** takes the money: Stripe, through **Link**, is the merchant of record (VAT, receipts, disputes, refunds). The statement reads `LINK.COM* MIMOIA`.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Esencial** (featured) | 4,99 € | 49,99 € («Sale a 4,17 €/mes») | Menu, list, pantry, shop orders, recipes and imports (capped), Mimo by chat in the app **and on WhatsApp** (capped) |
| **Plus** | 8,99 € | 59,99 € («Sale a 5 €/mes») | Everything in Esencial + **voice**, **Mimo writes to you on WhatsApp** (today's menu, what to thaw, the list), higher caps |

Prices include VAT. Caps have no figures yet (PRO-15 sets them on 2026-11-24 with real beta costs); the copy says «tienen un tope de uso al mes; en Plus es más alto».

## User Capabilities

- **`/precios`** (public, in the nav and footer): the two plans with a **Mensual / Anual** switch, Esencial first with «Recomendado», a table of **everything each plan includes** grouped by Planificar · Comprar · Cocinar · Mimo · Tu hogar (✓ / — / «Con tope mensual» / «Tope más alto»), the price row, and five questions (whole household, when you're charged, changing or cancelling, Esencial vs Plus, who charges). The call to action depends on who looks:
  - logged out → «Apúntate a la lista de espera» (`/#lista-de-espera`);
  - logged in, payments off → «Muy pronto podrás pagarlo aquí» (disabled);
  - logged in, not the household owner → «Lo contrata quien gestiona tu hogar»;
  - household owner → «Elegir Esencial · pagas el 29 de diciembre» (the date is `BillingState.firstChargeAt` for that user), which opens Stripe Checkout;
  - household already subscribed → «Es tu plan · ver suscripción» / «Cambiar a este plan» → `/profile/suscripcion`.
- **Perfil → Suscripción** (`/profile/suscripcion`; hub tile shows «Beta: Plus gratis», «Sin plan» or the plan with its state (see below)):
  - no subscription and payments on, owner: the two plans as a radio group (Esencial preselected, monthly) and «Suscribirme a Esencial · pago el 29 de diciembre» (again `firstChargeAt`);
  - with a subscription: the plan and price with a **status pill** and one line (`subscriptionSummary` in `@ona/shared`, 2026-10-15 — after cancelling, Miguel still saw only «Esencial · 4,99 €/mes»): trial → «En prueba hasta el 29 de diciembre» + «Lo has dejado pagado: no se cobra nada hasta el 29 de diciembre de 2026. Puedes cancelar antes sin pagar nada.»; cancelled during the trial → «Cancelada» + «Has cancelado: no se te cobrará nada. Sigues con tu prueba hasta el …»; cancelled while active → «Cancelada» + «…sigues con Esencial hasta el … y no se renueva.»; failed payment → «Pago pendiente»; active → «Activa» + «Próximo cobro: …». The profile hub tile says «Esencial · en prueba» / «· cancelada» / «· pago pendiente». **«Gestionar mi suscripción»** (owner only), which opens Stripe's customer portal: change card, receipts, switch plan or period, cancel (at the end of the period, with a reason), or undo a cancellation;
  - back from Checkout with `?estado=ok` it polls `GET /billing` every 2 s until the webhook has written the subscription («Estamos confirmando tu pago…»); `?estado=cancelado` says nothing was charged.
- **When the first charge happens** (`firstChargeInstant`, D-055/D-056/D-057, PRO-133). Launch day is **2026-12-15** (`LAUNCH_DATE`; was 2027-01-12). Everyone has **14 days** with everything from sign-up (`TRIAL_DAYS`), beta households from launch day, and the first charge is **never less than 15 days after leaving the card** (`MIN_DAYS_CARD_TO_CHARGE`, room for the legal notice). So every Checkout creates a Stripe trial that ends at max(max(sign-up, launch) + 14 days, now + 15 days), rounded up to **12:00 Madrid** (Stripe prints UTC dates; at 00:00 Madrid the portal read «finaliza el 11 ene», staging 2026-10-15). A beta household paying before 14 December is first charged on **2026-12-29** (`BETA_FIRST_CHARGE_DATE`). Cancelling before then costs nothing. Stripe/Link email a trial-start note and a reminder before it ends. Verified end to end in staging on 2026-10-15 (test card → webhook → «Lo has dejado pagado…»; portal; cancel → «Cancelada: sigue hasta…»), with the old date.
- **The beta runs with Plus for everyone** until launch day (`BillingState.beta`), paid or not.
- **Trial without card and the paused account** (PRO-135, D-056/D-057). **Only from launch day**: before it, `access.state` is always `beta` and nobody is paused (the server doesn't even read the DB for it).
  - From launch day a household has **14 days with everything, without card**, counted from when the household was created (no column: `households.created_at`); beta households from launch day, so **15 → 28 December**, both included. The trial ends at midnight Madrid after its last day (`trialEndsAt` in `@ona/shared`).
  - **Day 12 (2 days left):** the household owner gets one email «Te quedan 2 días de prueba en Mimoia» ([Email](./email.md)), and `/menu` shows to every member a card «Te quedan 2 días de prueba» / «Te queda 1 día…» / «Hoy es el último día…» (`trial-ending`): «Tu prueba dura hasta el 24 de enero, incluido. Si no eliges un plan, no se te cobra nada: la cuenta queda en pausa…» + «Ver los planes» (owner).
  - **After the 14 days without a plan the account is paused** (`access.state = 'paused'`): it still sees its menu, recipes, list and everything else, but **can't generate a new menu** (`POST /menu/generate`, except an empty week) **nor use Mimo** (`POST /assistant/:userId/chat`, `POST /advisor/:userId/ask`, `POST /stt`) → **402 `ACCOUNT_PAUSED`** «Tu prueba ha terminado y tu cuenta está en pausa. Elige un plan en Perfil → Suscripción… No se ha borrado nada.» Admins are exempt. **Nothing is ever deleted.**
  - On `/menu` a card «Cuenta en pausa · Tu prueba ha terminado» («Tu menú, tus recetas y tu lista siguen aquí y no se borra nada. Para generar menús nuevos y volver a hablar con Mimo, elige un plan.») with **«Elegir plan»** → `/profile/suscripcion` (a member reads «El plan lo elige quien gestiona tu hogar: pídeselo desde Casa.»). Mimo's panel shows the same card instead of the composer. `/profile/suscripcion` opens with «Tu prueba ha terminado y tu cuenta está en pausa…» (and, during the trial, «Estás en tu prueba de 14 días: dura hasta el …, incluido.») and the plans as usual.
  - **A paused account gets no other message from Mimo**: on WhatsApp every message it sends is answered only with «Tu prueba de Mimoia ha terminado y tu cuenta está en pausa… elige un plan…: <web>/profile/suscripcion» (kind `paused`); the proactive WhatsApp messages and the prep alerts (push + WhatsApp, cancelled «cuenta en pausa») are not sent. Control words (BAJA/ALTA/«persona») still work.
  - **Paying takes it out of the pause** at once: a subscription in `trialing`, `active` or `past_due` (`PAID_ACCESS_STATUSES`) = `subscribed`, for the whole household. `unpaid`, `canceled`, `paused`… after the trial = paused again.
  - The texts of the pause card, the day-12 card and email and the WhatsApp reply are for Legal to review before launch.
- **Founder price** (PRO-136, D-056/D-066). Today's prices **are** the founder prices; «para siempre» = a founder subscription is never moved to a new price (if prices ever go up, new Stripe prices via `transfer_lookup_key`, and these subscriptions keep theirs). Who keeps it (`founderPriceIfSubscribing`): every **«Hogar fundador»** (beta household), whenever it subscribes; from launch day, anyone who subscribes **before their trial ends**; not a paused household. Checkout then sets `founder_price: 'true'` in the session's and the subscription's Stripe metadata, and the webhook copies it to `subscriptions.founder_price`. «Tu suscripción» says «Tienes el precio de fundador: no te sube mientras sigas suscrito.»; a founding household without a plan reads «Tu hogar es fundador: cuando te suscribas, tienes el precio de fundador para siempre…» on Suscripción and on `/precios` (`pricing-founder`).
- **Day-7 offer** (PRO-136, only from launch day): from day 7 of the trial (Madrid calendar days from its first day; beta households: 22 December) and without a plan, `GET /billing` → `founder.offer`. The app shows «Quédate el precio de fundador» with «Llevas una semana con Mimoia. Si ya sabes que te quedas, deja la tarjeta antes de que acabe tu prueba (dura hasta el …) y te quedas el precio de fundador para siempre: no te sube mientras sigas suscrito. No se cobra nada hasta el … y puedes cancelar antes sin pagar nada.» (`founderOfferText`) as a card on `/menu` (`founder-offer`, «Elegir plan»; until the last 2 days, when «Te quedan 2 días» takes its place), on Suscripción above the plans, and as a message from Mimo at the top of Mimo's panel (`mimo-founder-offer`). **Once**, the owner also gets **one** message (PRO-234, D-080): from Mimo on WhatsApp if linked, with proactive messages on and inside the 24 h window (never a template), with the link to Suscripción; otherwise by email ([Email](./email.md)). Never both. Marked `households.founder_offer_at` (09:00–21:00 Madrid; if neither got it out it stays pending).
- **The Stripe trial** of a new subscription ends at max(end of the household's trial, today + 15 days), at noon Madrid (`firstChargeInstant`, now fed with the household's creation).
- **Legal notice 15 days before the first charge** (PRO-136): from the Madrid day 15 days before a trialing, not cancelled subscription's first charge, the payer gets one email with the date, the amount (`chargeAmount`, «4,99 €»), that it renews every month/year if they don't cancel and «Cancelar mi suscripción» → `/profile/suscripcion?cancelar=1`; recorded with its date in `subscriptions.charge_notice_at` (the webhook never writes that column). The app shows the same while it applies (`subscription.upcomingCharge` → `charge-notice` on `/menu` and on Suscripción: «El … te cobraremos 4,99 € (IVA incluido), el primer pago de tu suscripción. Si no quieres seguir, cancela antes de esa fecha y no se te cobrará nada.»). With `?cancelar=1` Suscripción shows «Cancelar mi suscripción», which opens Stripe's portal on its cancel flow (`POST /billing/portal { cancel: true }`; if Stripe refuses the flow, the normal portal). Not tied to launch day: the beta's first charges (29 December) get it on 14 December.
- The offer, the founder lines and the legal notice texts are for Legal to review before launch.
- The waitlist no longer shows plans or asks about price (2026-10-15, see [waitlist.md](./waitlist.md)).
- **«Borrar mi cuenta»** first cancels, at once, every live subscription the user pays; if Stripe can't be reached the account is not deleted (502 `SUBSCRIPTION_CANCEL_FAILED`, «inténtalo en un momento»).

- Table `price_survey_responses` (migration `0050_price_survey`): one row per user (`user_id` unique, cascades on account deletion), the four euros, `answered_at`, `dismissed_at`.

## API

- `GET /billing` (auth) → `BillingState`: `enabled`, `beta`, `launchDate`, `canManage` (the caller owns their primary household), `firstChargeAt` (if they subscribed now), `access` (`state`: `beta | trial | paused | subscribed`, `trialEndsAt`, `trialLastDay` `YYYY-MM-DD`, `daysLeft` after today while `trial`; PRO-135), `subscription` (`plan`, `period`, `status`, `nextChargeAt`, `cancelAtPeriodEnd`, `endsAt`) or null. A subscription counts while its status is `trialing`, `active`, `past_due`, `unpaid`, `incomplete` or `paused`.
- `POST /billing/checkout` (auth) `{ plan, period }` → `{ url }` (Stripe Checkout). 503 `BILLING_DISABLED`, 400 bad body, 403 `NOT_HOUSEHOLD_OWNER`, 409 `ALREADY_SUBSCRIBED`, 502 when Stripe fails. Reuses the household's previous Stripe customer if there is one.
- `POST /billing/portal` (auth, owner) `{ cancel?: true }` → `{ url }` (customer portal, returns to `/profile/suscripcion`; `cancel` opens the cancel flow of the live subscription). 404 `NO_SUBSCRIPTION`.
- `GET /billing` also answers `founder` (`household`, `priceIfSubscribing`, `offer`) and, on the subscription, `founderPrice` and `upcomingCharge` (`{ at, amount }` from 15 days before the first charge, else null).
- `POST /billing/webhook` (Stripe, signed with `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, raw body, mounted before `express.json()`). Handles `checkout.session.completed` and `customer.subscription.created|updated|deleted|paused|resumed`; for each it **re-reads the subscription from Stripe** (events can arrive late or out of order) and upserts it. Bad signature 400; payments off 503; a failure 500 so Stripe retries.

## Data

- `subscriptions` (migration `0049_billing_subscriptions`): one row per Stripe subscription — `household_id`, `user_id` (payer, `SET NULL` on delete), `stripe_customer_id`, `stripe_subscription_id` (unique), `plan`, `period`, `status` (Stripe's), `current_period_end`, `trial_end`, `cancel_at_period_end`, `canceled_at`. Written only by the webhook (and by the account-deletion cancel). Plan and period come from the price's **lookup key**, so a plan change in the portal is picked up; checkout metadata (`householdId`, `userId`, `plan`, `period`) is the fallback and the link to the household.
- `households.trial_ending_email_at` (migration `0069_trial_ending_email`): when the day-12 email went out, so it goes once.
- Migration `0070_founder_offer`: `subscriptions.founder_price` (boolean, from Stripe metadata), `subscriptions.charge_notice_at` (the legal notice, ours only), `households.founder_offer_at` (the day-7 offer).
- No card data ever reaches us.

## Configuration

- Env (API): `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`; links use `WEB_PUBLIC_URL`.
- `apps/api/scripts/stripeSetup.ts` (idempotent) creates in the Stripe account: products «Mimoia Esencial» / «Mimoia Plus» with tax code `txcd_10103000` (SaaS, personal use; required by Managed Payments), four EUR prices with `tax_behavior: inclusive` and lookup keys `esencial_mensual`, `esencial_anual`, `plus_mensual`, `plus_anual` (a changed price gets a new Price and the lookup key moves to it), the webhook endpoint (`--webhook-url`; its secret is written to `--secret-out`, never printed) and the default customer portal configuration.
- Managed Payments must be accepted in the Stripe Dashboard. Checkout never sends what it refuses (`automatic_tax`, `tax_id_collection`, `payment_method_types`, `invoice_creation`, `custom_text`…: Stripe answers 400 «You cannot use custom_text with Managed Payments», found in staging 2026-10-15). The «no se cobra nada hasta el …» line lives on our pages; Stripe's checkout shows the trial until that date.

## Constraints / known limitations

- **Plans aren't enforced yet** beyond the pause: a household in trial or with either plan gets everything; the per-plan caps (PRO-15) are still to build (TODO in `services/billing.ts`).
- The day-7 offer and the day-12 notice can reach the same person by email, WhatsApp and the app on the same day by design (D-056); there is no notification centre yet to merge them.
- The pause blocks generating a whole week and Mimo; changing one dish (`regenerate`, `random`), recipe imports and the newsletter (separate consent) are not blocked.
- A failed access check never pauses anyone (logged).
- Only the household **owner** can subscribe or manage; the plan covers the whole household.
- One live subscription per household (409 otherwise); changes go through the portal.
- Terms §8–9 and the privacy policy describe this; both still need the legal review on Miguel's list.
