# Ficha del hogar

Everything Mimoia knows about a household, with a section per person (D-064, PRO-150). It is the base the rest of the «ficha viva» builds on: the daily signal collector (PRO-151), the nightly refiner (PRO-152), «Tu ficha» in the app and Mimo / the menu reading it (PRO-153, PRO-139). **Today the data model, the daily signal collector (PRO-151), the nightly refiner (PRO-152), the ficha as the one source of what we know about a person (PRO-316) and the «Tu ficha» API (PRO-317) exist**: the refiner writes the ficha every night, **Mimo and the menu read it** (PRO-253, see «Who reads the ficha»), the API serves it to its reader and **«Tu ficha» shows it in Perfil** (PRO-317/318, see «Tu ficha»). The one exception is **how the household cooks** (PRO-217, below): asked in the sign-up, shown and changed in Perfil and by Mimo.

## User Capabilities

- **«¿Cómo cocináis en casa?»** (PRO-217, D-069): «Cocino cada día» · «Cocino unos días y aprovecho para otros» · «Batch cooking: cocino una o dos veces y dejo hecha la semana» · «Improviso: decido cada día». Asked in the web sign-up right after how often they cook ([Auth](./auth.md)); on `/profile` the card «Cómo cocináis» (`profile-cooking-style`) shows the answer (or «Sin contestar») and changes it with one tap, no «Guardar cambios»; Mimo changes it when the household says so («ahora hacemos batch los domingos», tool `set_cooking_style`, web chat, voice and WhatsApp) and reads it in its context. Only collected and shown: the menu uses it with PRO-219
- Every night, a household that used Mimoia that day gets its ficha refined from what happened (see «Nightly refiner»)
- Mimo knows what the ficha says (portrait and facts in force) in the web chat and on WhatsApp, and the menu respects it: with «sin horno hasta nuevo aviso» in the ficha, no oven recipe comes out (PRO-253)
- **«Tu ficha»** (PRO-318, `/profile/ficha`, first card of Perfil): every adult of the house reads what Mimoia knows — the portrait Mimo writes each night, the pending question («Sí» / «No» / «Ahora no»), «Lo último que he aprendido», then a chip per person («La casa», «Tú», each member, each person without an account) with the facts by topic and where each comes from. A tap on a fact opens «¿Por qué lo sé?» (what Mimo saw, how sure, since when, «Cómo lo uso») with «Así es» / «Corregir» / «Olvidar» when the reader may change it; another member's facts are read-only («Esto lo cambia Marta desde su ficha»). The reader's own part adds their health under «Solo lo ves tú», the switch «Aprender de lo que hago», «Editar mis datos uno a uno» (`/profile/memoria`) and «Descargar mi ficha»; a person without an account gets «Editar» (alias, age band) and «Quitar de la casa»; «+ Persona» adds one. «Añadir algo que deba saber» opens Mimo with the message started. `/profile/ficha/historial` lists «Lo que ha cambiado» by day with «Deshacer» on what Mimo did. A `child` member doesn't get it (see «Tu ficha: the screen»)
- **Mimo talks about the ficha** (PRO-319, web chat, voice and WhatsApp): «¿qué sabes de mí?», «apunta que…», «olvida que…», «¿por qué crees que…?», «pausa lo que aprendes de mí», «quiero proteína en cada plato»; and, at most once a week, Mimo asks the pending question at a natural moment (see «Mimo and the ficha»)
- Everything a person tells Mimoia about themselves or their home lands in the household's ficha at once (PRO-316, D-110): what they write in «Lo que recuerdo de ti», what they tell Mimo on the web, by voice or on WhatsApp, what they answer in the sign-up and in «Afina tu menú» — each fact with where it was said («Se lo dijiste a Mimo por WhatsApp: No le gusta: cilantro») and its history row; forgetting a memory key retires the fact

## Model

One ficha per **household** (the menu is the household's), with facts about the household as a whole, about a member with an account, or about a person without one.

- **Facts** (`profile_facts`): household, subject (none = the household; `subject_user_id` = a member; `subject_person_id` = a person without an account; never both), `type`, `value` (jsonb, validated per type), `origin` (`user` · `onboarding` · `ai_realtime` · `ai_nightly` · `ai_weekly`), `confidence` 0–1, `evidence` `[{ signal, date AAAA-MM-DD, summary }]`, `valid_until` (temporary situations), `status` (`active` · `retired`), `source_memory_id` (the `user_memories` row it was copied from)
- **Fact types**: every `user_memories` key, with the same value schema (`MEMORY_VALUE_SCHEMAS`), plus:

| Type | Value | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `fixed_dish` | `{ dish, days?, meal? }` | pizza los viernes por la noche |
| `skipped_meal` | `{ days, meal, reason? }` | el fin de semana no se cocina |
| `dish_frequency` | `{ dish, minWeeks, maxWeeks }` (1–52, min ≤ max) | coliflor gratinada cada 6–10 semanas |
| `affinity` | `{ kind: ingredient\|style, target, score −2..+2 }` | le encanta la cuchara (+2) |
| `effort` | `{ days, meal, activeMinutes, totalMinutes }` (active ≤ total) | entre semana, 15 min de trabajo aunque cueza 3 h |
| `temporary_situation` | `{ description }` + the fact's `valid_until` | sin horno hasta el 2-nov |
| `shopping` | `{ places?, days?, note? }` | frutería del barrio los sábados |
| `batch_cooking` | `{ batches, days?, lunchboxes? }` | cocina por tandas el domingo, con táperes |
| `novelty` | `{ score −2..+2 }` | prefiere la rutina (−2) |
| `cooking_style` | `{ style: daily\|cook_and_reuse\|batch\|improvise }` | «Batch cooking: cocino una o dos veces y dejo hecha la semana» (PRO-217) |
| `protein_minimum` | `{ scope: every_dish }` | «Proteína en cada plato»: 80 g de carne, pescado o huevo por ración también en entrantes y platos de arroz o legumbre (about a person; Miguel, 2026-10-21) |

  Days are `lunes`…`domingo`; meals `breakfast` · `lunch` · `snack` · `dinner`.
- **People without an account** (`household_persons`): `alias` and `age_band` (`0-2` · `3-5` · `6-12` · `13-17` · `adulto`). **A minor's name is never stored**: the schema has no name field (`.strict()`), and a minor's alias must be one of `MINOR_ALIASES` («la pequeña», «el mayor», «hija»…), optionally with a number («hija 2»). An adult (the grandmother) may have any alias up to 40 characters
- **Portrait** (`household_profiles`): one per household, `portrait` text of at most 250 words (`PORTRAIT_MAX_WORDS`, checked in the Zod schema), `version`, `model`, `generated_at`. No health and no judgements (constitution §6)
- **Pending questions** (`profile_questions`): what the AI suspects but doesn't know («¿seguís sin horno?»), with subject, `fact_type`, `origin`, `status` (`pending` · `asked` · `answered` · `dismissed`), `asked_at`, `resolved_at`
- **History** (`profile_changes`): `action` (`fact_added`, `fact_updated`, `fact_retired`, `portrait_updated`, `person_added`/`updated`/`removed`, `question_added`/`resolved`/`asked`), `fact_id`, `fact_type`, `before`, `after`, `reason`, `origin`, `actor_user_id`, date

## Constraints

- **Health rules are today's** (PRO-21, LEG-03): health types are the same keys as the memory's (`physical.*`, `restrictions`; `HEALTH_PROFILE_FACT_TYPES`). Writing one needs the health consent and never comes from WhatsApp (`canWriteProfileFact`). A health fact is about a person, never the whole household, and is never deduced by the nightly or weekly refiner (`origin` `ai_nightly`/`ai_weekly` is rejected for health types)
- **Withdrawing the health consent** also deletes that person's health facts in the ficha and their history rows (`services/healthConsent.ts`); deleting the account deletes the facts about that person (cascade)
- **Visibility per fact** (Miguel, 2026-10-16): a health fact about a member with an account is read only by that member; the rest of the ficha by every adult member (`owner`, `member`); `child` members read nothing; the menu generator and Mimo (`{ kind: 'system' }`) read everything so they can respect it. `visibleProfileFacts(facts, viewer)` is the filter the future API must apply
- **Copy from `user_memories`** (`applyProfileFromMemories`, after `db:migrate` on every boot): each memory row → one fact in the user's primary household (else the first one they joined; users with none are skipped and counted). Origin: `manual` → `user`, `onboarding` → `onboarding`, `inferred` → `ai_realtime`; same confidence; one evidence item (`signal: user_memories`). `household.adults`, `household.kids_2_to_10`, `equipment`, `weekly_budget_eur` and `region` (PRO-226) are about the household; every other key is about the user. Each copy writes a `fact_added` history row. Idempotent: `source_memory_id` is unique and a copied memory is skipped
- **The ficha is the one source; the memory is its mirror** (PRO-316, D-110, `services/profile/writer.ts`): `setMemoryFact`, `setMemoryBatch` and `deleteMemoryFact` write `user_memories` and the ficha **in the same transaction** (`mirrorMemoryWrite` / `mirrorMemoryDelete` → `upsertProfileFact` / `retireProfileFacts`), so the two never disagree. The fact points at its memory row (`source_memory_id`), so the boot copy never duplicates it. A single-valued type (every memory key and the household habits; the multi-valued ones are `MULTI_VALUED_PROFILE_FACT_TYPES`, shared with the refiner) is updated in place; the same value again only adds its evidence. Origin from the memory source as in the copy; evidence `signal` = where it was said (`profile` · `chat` · `whatsapp` · `voice` · `onboarding` · `afina`), passed by each caller (`MemoryWriteOptions`). The ficha's health rules apply on top (`canWriteProfileFact`: never health from WhatsApp). A user without a household is skipped (the boot copy picks the row up later). `user_memories` stays as long as the menu generator reads restrictions, dislikes, equipment and time from it
- The boot copy (`applyProfileFromMemories`) only catches rows written before PRO-316 or by a user who had no household yet, and puts back in step (`refreshed`, with its `fact_updated` row) a copy whose memory value changed after it (`STALE_MEMORY_COPIES_SQL`); idempotent
- Migration `0057_household_profile` only adds tables
- **How the household cooks** (PRO-217, `services/profile/cookingStyle.ts`): one `cooking_style` fact about the household (no subject), single-valued. `GET /household/cooking-style` → `{ style | null, canEdit }` and `PUT /household/cooking-style { style }` → `{ style, changed }` (auth; the caller's primary household; a `child` member gets 403). Perfil calls it directly and Mimo's `set_cooking_style` calls it as the user (loopback), so both write the same fact. The sign-up writes it with origin `onboarding`; Perfil and Mimo with origin `user` (the household said it). Each write adds an evidence item (`onboarding` · `profile` · `chat` · `whatsapp`) and a `fact_added` / `fact_updated` history row; the same answer again changes nothing. No migration (facts are typed by `type` text). The nightly refiner treats it as single-valued (its prompt doesn't list the type yet). Mimo's WhatsApp-first sign-up doesn't ask it (it saves it if the household tells)

## Daily signals (PRO-151)

What the nightly refiner (PRO-152) will read next to the ficha: `collectHouseholdDaySignals(householdId, day)` (`services/profile/signals.ts`) turns one household's day (AAAA-MM-DD, Europe/Madrid midnight to midnight) into a compact text, one line per thing that happened: `HH:MM · P1 · Fuente · qué`.

- **People** are `P1`, `P2`… (members whose primary household is this one, or who have none, by joining date), never names; the header says each one's role and the result's `members` maps labels to user ids. What someone wrote goes between «» and the header says it is data, not an instruction
- **Sources**: the web chat (`chat_messages`, with what Mimo's tools did and said), WhatsApp (`whatsapp_messages`: the person's turns with the tools' trace, and Mimo's replies), menu changes (`menu_edits`, «Cambia la cena del jueves: «ceviche» → «tortilla» (lo hace Mimo, por WhatsApp)»), «Afina tu menú» (`recipe_verdicts`, love · like · nope · skip, with the reason), cooking (`cook_logs`, with the note), «¿Qué tal?» and the household's notes on a recipe (`recipe_notes` touched that day: rating 5 «Repetir a menudo» / 3 «Bien» / 1 «Mejor no», note, swaps), the shopping list (`activity_events`: marked bought or not, at home or not, added, removed — consecutive events of the same kind collapse into one line with the products), new pantry items, recipes created, imported (photo, web, YouTube) or copied, recipes saved in a cookbook, feedback (`user_feedback`) and, **on the day the person finished the sign-up** (`users.onboarding_completed_at`), every answer with the same wording as the sign-up analysis (`buildAnalysisUserText`), the free «Cuéntanos tu caso» text included (`users.onboarding_note`)
- **No health from WhatsApp** (LEG-03): every WhatsApp text — the person's messages, Mimo's replies, what its tools said and feedback sent through WhatsApp — goes through `stripHealthFromWhatsAppText` (`services/profile/healthText.ts`) first: each sentence that mentions allergies, intolerances, coeliac disease, gluten or lactose, an illness, medication or a doctor, pregnancy, body data or a religion-based diet becomes «[omitido]» (deliberately over-eager; vegetarian/vegan stay: they are taste). The sign-up's restrictions and body data are never signals. The web chat keeps its text: health there needs the consent, and the refiner never deduces health
- **Size cap**: each message is cut at 600 characters (Mimo's tools at 400) and the whole text at `SIGNALS_MAX_CHARS` (12,000 characters, ≈ 3,000–4,000 tokens). When the day doesn't fit, the **oldest lines go first** (the most recent are kept) and a line says how many were left out; the sign-up answers are always kept. The text stays in time order. A day without anything says «Sin actividad.»
- `recipe_verdicts` doesn't exist yet (PRO-146): `loadRecipeVerdicts` returns nothing until its table is there (`to_regclass`), then reads it with the columns PRO-146 describes — `TODO(PRO-146)` in the code to check them when it lands
- Known limitations: favourites have no date (`user_favorites`), so marking one is not a signal; shopping rows from before migration 0063 and the WhatsApp/voice skills other than «marcar» and «en casa» carry no product name; the sign-up answers of accounts that finished it before 0063 have no date, so they never come in as a day's signals (they are already in the ficha through `user_memories`)
- Migration `0063_activity_item_onboarding_note` adds `activity_events.item` / `toggled_on` and `users.onboarding_completed_at` / `onboarding_note` (only adds columns)

## Nightly refiner (PRO-152)

`services/profile/refiner.ts`, from the scheduler tick (`notificationScheduler`, like the conversation reviewer). **In the shadow**: it writes `profile_facts`, `household_profiles`, `profile_questions` and `profile_changes` (origin `ai_nightly`); nothing reads them yet.

- **When**: from 03:00 Madrid (`REFINER_HOUR`), once per day, for the day that just ended (`profile_refiner_runs`, unique per day and kind; claimed before calling Anthropic so a second tick or process never sends it twice). Results are collected on the following ticks (every 5 min) once the batch has ended. Without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` it doesn't run
- **Which households**: with activity that day (`collectHouseholdDaySignals` returns at least one signal) **and** with an active plan, in trial (`trialing`, `active`) or **in the beta** (before `LAUNCH_DATE`) — never a `paused` subscription nor a suspended owner — **and** whose owner is under the monthly AI cap (`spendCapStatus`, `USER_MONTHLY_SPEND_CAP_EUR`; if used up, the ficha waits). Plan, pause and cap are checked before reading the day. The run records how many were left out and why (`skipped`: `no_activity`, `paused`, `not_subscribed`, `suspended`, `spend_cap`, `no_members`)
- **The call**: one request per household in one **Message Batch** (Anthropic Batches API, half price, results within 24 h): Claude Opus 5.5 (`REFINER_MODEL` = `CLAUDE_MODEL`, PRO-232) with adaptive thinking at effort `medium`, `max_tokens` 16 000, the rules as a cached system prompt, and as user text the ficha between `<ficha>` and the day's signals between `<senales>`. The model sees short references only — facts `F1…`, members `P1…` (as in the signals), people without an account `X1…` — and the household row of the run stores what each reference meant (`profile_refinements.refs`), so the answer is read against the ficha as it was sent
- **The answer** is a closed JSON format (structured outputs, `REFINER_OUTPUT_SCHEMA`, checked again with Zod) with **only changes**: facts to `add` / `update` / `retire` (subject, type, value as JSON, confidence, evidence, valid until, reason, and a question in case it contradicts the user), a new portrait (or empty) and pending questions. A refusal, a cut answer or a broken format writes nothing
- **Rules in code** (`planRefinement`, never trusting the model):
  - **No deduced health**: a change on a health type, or whose value, reason, question or evidence mentions health (`HEALTH_TEXT_PATTERNS`: «diabético», «celíaca», «embarazada», «alergia»…), is discarded; the same for the portrait and questions. It is recorded in `profile_refinements.discarded` as `{ reason: 'health', op, type }` only — the content is never stored — and logged
  - **What the user said wins**: an update or retirement of a fact with origin `user` (also an `add` of a single-valued type that already has a user fact for that subject) becomes a pending question (the model's, or «¿Sigue siendo así lo que nos contaste (tipo)?»)
  - **At most 10 changes a night** (`REFINER_MAX_CHANGES`; facts, portrait and questions, in the model's order); the rest is recorded as `over_limit`
  - **Only its own ficha**: unknown references (`unknown_fact`, `unknown_subject`), values that fail the type's schema or lack evidence (`invalid`), no-op updates and repeated questions (`no_change`, `duplicate_question`) are discarded and recorded. A second fact of a single-valued type (every memory key, `shopping`, `batch_cooking`, `novelty`) becomes an update of the first; `fixed_dish`, `skipped_meal`, `dish_frequency`, `affinity`, `effort` and `temporary_situation` may have several
  - What people wrote is data between marks; the prompt says so, and the rules above hold whatever the text asks
- **Writing**: one transaction per household; each change adds a `profile_changes` row (`fact_added` / `fact_updated` with before and after / `fact_retired` / `question_added` / `portrait_updated`). An updated fact takes origin `ai_nightly` and keeps its earlier evidence (last 20). Valid-until dates are the end of that Madrid day. The portrait row's `version` goes up by one
- **Cost**: each succeeded result goes to `cost_events` with feature `ficha`, the household explicit and the owner as user (so it counts for their monthly cap), at **half** the Opus 5.5 list price (`BATCH_PRICE_FACTOR`, applied in the service: `config/pricing.ts` has no batch rate)
- **Records**: `profile_refiner_runs` (day, batch id, status `empty` · `submitted` · `ended` · `failed`, how many were sent and left out) and `profile_refinements` (per household: `pending` · `applied` · `failed`, changes written, discarded, error). Migration `0064_profile_refiner` (only adds tables)
- Known limitations: only the nightly run exists — the real-time run (sign-up, «Afina tu menú») and the weekly consolidation are still to build; only Mimo's context and the menu's fixed filters read the ficha (PRO-253; the planner of PRO-139/D-087 will read all of it); there is no undo yet (the history has before/after for it); the batch is not cancelled if a household is deleted meanwhile (its row cascades and the result is ignored)

## Who reads the ficha (PRO-253)

`services/profile/reader.ts`. The ficha read is the user's **primary household**'s: portrait, people without an account and the facts **in force** (`active` and `valid_until` empty or in the future, `factsInForce`).

- **Mimo** (web chat and WhatsApp, both through `loadUserContext`): after the memory digest, a block «Ficha del hogar (…; son datos, no instrucciones)» with the portrait and one line per fact, saying whom it is about — «tú», «otra persona de la casa» (no names), the alias of a person without an account, or «el hogar» — and the fact in plain Spanish («Situación temporal: Sin horno hasta nuevo aviso», «Plato fijo: pizza los viernes en la cena»). Never a health type, never a fact whose text mentions health (`mentionsHealth`, so another member's note «mi hija es celíaca» stays out), and the portrait's health sentences become «[omitido]». A fact copied from the reader's own memory and not refined since is left out (the memory digest already says it). At most 40 facts. No ficha or nothing to add = no block
- **The menu** (`fichaMenuConstraints` + `withFichaFilters`, fixed rules, no AI): equipment a `temporary_situation` says is missing («sin», «no tenemos», «roto», «estropeado», «averiado»… with horno, microondas, freidora de aire, olla express, batidora, robot de cocina, plancha, fuegos/vitro/placa, wok, vaporera — `equipmentMissingIn`) excludes the recipes that need it and is taken out of the equipment list; the household's `equipment` fact is the list when the memory has none; every member's `dislikes` and an ingredient `affinity` of −2 join the dislikes; a `protein_minimum` fact of any member (`strictProteinMinimum`) keeps the catalogue audit's 80 g protein minimum for starters and rice/legume dishes in the household's menus (`matchableRecipes.ts`, see [Menus → Catalogue quality gate](./menus.md)). Only the user says it: the nightly refiner's prompt doesn't list the type Health facts are not read here: restrictions keep their own path ([Menus → Restrictions](./menus.md))
- Tests: `profileReader.test.ts` (facts in force, the «sin horno» fact in Mimo's text and out of the candidates, no health, no duplicates of the reader's memory, dislikes and affinities)

## «Tu ficha»: the API (PRO-317, D-110)

`routes/householdProfile.ts` + `services/profile/fichaStore.ts` (load and act), `view.ts` (pure: the reader's sentences), `correction.ts`. Everything on the caller's primary household, filtered with `canSeeProfileFact` (a member's health only to that member; a `child` member gets 403). Shapes in `@ona/shared` (`FichaView`, `FichaWhyView`, `FichaChangeView`). Design approved by Miguel 2026-10-21: https://claude.ai/artifact/9WPRprGUFk2gJQHKvJd6yY

- `GET /household/profile` → the portrait and its date; the people (`La casa`, `Tú`, the other members by name, the people without an account by alias and age band); every fact in force as **one sentence for its reader** (`factSentence`: «Te encanta la cuchara», «No os gusta el cilantro», «No le gusta el ajo»; all 30 types), its **topic** (Cómo coméis · En la cocina · Tiempo y compra · Gustos · Cómo comes · La casa · Salud · Otras notas), its **origin in words** («Lo dijiste tú» · «Lo dijo Marta» · «Del alta» · «Lo he deducido»), whether it is temporary, and whether the reader may change it (`canEdit`: the household, themselves or a person without an account — never another member's facts, which that member changes); the pending question (one, not one put off in the last 7 days, with the fact it is about); the last 14 days of history (5); whether the reader paused the learning
- `GET …/facts/:id/why` («¿Por qué lo sé?») → the evidence in sentences with dates (newest first), the confidence in words only for what Mimo deduced («Casi seguro» ≥ 0,85 · «Bastante seguro» ≥ 0,6 · «Es una sospecha»), since when, and **«Cómo lo uso»** — only what the code does (the menu filters for dislikes, affinity −2, equipment, «sin horno», protein in every dish, time; otherwise «Mimo lo tiene en cuenta…»)
- `POST …/facts/:id/confirm` («Así es»): origin `user`, confidence 1. `POST …/facts/:id/correct { text, validUntil? }`: an Opus call (low effort, cost `ficha` to the reader) turns the reader's words into the type's value, checked against its schema; when it doesn't fit (a nuance, an exception) the fact is retired and the words go to the subject's `notes`, as theirs. `DELETE …/facts/:id` («Olvidar»): retired. Each with its history row (origin `user`, the reader as actor); a memory key about the reader or the household also goes to `user_memories` (household-level keys to every adult member), the mirror the generator reads
- `GET …/changes?days=60` and `POST …/changes/:id/undo` («Deshacer», only Mimo's changes: an add is retired, an update goes back to the value before, a retirement comes back, a portrait goes back to the previous one; 409 when the fact has moved on)
- `POST …/questions/:id { answer: yes | no | later }`: «Sí» confirms the fact the question is about, «No» forgets it, without such a fact the answer waits for the night (signal «Tu ficha»); «Ahora no» hides it 7 days. Stored in `profile_questions.answer` / `answered_by_user_id` (migration 0091)
- `PUT …/learning { paused }` — **«Aprender de lo que hago», per person** (`users.profile_learning_paused_at`, migration 0091; RGPD art. 21): the nightly signals leave that person's lines out and the refiner discards any change about them (`paused`); what the ficha knows stays
- `POST/PATCH/DELETE …/persons` — people without an account: alias and age band (`householdPersonSchema`: a minor only with a generic alias, «el pequeño», «hija 2» — never a name); removing one takes the facts about them (cascade); `person_*` history rows
- `GET …/export` («Descargar mi ficha», right of access): everything the reader may see — facts with origin, confidence, evidence, dates and status, the portrait, the people without an account, the questions about them or the household, and the history — as a JSON attachment
- **What the reader forgot stays forgotten**: a fact retired by the household in the last 90 days, unless a later «Sí» took it back, comes back from the refiner only as a question («Me ha parecido ver otra vez algo que olvidaste: «…». ¿Lo apunto?»)
- Tests: `fichaView.test.ts` (sentences for the 30 types and the three readers, topics, origins, permissions, history, the correction's reply), `profileRefiner.test.ts` (pause, forgotten), `fichaRoute.smoke.ts` (two adults against Postgres: health only to its owner, why, confirm, the other member's facts are theirs, correct, forget with the memory mirror, undo once, the question, the pause per person, a minor's name refused, export)

## «Tu ficha»: the screen (PRO-318)

`app/profile/ficha/page.tsx` + `historial/page.tsx`, blocks in `components/ficha/` (FichaUI, FichaSheets on `MenuSheet`, LearningSwitch, FichaHubCard), hooks `hooks/useFicha.ts` (TanStack; every action invalidates `["ficha"]` and `["memory"]`, and a memory write invalidates the ficha), pure helpers `lib/ficha.ts`. Texts and order from the approved design; the look is in [Design System](./design-system.md).

- **Order**: header «Tu ficha · Así veo *tu casa*»; the portrait (paragraphs, «Lo escribo cada noche… Actualizado hoy a las 03:10.», «¿Algo no cuadra? Dímelo» → Mimo) or, without one, «Todavía os conozco poco» (Afina tu menú · Cuéntale a Mimo cómo coméis); the question; «Lo último que he aprendido» (up to 3 added/changed, «Ver todo lo que ha cambiado»); the chips; the chip's part. Portrait, question and «lo último» stay on top whatever chip is picked: the chips switch only the part below, in place
- **La casa**: its facts by topic (empty topics don't show), «Añadir algo que deba saber» (Mimo opens with «Recuerda que en casa »), «Cómo aprendo» with «Pausar lo que aprendo de ti» (→ the reader's switch, focused; «Reanudar…» when paused) and «Descargar mi ficha». **Tú**: their facts by topic (their beliefs link to `/profile/creencias`, «Ver tus creencias»), **Salud** with a padlock «Solo lo ves tú» (body data is not listed: one «Datos físicos» row → `/profile#datos-fisicos`), «Aprender de lo que hago», «Editar mis datos uno a uno», «Descargar mi ficha». **Another member**: their facts, read-only. **A person without an account**: «De 6 a 12 años · sin cuenta en Mimoia», «Editar», «Lo que sé» (or «Añadir algo que deba saber» while empty), the minors note when under 18, «Quitar de la casa» (with a confirm)
- **A new household** (no portrait and at most 6 facts, `FEW_FACTS`): the short screen — «Todavía os conozco poco», the question if any, the facts in one list («Lo que me contaste al darte de alta» when they all come from the sign-up, else «Lo que sé»), «Cómo aprendo» with the pause and the export. Once the full screen has shown it stays for the visit (forgetting a fact never flips back)
- **Sheets**: «¿Por qué lo sé?» asks `…/why` when it opens; «Así es» only for what the reader didn't say themselves. «Corregir»: «¿Cómo es en realidad?» + «¿Hasta cuándo?» (Siempre → `validUntil: null`; Hasta una fecha → end of that day); a reply `note` says «Lo he apuntado con tus palabras, en «Otras notas».». «Olvidar»: «¿Olvido que …?» (the sentence quoted when it isn't a clause), «Cancelar» goes back to «¿Por qué lo sé?». Person: «Apodo» + age pills («0–2 años» … «13–17 años», «Adulto»); the API's 422 shows as it comes. Errors show in the sheet; outside sheets a short status line (`role="status"`) confirms («Olvidado.», «Deshecho.») or shows the API's message (409 «Ya no está en tu ficha»)
- **Perfil card** (`profile-ficha`, replaces «Memoria»): «Actualizada anoche» (the latest of portrait, history and facts), the portrait's first sentence in «…», «N cosas que sé», «1 pregunta para ti»; hidden for a `child` member (403) or without a household (404). The page shows the API's message in those cases
- **History**: grouped by day («Anoche» = after 21:00 the day before or before 06:00 today, «Hoy», «Ayer», «Hace N días», «Hace una semana»…, then the date, Madrid time); «Deshacer» only where `canUndo`, once («Deshecho»)
- Constraints / known limitations: a question about another member is hidden here (`visibleQuestion`) because `pickQuestion` doesn't filter by subject and the answer would be 403 — the API should pick per reader; the history and «lo último» carry no fact id, so a «lo último» row opens the fact whose sentence matches (else it isn't tappable); a correction kept as a note loses its end date (the API's note path takes no `validUntil`); «Descargar mi ficha» fetches the JSON with the session and saves it from the browser (`mi-ficha-mimoia-AAAA-MM-DD.json`)
- Tests: `apps/web/e2e/profile-ficha.spec.ts` (390×844: a real household from new to known — why, correct, forget and back, a minor's name refused with the API's text, edit and remove a person, pause, export, history, the Perfil card; a simulated view for the portrait, the question, «Así es», another member, the reader's health and «Deshacer»; a `child` member; the pure helpers)

## Mimo and the ficha (PRO-319, D-051, D-065)

`services/assistant/fichaSkills.ts` (seven tools, on every channel) over the «Tu ficha» routes as the user (`appApi.ts`, which sends `x-mimoia-channel`), so Mimo sees and changes exactly what the reader may in the app. The tools' summaries are guidance; Mimo writes the reply in its own words (no fixed texts).

- **«¿Qué sabes de mí?»** (`get_ficha`, `GET /household/profile`): the portrait and, per person, the facts with topic and origin (`fichaSummary`). Never another person's health — a member's (the API already hides it), a person without an account's, a note about someone else that mentions health (`mentionsHealth`) or a health sentence of the portrait. On WhatsApp no health at all, not even the reader's: one line points to Tu ficha on the web
- **«Apunta que…»** (`note_ficha_fact` → `POST /household/profile/facts { type, value, about: me | household | person, personId?, validUntil? }`, `writeProfileFact` in `fichaStore.ts`): origin `user`, confidence 1, evidence «Se lo dijiste a Mimo por WhatsApp / en la web», history row with the reader as actor; a memory key also goes to `user_memories` (as «Corregir»). A list (dislikes, notes…) adds to what is there (`mergeListValues`). A person without an account is named by alias. «Hasta el 2 de noviembre» = end of that Madrid day. A child member gets 403
- **«Quiero proteína en cada plato»**: `note_ficha_fact` with `protein_minimum { scope: every_dish }`, always about whoever says it (`fichaSubjectFor`: «para la casa» lands on the person; or a person without an account by alias). The menu reads it (see «Who reads the ficha»)
- **No health from WhatsApp**: the tool refuses a health type or a value whose text mentions health before calling, and the route refuses it again by the channel header (`canWriteProfileFact`, plus `mentionsHealth` on the value: «apunta que soy celíaca» stays out); on the web, health needs the consent. Forgetting or explaining a health fact on WhatsApp sends to the web
- **«Olvida que…»** (`forget_ficha_fact`, `DELETE …/facts/:id`) and **«¿Por qué crees que…?»** (`explain_ficha_fact`, `GET …/facts/:id/why`, the evidence with dates, how sure in words, «Cómo lo uso»): by the fact's id from `get_ficha`; another member's facts are theirs to change
- **«Pausa lo que aprendes de mí»** (`set_ficha_learning`, `PUT …/learning`): the reader's own switch
- **The pending question** (`get_ficha_question` → `POST /household/profile/question/ask`, `askPendingQuestion`; `answer_ficha_question` → `POST …/questions/:id`, as the screen's «Sí» / «No» / «Ahora no»): **at most one a week per person** (`FICHA_QUESTION_EVERY_DAYS`; a `question_asked` history row with the person as actor, origin `ai_realtime`, not shown in «Lo que ha cambiado») and **on WhatsApp only inside the 24 h window** (`whatsapp_links.last_inbound_at`, `SERVICE_WINDOW_MS`); a question about another member or, on WhatsApp, about health is never picked (`mimoMayAskQuestion`). Mimo's context gets one line when a question is due this week (`fichaQuestionHint`) telling it to ask at a natural moment (showing the menu, after closing something), never mid-task, with «[[opciones: Sí | No | Ahora no]]». The question stays on «Tu ficha» until answered. No migration (`profile_changes.action` is text)
- Tests: `fichaMimo.test.ts` (each tool on the web and on WhatsApp against a fake API, the weekly cap and the window, no health from WhatsApp, never another person's health in the summary), `fichaMimo.smoke.ts` (the routes against Postgres)
