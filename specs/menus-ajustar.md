# Menus · Ajustar la semana a mano

> Parte de [Menus](./menus.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): imprimir, quitar y añadir comidas, comensales, vetar, días sin cocinar, notas, sobras y táper, congelador, «antes de planificar», la nevera, rehacer la semana, una comida nueva y los bloqueos.

## User Capabilities: ajustar la semana

- **Imprimir la semana** (PRO-112): the week's «···» sheet (`WeekActionsSheet`, when the week has a menu) has «Imprimir la semana» → `/menu/imprimir?week=YYYY-MM-DD`. The page previews an A4 landscape sheet: «Menú · semana del …», the 7 days as columns and one row per meal the week has (dish names — recipes and written dishes — and the first recipe's minutes), with «Mimoia» discreet in the corner. «Incluir la lista de la compra» (ticked by default) adds a second sheet with the week's shopping list by aisle (items in stock left out; `GET /shopping-list?from=<lunes>&to=<domingo>`), each with a ☐ to tick on paper. «Imprimir» opens the browser's print dialog, which also saves as PDF; the toolbar and the app chrome don't print (`@page A4 landscape`). Nothing is generated on the server. Logic in `lib/printWeek.ts`; e2e `print-week.spec.ts`
- Users can shape a single week without touching their preferences. **Quitar <comida>** removes the slot (`DELETE /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal`), and the day's **"Añadir comida"** opens a sheet («Añadir comida al jueves») listing the meals the day lacks; choosing one opens «Sin plato todavía» for it and **adds nothing until something is chosen there** (PRO-1177; before, the matcher put a recipe at once). Its «Que elija Mimoia» is `POST …/meal/:meal`: the matcher picks a recipe, and when none fits — no breakfast in the catalogue, or all used this week — the meal is added **empty**, with «Añadir plato», instead of the old 404 that left the button doing nothing. Both apply to **this menu only**: the profile's `mealTemplate` is untouched, so next week follows the original preferences
- Users can override the diner count for one slot with **Comensales** −/+ in the meal sheet's «Más opciones» («Comensales: 4», "Solo hoy · Quitar"). It is persisted as the slot's `servings` (`PATCH /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal` `{ servings: number | null }`; `null` clears). Changing the slot's dish keeps it: the web picker and «Que elija Mimoia» (`PUT …/meal/:meal`, dish routes) and Mimo's `swap_meal` (`slotWithDishes`, PRO-102: the Ruiz family's «Solo hoy: 4» went back to 5 after a swap with Mimo). `sumDinersByRecipe` in `shoppingList.ts` sums per-slot diners, so repeated recipes with different overrides scale independently. The caption's "N raciones", the dish's link to its recipe (`/recipes/:id?servings=N`; the detail opens at N) and the cook link use the **batch** (`batchServings` in `lib/menuDay.ts` → `batchServingsOf` in `@ona/shared`, PRO-75, PRO-298): the meal's own diners plus what each planned leftover of that meal adds, the same rule as the shopping list and Mimo's menu facts (see [Shopping](./shopping.md) → `withLeftoverBatches`). A «Solo hoy: N» up to the household's size is only who eats that meal, so the leftovers add their diners on top (PRO-298: Dani y Sara's bowl, Monday with the template's 2 and leftovers Tuesday to Thursday, showed «2 raciones» and bought for one day; now 8); one above the household already pays for tápers, and the batch is max(N, household + each leftover)
- Users can **veto** a recipe for the rest of the week: **No la pongas más esta semana** (first level of the meal sheet; «Vetar esta receta» until PRO-1175) → confirm ("No ponerla"). It goes into `menu.banned_recipe_ids` and the slot it was in gets another recipe at once (the web chains `POST /menu/:menuId/ban` with an Aleatorio of that slot, PRO-72; other days that already hold it keep it; vetoing with Mimo changes every appearance from today on, see [advisor.md](./advisor.md) `ban_recipe_this_week`, PRO-113), and the matcher excludes it from every later Aleatorio / Elegir-without-recipe / Añadir / whole-week regenerate. A collapsible "Vetadas esta semana" panel under the day lists each veto with "Levantar veto". Vetoes carry over when `POST /menu/generate` re-runs for the same week
- Users can mark a whole day **"sin cocinar"** with **«Este día no cocino»** («Saltar día» until PRO-1177; confirm: «¿El viernes no cocinas?» → «Este día no cocino»). This empties every non-locked slot and persists the index on `menu.skipped_days`; whole-week regenerate skips the day. The day then shows "Día marcado sin cocinar." + **«Volver a cocinar este día»** («Reactivar día» until PRO-1177; «Volver a cocinar» in the desktop «La semana» cell), which only clears the flag (slots are re-added by hand or Aleatorio). Skipped days carry over across regenerates. **A dish put on a skipped day reactivates it (PRO-349):** every route that places a dish on a day (`PUT`/`POST …/meal/:meal`, `POST …/dish`, `…/dish/random`, `move-slot` on the target day, `…/leftover`, `…/taper`) and Mimo's own writes (`swap_meal`, the «con lo que tengo» pick) store `skippedDays` without that day (`unskipDay`, `services/menuWeek.ts`). Before, Mimo put «arroz de limpieza» on a skipped Saturday: the list counted it and the day still said «Día marcado sin cocinar» (Dani y Sara, 2026-10-22). Smoke: `tests/skipDayReactivate.smoke.ts`

- **Note dishes** ("comemos fuera", "+ pan") are edited inline: in the meal sheet's "Platos" list (Vista día / desktop) and in Vista semana rows. Enter / blur persists via `PATCH /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal/dish/:position` `{ text }`, and Esc reverts
- Users can mark a slot as a **leftover** from a previous slot via `POST /menu/:menuId/day/:targetDay/leftover`, body `{ sourceDay, sourceMeal, targetMeal }`. The target slot is cloned with `kind: 'leftover'` and a `leftoverOf` back-reference; the shopping-list aggregator collapses the repeated `recipeId` onto the source row. The leftover does **not** copy the source's `servings` (PRO-75). The source cooks for every meal it covers (PRO-298): each leftover adds its diners to the batch, unless the source's «Solo hoy: N» is above the household's size, which already pays for tápers up to N (Laura alone, lasaña «Solo hoy: 2» + sobras → 2); see [Shopping](./shopping.md) → `withLeftoverBatches`. Leftovers link only within the same week; cooking on Sunday for the next week's meals is PRO-219. The meal shows a terracotta "Sobras de [día] [comida] · ya en la compra" chip instead of «Solo hoy: N» (Vista semana drops its «N pax» chip) and its sheet hides «Cambiar plato» and the veto, since the leftover is tied to its source. *UI affordance to trigger the endpoint from the card itself is deferred to a follow-up — the endpoint is available for the assistant via the voice skill.*
- **El táper de mañana** (PRO-223, D-069): «Mañana como fuera: haz ración y media de la cena y te llevas el táper». A lunch (or breakfast / snack) from Tuesday on can be marked as táper: in the meal sheet's «Más opciones → Ya está hecho / táper…», **«Sobras de la cena de ayer»** (hint «Lentejas, cena del lunes: se hace una ración más»; «Me lo llevo en táper» until PRO-1175; only when the day before has a dinner with a cooked recipe), in **Perfil → «Tu semana tipo»** («Táper de la cena» on a breakfast, lunch or snack from Tuesday on, the slot's only option — D-182; applied every time a week is generated, `applyWeekTypeTapers`), or telling Mimo «mañana me llevo táper» (`set_taper`). `POST /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal/taper` `{ count?: 1-6 }` replaces that meal with the previous day's dinner as a leftover (`variant: 'leftover'`, the existing hueco de sobras, chip «Sobras de lun cena · ya en la compra») with `servings` = tápers and `taper` = tápers on the slot (`services/taper.ts`); the dinner itself doesn't change. The list adds one ration per táper to that dinner's recipe, once (`sumDinersByRecipe`; `withLeftoverBatches` keeps táper leftovers even on top of «Solo hoy: N» → N + 1), and the dinner's cook link opens with that ration more (`batchServings`). Refused: Monday (409, the dinner before is last week's), a dinner, a locked meal (400), a day before without a cooked dinner (404). **«Quitar táper»** (`DELETE …/taper`) leaves the meal empty. The menu change log records it as `remove` + `leftover`. Reminder: if Mimo sends that day a «toca empezar» (else the morning brief), it carries «🥡 Mañana te llevas táper: haz 1 ración más de *Lentejas*.»; no extra message just for the táper. Tests: `taper.test.ts`, `taperRoute.smoke.ts`, `e2e/taper-tomorrow.spec.ts`

## Single-Meal Regeneration

`PUT /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal` replaces one slot:
- A locked slot changes too (PRO-1175); `{ recipeId }` or `{ note }` locks it (the dish was chosen by hand), the matcher's pick leaves the lock as it was
- `{ dishType }` (a `DISH_TYPES` key, «Que elija Mimoia» with a chip): the first recipe dish gets a recipe of that type only, or **409 `NO_RECIPE_OF_TYPE`** and nothing is written
- Excludes recipes already used in the rest of the week from the candidate pool
- Applies the same restriction/season/favorites logic
- **With sense** (2026-11-19, `menuSlotPick.ts`; the same for this route, `POST …/dish/:position/regenerate` and Mimo's `swap_meal` without a dish — before, Mimo's was a random catalogue recipe: a khachapuri of 151 min for a Tuesday dinner, a sweet mutabbaq on Sunday night, higadillos twice in a week): the household's menu rules (weekly limits counting the rest of the week, light meals — see «Lo que el hogar ha dicho del menú»), no sweet, and on a **working day** (Mon–Fri, not a holiday) a lunch or dinner of at most **60 min** in total when the household said no time for that day (an `effort` fact of the ficha for that day and meal sets it; unknown times pass). Then, while one fits: a dish whose **main food** (`mainFoodKey`: hígado, pollo, merluza, lenteja…) isn't elsewhere in the week, with protein when it is a single dish or the main (`pickForSlot`)
- Returns 404 if no matching recipe is available (Mimo says nothing fits and asks what they fancy)

## Manual Slot Shaping (per-week overrides)

The user can adapt one week without editing the saved `mealTemplate`:

- `POST /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal` (auth) — add a slot the template didn't include. Optional body `{ recipeId }` (then the slot is locked, PRO-1175); if absent the matcher picks one. Returns **409** when the slot already exists (use PUT to replace it instead), **404** when the matcher can't find a recipe, **201** + the updated menu otherwise.
- `DELETE /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal` (auth) — drop the slot for this week, locked or not (its lock goes with it, PRO-1175). **404** when the slot doesn't exist. The user's `mealTemplate` is **not** mutated, so next week starts fresh.
- `PATCH /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal` (auth) — partial update for slot metadata. v1 only honours `{ servings: number | null }`: a positive integer (1–24) sets a per-slot diner-count override, `null` clears it. The override is consumed by the shopping-list aggregator and ignored by the recipe matcher. Returns **400** on out-of-range servings, **404** when the slot is empty.
- `POST /menu/:menuId/move-slot` (auth) — atomic move/swap of a slot to another day/meal. Body `{ fromDay, fromMeal, toDay, toMeal }`. Empty target → move (source slot becomes empty). Occupied target → swap. A locked slot moves too and takes its lock with it (`swapLocks`, PRO-1175; before, **400**). Used by the drag-and-drop in "Vista semana" so the client doesn't sequence DELETE + POST and risk leaving the menu half-mutated. Each slot travels whole («Solo hoy» included), and every leftover that points at a moved slot (`leftoverOf`) is repointed to its new place, so the moved dish still cooks the táper and the list counts it the same (PRO-102, `moveSlotInWeek` in `services/menuDishes.ts`; before, Laura's Monday curry for 2 moved to Thursday and its táper still pointed at the empty Monday). Mimo's `move_meal` goes through this route.

The recipe matcher is unaffected by manual shaping. A dish added by hand (`POST …/dish`, a recipe or a note) locks its slot (PRO-1175).

## Lock Behavior

`PUT /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal/lock` toggles `locked[day][meal]` — on the web, **«Que no cambie si rehago la semana»** in the meal sheet's «Más opciones» («Fijar» / «Desfijar» until PRO-1175):
- **What a lock means (PRO-1175, D-182 answer A, «bloqueo implícito»):** the meal is kept when the week is redone (Regenerar, Vaciar semana, «Este día no cocino», Mimo's `generate_weekly_menu`), and **nothing else**: it never greys the sheet nor refuses a change to that meal — the routes `PUT`/`DELETE …/meal/:meal`, `move-slot` and `…/taper`, Mimo's «con lo que tengo» pick, «Cocinar ahora» (`POST /cook-now/choose`) and the recipe's «Añadir al menú», a veto by Mimo across the week and «Cocinar ahora»'s «Pasarlo a otro día» all change a locked meal; removing its last dish removes its lock. **What you choose by hand locks it** (`withLock` in `@ona/shared`): a recipe from the picker or the recipe page (`PUT`/`POST …/meal/:meal { recipeId }`), a written dish (`{ note }`) and a dish added (`POST …/dish`, also Mimo's). What Mimoia draws («Que elija Mimoia», «Aleatorio», the generator) doesn't. On screen the lock is a small padlock (aria «Se queda si rehaces la semana»), not the «Fijada» chip: with the implicit lock most meals chosen by hand carry it. Still skipping locked meals, as before (they are automatic): the week-type tápers of the generator, «Mete lo del congelador» and `fridgeBoost`. Tests: `mealSheetOptions.test.ts` (`withLock`, `swapLocks`, the screen's copy), `mealLockRoute.smoke.ts` (a hand pick locks, a locked meal still changes, moves with its lock and goes, `{ dishType }` and its 409), `appSkills.test.ts` (Mimo's veto changes a locked meal)
- Locked slots (with vetoes and "sin cocinar" days) carry into every new row for the week: `POST /menu/generate` (regenerate and "Vaciar semana") and the assistant's `generate_weekly_menu`, via `services/menuWeek.ts`. The assistant's `generate_weekly_menu` never remakes a week that already has dishes unless the user confirmed it (`replaceExisting: true`), and its dish tools edit the menu of the week the named day falls in, not the newest row (PRO-63, see [Advisor → Conventions](./advisor-herramientas-app.md)). Before 2026-10-07 regenerate wrote `locked: {}` and the locked dishes were lost. Their recipes go into `usedRecipeIds` first, so the rest of the menu doesn't repeat them
- **No accidental wipes**: `empty: true` over a week that has dishes → **409 `MENU_NOT_EMPTY`** unless `force: true`. Only the confirmed "Vaciar semana" sends `force`. The /menu page auto-creates an empty week only on a real 404 (`ApiError.status`). A failed GET (500, offline) shows "No hemos podido cargar tu menú" + "Reintentar" and never writes; so does **a GET still unanswered after 8 s** (`API_SLOW_MS`, `useQueryLoad` as in the profile sub-pages, PRO-143): «Reintentar» cancels the hung request and asks again (2026-10-21: after a slow first menu the installed app on Windows showed an empty cream page; `e2e/desktop-windows.spec.ts`). Covered by `menuWeekRoute.smoke.ts` + `e2e/menu-load-failure.spec.ts`. It also self-heals a legacy row stored as `[{}, …]` (no slot on any day) with an empty week — **but not when days are «sin cocinar»** (PRO-115): a week left without meals by «Este día no cocino» stays as it is after a reload, and an empty week (`empty: true`) never gives slots to a skipped day (`e2e/menu-skip-whole-week.spec.ts`)
