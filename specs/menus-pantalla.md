# Menus · La pantalla del menú

> Parte de [Menus](./menus.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): generar la semana, vista día y vista semana, la hoja de la comida y la de la semana, cambios al momento, confirmaciones y el escritorio.

## User Capabilities

- Users can generate a weekly menu (Monday–Sunday) for the current week or any future week with one click. A week with no dishes (new or emptied) shows a "Tu semana está *en blanco*" card above its empty slots (no sparkles since PRO-1186: configuring weighs more than asking the AI; «Generar mi menú» until then). **With a semana tipo** (PRO-1180; the household said something: a dish, a «no hay», a meal added, more dishes — `weekTypeConfigured`, `lib/blankWeek.ts`) it shows the semana tipo in two lines (`weekTypeSummary`: the meals, «Comida y cena», and what it asks for, «legumbres lunes y miércoles · viernes cena: pizza», three at most and «y N más») with **«Editar»** (→ `/profile/semana-tipo`) and the main button **«Llenar con mi semana tipo»**; **without one** (lunch and dinner every day, any recipe: every household until it says something) the button says **«Hacer mi menú»** and under it the link **«Primero, cuéntame cómo coméis»** (→ `/profile/semana-tipo`). Both generate the same way; «Empezar de cero», the optional questions and the «Menú del mes» stay. Otherwise **«Rehacer la semana…» → «Con mi semana tipo»** in the week sheet (PRO-1179; «Regenerar semana» until then). **While a week is generated** («Llenar con mi semana tipo», «Con mi semana tipo», «Solo con mis favoritas»; not the empty scaffold of a new week nor «Vaciar»), «La semana» shows at once a skeleton of the household's semana tipo for that week (`WeekTypeSkeleton`: the `WeekTypeGrid` of «Tu semana tipo», not tappable, each slot with the icon of what its turn takes that week, pulsing, and «Buscando… las recetas de tu semana tipo»), replaced whole by the menu when it arrives (one call, not slot by slot; PRO-1186)
- The **first menu** is built when the onboarding ends (web or WhatsApp): the current week **from the sign-up day on** — the days that already went by are not generated (PRO-244: `fromDate` on `POST /menu/generate`; they are left without dishes, are not stored as «sin cocinar» and add nothing to the list) — plus the next one when onboarding finishes Friday to Sunday (Madrid time), and `/menu` then opens on the next week (PRO-65, see [Auth](./auth.md)). **Since 2026-11-19 it starts with the next meal that hasn't gone by** (`firstMenuWeeks(now, { fromNextMeal: true })`; `pastMealsOn` / `withoutPastMeals` on the API): from 21:00 Madrid the first day is tomorrow; before, today's meals past their cut-off (breakfast 10:00, lunch 15:00, merienda 18:00) stay empty — a 15:00 sign-up starts with tonight's dinner. The WhatsApp sign-up also puts the dishes the household said it makes often in that first menu (see [WhatsApp](./whatsapp.md))
- **Layout "D · Luz y foto"** (2026-10-08). The header is compact: eyebrow "Semana del 5 al 11 de octubre", h1 "Hoy, *jueves*" (or "Mañana, *viernes*" / "*Viernes* 9" for another day), and one **"···"** that opens the **week sheet**. There is no greeting, "Nº", or progress bar. Week actions live in that sheet; every per-meal action lives behind the meal's own "···" (the **meal sheet**). Nothing is lost: each action below says where it lives
- **The week sheet** (`WeekActionsSheet`, PRO-1179, propuesta de Producto 2026-11-11 §1.7 y §3): first **«Tu semana tipo»** («Qué coméis cada día; la próxima semana sale así» → `/profile/semana-tipo`; mobile only, on desktop it's the header button), then **Vista semana / Vista día** (mobile), **Compartir**, **Imprimir la semana** (with a menu) and **Historial**; last, under a line, **«Rehacer la semana…»** (not on past weeks), which opens a subsheet «Rehacer la semana»: **«Con mi semana tipo»** (`POST /menu/generate`, keeps locked meals), **«Solo con mis favoritas»** (PRO-377) and, when the week has a menu, **«Vaciarla y rellenar a mano»** (destructive, asks in a `ConfirmSheet`). No ‹ › nor «Volver a esta semana»: the header has them. What shows when is `weekSheetOptions` (`lib/weekSheet.ts`). Tests: `weekSheetOptions.test.ts` (current, future and past week, desktop), `e2e/menu-week-sheet.spec.ts` (390×844 and 1440×900)
- Users can move between weeks with **‹ ›** beside the week range in the page header ("Semana anterior" / "Semana siguiente", mobile and desktop, since 2026-10-10) (the week sheet doesn't navigate since PRO-1179), or by editing `?week=YYYY-MM-DD`. On another week a "<label> · volver a hoy" link sits under the h1. The selected day resets to today (current week) or Monday when the week changes. The day is in `?day=0…6` (0 = lunes) and the week in `?week=`, both kept with `replaceState`, so **«Ver receta» and back** (the recipe's «Volver» / «Menú», or the phone's back) lands on the same week and day (2026-11-05; `lib/menuReturn.ts`, see [recipes.md](./recipes.md)).
- Past weeks are read-only. Meals stay visible (with "Empezar a cocinar"), but the "···" buttons (also the Vista semana rows' since PRO-1178), Regenerar, Vaciar and Generar are hidden. A past week without a menu reads "Sin menú esta semana. Esta semana ya pasó. Vuelve a la actual para planificar."
- **Vista día** (mobile default) shows one day. A day strip (L M X J V S D + date; selected = ink pill, today in terracotta, "sin cocinar" days struck through) switches the day. The day's **featured meal** (lunch, else dinner, else the first other meal that holds a recipe — `FEATURED_MEAL_ORDER`; until 2026-10-20 the first one chronologically, so a breakfast took the hero) is a full-bleed photo with an overlapping caption card: eyebrow "Comida · 28 min · 3 raciones", the title (links to the recipe), an ink pill **"Empezar a cocinar"** (→ `/recipes/:id/cook?servings=N&menu=<menuId>&day=<0-6>&meal=<meal>`, `cookHref`: «Terminar» then marks the meal «✓ Comido» and comes back to /menu, see [Cooking mode](./cooking-mode.md)) and "···"; on **today** only (not a past week), a quiet link **«¿Otra cosa? Cocinar ahora»** under them → `/cocinar` (PRO-1108; mobile and desktop hero). There, **«Cocinar esta»** writes the menu: the chosen recipe replaces every dish of the meal of now, and when that meal had recipes planned a sheet **«¿Y las *lentejas*?»** moves them to another day of the week, same meal («Mañana», «El jueves», each «libre» or «en lugar de …»; skipped days greyed out), or takes them out; closing it takes them out too (`POST /cook-now/choose`, written to the menu history; [Cook now](./cook-now.md)). The «Con lo que tienes» card that sat below the day's meals is gone (PRO-1108): it moves to «Cocinar». The other meals are rows (photo, eyebrow "Cena · 25 min", title → recipe, "···"). The minutes of a meal are those of its **longest** recipe (`slotMinutes`; PRO-1078, until then the first one's: «Cena · 34 min» with 43-min buñuelos as the second dish); also in Vista semana and the printed week. **Every dish of a meal shows** (2026-11-10, Miguel: «una nota de Romanescu que no se ve por ningún sitio»; `slotDishParts` in `lib/menuDay.ts`): the main one — the first recipe, else the first dish — is the big title, and each other dish is a line under it (Fraunces, a terracotta dot; a recipe links to it, a written dish in italics) with **«Quitar «<plato>»»** (×, `DELETE …/dish/:position`, counted as `menu_sheet_action` `plato` / `quitar_plato`; hidden on a past week). Until then the others were a small «+ Romanescu» next to the chips. An empty slot is one quiet **"Añadir plato"** card + "···"; tapping the card opens the same sheet as "···" (2026-11-05, before it opened AddDishSheet). A meal the rest of the week has and this day lacks (no slot; `absentMeals`: at least half of the cooked days have it, and two at least, so a household that never has lunch at home sees nothing) is a dashed card **«El viernes no hay comida»** + «Añadir» (2026-10-19, a day's dinner passed for its lunch). **Both open the same sheet, «Sin plato todavía»** (PRO-1177, E-72; `NoDishSheets`, which options show is `noDishSheetOptions` in `lib/mealSheet.ts`), on mobile and desktop, from the empty card and its "···", the «El viernes no hay comida» card, the desktop «La semana» cells («Añadir» of an empty slot, «no hay» of a missing meal), the mobile Vista semana's empty rows and «Añadir comida» once the meal is chosen: eyebrow «Cena · viernes 14», then **Elegir receta** (the picker with its type chips and **«Que elija Mimoia»**; «Usar "<lo escrito>" como plato» writes it as the dish), **Escribir un plato** (a note, «Pizza casera», «cenamos fuera»: `PUT …/meal/:meal { note }` on an empty slot, `POST …/dish { kind: 'note' }` on a missing one) and **Ya lo tengo hecho** (the same picker with two chips instead of the types, **«Nevera»** (on by default) / **«Congelador»**: the recipe goes in with `variant: 'fridge'` or `'freezer'` and never adds to the list); on an empty slot, apart, **«Quitar <comida> de este día»** (with confirmation; «Solo este día. Para quitarla siempre, en tu semana tipo»); and, at the bottom, the link **«¿Todos los viernes? Ponlo en tu semana tipo»** → `/profile/semana-tipo` (not on a past week). - **Changes show at once** (2026-11-10, Miguel: «hago cambios sobre el menú y tardan mucho en mostrarse… elegir una receta o crear una nota»). Elegir otra receta, a note or a dish added, Quitar (a dish or the meal), a note edited or reordered, drag and drop, Comensales, Fijar and Marcar como cocinada change the cached week before the request leaves (`lib/menuOptimistic.ts`, each one mirroring its route; the recipe's name and photo come from the picker's list). The requests of these changes go **one after the other** (one TanStack mutation `scope`, `MENU_CHANGE` in `hooks/useMenu.ts`): two at once each read the week and wrote it back, so the second wiped out the first; the server's answer and the refetch only paint once the queue is empty, and while it isn't, the week is not re-read on window focus and a read that lands anyway (Mimo's `invalidateQueries()`) keeps the week on screen. Each request gives up after **20 s** (`AbortSignal.timeout`). A change by position carries what it saw there (`expect` — `dishKey` / `slotKey` in `@ona/shared`; `expectFrom` / `expectTo` on `move-slot`): «Quitar» a dish or a meal, edit / reorder / «Cambiar» a dish, drag and drop, «Usar como plato»; the route answers **409 `MENU_CHANGED`** and writes nothing when the week no longer has it (without `expect`, as Mimo calls them, nothing is checked). If a change fails (error, 409 or the 20 s), the week goes back to how it was before it, the changes queued behind it go back too and are **never sent** (they were drawn on top of it: on [Lentejas, Romanescu, Pan], «Quitar Pan» after a failed «Quitar Romanescu» would have removed Romanescu), and an ink line says «No se ha podido guardar el cambio. Lo he dejado como estaba: prueba otra vez.» or, with more, «No se ha podido guardar; he deshecho N cambios. Prueba otra vez.» (`ChangeFailedNotice`). «Una al azar» / «Cambiar al azar» still wait for the server, which picks the dish. Why it was slow: the picker's catalogue (`GET /recipes?fitsMe=1`, asked for on every /menu load by each slot's closed picker), `/taste/status` and every «al azar» re-read every recipe's step mentions and catalogue audit — ~250 ms of CPU over 400 recipes on a laptop, 2–4 s on the server — and Node does one thing at a time, so a change sent meanwhile waited behind them (a pick that takes 5–10 ms alone took 240–525 ms locally during a load). Those two reads are now kept per recipe while its inputs don't change (`derivedRecipeReads`, `services/matchableRecipes.ts`), and the picker only asks while open. The step mentions are kept per recipe and the audit per recipe and «proteína en cada plato» on/off, so households with and without it don't throw each other's out. Tests: `matchableRecipesMemo.test.ts`, `menuOptimistic.test.ts`, `menuChangeExpect.smoke.ts` (409 on a stale position, `PUT { note }`), `e2e/menu-dia-completo.spec.ts` (the change on screen while the request is held; a refused one goes back with the ones behind it; a hung one gives up; the focus doesn't paint the old week)
- **Confirmations** (PRO-72): «Este día no cocino» («Saltar día» until PRO-1177), «Quitar <comida>», «No la pongas más esta semana» (→ «No ponerla») and «Vaciar semana» ask in the app's own `ConfirmSheet` (a `MenuSheet` with "Cancelar" + the action), never the browser's `window.confirm`: in-app and automated browsers don't always show that window and count it as «Cancelar», so the action silently did nothing (test households, 2026-10-11/14). Guarded by `apps/api/src/tests/menuNoNativeConfirm.test.ts` (no `confirm(` under `components/menu/` or `app/menu/`) and `e2e/menu-confirm-sheets.spec.ts` (fails on any native dialog)

## Desktop layouts (lg+)

At `lg+` (≥1024 px) `/menu` always renders `MenuDesktop`; the Día/Semana preference only applies below `lg`.
- **Header row**: eyebrow + "Hoy, *jueves 8*" on the left; **Compartir semana**, **Semana tipo** (→ `/profile/semana-tipo`; «Regenerar semana» until PRO-1179, now «Rehacer la semana…» in the «···») and the week "···" on the right.
- **Hero row** (`1.75fr / 1fr`): the selected day's featured meal as a 420 px photo card with the caption card and "Empezar a cocinar" (no photo → the same compact icon card as mobile, so the row is shorter). On the right sit the day's other meals, main meals first: the other of lunch / dinner as a 222 px photo card, breakfast and snack as small rows (a breakfast or snack only gets the photo card when no main meal is left with a dish; each with "···") and the meals it lacks («El viernes no hay comida» + «Añadir», see Vista día), then «Añadir comida» / «Este día no cocino» right under them (they're the selected day's). Every dish of each meal shows, as in Vista día: the hero and the side meals list the other dishes under the main one, each with «Quitar». **No next-day preview** (2026-11-10, Miguel: «empuja para abajo el menú de la semana, dejando un hueco importante a la izquierda»; until then «Mañana, viernes» + its meals sat at the bottom of the right column, as drop targets and a link to that day): «La semana» starts right under the hero row. The recipe opens from the hero (photo, title, "Ver receta" in "···").
- **"La semana"** (opción C, Miguel 2026-10-27): a grid with a row of day labels («Lun 26», «Hoy, mar 27»; today in terracotta) over **one row per meal the week uses** (`weekMealRows`: a cooked day has a slot for it, empty or not; «COMIDA», «CENA» and, when the week has them, desayuno and merienda, in the order of the day; a week with none still gets comida and cena), labelled on the left in 11 px small caps, with **one cell per day**: the dish's 104 px photo (`RecipeCover`, bone cover when it has none) and its full name in Fraunces under it and, when the meal has more dishes, each other one as a short line «+ Romanescu» (2026-11-10; Fraunces 13 px, a written dish in italics), every photo the same size (until then only the featured meal had a photo and the dinner was a text line, «Cena: …», whose photo peeked onto the tile on hover; that's gone). An empty slot is a dashed «Añadir» cell and a meal the day doesn't have a dashed «no hay» cell; both open that day's «Comida · viernes — Sin plato todavía» sheet (the same as Vista día's, PRO-1177; the «Añadir» cell opened AddDishSheet until then) **without changing the day or moving the page** (2026-11-10; until then it showed the day and scrolled up to the hero); a skipped day shows «Sin cocinar» + «Reactivar» once, in its first row, and leaves its other cells blank. The selected day is tinted (`cream-deep`) down its whole column, label to last row. **Clicking a dish or the day label** shows that day in the hero row and, when the hero row is scrolled off, scrolls back up to it; it doesn't open the recipe (Miguel, 2026-10-19: Tuesday couldn't be seen the way Monday is). Each day is one DOM column laid on the shared rows with `grid-rows-subgrid` (`data-day-column`, cells `data-week-cell="<day>-<meal>"`), so the rows line up whatever the length of the names or the number of dishes: a cell with three dishes makes its whole row taller, and the next meal's row starts on the same line for the seven days (`e2e/menu-dia-completo.spec.ts`).
- **Drag-and-drop** spans the hero row and "La semana" (one context; hint «Arrastra un plato para cambiarlo de día o de comida»): every dish — week cells, the hero, the side meals — is a drop target and, when it holds a dish — a recipe or a written one («Cenamos fuera»; until 2026-10-20 only recipes moved and pulling a note selected the page) — a drag source; so are empty slots and every «no hay» cell / «El viernes no hay comida» card (targets only). A drop swaps the two slots, or, onto a meal the day lacks, moves the dish there and the source slot goes away (e.g. Friday's dinner onto the «no hay» of its lunch row becomes its lunch), via `POST /menu/:menuId/move-slot`, the same contract as Vista semana. Week cells also drag with the keyboard (Space); the hero row drags with the mouse only, and never from inside a meal sheet. A drop back where it started doesn't count as a click (2026-10-19: the hero row and the «no hay» lines took no drops). The «no hay» cell is still a drop target after it learnt to open its sheet (2026-11-10).
- Each week cell with a slot has a "···" for the same meal sheet (and a small padlock when locked); it shows on hover/focus with a hover-capable pointer and is always visible on touch. Covered by `e2e/menu-redesign.spec.ts` (a row per meal with seven cells, lunch and dinner photos the same size, drags between cells) and `menuDayView.test.ts` (`weekMealRows`).
