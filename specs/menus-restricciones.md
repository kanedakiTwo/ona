# Menus · Alergias, dietas y restricciones

> Parte de [Menus](./menus.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): alergias, dietas, halal, sin alcohol, reglas prudentes, ingredientes compuestos, lo que no gusta y qué pasa cuando cambian las alergias o la dieta.

### Restrictions & allergies

`services/dietaryRestrictions.ts` compiles each entry once per call. Restrictions come from the profile (`users.restrictions`) **and** long-term memory (`user_memories.restrictions`, e.g. "soy celíaco" told to the assistant), merged by `mergeRestrictions`. **Every member's** (PRO-271): the weekly generation adds the restrictions of everyone in the household (profile + memory, `loadHouseholdRestrictions` in `services/householdAllergies.ts`), so the owner's «Generar mi menú» respects what a member who joined by invitation said («marisco» → no shrimp). A read error keeps the generating user's own. **Changing one dish too** (PRO-305): «Cambiar al azar», adding a meal, regenerating a dish and «Aleatorio» (`routes/menus.ts`), and Mimo's `swap_meal` when it picks the recipe itself, all go through `withHouseholdRestrictions` (same file), so the owner swapping a dinner never brings back shellfish for a member allergic to marisco. A recipe picked by hand («Elegir otra receta», Mimo naming one) is not filtered. Tests: `householdAllergies.test.ts`, `householdAllergiesRoute.smoke.ts`.
- **Allergies** ("sin gluten", "sin lactosa", "leche (alergia)", "huevo", "frutos secos" (also excludes peanuts), "cacahuetes", "marisco" (crustaceans + molluscs), "pescado", "soja", "apio", "mostaza", "sésamo", "sulfitos"…, with prefixes like "alergia a…", "intolerante a…", "soy…" stripped, so «soy celíaco» told to Mimo is the gluten rule and «soy vegetariana» the diet, PRO-197) map to EU allergen tags. A recipe is out if the tag appears in its `allergens` union, in any ingredient's catalogue `allergen_tags` or `contains_tags`, or in the name-based inference (`inferAllergenTagsFromName`, conservative).
- **Diets**: "vegetariano" (no meat or fish/seafood, by curated whole-word ingredient terms + fish tags), "vegano" (also no dairy, egg, honey, gelatine), "pescetariano", "sin carne", "sin cerdo", "halal".
- **Halal** (2026-10-25, Miguel: «que la app sea utilizable por musulmanes»): a chip in onboarding and the profile (`RESTRICTION_PRESETS`); also understood as «dieta halal», «comida halal», «solo halal», «soy musulmán», «somos musulmanes» (`DIETS`, and «musulmán» with typos in the allergies box, `restrictionReader.ts` → stored as «halal»). Its own rule (`nameIsHaram`): pork and everything made from it (`PORK_TERMS`), blood, wild boar, and what usually carries pork without saying so — gelatine, lard («manteca»), sausages, frankfurt, pepperoni, «embutido», «carne picada mixta» — plus the alcohol rule below. An ingredient whose name says «halal» («salchichas halal», «gelatina halal») passes, and so do «lomos de salmón / de bacalao», «manteca de cacao / de cacahuete» and «naranja sangre». Meat itself is not excluded: whether it was slaughtered halal is decided at the butcher's, and the butcher's order asks for it ([Shop orders](./shop-orders.md)). Like every restriction it is special-category data (a religious belief, RGPD art. 9): stored only with the health consent, whose text names it since `salud-v2-2026-10` — someone who consented under v1 is asked for the current text when they mark it ([Privacy](./privacy.md)) — and never from WhatsApp. Under the chip: «Sin cerdo ni alcohol. La carne y los productos, búscalos con sello halal.» (Legal: Mimoia leaves things out, it never certifies). Tests: `dietaryRestrictions.test.ts` («halal»), `restrictionReader.test.ts`, e2e `profile-restrictions.spec.ts`.
- **«Sin alcohol»** (PRO-144, beta 2026-10-16: a household with a 3-year-old got «Riñones al jerez»): a chip in onboarding and the profile; also understood as «nada de alcohol», «cocinamos siempre sin alcohol», «sin bebidas alcohólicas». Skips every recipe with an alcoholic drink among its ingredients — vino, jerez, cerveza (also «sin alcohol»), sidra, brandy, coñac, licor, ron, oporto, vermut, cava, champán, whisky, ginebra, vodka, mirin, sake, and since 2026-10-25 anise spirits (arak, anís seco/dulce, ouzo, raki, pastís, sambuca) and other liqueurs and wines (limoncello, prosecco, sangría, mezcal, pisco…; «anís estrellado» and «anís en grano» are spices) — even when it could be left out (the wine of a sofrito): better too many than too few. Whole words («ron» ≠ «ronqueta», «cava» ≠ «cavar»); vinegars («vinagre de vino / de Jerez / de sidra») and «levadura de cerveza» don't count. It is the only rule that also reads the **recipe name** («Carrilleras de ternera con vino tinto», whose stored ingredients omit the wine). `ALCOHOL_TERMS` / `nameHasAlcohol`; over the catalogue fixture it hides exactly 5 recipes (`dietCatalog.test.ts`). Like every restriction it is stored in `users.restrictions` and so, today, only with the health-data consent and never from WhatsApp.
- **Prudent rules** (PRO-61, Miguel 2026-10-12, after the hogares de prueba got «Costillas a la riojana», «Ropa vieja del cocido», a pizza with «Harina de fuerza» and «Arroz tres delicias» with «Salsa de soja»): a false positive costs a recipe, a false negative a reaction. *Meat* also covers cuts and offal named without the animal («Costillas», «Rabo», «Oreja», «Riñones», «Morcillo», «Hueso de caña», «Entraña»…), the dish «cocido» (an ingredient starting with «Cocido» or saying «del/de cocido» — «garbanzos cocidos» stays vegetarian), «Ropa vieja», Worcestershire sauce (anchovies) and «ave» as a whole word (PRO-363, 2026-10-24: «Risotto al azafrán con láminas de champiñón», «Sopa de pan y ajo» and «Arroz a la mexicana» passed the vegetarian filter with «caldo de ave»; «avena» stays vegetarian). *Gluten* covers any flour, pasta, noodle, semolina or dough that doesn't name a gluten-free base (arroz, maíz, garbanzo, almendra, trigo sarraceno, mandioca), soy sauce/tamari/shoyu unless «sin gluten», beer, malt, Worcestershire, biscuits, puff pastry, batter; anything labelled «sin gluten» never counts. Rules live in `inferAllergenTagsFromName` (allergens) and `MEAT_TERMS`/`FISH_TERMS` (diets). Checked over the whole catalogue (fixture of the 78 system recipes, `dietCatalog.test.ts`): generated weeks with «vegetariano» or «sin gluten» never contain a recipe that breaks them.
- **Compound ingredients** (PRO-198, el recién llegado 2026-10-17: «sin cerdo» got «Ropa vieja del cocido», whose «Sobras del cocido madrileño» names no pork): each tag of an ingredient's `contains_tags` («cerdo», «pollo», «gluten», «alcohol»…) counts like a word of its name for diets, free text, dislikes and «sin alcohol», and like a catalogue allergen tag for allergies. So «sin cerdo» and «halal» skip the ropa vieja (and anything with «Caldo de cocido», generic sausages or «Foie gras de untar»), «vegetariano» skips a stock labelled «pollo», and «vegetal» never excludes. Who labels what: [Nutrition → What a compound ingredient contains](./nutrition.md) (`compoundIngredients.test.ts`)
- Known false positives kept on purpose: «panceta» and «pasta de curry/miso» count as gluten (substring rules). Known data gap: a recipe whose ingredient list omits the animal (e.g. «Arroz al caldero» = «Arroz, Ajo») can't be caught; recipe names are not checked by allergies and diets (except by «sin alcohol»; dislikes do, below).
- **Anything else** ("cilantro", "picante") and every **dislike** are whole-word, accent/case-insensitive, plural-tolerant ingredient terms ("cebolla" excludes "cebolla morada" and "cebollas", not "cebollino").
- **A dislike also reads the recipe's name** (PRO-1142, E-68, Miguel 2026-11-05: «me lo pones cada 15 días y cada 15 días te digo que no»). «Oreja a la plancha» saved as a dislike never matched the ingredient «oreja de cerdo», and a «Me apetece» kept boosting it. Now every dislike (memory and ficha, every member) is compared with the name too, same whole-word rule (`dishTerms` in `compileRestrictions`, `violatesRestrictions`): «Oreja a la plancha» or «pimiento» keep out «Oreja a la plancha» or «Pimientos rellenos». **Between a dislike and a positive on the same recipe, the latest wins:** a «Me apetece» or «Me encanta» of any member, or 5 stars («Repetir a menudo», the «¿repetimos?» of WhatsApp), said **after** the dislike lets the recipe come out again (its name only: «pimiento» in its ingredients still keeps it out); a dislike said after it keeps it out entirely, the «Me apetece» boost doesn't help. Dates: when each item was added to a `dislikes` fact (`profile_changes`; without a row, when the fact was created; an ingredient affinity ≤ −2, when it was updated), or the memory row's `updated_at` for a user without a household; positives from `recipe_verdicts.updated_at` and `recipe_notes.updated_at`. A dislike with no date is never beaten. Computed on read in `matchableRecipes.ts` (`dislikesOverridden`, `services/dishDislikes.ts`), so the weekly generator, «Cambiar al azar», adding a meal, regenerating a dish, Mimo's random swap and the review of saved menus all apply it; a read error keeps every dislike. «Elegir otra receta» hides the dishes a dislike names too (below). Tests: `dishDislikes.test.ts` (10 generated weeks, both orders, «pimiento»), `dishDislikes.smoke.ts`.
- **A food family counts whole** (2026-11-19, Miguel: «que legumbres se traduzca a todo tipo de legumbres»). A dislike or restriction that names a family (`foodCategory` in `dietaryRestrictions.ts`) excludes every food in it, in the ingredients and the recipe's name, as «marisco» does with its allergen tags: **legumbres** (lentejas, garbanzos, alubias/judías secas, pochas, fabes, verdinas, frijoles, guisantes y habas (secos o frescos), soja, edamame, tofu, tempeh, azukis, altramuces, hummus, falafel, dal…; not «salsa de soja» nor «judías verdes»), **arroz** (also paella, risotto, sushi; not «vinagre de arroz»), **patata**, **vísceras/casquería** (hígado, higadillos, riñones, callos, mollejas, oreja, morro, manitas, lengua…), **cerdo**, **carne roja**, **marisco**, **pescado**, **carne**. Before, «legumbres» only matched an ingredient called «legumbres»: a household that asked for none got chili sin carne and hummus
- Before 2026-10-07 this was an exact ingredient-name match, so allergies and diets filtered nothing.
- Every path that picks recipes on Mimoia's own initiative applies it (also «Afina tu menú», with every household member's restrictions and vetoes and the recipe name, PRO-197, [taste.md](./taste.md)): weekly generation, "Aleatorio" slot regenerations, add-course, and the assistant's random swap and `suggest_recipes`. Those paths all load through `services/matchableRecipes.ts`. An explicitly named recipe (assistant `swap_meal` by name) that breaks an allergy/diet is **not placed**: the tool returns the conflict and the model must get the user's confirmation (`confirmRestriction: true`). Dislikes still yield to explicit requests. The menu's **«Elegir otra receta»** picker hides recipes that break an allergy or diet (`GET /recipes?fitsMe=1`), and since PRO-1142 the dishes a household dislike names, with the same «latest wins» rule (`recipeIdsNamedByDislikes`); ingredient dislikes are not hidden there. A recipe id sent straight to `PUT /menu/…/meal/:meal { recipeId }` is not re-checked.
- **Milk allergy is not «sin lactosa»** (PRO-312, Seguridad; Reg. (UE) 1169/2011 annex II: «leche y sus derivados (incluida la lactosa)»). The chip **«leche (alergia)»** sits next to «sin lactosa». It — and «aplv», «leche», «proteína de (la) leche (de vaca)», «alergia a la leche», as already saved or typed — compiles to the `lactosa` tag (every dairy by name, «leche/nata/queso/yogur sin lactosa» included, and by catalogue tag) **plus** `MILK_TERMS`, dairy whose name doesn't say so (kéfir, ghee, caseinato, gouda, burrata, bechamel, helado, flan…). Plant drinks («bebida de avena», «leche de coco») stay. «sin lactosa» / «intolerancia a la lactosa» is the intolerance and keeps its rule. **«sin lácteos», «lácteos», «no tomo lácteos»** (and «productos/derivados lácteos», «leche y derivados») mean no dairy at all, so they are the milk allergy too (PRO-333, Miguel 2026-10-22: A; nothing stored is migrated, only how it is read); a fridge text that says «no tomo lácteos» proposes saving «leche (alergia)» (`fridgeReader.ts`). A trailing «(alergia)» on any entry is dropped («marisco (alergia)» = «marisco»). `hasMilkAllergy` (`dietaryRestrictions.ts`) tells the two apart for Mimo ([Advisor](./advisor.md)). Nothing stored is rewritten: the old words are read as the allergy. Tests: `dietaryRestrictions.test.ts`.
- **Runny yolk and pink mince, never for a baby** (PRO-1181, D-1009, seguridad de la dieta; Miguel 2026-11-12): Cocina's list (COC-510 and its audit up to 2026-11-12: 19 recipes with a runny or raw yolk — huevos al plato, shakshuka, carbonara, César… — and 3 with minced meat left pink — hummus kawarma, sfiha, hamburguesa casera; PRO-1231 adds 20 from its audits of 13 to 17-nov, among them the six «de diario» with a runny yolk — arroz a la cubana, huevos rotos, pisto con huevo frito, pasta carbonara, tostadas con aguacate y huevo, revuelto de calabacín — plus steak tartar and kofta b'siniyah under the mince tag) carries the hidden tag `yema-liquida` or `carne-picada-poco-hecha` (`internalTags`, never shown). A household whose ficha has a person aged **0 to 2** (`household_persons.age_band = '0-2'`, `babyAtHome` in `fichaMenuConstraints`) never gets them from `loadMatchableRecipes`, so not in any generated week, «Aleatorio», swap or Mimo pick; an explicitly chosen recipe still goes. Mimoia doesn't keep pregnancy (no «Embarazo» in the profile), so **for everyone** the cooking step of each one ends with a line «Para embarazo o bebés: tapa 4 minutos más, hasta que la yema esté firme.» (or the recipe's own: «cuécelos 10 minutos…», «usa mayonesa de bote (pasteurizada)…», «hasta que no quede nada rosa…»). Both are written by `applyUndercookedMarks` (`services/undercookedRecipes.ts`) on every boot after `db:migrate`: catalogue recipes found by production id or by name (drafts too), the line in the last step that names the egg or the meat, never twice; users' copies get the tag only (their steps are theirs). It logs the names of the list not in the catalogue yet. A recipe added later needs its entry in that list. Tests: `undercookedRecipes.test.ts`.
- The onboarding and profile chips share one list, `RESTRICTION_PRESETS` (`@ona/shared`), and a test checks that every preset compiles to a real rule.

### Cuando cambian las alergias o la dieta (PRO-364)

A saved menu was checked when it was built; restrictions can arrive later (2026-10-23: a household's menu was built before the health consent, the gluten and lactose allergies came afterwards from the profile, and Saturday kept a pizza). So whenever someone **saves restrictions** — `PUT /user/:id` with `restrictions` (the profile, «Tus alergias y tu dieta» after joining a household, «Tu menú está listo»), the `restrictions` memory key (Mimo's `update_memory`, `PATCH /memory`, the fridge question; `setMemoryFact` / `setMemoryBatch` in `userMemoryStore.ts`), the **ficha del hogar** (PRO-1067: «Así es», «Corregir», «Olvidar», «Deshacer» and Mimo's «Apunta que…» on a `restrictions` fact, `fichaStore.ts`) — or **gives the health consent** (`POST /user/:id/health-consent { consent: true }`), the household's saved menus from this week on are reviewed (`reviewHouseholdMenus`, `services/menuRestrictionsReview.ts`), before the response:
- Only the latest menu of each week (household-scoped, like the shopping list). Each recipe dish is checked with `violatesRestrictions` against everyone's restrictions (`withHouseholdRestrictions`, PRO-305), reading the recipe as the matcher does: allergens, ingredients and what the steps name (`stepMentions`, PRO-346), so «halal» and «sin alcohol» count too. A dish id that isn't a uuid (old data) is skipped.
- **A dish picked by hand** (a `menu_edits` row that put that recipe in that slot after the menu was made; a leftover of one too) that breaks a rule — the household chose it knowing, after Mimo asked (`confirmRestriction`) — **stays, unless what was just saved is what it breaks** (PRO-1067, D-142). Each save path reads everyone's restrictions before writing (`householdRestrictionsBefore`) and the review compares: only the entries that weren't there (`savedRestrictions`, case and spaces aside) move a hand pick. When the restrictions from before can't be read, everything counts as just saved.
- **Days already past, and today's meals past their hour** (breakfast 10:00, lunch 16:00, snack 19:00, dinner 23:00 Madrid, the shopping list's cutoffs), and slots marked eaten are **never touched**.
- A dish that breaks them is re-picked with the same logic as «Cambiar» (`PUT …/meal/:meal`): the same context — dislikes, equipment, favourites, ratings, vetoes, time budget, school dinners — now shared in `services/menuSlotPick.ts`, keeping the dish's course; other dishes of the slot stay. A leftover follows the dish it came from. **A «Fijada» slot is changed too** (PRO-1067: safety first, like PRO-345; the slot stays fixed with the new dish). The new dish doesn't keep the old one's marks (`restrictionConflict`, an older `replaced`, the smart menu's `why`) and carries `replaced: { recipeName, reason: 'restrictions' }`: the day's card says «En lugar de «Pizza margarita», que no encaja con vuestras alergias o dieta» (`replacedDishLine`, `data-testid="dish-replaced"`, the same chip as PRO-345), and so does its leftover, until the dish is changed by hand.
- When there's no candidate, or for a táper already cooked (freezer, fridge, «sobras de otro día»), the dish stays with `restrictionConflict: true` and the day's card shows «No encaja con las alergias o la dieta de casa · cámbialo en «···»» (`SlotChips` in `DayMeals.tsx`, `data-testid="restriction-conflict"`), first among the chips; a dish PRO-345 put in keeps its «En lugar de …» chip beside it. Any later change of the dish drops it, and the next review drops it if the dish fits again.
- Each change is a `swap` row in the change log (actor `user`, who saved; PRO-345's swaps write none). Prep alerts are rebuilt. The rolling shopping list (`GET /shopping-list`) is rebuilt from the menus on every read; a list saved for one menu gets its menu lines rebuilt, keeping manual ones.
- Best-effort: an error is logged and never fails the save. Withdrawing the consent (fewer restrictions) doesn't change the menus.
- Tests: `menuRestrictionsReview.test.ts` (which slots are past; PRO-1067: fixed slots, hand picks, «En lugar de …», what was just saved), `menuRestrictionsReview.smoke.ts` («Pizza» on Saturday, «sin gluten» saved by the profile, by the memory and by the ficha; this Monday untouched; the pizza picked again by hand stays when «sin lactosa» comes next), e2e `menu-dish-replaced.spec.ts` (the chip).

Then picks one at random from the pool. **Favorites get double weight** — they appear twice in the random pool.

**Menú inteligente a la sombra (PRO-255):** after a week is generated, Mimo thinks the same week in the background and stores its proposal in `menu_plans`; the menu the household sees is still this generator's — see [Menú inteligente](./smart-menu.md).
