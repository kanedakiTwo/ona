# Menus · Semana tipo, comidas fijas, comedor escolar y festivos

> Parte de [Menus](./menus.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): la semana tipo (D-182), las comidas fijas, el menú del comedor escolar y los festivos.

## Semana tipo (D-182)

- **What it is:** what the household usually eats each day, said once («los viernes por la noche, pizza»; «los jueves, una semana ceviche y otra carpaccio»), and every week's menu comes from it. **One per household** (`household_week_types`, `slots` = 7 days × meal → slot), whoever generates — a member, the Thursday autoplan or Mimo (before, each person's own plantilla). Design: [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-semana-tipo-design.md](../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-semana-tipo-design.md).
- **A slot** (`WeekTypeSlot`, `@ona/shared` `types/weekType.ts`): **libre** (no options: Mimo picks as before), **«no hay»** (`off`: that meal isn't planned that day) or **up to 4 options**: a **dish type** (`DISH_TYPES`, [Recipes](./recipes.md)), a **concrete recipe**, **something fixed with no recipe** (the comida fija, with what it carries per person, or just a name), **«Táper de la cena»** (breakfast, lunch or snack, Tuesday to Sunday, and only option) and **«Cualquier receta»**. **By turns** (default): one a week, in order, counting from the Monday it was saved (`since`), whether or not those weeks were cooked; «pizza, cada 2» is `[Pizza, Cualquier receta]`. **Together:** all of them in that meal, one dish each. One option in a meal of 2–3 dishes takes the first dish and the rest are drawn free. Plus the slot's diners and dishes.
- **API:** `GET /households/me/week-type` (the household's row: created with the household, `createSoloHouseholdForNewUser`; the households from before got theirs from the migration) and `PUT /households/me/week-type/:day/:meal` with the whole slot: only that slot is written (two people on the same slot: the last wins). Changing its options starts the turns again this week. Refused: a táper on a dinner, on Monday or with other options, an unknown type (400), a recipe the household can't see (404), a child of the household (403).
- **The generator** (`menuGenerator.ts` + `weekTypePlan.ts`), in this order — the first that applies wins: a «sin cocinar» day; a meal out this week («¿Alguna comida fuera?», even if the week type asks for something); a locked slot; «no hay» (unless «¿Viene alguien?» adds that meal this week); the option of the turn (or all together), with the school menu as a filter inside; a free slot as before. A **type** draws only recipes of that type, with every usual filter (allergies, diet, audit, vetoes, the day's time, holidays, equipment, school, the week's variety) and **before the rest of the week**, so a free slot never takes the only ceviche; when none fits, a **dish** type (Pizza, Ceviche…) is written by its name (`NoteDish.fixedDish`, nothing on the list) — unless the name itself breaks an allergy or diet («Pasta» in a celiac household), then the slot is drawn free — and a **broad** one (Carne, Indio…) takes any recipe; both leave `dish_type_missing_<type>_<meal>_d<day>` and a catalogue wish for Cocina (`recordCatalogWish`, source `mimo`). **The day card says so** (PRO-1186): the dish carries `fromWeekType: { type, missing: true }` and shows a warning chip — «Ninguna receta de «Pizza» encaja: lo dejo escrito» (the note) or «Ninguna receta de «Carne» encaja: he puesto otra» (the free draw). A dish drawn for a type carries `fromWeekType: { type }` and the chip **«<tipo> · semana tipo»** («Legumbres · semana tipo», with its icon); a concrete recipe or a dish written there (`fixedDish`), **«Semana tipo»**; a «Fijo: …» none (its text says it), nor a leftover copied from it (`weekTypeChipOf`, `@ona/shared`). Desktop «La semana» cells show the short line too. Any change by hand drops it. A **concrete recipe** goes in with only the allergy and diet check (`fixedDish: true`; not again elsewhere that week); if it breaks someone's allergy or diet the slot is drawn free and says «En lugar de «…», que no encaja con vuestras alergias o dieta» (PRO-345 chip, `week_type_recipe_restricted_<meal>_d<day>`). **Something fixed** is the whole meal: «Fijo: <name>» with its things (see Comidas fijas), or just its name when it carries nothing — that name goes through the allergy and diet check as an ingredient (as PRO-1141: «Jamón con melón» isn't written in a vegetarian household; the slot is drawn free and says «En lugar de «Jamón con melón»…»). The **táper** follows PRO-223 (applied after the draw).
- **A household that touched nothing gets the same menu:** the migrated week type of a plain plantilla draws exactly the old week (`weekType.test.ts`).
- **«Que elija Mimoia» without a chip («Cambiar al azar» until PRO-1175), regenerating a dish and Mimo's `swap_meal` without a recipe respect the slot:** another recipe of the type it asks for there that week (`weekTypeDishTypeAt`), with its chip (`fromWeekTypeOf`; none of that type → any, with the warning); a concrete recipe or something fixed is changed for any recipe (no 409 any more).
- **From the menu (phase 5, PRO-1186):** the meal sheet's «Más opciones» → **«Guardar en mi semana tipo»** («Lo de esta comida, los sábados») opens its sheet: «Los sábados, cena: todas las semanas.», then each dish type of the meal's recipe (the one the week type already asked for first; `dishTypes` is hydrated on every menu read, not stored), **«Siempre esta receta»**, or a written dish as it is («Siempre «Tortilla de patatas»», something fixed with nothing) — `weekTypeChoices`, `lib/mealSheet.ts` —, and **«Otro tipo de plato»** with every type by family (the panel's chips, `DishTypeChips`); a táper, a leftover or an empty meal offer only the types. `POST /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal/week-type { option }` (a type, a recipe the household can see — its name from the recipe — or something fixed; a táper or «cualquier receta» 400, an unknown type 400, an unseen recipe 404, a meal the day doesn't have 404, a child 403) writes that day and meal of the household's week type — that one option every week from this week, diners and dishes kept, «no hay» turned on (`weekTypeSlotSaving`) — and **applies it to the week on screen** (`weekTypeMealChange`): if the meal already is it (a recipe of that type, that recipe, that written dish) it only gets its chip (`mark`); else, if the meal is **locked** (also a dish chosen by hand, PRO-1175), **eaten**, a táper / leftover or **on a day that already went by** (Madrid; `pastDayIndexes`, 2026-11-18), this week stays (`kept`); else a recipe of that type is drawn in the main dish (the usual filters, never the one there; none → `missing`, nothing changes), or that recipe / written dish is put (`changed`). The answer carries `weekTypeApplied` and the menu's pill says «Guardado en tu semana tipo: los sábados, cena, «Legumbres».» (+ «Esta semana se queda como está.» when kept; + «Esta semana ninguna receta de ese tipo encaja aquí: se queda como está.» when missing; `showMenuNotice`). Counted as `menu_sheet_action` `semana_tipo` (opening it) and `template_changed` `week_type` (saving). Also from the mobile Vista semana rows, which open this same sheet since PRO-1178.
- **Weeks already made don't change** when the week type changes; the next generated ones follow it («Regenerar semana», «Rehacer con mis favoritas», the Thursday autoplan, `copy` of the month menu takes its meals). `customTemplate` on `POST /menu/generate` (tests) is a plain on/off plantilla with no options.
- **Seen everywhere (PRO-1204):** Perfil opens with «Vuestra semana», the week type drawn small (see [Household profile](./household-profile.md)); and **each day of the menu** (mobile and desktop day view, not a skipped day) has one quiet line with what the week type asks for that day — **«Según tu semana: legumbres y cena fuera»** (`weekTypeDayLine`, `@ona/shared`: lunch first without its name, then «… en la cena», «… en el desayuno», «… en la merienda»; a lunch or dinner «no hay» is «comida fuera» / «cena fuera»; a free weekday lunch on a school-menu weekday, «cole a mediodía»; the option of that week's turn; nothing when every meal is free) linking to `/profile/semana-tipo?dia=N`, which marks that day in the grid. It is never a check: a dish the person changed is not flagged. Tests: `weekTypeSummary.test.ts`, e2e `week-type-head.spec.ts` (390×844)
- **Who writes it:** **Perfil → «Tu semana tipo»** (`/profile/semana-tipo`, phase 2; it replaced the «Plantilla semanal» card with its «Comidas fijas», «Días de táper» and phase 1's «Platos fijos» lines). A grid of 7 days × the household's meals (days in rows on mobile, in columns at `md+` like «La semana»; lunch and dinner always; breakfast and snack when some day has them, or after «+ Desayuno» / «+ Merienda»), one icon and its name per option: one option fills the cell, two split it in two, three or four in four bands; free cells say «Cualquier receta», off ones «no hay». Tapping a cell opens its panel (a sheet on mobile, the right column on desktop): «Por turnos / Juntos» (two options or more), «Cada cuánto» (one option: every week, 2, 3 or 4 — stored as the turn `[option, Cualquier receta…]`, `weekTypeSlotView` / `weekTypeSlotFromView`), the options with ↑ ↓ and «Quitar», «+ Añadir» (a concrete recipe through the recipe search, the dish types by family, and the wildcards: «Cualquier receta», «Táper de la cena» only where it can go and alone, «Algo fijo sin receta» with its name and what it carries per person for the list), comensales and platos of the slot, «Así lo pondría Mimo» (the next 4 weeks, `weekTypePreview`), «Igual otros días» (copies the slot to that day) and «No hay <comida> este día» / «Hay <comida> este día». Every change writes that one slot at once (`useSaveWeekTypeSlot`, optimistic). Also: Mimo's `update_weekly_template` (through the route; a slot without its own diners counts the household's real diners) and `set_fixed_meal`; the sign-up's «¿Algo más?» analysis (who doesn't eat at home lowers those slots' diners); a `fixed_dish` **with day and meal said to Mimo** (`upsert_profile_fact` → those slots take it from this week on, no fact is written). **The nightly and weekly refiner never write it** (such a fact is discarded as `week_type` until phase 3). A `fixed_dish` without day or meal stays a taste in the ficha. **A comida fija that turned a «no hay» meal on** (`offWhenFree`, from the migration or Mimo's `set_fixed_meal`) leaves it «no hay» again when it's removed.
- **Migration, once and repeatable** (`applyWeekTypeMigration`, on every boot after `db:migrate`; only for households from before, new ones get their row when created): each household without a row gets one from the plantilla of **whoever generated its last menu** (else the first member whose plantilla isn't the default, else the owner's — `pickPlantillaOwner`): meals, diners, dishes, comidas fijas, días de táper; and from the ficha's `fixed_dish` with day and meal: a **dish type** when the name is one («pizza» → every week a pizza that fits, not always the same), else the recipe the PRO-1141 name search finds **when it finds exactly one**, else the name written; two in a slot go by turns. Those facts are then **archived in the ficha** (`fact_retired`, «Pasa a la semana tipo del hogar»), so they never live in two places. `skipped_meal` is **not migrated** in phase 1 (an untouched household keeps its menu; it comes with Mimo in phase 3). A second run creates nothing and changes nothing. `pinnedType` isn't migrated (it never did anything); `dish_frequency` has no day nor meal.
- **Usage events:** each changed part of a slot is one `template_changed` (`meals`, `diners`, `dish_counts`, `fixed_meals`, `taper_days`, `week_type`).
- Limits: `skipped_meal` and `dish_frequency` facts aren't read by the menu; the smart menu (planner, off in production) only reads its diners; the blank week tells a semana tipo from the default one by what it asks (`weekTypeConfigured`), not by whether the household saved it (every household has a row).
- Tests: `weekType.test.ts` (same menu for an untouched household, also with a comida fija, a táper and a plato fijo; turns and «cada 2»; together; the §5 table; a type with no recipes; a recipe or a written dish that breaks an allergy; the migration — whose plantilla, type / one recipe / written, `skipped_meal` left out —; removing a comida fija; the «Platos fijos» lines; `dishTypesOf` with seed recipes; the profile writes only what changed), `profileRefiner.test.ts` (the refiner doesn't write it), `appSkills.test.ts` (real diners), `weekTypeRoute.smoke.ts` (row on sign-up, PUT one slot keeps the rest, refusals, settings save keeps it, migration twice archiving the fact, two weeks generated), e2e `week-type.spec.ts` (saving the profile keeps it; the grid end to end: a type, «cada 2», turns, together, remove, «no hay» and the next week generated) and `fixed-meals.spec.ts`; `weekTypePanel.test.ts` (the panel's view of a slot and its preview). Phase 5 (PRO-1186): `weekTypeFromMenu.test.ts` (saving the slot; applying it or not — already it, locked, eaten, táper, free —; the chip and the warning; what the sheet offers; the pill's line), `weekType.test.ts` (a drawn type carries its chip), `weekTypeFromMenu.smoke.ts` (kept when locked, changed with its chip when free, mark again, kept when eaten, refusals, kept on a free dinner of a past week) and e2e `week-type-menu.spec.ts` (390×844 and 1440×900: «Más opciones → Guardar en mi semana tipo → Otro tipo de plato → Legumbres» on a free Saturday dinner leaves a legume recipe with «Legumbres · semana tipo» and «Sábado, cena: Legumbres» in «Tu semana tipo»; «Llenar con mi semana tipo» shows the skeleton with «Buscando…» and the week type's cells until the menu arrives).

## Comidas fijas (PRO-218)

- A meal (desayuno, comida, merienda or cena) of some days can be **fija**: «todos los días desayuno yogur con cereales y dos galletas» is said once. It has a short name and a short list of things with amounts **per person** (u, g or ml; up to 10). One per meal type in the profile and for Mimo. Since D-182 it is the «something fixed» option of those slots of the household's **semana tipo**.
- **Where:** Perfil → «Tu semana tipo»: a slot → «+ Añadir» → «Algo fijo sin receta» (name, «Qué lleva, por persona»), and «Igual otros días» to put it on other days; «Quitar» takes it away. And Mimo: `get_fixed_meals` / `set_fixed_meal` (web and WhatsApp).
- **The generator never draws a recipe for it:** the slot gets one note «Fijo: <name>» carrying the items (`NoteDish.fixed`), with the slot's diners when set; it doesn't count for the calorie target. The meal shows up on its days even if it was off before. A locked slot keeps what it had; a «sin cocinar» day stays empty.
- **The shopping list** adds the week's amounts (see [Shopping → Comidas fijas](./shopping-suma.md)).
- Limits: it applies when a week is generated or regenerated (a week already made doesn't change); editing the note's text by hand turns it into an ordinary note (no longer on the list).
- Tests: `fixedMeals.test.ts` (list sums, generator leaves it alone, storage, Mimo), smoke `fixedMealsRoute.smoke.ts`, e2e `fixed-meals.spec.ts`.

## Menú del comedor escolar (PRO-224)

- **What it does:** the household passes the school's monthly menu and the menu's **dinner of a school day doesn't repeat the group** of the kids' lunch: lentejas at school → that dinner is not a pulse. Groups: legumbre, pescado, carne, huevo, pasta o arroz, verdura. Days without a school menu (holidays, vacations, weekends) have no rule. Wording is «para no repetir», never «para equilibrar» (constitution §6).
- **Where:** discreet on purpose (D-069). In the app only when the household has a child — a member with the «Niñ@» role, a member who counted children 2–10 in the web sign-up, a person without an account in «Tu ficha» under 18, or children 2–10 in the household's ficha (the WhatsApp sign-up and Mimo write it there; the last two since 2026-10-22, when Miguel and a beta tester with children only reached it by its URL) (`householdShowsSchoolMenu`): `/profile/casa` shows a «Menú del cole» row → `/profile/casa/cole`: «Subir foto o PDF» (JPG, PNG, WEBP or PDF, ≤ 15 MB) → «Lo que he leído · N días» with each day's dish and group editable (or quitar) → «Guardar»; below, «Guardado» with a «Quitar» per day. Without a child it works through Mimo: on WhatsApp a photo with «menú del cole / del colegio / del comedor / comedor escolar» in the caption or in a message of the last 30 min, or any **PDF**, is read and saved and Mimo replies with what it read («He apuntado el menú del cole (N días): lunes 2: Lentejas (legumbre)… Si algo no está bien, dímelo»); corrections and questions go through `get_school_menu` / `set_school_menu_days` (web chat and WhatsApp; the web chat has no file upload, so Mimo points to WhatsApp or the profile page).
- **The reader** (`services/schoolMenu.ts`): one **Claude Opus 5.5** call (D-070, `SCHOOL_MENU_MODEL`, feature `school_menu_read` billed to the user, behind the monthly spend cap) with the image or the PDF and today's date (for the year). It returns per school day the main dish (with a pulse/pasta first course and a meat/fish/egg second, both, grouped by the second) and its group; `parseSchoolMenuAnswer` drops bad dates, weekends, empty dishes and repeated dates, and takes an unknown group from the dish name (`dishFoodGroup`). Nothing is saved until the household saves it (web) — WhatsApp saves at once and shows it to fix. A photo is first made upright and at most 1568 px on its long side as JPEG (`fitImageForClaude`, `services/visionImage.ts`; one already small, or that can't be decoded, goes as it is): 2026-10-23, a camera photo uploaded from the web failed with «Internal server error» — over Claude's 5 MB per image — while the same menu through WhatsApp, already compressed, was read.
- **Storage:** `school_lunches` (migration `0078_school_lunches`, only adds a table): household, date (unique per household), dish, `food_group`, who added it. Only a dish per date: no child data, no school name. `GET /school-menu?from&to` (days + `visible`), `POST /school-menu/read` (multipart `file` up to 25 MB, not saved; 422 when it isn't a school menu, 413 «El archivo es demasiado grande…», 415 `HEIC` for an iPhone HEIC photo (by type or `.heic` name; sharp can't decode it) saying to upload a screenshot or send it by WhatsApp, 413 `TOO_BIG` for a PDF over 20 MB or 100 pages (checked before calling the model) or when the model answers «too large», saying to upload only the month's page (PRO-355; WhatsApp says the same in its words: send it as a photo, not a document / only the month's page), 503 «No he podido leer el menú ahora. Prueba otra vez en un momento o mándamelo por WhatsApp con «menú del cole».» when the reading fails — the web shows that, never a bare «Internal server error»), `PUT /school-menu` `{ days, clear? }` (replace those dates, remove `clear`).
- **The rule** (`withoutFoodGroup`, `recipeFoodGroup` in `@ona/shared` `types/schoolMenu.ts`): a recipe's group comes from its name (the group whose word comes first: «Arroz con pollo» → pasta o arroz) or else from its ingredients (legumbre, pescado, carne, pasta o arroz, huevo); a recipe with no group is never excluded. The weekly generator (`dinnerAvoidGroups` in `buildRandomMenu`) and every single-slot path on a dinner (Cambiar al azar, add a meal, regenerate a dish, Mimo's `swap_meal`: `schoolDinnerPool`) draw from the pool without that group. Picking a recipe by hand is never blocked.
- Limits: the group is read from names (no catalogue field yet); one school menu per household (not per child); lunch at home on a school day is not touched.
- Tests: `schoolMenu.test.ts` (the reader with an invented sample menu, groups, «lunes con lentejas → cena sin legumbre», WhatsApp intent/reply/PDF parsing), `schoolMenuReadRoute.test.ts` (the read route never answers 500: HEIC, a big PDF, not a menu, the model failing), smoke `schoolMenuRoute.smoke.ts`, e2e `school-menu.spec.ts` (the row only with a child). `visionImage.test.ts`: a camera-sized photo reaches the reader as a JPEG ≤ 1568 px and < 5 MB; a PDF as it is.
## Festivos (PRO-226)

- The household tells its **comunidad autónoma** — memory key `region` (ISO 3166-2:ES code: `MD`, `CT`…), chosen in `/profile/memoria` → Hogar → «Comunidad autónoma» or told to Mimo (`update_memory`, web and WhatsApp); it is copied to the ficha as a household fact. Not asked in the sign-up yet.
- **Holidays table** (`packages/shared/src/constants/holidays.ts`, `PUBLIC_HOLIDAYS`): the national and regional fiestas laborales of **2026 and 2027**, copied from the annex of the Dirección General de Trabajo's yearly resolution in the BOE — 2026: Resolución de 17 de octubre de 2025, BOE núm. 259 de 28-10-2025, [BOE-A-2025-21667](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2025/10/28/pdfs/BOE-A-2025-21667.pdf); 2027: Resolución de 8 de octubre de 2026, BOE núm. 254 de 13-10-2026, [BOE-A-2026-21254](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2026/10/13/pdfs/BOE-A-2026-21254.pdf). Every comunidad, Ceuta and Melilla has its 12 fiestas (Canarias 11 in the annex: its 12th is per island). **Not in the table:** local holidays (two per town, in each provincial bulletin), the Canarias island fiestas and the Arán substitution (annex notes 1 and 2), and any year other than 2026–2027 (a year missing → no holidays). Adding a year = copying its annex when the BOE publishes it (usually October).
- **Menu rule** (`services/menuHolidays.ts`, `holidayTimeBudgets`): a Monday–Friday holiday of the household's comunidad is cooked like a weekend day: its time limit becomes **Saturday's** (none unless the household set one for Saturdays). So with «Rápido» (≤ 30 min Mon–Fri) a holiday Thursday gets any recipe, while in another comunidad that Thursday keeps the 30 minutes. The weekly generator and every single-slot path (`slotTimeBudget`: Cambiar al azar, add a meal, regenerate a dish) apply it. The region read is the generating user's memory. `weekends_only` recipes are still kept off weekday holidays.
- **Saying it**: a generated week with a holiday among the days the WhatsApp message shows carries one line under the opener (digest and picture caption): «El jueves es festivo: te he puesto algo con más calma.» when it lifted a limit, «El jueves es festivo.» otherwise, «El lunes y el martes son festivos…» for two (`holidayLine` in `menuText.ts`). Not when the user only asks to see the week. Mimo's `generate_weekly_menu` result lists the week's holidays so it says it in the web chat too (`weekHolidaySummary`).
- Tests: `menuHolidays.test.ts` (both years complete per comunidad, BOE spot checks, a holiday Thursday without the 30-min limit in Aragón and with it in Madrid — rule and generator —, single-slot paths, the message line); e2e `profile-region.spec.ts`.
