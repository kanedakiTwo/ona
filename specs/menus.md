- Users can generate a weekly menu (Monday–Sunday) for the current week or any future week with one click. A week with no dishes (new or emptied) shows a "Tu semana está *en blanco*" card above its empty slots (no sparkles since PRO-1186: configuring weighs more than asking the AI; «Generar mi menú» until then). **With a semana tipo** (PRO-1180; the household said something: a dish, a «no hay», a meal added, more dishes — `weekTypeConfigured`, `lib/blankWeek.ts`) it shows the semana tipo in two lines (`weekTypeSummary`: the meals, «Comida y cena», and what it asks for, «legumbres lunes y miércoles · viernes cena: pizza», three at most and «y N más») with **«Editar»** (→ `/profile/semana-tipo`) and the main button **«Llenar con mi semana tipo»**; **without one** (lunch and dinner every day, any recipe: every household until it says something) the button says **«Hacer mi menú»** and under it the link **«Primero, cuéntame cómo coméis»** (→ `/profile/semana-tipo`). Both generate the same way; «Empezar de cero», the optional questions and the «Menú del mes» stay. Otherwise **«Rehacer la semana…» → «Con mi semana tipo»** in the week sheet (PRO-1179; «Regenerar semana» until then). **While a week is generated** («Llenar con mi semana tipo», «Con mi semana tipo», «Solo con mis favoritas»; not the empty scaffold of a new week nor «Vaciar»), «La semana» shows at once a skeleton of the household's semana tipo for that week (`WeekTypeSkeleton`: the `WeekTypeGrid` of «Tu semana tipo», not tappable, each slot with the icon of what its turn takes that week, pulsing, and «Buscando… las recetas de tu semana tipo»), replaced whole by the menu when it arrives (one call, not slot by slot; PRO-1186)
- The **first menu** is built when the onboarding ends (web or WhatsApp): the current week **from the sign-up day on** — the days that already went by are not generated (PRO-244: `fromDate` on `POST /menu/generate`; they are left without dishes, are not stored as «sin cocinar» and add nothing to the list) — plus the next one when onboarding finishes Friday to Sunday (Madrid time), and `/menu` then opens on the next week (PRO-65, see [Auth](./auth.md)). **Since 2026-11-19 it starts with the next meal that hasn't gone by** (`firstMenuWeeks(now, { fromNextMeal: true })`; `pastMealsOn` / `withoutPastMeals` on the API): from 21:00 Madrid the first day is tomorrow; before, today's meals past their cut-off (breakfast 10:00, lunch 15:00, merienda 18:00) stay empty — a 15:00 sign-up starts with tonight's dinner. The WhatsApp sign-up also puts the dishes the household said it makes often in that first menu (see [WhatsApp](./whatsapp.md))
- **Layout "D · Luz y foto"** (2026-10-08). The header is compact: eyebrow "Semana del 5 al 11 de octubre", h1 "Hoy, *jueves*" (or "Mañana, *viernes*" / "*Viernes* 9" for another day), and one **"···"** that opens the **week sheet**. There is no greeting, "Nº", or progress bar. Week actions live in that sheet; every per-meal action lives behind the meal's own "···" (the **meal sheet**). Nothing is lost: each action below says where it lives

No ‹ › nor «Volver a esta semana»: the header has them. What shows when is `weekSheetOptions` (`lib/weekSheet.ts`). - Users can move between weeks with **‹ ›** beside the week range in the page header ("Semana anterior" / "Semana siguiente", mobile and desktop, since 2026-10-10) (the week sheet doesn't navigate since PRO-1179), or by editing `?week=YYYY-MM-DD`. On another week a "<label> · volver a hoy" link sits under the h1. The selected day resets to today (current week) or Monday when the week changes. The day is in `?day=0…6` (0 = lunes) and the week in `?week=`, both kept with `replaceState`, so **«Ver receta» and back** (the recipe's «Volver» / «Menú», or the phone's back) lands on the same week and day (2026-11-05; `lib/menuReturn.ts`, see [recipes.md](./recipes.md)).

- **Changes show at once** (2026-11-10, Miguel: «hago cambios sobre el menú y tardan mucho en mostrarse… elegir una receta o crear una nota»). Elegir otra receta, a note or a dish added, Quitar (a dish or the meal), a note edited or reordered, drag and drop, Comensales, Fijar and Marcar como cocinada change the cached week before the request leaves (`lib/menuOptimistic.ts`, each one mirroring its route; the recipe's name and photo come from the picker's list). The requests of these changes go **one after the other** (one TanStack mutation `scope`, `MENU_CHANGE` in `hooks/useMenu.ts`): two at once each read the week and wrote it back, so the second wiped out the first; the server's answer and the refetch only paint once the queue is empty, and while it isn't, the week is not re-read on window focus and a read that lands anyway (Mimo's `invalidateQueries()`) keeps the week on screen. Each request gives up after **20 s** (`AbortSignal.timeout`). A change by position carries what it saw there (`expect` — `dishKey` / `slotKey` in `@ona/shared`; `expectFrom` / `expectTo` on `move-slot`): «Quitar» a dish or a meal, edit / reorder / «Cambiar» a dish, drag and drop, «Usar como plato»; the route answers **409 `MENU_CHANGED`** and writes nothing when the week no longer has it (without `expect`, as Mimo calls them, nothing is checked). If a change fails (error, 409 or the 20 s), the week goes back to how it was before it, the changes queued behind it go back too and are **never sent** (they were drawn on top of it: on [Lentejas, Romanescu, Pan], «Quitar Pan» after a failed «Quitar Romanescu» would have removed Romanescu), and an ink line says «No se ha podido guardar el cambio. Lo he dejado como estaba: prueba otra vez.» or, with more, «No se ha podido guardar; he deshecho N cambios. Prueba otra vez.» (`ChangeFailedNotice`). «Una al azar» / «Cambiar al azar» still wait for the server, which picks the dish. Why it was slow: the picker's catalogue (`GET /recipes?fitsMe=1`, asked for on every /menu load by each slot's closed picker), `/taste/status` and every «al azar» re-read every recipe's step mentions and catalogue audit — ~250 ms of CPU over 400 recipes on a laptop, 2–4 s on the server — and Node does one thing at a time, so a change sent meanwhile waited behind them (a pick that takes 5–10 ms alone took 240–525 ms locally during a load). Those two reads are now kept per recipe while its inputs don't change (`derivedRecipeReads`, `services/matchableRecipes.ts`), and the picker only asks while open. The step mentions are kept per recipe and the audit per recipe and «proteína en cada plato» on/off, so households with and without it don't throw each other's out. Tests: `matchableRecipesMemo.test.ts`, `menuOptimistic.test.ts`, `menuChangeExpect.smoke.ts` (409 on a stale position, `PUT { note }`), `e2e/menu-dia-completo.spec.ts` (the change on screen while the request is held; a refused one goes back with the ones behind it; a hung one gives up; the focus doesn't paint the old week)

- Users can shape a single week without touching their preferences. **Quitar <comida>** removes the slot (`DELETE /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal`), and the day's **"Añadir comida"** opens a sheet («Añadir comida al jueves») listing the meals the day lacks; choosing one opens «Sin plato todavía» for it and **adds nothing until something is chosen there** (PRO-1177; before, the matcher put a recipe at once). Both apply to **this menu only**: the profile's `mealTemplate` is untouched, so next week follows the original preferences
- Users can override the diner count for one slot with **Comensales** −/+ in the meal sheet's «Más opciones» («Comensales: 4», "Solo hoy · Quitar"). It is persisted as the slot's `servings` (`PATCH /menu/:menuId/day/:day/meal/:meal` `{ servings: number | null }`; `null` clears). Changing the slot's dish keeps it: the web picker and «Que elija Mimoia» (`PUT …/meal/:meal`, dish routes) and Mimo's `swap_meal` (`slotWithDishes`, PRO-102: the Ruiz family's «Solo hoy: 4» went back to 5 after a swap with Mimo). `sumDinersByRecipe` in `shoppingList.ts` sums per-slot diners, so repeated recipes with different overrides scale independently. The caption's "N raciones", the dish's link to its recipe (`/recipes/:id?servings=N`; the detail opens at N) and the cook link use the **batch** (`batchServings` in `lib/menuDay.ts` → `batchServingsOf` in `@ona/shared`, PRO-75, PRO-298): the meal's own diners plus what each planned leftover of that meal adds, the same rule as the shopping list and Mimo's menu facts (see [Shopping](./shopping.md) → `withLeftoverBatches`). ## Menu Structure

A menu is stored per-user per-week:
- `weekStart` is the Monday of the week (`YYYY-MM-DD`)
- `days` is a 7-element array; each day is `{ breakfast?, lunch?, dinner?, snack? }`
- Each filled slot is `{ recipeId, recipeName, servings?, kind?, leftoverOf?, imageUrl? }` — `imageUrl` is **not** persisted in the JSONB; the API resolves it per request from the joined `recipes.image_url` so a regenerate-image takes effect on the very next response. `servings` is the optional per-slot diner override; `kind: 'leftover'` plus `leftoverOf: { day, meal }` marks a slot cloned from another via the leftover endpoint.
- `locked` is a nested object: `{ "<dayIndex>": { "<meal>": true } }`
- `bannedRecipeIds` is a string-array of recipe ids the user vetoed this week; the matcher excludes them across every regeneration call. Carries over when the user re-runs `POST /menu/generate` for the same week.
- `skippedDays` is an integer-array (0-6) of day indices the user marked "sin cocinar"; whole-week regenerate leaves these days empty (no slots get inserted).

## Menu Logs

Every generated menu also creates a `menu_logs` row with:
- Total calories for the week
- Aggregated nutrient profile (vitamins, minerals, etc.)
- Used to update `user_nutrient_balance` (running balance for the advisor)

## Access control (IDOR guards)

Every menu route is scoped to the caller — a logged-in user can only read or
mutate their own menus (or, when `SHARED_HOUSEHOLD_SCOPE` is on, those of a
fellow household member). This closes a former gap where any authenticated
user could read/modify any menu by id.

- `POST /menu/generate` **requires auth** and the body `userId` must equal the
  authenticated user (`403` otherwise). It used to be open — anyone could
  overwrite any user's week by passing their id; that is no longer possible.
- `GET /menu/:userId/:weekId` and `GET /menu/:userId/history` resolve the read
  scope from the **token**, never the path param. Requesting another user's id
  returns `403` unless they share your household.
- Every `/menu/:menuId/...` mutation (regenerate, add/delete/move slot, lock,
  ban, leftover, skip, servings) passes through a `:menuId` param guard:
  `400` if the id isn't a UUID, `404` if no such menu, `403` if it belongs to
  another user/household. Owner-or-same-household access mirrors the shopping
  list rule (`canAccessRow` in `scopeResolver.ts`).

- Catalogue times: on every boot (`db:migrate`) and at the end of `db:seed`, `catalogRecipeTimes.ts` fills **only empty** times of system recipes: Cocina's prep/cook/total for the 10 recipes that had none, `totalTime = prepTime + cookTime` when missing, and `activeTime` from the steps (sum of hands-on steps; simmering, oven, resting, marinating, soaking, cooling (enfriar), fridge (nevera, frigorífico), freezing, «templar», rising (levar), fermenting and boiling with minutes («Cuece a fuego medio 40 minutos») don't count — the last ones since PRO-187; a hands-on step without minutes → `prepTime`; never above the total). User recipes are never touched. Tests: `catalogRecipeTimes.test.ts`.
- **Realistic totals (PRO-284, 2026-10-21):** Miguel found the totals too long (cooking them took ~20 % less). Right after the fill, on every boot and at the end of `db:seed`, `recipeTimeTrim.ts` (`applyCatalogTimeTrim`) recomputes the times of system recipes: **total** = prep + cook − overlap, where the overlap is the prep done while a step goes by itself (the same passive steps as above; up to half the prep, never more than those steps' minutes; soaking the day before doesn't count) — never below `cookTime` nor the passive steps' minutes, never above the old total — except to count the waits (PRO-1078: resting, rising, the fridge…; see [Recipes](./recipes.md) → `totalTime`); **prep** × 0.8 once per recipe (internal tag `tiempos-pro284`, which a `PUT` keeps); **hands-on** drops by what the prep dropped, never above the total. `cookTime` is never changed. Idempotent: a second boot changes nothing, and a total an admin edit puts back to prep + cook is recomputed. Each recipe is logged `[catalog-time-trim] «…»: preparación A → B · total A → B · trabajo A → B min (cocción N, sin cambios; …)` for Cocina, plus a summary with **how many recipes now fit a 30-minute limit** by total and by hands-on time, and the mean total of the recipes of up to 45 min before → after. The time limits of the menu read these new values, so short days now take recipes that didn't fit before. A recipe saved by the batch import gets the total rule and the tag at once (its prep comes from the rewriter, told to estimate for someone who cooks with ease and to count prep done meanwhile only once). User recipes are never touched. Tests: `recipeTimeTrim.test.ts`, `recipeTimeTrim.smoke.ts`.

## Semana tipo (D-182)

- **What it is:** what the household usually eats each day, said once («los viernes por la noche, pizza»; «los jueves, una semana ceviche y otra carpaccio»), and every week's menu comes from it. **One per household** (`household_week_types`, `slots` = 7 days × meal → slot), whoever generates — a member, the Thursday autoplan or Mimo (before, each person's own plantilla). Design: [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-semana-tipo-design.md](../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-semana-tipo-design.md).
- **A slot** (`WeekTypeSlot`, `@ona/shared` `types/weekType.ts`): **libre** (no options: Mimo picks as before), **«no hay»** (`off`: that meal isn't planned that day) or **up to 4 options**: a **dish type** (`DISH_TYPES`, [Recipes](./recipes.md)), a **concrete recipe**, **something fixed with no recipe** (the comida fija, with what it carries per person, or just a name), **«Táper de la cena»** (breakfast, lunch or snack, Tuesday to Sunday, and only option) and **«Cualquier receta»**. **By turns** (default): one a week, in order, counting from the Monday it was saved (`since`), whether or not those weeks were cooked; «pizza, cada 2» is `[Pizza, Cualquier receta]`. **Together:** all of them in that meal, one dish each. One option in a meal of 2–3 dishes takes the first dish and the rest are drawn free. Plus the slot's diners and dishes.
- **API:** `GET /households/me/week-type` (the household's row: created with the household, `createSoloHouseholdForNewUser`; the households from before got theirs from the migration) and `PUT /households/me/week-type/:day/:meal` with the whole slot: only that slot is written (two people on the same slot: the last wins). Changing its options starts the turns again this week. Refused: a táper on a dinner, on Monday or with other options, an unknown type (400), a recipe the household can't see (404), a child of the household (403).
- **The generator** (`menuGenerator.ts` + `weekTypePlan.ts`), in this order — the first that applies wins: a «sin cocinar» day; a meal out this week («¿Alguna comida fuera?», even if the week type asks for something); a locked slot; «no hay» (unless «¿Viene alguien?» adds that meal this week); the option of the turn (or all together), with the school menu as a filter inside; a free slot as before. A **type** draws only recipes of that type, with every usual filter (allergies, diet, audit, vetoes, the day's time, holidays, equipment, school, the week's variety) and **before the rest of the week**, so a free slot never takes the only ceviche; when none fits, a **dish** type (Pizza, Ceviche…) is written by its name (`NoteDish.fixedDish`, nothing on the list) — unless the name itself breaks an allergy or diet («Pasta» in a celiac household), then the slot is drawn free — and a **broad** one (Carne, Indio…) takes any recipe; both leave `dish_type_missing_<type>_<meal>_d<day>` and a catalogue wish for Cocina (`recordCatalogWish`, source `mimo`). **The day card says so** (PRO-1186): the dish carries `fromWeekType: { type, missing: true }` and shows a warning chip — «Ninguna receta de «Pizza» encaja: lo dejo escrito» (the note) or «Ninguna receta de «Carne» encaja: he puesto otra» (the free draw). A dish drawn for a type carries `fromWeekType: { type }` and the chip **«<tipo> · semana tipo»** («Legumbres · semana tipo», with its icon); a concrete recipe or a dish written there (`fixedDish`), **«Semana tipo»**; a «Fijo: …» none (its text says it), nor a leftover copied from it (`weekTypeChipOf`, `@ona/shared`). Desktop «La semana» cells show the short line too. Any change by hand drops it. A **concrete recipe** goes in with only the allergy and diet check (`fixedDish: true`; not again elsewhere that week); if it breaks someone's allergy or diet the slot is drawn free and says «En lugar de «…», que no encaja con vuestras alergias o dieta» (PRO-345 chip, `week_type_recipe_restricted_<meal>_d<day>`). **Something fixed** is the whole meal: «Fijo: <name>» with its things (see Comidas fijas), or just its name when it carries nothing — that name goes through the allergy and diet check as an ingredient (as PRO-1141: «Jamón con melón» isn't written in a vegetarian household; the slot is drawn free and says «En lugar de «Jamón con melón»…»). The **táper** follows PRO-223 (applied after the draw).
- **Normas por día, nunca rotas (PRO-1296; examen del histórico de Mimo, 23-nov: 9 turnos con un plato fijo de un día, «solo cenas» o «el fin de semana, nada» rotos, y Mimo enumerándolos y pidiendo permiso).** What the week type says for a day — its concrete recipe or type, a «no hay» meal, a day with nothing — is kept by every path that fills the menu by itself: the generator (`POST /menu/generate`, «Rehacer…», Mimo's `generate_weekly_menu` and the Thursday autoplan, all through `generateMenu`), «Cambiar» / «Que elija Mimoia» (`PUT …/meal/:meal`: a meal the day doesn't have is 404, never created), regenerating a dish and Mimo's `swap_meal` without a dish, which **never creates a meal the week type says «no hay» to** (before, «cámbiame la comida del martes» in a «solo cenas» household drew a recipe and created that lunch): it answers «No he cambiado nada: la comida del martes no está en el menú (en su semana tipo ese día no hay comida)» and points Mimo to another meal, `add_dish` (this once) or `set_week_type_slot` (always) (`weekTypeHasMeal`, `services/weekType.ts`). What places dishes on its own never takes the place of what the week type asks for there — its recipe (`fixedDish`) or a recipe drawn for its type (`fromWeekType`, not the «none fits» stand-in): the fridge dish (`ensureFridgeRecipe`) and the sign-up's dishes (`placeSignupDishes`) skip it (`asksWeekType`, `weekTypePlan.ts`; before, a fridge dish could replace Thursday's legumes). **What couldn't be kept is said, not asked:** Mimo's `generate_weekly_menu` and `complete_onboarding` add to their answer each rule the week couldn't keep, by day and meal (`unmetRulesLine`: a type with no recipe that fits — written by its name or another recipe —, the week type's recipe that breaks an allergy or diet, no light dish for a light meal, a meal no recipe fits), with the instruction to say it in one line and propose a concrete way out (another recipe or type that day, their own recipe, changing the week type), never to ask whether to fix it; the rest of the week already keeps everything. Clicking «Cambiar» on the week type's own dish (Tuesday's fixed recipe) is an explicit request and changes it, as before. ## Comidas fijas (PRO-218)

- A meal (desayuno, comida, merienda or cena) of some days can be **fija**: «todos los días desayuno yogur con cereales y dos galletas» is said once. - **The shopping list** adds the week's amounts (see [Shopping → Comidas fijas](./shopping.md)).
- Limits: it applies when a week is generated or regenerated (a week already made doesn't change); editing the note's text by hand turns it into an ordinary note (no longer on the list).

## Menú del comedor escolar (PRO-224)

- **What it does:** the household passes the school's monthly menu and the menu's **dinner of a school day doesn't repeat the group** of the kids' lunch: lentejas at school → that dinner is not a pulse. Groups: legumbre, pescado, carne, huevo, pasta o arroz, verdura. Days without a school menu (holidays, vacations, weekends) have no rule. Wording is «para no repetir», never «para equilibrar» (constitution §6).
- **Where:** discreet on purpose (D-069). In the app only when the household has a child — a member with the «Niñ@» role, a member who counted children 2–10 in the web sign-up, a person without an account in «Tu ficha» under 18, or children 2–10 in the household's ficha (the WhatsApp sign-up and Mimo write it there; the last two since 2026-10-22, when Miguel and a beta tester with children only reached it by its URL) (`householdShowsSchoolMenu`): `/profile/casa` shows a «Menú del cole» row → `/profile/casa/cole`: «Subir foto o PDF» (JPG, PNG, WEBP or PDF, ≤ 15 MB) → «Lo que he leído · N días» with each day's dish and group editable (or quitar) → «Guardar»; below, «Guardado» with a «Quitar» per day. - **The reader** (`services/schoolMenu.ts`): one **Claude Opus 5.5** call (D-070, `SCHOOL_MENU_MODEL`, feature `school_menu_read` billed to the user, behind the monthly spend cap) with the image or the PDF and today's date (for the year). A photo is first made upright and at most 1568 px on its long side as JPEG (`fitImageForClaude`, `services/visionImage.ts`; one already small, or that can't be decoded, goes as it is): 2026-10-23, a camera photo uploaded from the web failed with «Internal server error» — over Claude's 5 MB per image — while the same menu through WhatsApp, already compressed, was read.

Only a dish per date: no child data, no school name. `GET /school-menu?from&to` (days + `visible`), `POST /school-menu/read` (multipart `file` up to 25 MB, not saved; 422 when it isn't a school menu, 413 «El archivo es demasiado grande…», 415 `HEIC` for an iPhone HEIC photo (by type or `.heic` name; sharp can't decode it) saying to upload a screenshot or send it by WhatsApp, 413 `TOO_BIG` for a PDF over 20 MB or 100 pages (checked before calling the model) or when the model answers «too large», saying to upload only the month's page (PRO-355; WhatsApp says the same in its words: send it as a photo, not a document / only the month's page), 503 «No he podido leer el menú ahora. Prueba otra vez en un momento o mándamelo por WhatsApp con «menú del cole».» when the reading fails — the web shows that, never a bare «Internal server error»), `PUT /school-menu` `{ days, clear? }` (replace those dates, remove `clear`).

## Festivos (PRO-226)

- The household tells its **comunidad autónoma** — memory key `region` (ISO 3166-2:ES code: `MD`, `CT`…), chosen in `/profile/memoria` → Hogar → «Comunidad autónoma» or told to Mimo (`update_memory`, web and WhatsApp); it is copied to the ficha as a household fact. Not asked in the sign-up yet.

Adding a year = copying its annex when the BOE publishes it (usually October).
- **Menu rule** (`services/menuHolidays.ts`, `holidayTimeBudgets`): a Monday–Friday holiday of the household's comunidad is cooked like a weekend day: its time limit becomes **Saturday's** (none unless the household set one for Saturdays). So with «Rápido» (≤ 30 min Mon–Fri) a holiday Thursday gets any recipe, while in another comunidad that Thursday keeps the 30 minutes. The weekly generator and every single-slot path (`slotTimeBudget`: Cambiar al azar, add a meal, regenerate a dish) apply it. The region read is the generating user's memory. `weekends_only` recipes are still kept off weekday holidays.
- **Saying it**: a generated week with a holiday among the days the WhatsApp message shows carries one line under the opener (digest and picture caption): «El jueves es festivo: te he puesto algo con más calma.» when it lifted a limit, «El jueves es festivo.» otherwise, «El lunes y el martes son festivos…» for two (`holidayLine` in `menuText.ts`). Not when the user only asks to see the week. Mimo's `generate_weekly_menu` result lists the week's holidays so it says it in the web chat too (`weekHolidaySummary`).
- Tests: `menuHolidays.test.ts` (both years complete per comunidad, BOE spot checks, a holiday Thursday without the 30-min limit in Aragón and with it in Madrid — rule and generator —, single-slot paths, the message line); e2e `profile-region.spec.ts`.

## Menú del mes (PRO-378)

A week of the house's dishes that Cocina prepares each month (idea of FuturApp, 2026-10-24/25), to copy into your own week in one tap.
- **Where**: a card «Menú de <mes>» («De la cocina de Mimoia», up to three photos, «N días pensados para ti») on `/recipes` (signed in, nothing filtered) and inside the blank week's card on `/menu` (current or future week, `MonthMenuCard`). No menu this month → no card. It opens `/menu/mes?week=<lunes>` (the week the household was looking at; without `?week`, this week): the 7 days, Monday first, each meal with its dishes (link to the recipe, minutes).
- **«Copiar a mi semana»** (`POST /month-menus/:month/copy` `{ weekStart, force? }`, `copyMonthMenuToWeek`): writes a new `menus` row for that week (the previous stays as history, as with «Generar»). Only the meals of the household's **plantilla** go in (a household without breakfasts gets lunches and dinners; a meal the plantilla has and the month lacks stays empty, «Añadir plato»); «sin cocinar» days stay without slots; **locked slots keep what they had**; vetoes and skipped days carry over. A dish that breaks the allergies or diet of anyone in the household (owner's profile + Mimo's memory + every member) is **swapped for a similar one** (`similarRecipePicker`: `findForCourse` with the same course and meal, the household's rules and the week's vetoes, not repeating the week; first among the recipes with the same main protein, then any); with nothing that fits the slot stays empty. The notice says it: «Hemos copiado 13 platos a tu semana. Uno no encajaba con vuestras alergias o dieta y lo hemos cambiado por otro parecido.» (+ «N huecos se han quedado sin plato…»), with «Ver mi semana». A week that already has dishes answers 409 `MENU_NOT_EMPTY` and the page asks first: «Se cambiarán los platos de esta semana» → «Copiar igualmente» (`force`). Prep alerts are enqueued; the list follows by itself.
- **The data**: `catalog_collections` (PRO-374's table; migration 0099 creates it if it isn't there and only adds columns) with `kind = 'menu_mes'`, `month` (YYYY-MM, one per month: a new import replaces it) and `week` (7 days, per meal the recipe ids). `GET /month-menus/current` gives this month's (Madrid), only the published recipes. How Cocina imports it and the rules it must pass: [Catalogue import](./catalog-import.md) → «Menú del mes».
- Tests: `monthMenus.test.ts` (rules on import, a household without breakfasts gets only lunches and dinners, a household allergic to shellfish gets another dish instead of the rice with prawns, the notice, the routes), `e2e/month-menu.spec.ts` (card on /recipes → copy → the dishes on /menu; again over a week with dishes asks first).

## Acierto: cuánto se parece a lo que os gusta (PRO-155)

- Right after a week is generated (web `POST /menu/generate`, Mimo's `generate_weekly_menu` / `complete_onboarding`), one small **Claude Opus 5.5** call at effort low (`services/menuFit.ts`, `CLAUDE_MODEL`, 12 s limit, feature `menu_fit` billed to the household) compares the week's recipes with what we know the household likes: the favourite dishes it told us (`users.favorite_dishes`) and its «Me encanta» (`recipe_verdicts`). For each dish the model also sees the catalogue recipes sharing a word with it (published catalogue + own recipes).
- Result (`MenuFit`, `@ona/shared`, stored in `menus.fit`, migration 0065): `score` 0–100 and level (**alto** ≥ 70, **medio** ≥ 40, **bajo**), what's met (with the recipe) and what's missing and why: **no está en el catálogo** or **no cabía esta semana**. Hand check: a dish that a catalogue recipe covers word for word (`catalogCoversDish`) or a «Me encanta» is never «no está en el catálogo». Also `unusedAddMethods`: photo, link or written recipe the household hasn't used yet (`usage_events` `recipe_imported` / `recipe_created`).
- Missing favourites («no está en el catálogo», only dishes they told us) become **deseos del catálogo** (`recordCatalogWish`, source `evaluador`; [Catalog import](./catalog-import.md)).
- `/menu` shows **one line under the week** (`MenuFitLine`, `data-testid="menu-fit"`, current and future weeks), **without the percentage** (PRO-1051: «lo de 12 % no lo entiendo»): alto «Este menú se acerca mucho a lo que os gusta: hay …»; medio «Se acerca bastante… No he podido meter …; puedes ponerlo en lugar de otro plato»; bajo «Con las recetas que tengo no me he acercado mucho a lo que os gusta: aún no tengo …. Si añades tus recetas, las tendré en cuenta.» + «Añadir una receta» → `/recipes/new`; «aún no tengo» lists only `no_catalogo` (nothing listed when every miss is `no_cabia`). Mimo tells it in its own words ([Advisor](./advisor.md)).
- **The verdict follows the week (PRO-1051).** It is written once, when the week is made, and the week changes afterwards; the model also misses dishes that are right there. So `reconcileFit` (`menuFit.ts`) checks it by look-alike name (`recipeCoveringDish`: every significant stemmed word of the dish, or of one of its alternatives, in the recipe name; «merluza» → «Merluza rebozada con ensalada», «pescado a la plancha» ≠ «Merluza a la plancha»): a missed dish a recipe of the week covers is met; a met dish whose recipe left the week is missed again; a missed dish the catalogue (+ own recipes) covers is `no_cabia`, never `no_catalogo`. When something moved, the score becomes the share met. It runs on the model's answer (`normalizeFit`) and on **every menu response** (`hydrateMenuImages` → `currentMenuFit`, the stored `fit` is not rewritten). Tests: `menuFit.test.ts`.
- **Best effort**: no API key, error, > 8 s or an unusable answer → `fit: null`, the menu goes out the same and no line is shown. Without favourites or «Me encanta» there is nothing to compare: no call. «Vaciar semana» / empty weeks don't run it.
- Limits: the verdict is about the week **as generated**; later swaps don't update it (the next generation does). Only the generating user's favourites and «Me encanta» count (not the rest of the household, not the ficha yet). The menu matcher still weighs favourites by shared words (`recipesMatchingDishes`): the smart menu is PRO-139.
- Tests: `menuFit.test.ts` (the real case of 2026-10-16, reasons, wishes, cost, fallbacks), `assistant-skills.test.ts` (reaches Mimo), `e2e/menu-fit.spec.ts` (the line, and none without a verdict).

## Cuando cambia una receta (PRO-345)

A saved menu holds recipe ids; the recipe behind one can change or be deleted later (2026-10-22: «Fajitas de pollo» got «Tortillas de trigo» and stayed in a gluten-free Thursday; a deleted «Crema de calabaza» showed «Esta receta ya no está» and its ingredients left the list). `services/menuRecipeRecheck.ts` re-checks the saved menus:
- **What**: from today on (Madrid) and only meals not eaten, a dish whose recipe was **deleted**, or whose recipe **changed after it entered the slot** (`recipes.updated_at` later than the menu's creation and the last hand edit in `menu_edits` that put it there) and now breaks the allergies or diet of **anyone in the household** (owner's profile + Mimo's memory + every member, as in generation). The recipe is read as the matcher reads it: allergens, ingredients and, since PRO-1067, what the steps name and the ingredients don't (`stepMentions`, PRO-346; `toRecheckRecipe`). A recipe the household picked by hand knowing it breaks a rule stays: only a later change makes it stale, and picking it again after the swap writes a newer edit. Locked slots are changed too (safety first). A retired recipe (PRO-140) still exists: only the diet check applies.
- **Replacement**: the matcher picks one of the same course for that day and meal, with the household's restrictions and the week's vetoes, not repeating the week. The dish keeps `replaced: { recipeName, reason: 'diet' | 'removed' }` and the day card shows «En lugar de «Fajitas de pollo», que ha cambiado y ya no encaja con vuestra dieta» / «…, que ya no está en el catálogo» (`replacedDishLine`, `data-testid="dish-replaced"`) until the dish is changed by hand (or by the review of [«Cuando cambian las alergias o la dieta»](#cuando-cambian-las-alergias-o-la-dieta-pro-364), which handles the other case: the recipe stays and the household's restrictions change). A leftover of a replaced dish follows it. Nothing fits → a note ««X» ya no encaja con vuestra dieta: elegid otro plato» (or «ya no está en el catálogo»). A táper or leftover already cooked stays on a diet change and becomes the note «Táper de «X»» when its recipe is deleted.
- **The list** is rebuilt from the menus on every read, so it follows by itself; prep alerts are re-enqueued. The swap is not a `menu_edits` row (nobody in the household made it); the server log says `[menu-recheck]`.
- **When**: right after `PUT /recipes/:id` and `DELETE /recipes/:id` (that recipe only, without making the response wait), and a full pass a minute after boot and every hour (`startMenuRecipeRecheck`), which catches the catalogue fixes of a deploy (`catalogRecipeFixes.ts`) and scripts. A menu changed meanwhile is not overwritten (the next pass retries).
- Tests: `menuRecipeRecheck.test.ts` (fajitas edited to carry gluten in a gluten-free household, the deleted crema, hand picks, tápers, notes, lentils whose step adds chorizo in a vegetarian household), `menuRecipeRecheck.smoke.ts` (real Postgres), `dietCatalog.test.ts` (the fajitas of 2026-10-22).

## Piloto automático: cada jueves, la semana siguiente hecha (PRO-381)

## Constraints

- All menu routes require auth (including `POST /menu/generate`, which also
  enforces that the body `userId` matches the token)
- A household without a semana tipo yet (and without an old plantilla) plans lunch + dinner every day (no breakfast, no snack)
- The meals of each day, their diners and their dishes are the household's **semana tipo** (`household_week_types`, below). The old per-user `userSettings.template` (`mealTemplate` day → meal → diners, `mealDishCounts`, `fixedMeals`, `taperDays`; parsed by `legacyPlantilla.ts`) only feeds its one-off migration and is never written again: `PUT /user/:id/settings` keeps those keys as they were stored, whatever the profile sends. A slot's diners seed `servings` of the slot generated there; per-slot overrides on the menu card still win
- If no menu exists for the requested week, `GET /menu/:userId/:weekId` returns 404
- **Mimo makes one menu per request** (PRO-1298, `services/menuRequestGuard.ts`): while a week is being made for a person (`generate_weekly_menu`, or a week of the WhatsApp sign-up's first menu), another request for that same week starts no second generation («No he lanzado otro: … ya se esta preparando»); a WhatsApp message sent before the week's menu was made gets no second one either, and a tapped button only makes a whole week when it or the question it answered asks for one (see [WhatsApp](./whatsapp.md)). The in-flight check lives in the API process (Railway runs one). `POST /menu/generate` on the web is unchanged. Tests: `menuOneRequest.test.ts`, `menuOneRequest.smoke.ts` (two at once → one row in `menus`)
- The shopping page redirects users to `/menu` if no menu exists for the current week
- **Household scope (PR 1B):** menus carry both `user_id` and `household_id`. Inserts always populate both; reads filter by `household_id` when the env flag `SHARED_HOUSEHOLD_SCOPE=true` (default ON in dev/test, OFF in prod). When the flag is on, every member of a shared household reads the same menus. See [Household](./household.md)
