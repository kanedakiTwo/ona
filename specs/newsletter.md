# Newsletter «El menú de los viernes»

The weekly email for people who ticked «Quiero recibir cada viernes por email una receta de la semana y novedades de Mimoia» (until 2026-10-17: «Quiero recibir cada viernes el menú de la semana por email») on the [waitlist](./waitlist.md) (PRO-94), at `/register` or in the profile (PRO-95). Very visual: the star recipe's photo opens it. Since D-176 (PRO-1123) Miguel writes it: Marketing recommends, he corrects and saves the template in Admin, each person gets their own letter, and nothing goes out without his proof. Sent through Resend (see [Email](./email.md)).

## User Capabilities

- People who ticked the box get, each Friday, one email from **Mimoia <hola@mimoia.com>**, in the brand (cream, ink, one touch of terracotta, a forest button; Fraunces, Inter and a Cormorant italic accent), in this order:
  - masthead: the Mimoia logo (links to mimoia.com), «El menú de los viernes» and the issue's edition line (e.g. «N.º 1 · 16 de octubre de 2026»);
  - an optional short **greeting** above the headline (`preface`, e.g. «Hola a todos…»), an optional **headline** (a `*word*` goes in the terracotta italic) and an opening **letter** — since issue 1 written and signed by Miguel, in a personal, family tone;
  - **the star recipe**: its photo full width (links to the recipe), «La receta de la semana», title, an optional one-line meta, two short paragraphs and the issue's single call to action (a forest pill button);
  - **accounts only**, when their household did something with Mimoia in the week (or the month): **«Tu semana con Mimoia»** (PRO-1199, below);
  - up to four free **sections** (e.g. «Por qué existe Mimoia» on a cream-deep panel with a big italic quote, «Qué es esta carta» after a thin rule);
  - optionally a **«Novedad en Mimoia»** card and an **«Un truco con Mimo»** card, each with an image when there is one and «Ver más»;
  - **«Corre la voz»** (PRO-1019, D-122 point 4; from issue 3, 30-oct), in every email: a paper card with the recipient's **own** invite link and two pills, «Compartir por WhatsApp» (ink) and «Copiar el enlace» (outlined), as in the app's «Invitar a otro hogar»:
    - **accounts**: «¿Conoces otra casa a la que le vendría bien?» · «Mándale tu enlace para que pruebe Mimoia: mimoia.com/i/<their code>» (the household's link, D-132; the code of `GET /referrals/me`, PRO-162, assigned at send if they had none); WhatsApp opens with `inviteHouseholdShareText`;
    - **waitlist**: «Invita a tu hogar y entráis antes» until 15-nov, «Invita a tu hogar a Mimoia» from 16-nov (Madrid, PRO-311) · «Este es tu enlace: mimoia.com/?invita=<their code>» (PRO-130, the same code as on the [Waitlist](./waitlist.md)); WhatsApp opens with `waitlistShareText`;
    - an **admin proof** carries the admin's own household link (never a recipient's);
    - «Copiar el enlace» opens `/newsletter/comparte?c=<code>&k=hogar|lista` (an email can't copy): «Este es tu enlace», the link, «Copiar el enlace» (→ «Copiado») and «Compartir por WhatsApp»; a malformed code → «Este enlace no es válido.». Nothing is counted or stored there;
    - in the text version too («CORRE LA VOZ: …», the link, both buttons' URLs);
  - the signoff and, when a person signs, their name in the italic accent with their role underneath;
  - a cream-deep footer (the invite line that was here until PRO-1019 now lives in «Corre la voz»): Mimo's round symbol, small print (e.g. how the photos are made), «Te llega porque pediste el menú de los viernes de Mimoia.», «No quiero recibir más el menú de los viernes» · «Salir de la lista de espera», and «Mimoia · mimoia.com».
- **Newsletter-only opt-out** `/newsletter/baja?t=<token>`: «¿Dejamos de mandarte el menú de los viernes?», explains the place on the list stays, one button «No quiero recibirlo más». Works on click, not on load (mail scanners). Then «Hecho. Ya no te mandaremos el menú de los viernes.»; a second time «Ya no recibías el menú de los viernes.»; a bad link «Este enlace de baja no es válido.». Inboxes that support it (Gmail, Apple Mail) show their own «Cancelar suscripción» (RFC 8058 one-click).
- The waitlist box carries the hint «Te lo recomendamos: te va a sorprender. Es opcional y aparte de la lista; te das de baja con un clic.»
- **Beta households (PRO-95)**: the same box, unticked, at `/register` (see [Auth](./auth.md)), and a switch in **Perfil → Recordatorios** («Quiero recibir cada viernes por email una receta de la semana y novedades de Mimoia», `NEWSLETTER_OPT_IN_LABEL`, the same words in the three places; off: «Te lo recomendamos: te va a sorprender.», on: «Activado · te llega cada viernes»). The switch saves on tap (`GET`/`PUT /newsletter/me { optIn }`, JWT); turning it on records the date and `WAITLIST_NEWSLETTER_CONSENT_VERSION`.
- **One person, one switch**: turning it off (profile, `/newsletter/baja` or the inbox) turns it off for every row with that email — the account and the waitlist entry.

## Sending (admins)

`POST /admin/newsletter/send` (admin JWT) with `{ issueId, subject, preheader?, content, testTo?, dryRun? }`:
- `issueId`: lowercase slug (`2026-10-16`), the idempotency key.
- `content`: `{ edition?, preface?, headline?, intro?, star { title, body, imageUrl, imageAlt, url, meta? }, sections?, news?, tip?, cta { label, url }, note?, signoff?, from? }`; `news`/`tip` = `{ title, body, imageUrl?, imageAlt?, url? }`; `sections` = up to 4 × `{ label?, title?, quote?, body, tone?: 'band' | 'plain' }`; `from` = `{ name, role? }`. Image and link URLs must be absolute (`http(s)://`), the star needs `alt`. The template lays it out; nobody sends raw HTML.
- `dryRun: true` → `{ mode: 'dry_run', recipients, alreadySent, bySource { waitlist, user }, includes? }`, sends nothing; `includes: <email>` says whether that address would get it.
- `testTo` → one proof, subject prefixed «[Prueba] », only to the email of an **admin** account (else 400 `NEWSLETTER_TEST_NOT_ADMIN`); its opt-out links are dummies; nothing recorded but the issue's links (its tracked links carry `?prueba=1` and never count).
- Otherwise → `{ mode: 'send', sent, failed, skipped, alreadySent }`, through Resend's batch API (≤ 100 per call), audited as `newsletter.send` (counts only). **Since PRO-1123 a real send needs the issue's template saved in the Admin editor in the last 7 days, its proof sent after that save, and the body equal to that template** (else 409 `NEWSLETTER_NOT_READY` with `reason`: `sin_plantilla`, `plantilla_antigua`, `sin_prueba`, `otro_texto` or `ya_enviada`); it claims the issue once (`newsletter_drafts.sent_at`; a crash releases it). `dryRun` and `testTo` stay free.

Recommended order (D-176): the Admin «Newsletter» editor — save → preview → «Enviarme la prueba» → «Programar» (below).

## Carta personal (PRO-1123, D-176; from issue 4)

The letter goes from Miguel to each person and isn't the same for everyone. Miguel's template takes three placeholders (and a fourth, `{numeros}`, PRO-1199, below), filled in for each recipient when the email is composed (`withPersonalLetter` in `newsletter.ts`, the pure rules in `services/newsletterLetter.ts`), in the subject, the preheader, the greeting (`preface`), the headline, the letter (`intro`), the signoff, the small print, the star's meta and text and the sections:
- **`{nombre}`**: the first name they gave on the waitlist (accounts: the waitlist entry with their email), else the account's username only when it looks like a name (letters only, 2–20: «miguel» → «Miguel»; «mmartin84» or «ana_g» → none). Names are cleaned (letters, spaces, hyphen, apostrophe, ≤ 30). Without a name the placeholder goes with its comma: «Hola, {nombre}:» → «Hola:», «{nombre}, esta semana…» → «Esta semana…»; never «estimado usuario».
- **`{receta}`**: the title of the recipe they get (their own, PRO-1020, or the star).
- **`{porque}`**: one sentence, by rules (no API call, no cost), from what the product already knows of the household: how many eat at home (`users.adults` + `kids_2_to_10`: «para ti», «para los dos», «para los cuatro, peques incluidos», «para toda la casa»), the recipe's season when it is the current one («es muy de otoño»), that it has nothing they said they don't like (never which: «no lleva nada de lo que me dijisteis que no os gusta»), and the most recent dish the household cooked in the last 7 days (`cook_logs`: «cambia de registro después de «Pollo al curry» la semana pasada»). E.g. «La he elegido para los cuatro, peques incluidos: es muy de otoño y no lleva nada de lo que me dijisteis que no os gusta.» Nothing known → «La he elegido pensando en tu casa.»
- **Never their health** (D-064, D-110): allergies, diets and restrictions are never read for the sentence, and a cooked dish whose name gives one away («sin gluten», «vegana», «para diabéticos», «keto», «light»… `safeDishName`) is never named.
- **Waitlist**: what the entry told us — the name, the household size («1», «2»; «3-4» and «5+» → «para toda la casa») and «improviso» → «para que esta semana no toque improvisar la cena».
- The **proof** carries the admin's own letter (their name, their household). A failure reading the households only warns: name-less letters with the generic reason. A template without placeholders comes out as written (the editor warns).

## Tus números con Mimoia (PRO-1199)

Each **account**'s letter carries a short block of what its household already solved with Mimoia, so the letter is of that house. By rules, no API (D-156), from what the product already keeps (`services/newsletterNumbers.ts`):
- **The period**: the last 7 days (Madrid dates, the send day included). If the week comes out empty, the last 30 days. Both empty → **no block** (never «0 recetas»).
- **The lines**, only the figures that aren't zero, in this order:
  - the menu: «El menú de la semana hecho (14 platos)» (the month: «3 semanas con el menú hecho (42 platos)») — weeks (`menus.week_start`) with at least one dish on a day of the period, and those dishes; leftovers, tápers (`variant` not `planned`, `taper` slots) and days «sin cocinar» don't count; when two people of the house have a menu for the same week, the latest;
  - «2 recetas cocinadas» — `cook_logs` of the household in the period, the same recipe on the same day once;
  - «1 receta nueva probada» — of those, the recipes the household had never cooked before the period;
  - «La lista de la compra mandada» — «Mandar la lista» (`shopping_list_shared`, PRO-380) or an order sent to a shop or the súper (`shop_orders.sent_at`, not cancelled).
- **Where**: a paper card «Tu semana con Mimoia» («Tu mes con Mimoia»), one line each, after the star recipe's button and before the sections; in the text version too. **`{numeros}`**, a fourth placeholder for Miguel's letter, says it in one sentence («Tu semana con Mimoia: el menú de la semana hecho (14 platos) y 2 recetas cocinadas.»); without figures it goes away (a paragraph that was only `{numeros}` leaves no blank). When the template carries `{numeros}` anywhere, the block doesn't go: the letter already says it.
- **Who**: accounts only (`letters.numbers`, read with the letter's facts); the **waitlist never** (no household). The **proof** carries the admin's own household's figures; the **preview** each of the 3 people's.
- **Never** anything of health, diet or allergies (D-064, D-110): only counts, no dish is named; never a comparison with other households.
- A failure reading the figures only leaves the block out (a warning); the email goes.

## Editor en Admin (PRO-1123, D-176)

`/admin/newsletter` («Newsletter» in the admin menu; `sections/NewsletterSection.tsx`), one issue at a time (its id is the Friday's date, `2026-11-06`, prefilled with the next Friday; the last 12 issues as chips):
- **La recomendación de Marketing**: its subject and letter, and «Empezar desde la recomendación», which copies it into the form. Marketing leaves it with `PUT /admin/newsletter/drafts/:issueId/recommendation` (admin JWT, the same `{ subject, preheader?, content }` as a send); without it, «Marketing aún no ha dejado su recomendación para este número.» (Proposal, PRO-1123: Marketing's weekly run, MKT-504, calls this API on Tuesdays; a file in the repo would need a deploy per issue.)
- **Tu carta**: Asunto, Preheader, Saludo, Titular, Carta, Despedida, and the rest of the issue as JSON (star, `recipes`, sections, news, tip, button, note, signer). The placeholders are explained above the fields; without any, «La carta no lleva ningún hueco: saldría igual para todos.» «Guardar plantilla» (`PUT …/template`, audited `newsletter.template_save`) **clears the proof and the schedule**: what is proved is what goes out.
- **Ver vista previa** (`POST …/preview`, audited `newsletter.preview` with the count): the saved template for 3 real recipients (2 accounts and 1 waitlist person when there is one), each with their own recipe, tip and letter, the address masked, in a sandboxed frame. Reads only: nothing claimed, no code assigned, no tracked links, dummy opt-out links. Admins only.
- **Enviarme la prueba** (`POST …/proof`, audited `newsletter.proof`): the saved template, as a proof («[Prueba] …»), to **the signed-in admin's own account email** with their own case; it only counts as seen when it went out (`sent`; without Resend it says so and doesn't count).
- **Programar para el viernes a las 9:00** (`POST …/schedule`, audited `newsletter.schedule`): the next Friday 9:00 Madrid (today if it is Friday before 9). Only with the template saved in the 7 days before that time and its proof; else 409 with the reason, and the button stays off with the line of what's missing. «Desprogramar» (`DELETE …/schedule`).
- **The scheduler** (`runScheduledNewsletterIfDue`, every tick of `notificationScheduler`, under its lock) sends each due issue once (claimed in `sent_at`, audited `newsletter.send` as the admin who scheduled it, `scheduled: true`); one no longer ready (re-saved without a proof, or too old) doesn't go and is unscheduled. A failing send is released for the next tick (`newsletter_sends` keeps anyone from getting it twice).
- `GET /admin/newsletter/drafts` → `{ drafts: [{ issueId, recommendedAt, savedAt, proofSentAt, scheduledFor, sentAt, sendResult, ready, notReady }], nextFriday }`; `GET /admin/newsletter/drafts/:issueId` → `{ draft (+ recommendation, template) | null, nextFriday }`.
- Table `newsletter_drafts` (one row per issue: `recommendation`, `template`, `saved_by`/`saved_at`, `proof_sent_at`, `scheduled_for`/`scheduled_by`, `api_url` for the one-click opt-out, `sent_at`, `send_result` counts); migration `0106_newsletter_drafts` (adds the table). Who saved or scheduled goes to null with their account.
- What doesn't change: tracked links, «Corre la voz», the opt-outs and the LSSI footer, idempotency per recipient.

## Clicks and opt-outs per issue (PRO-191, 2026-10-19)

Resend's open and click tracking stay **off** on mimoia.com (the open pixel needs consent; Resend's click tracking keeps who clicked). Instead, our own aggregated counter:
- At send, the content's links (star recipe, call to action, news, tip; the same URL twice is one link, numbered from 1: `contentLinks`) are saved in `newsletter_clicks (issue_id, link_index, destination, clicks)` and every email carries `https://mimoia.com/n/<issue>/<n>` instead (`withTrackedLinks`). The footer's personal links (opt-out, invite) are never rewritten.
- `mimoia.com/n/<issue>/<n>` (web route `app/n/[issue]/[n]/route.ts`) asks `GET /newsletter/click/:issueId/:n` (public), which adds 1 and answers `{ url }` with the destination **saved at send**; the web redirects there with a 302. Never to a URL that comes in the request (no open redirect). An unknown issue or link → 404, no redirect.
- **Slots (PRO-1019):** places whose destination is different for each recipient. Today one, **`corre_la_voz`**: index 41 = «Compartir por WhatsApp», 42 = «Copiar el enlace» (`NEWSLETTER_SLOT_LINKS`; content links are at most 4). Saved at send with the content links (destination `hueco:corre_la_voz:<button>`, a descriptor). Each email's buttons are `mimoia.com/n/<issue>/41|42?c=<their code>&k=hogar|lista`; the web passes `c` and `k` on, the API checks the code's format (`REFERRAL_CODE_RE`) and the kind and **builds** the destination (`slotDestination`, `newsletterShare.ts`): `wa.me/?text=<the share text with their link>` or `<web>/newsletter/comparte?c=…&k=…`, never a URL from the request. No code, a malformed code or kind → 404, nothing counted. The code is not stored: the counter only knows the slot and the button. Without a stats repo the buttons go straight to wa.me and the copy page.
- Not clicks: `HEAD` requests, proofs (`?prueba=1`) and known mail scanners or link previewers by user-agent (`countsAsClick`). The metric is clicks, not people. No IP, browser or recipient is stored.
- Opt-outs: the opt-out page and the one-click URL carry `i=<issue>`; a successful opt-out adds a row to `newsletter_unsubscribes (issue_id, source user|waitlist, created_at)` — counts only. Earlier emails have no issue and count under `unsubscribesWithoutIssue`.
- The `i` comes in the request, so it is only trusted when that issue was **sent to that very address**: `repo.unsubscribe` returns the address's `recipientHash` and `wasSentTo(issueId, hash)` looks for its `newsletter_sends` row with `status = 'sent'` (unique index `uq_newsletter_sends_issue_recipient`). Otherwise — an invented issue, a real issue that person never got (e.g. a waitlist signup made after it went out), or the check failing — the opt-out still happens and its row is saved with `issue_id` null. Without this, anyone with an opt-out token (a public waitlist signup gives one) could add made-up issues, with any text, to `/admin/newsletter/stats`, or inflate a real issue's opt-outs.
- The stats only show **known** issues: those with sends or saved links (`newsletter_clicks`). Only an admin's send or proof creates them, and links are never deleted, so forgetting an address's sends (PRO-132) doesn't move earlier opt-outs. An opt-out row naming any other issue (rows saved before this check) counts under `unsubscribesWithoutIssue`.
- `GET /admin/newsletter/stats` (admin JWT or the read-only metrics token `x-metrics-token` used by internal reporting) → `{ issues: [{ issueId, firstSentAt, sent { total, user, waitlist }, clicks { total, links [{ index, destination, clicks }], slots { corre_la_voz: { total, buttons { whatsapp, copiar } } } }, unsubscribes { total, user, waitlist } }], unsubscribesWithoutIssue, definitions }` (`links` = the content links only; `total` = links + slots). Internal reporting reads it with that token.
- Migration `0083_newsletter_clicks` (adds the two tables). `/privacidad` changes only if Legal asks (proposed line: «contamos cuántas veces se pulsa cada enlace, sin saber quién»).

## Receta y truco por persona (PRO-1020, D-122 point 4; from issue 4, 6-nov)

Each **account** gets its own recipe in place of the star and its own tip in place of the general one, chosen at send in `services/newsletterTips.ts`. Waitlist people (no household) get the issue's `star` and `tip` as before.

- **The recipe** (`pickRecipe`): among the issue's `content.recipes` (up to 8 catalogue recipes `{ recipeId, title, body, imageUrl, imageAlt, meta? }`), never one that breaks the allergies, diet or dislikes of anyone in the household (the same `violatesRestrictions` as the menu, with each member's restrictions and the step mentions of PRO-346), one rated «Mejor no» or «No para mí» (`loadHouseholdRatingSets`), or one already sent to them in an earlier issue; among the rest, a stable order per person and issue (sha256 of issue + person + recipe), so two people rarely get the same one. None left (or the check failed) → the `star`. The recipe links to `/recipes/<id>`.
- **The tip** (`pickTip`, the rules of §6.3 in Marketing's `trucos.md`, validated by Miguel on 2026-10-23): `NEWSLETTER_TIPS` holds the tips whose use can be measured today (39 WhatsApp, 50 «Sí, avísame», 33 tachar, 21 and 22 guardar recetas, 45 invitar a casa, 47 tiendas, 1 afinar, 24 modo cocina, 4 instalar, 54 notificaciones, 59 otro hogar), each with its line, its screen, its priority, its week in the §5 order and how we know it's used: a feature of the usage table (PRO-1023, `household_feature_usage`, the household's rows and the person's) or the account's state (WhatsApp connected, avisos on, two accounts, push). Then:
  1. only `enProduccion` and not `enPausa` (nothing of §2 «En pausa» is in the list);
  2. its condition («Para quién»: uses the list, WhatsApp connected or not, owner of a one-account household with 2 adults…);
  3. out if already used;
  4. a «no» is for good: 50 with `whatsapp_links.opt_in_declined_at`, 45 with `family_invite_offers.mimoDeclined`, 59 with `invite_offer_declined_at`;
  5. rest: out if shown in the last 28 days; after 2 shows, 84 days; 3 shows at most;
  6. order: the §5 tips already due by weeks since sign-up (the sign-up week is 1), in that order; then A, B, C; tie: the one shown fewer times;
  7. none left → an A tip they used whose last use is 60 days old or more («por si se te había olvidado»); else the issue's general `tip`;
  8. one sharing tip (57–59) every 30 days at most.
- **Log** `newsletter_tip_log (issue_id, user_id, household_id, tip, recipe_id, shown_at, clicked_at, adopted_at)`, one row per issue and account (unique), written after the send for the emails that went out (`tip` null = the general one, `recipe_id` null = the star). It feeds «no repeating», the rest and adoption: **adopted** = the tip's feature first used within 14 days after `shown_at`, stamped when the next issue is composed. `clicked_at` stays empty while clicks are anonymous (PRO-191). CASCADE with the account and the household; only the composer reads it.
- **Clicks per slot**: the recipe goes through `mimoia.com/n/<issue>/43?r=<recipe id>` and the tip through `/44?t=<tip id>` (slots `receta` and `truco`, saved with the issue's links only when it is personalised). The API builds the destination (`personalSlotDestination`: `<web>/recipes/<id>` or the tip's screen), never from a URL in the request; a malformed id or an unknown tip → 404. The stats show `slots.receta` and `slots.truco` (`buttons.abrir`), without who or which.
- The **proof** carries the admin's own recipe and tip and is not logged. A failure choosing (the DB) leaves everyone with the star and the general tip; a failure logging only warns.
- Migration `0098_newsletter_tip_log` (adds the table).

## Constraints

- **Recipients**: accounts with `users.newsletter_opt_in = true` that aren't suspended, plus waitlist entries with `newsletter_opt_in = true`, status not `unsubscribed` (waiting, invited or joined) and an email; one per email, the account first (`selectNewsletterRecipients`). Account emails have no «Salir de la lista de espera» link.
- **Idempotent per issue**: before sending, each recipient is claimed in `newsletter_sends (issue_id, recipient_hash)` (unique; `recipient_hash` = sha256 of the lowercased email, so no second copy of the address). Re-running the same issue emails nobody twice; a concurrent run gets only what it claimed. A `failed` or `skipped` (no `RESEND_API_KEY`) send releases its claim, so a re-run retries just those. A claim left `pending` by a crash counts as sent (no double email; check Resend's dashboard).
- **Invite link (PRO-130, PRO-1019)**: per recipient, built in `buildNewsletterMessage` (`correLaVozLinks`): accounts `<web>/i/<users.referral_code>` (assigned at send by `repo.ensureAccountCode` → `ensureReferralCode` when missing; a failure only leaves out that email's block), waitlist entries `waitlistReferralUrl(<web origin>, waitlist_entries.referral_code)`; each email carries only its own recipient's code (test in `newsletter.test.ts`). When an address is both an account and a waitlist entry, the account wins (its household link). «entráis antes» only holds while people get in by batches: from the day registration opens (`NEWSLETTER_OPEN_REGISTRATION_DAY` = 2026-11-16, D-057, Madrid date of the send) `newsletterInviteLine` gives «Invita a tu hogar a Mimoia:» instead (PRO-311, Legal 2026-10-21: no false urgency). Pinned in `newsletter.test.ts` (13-nov says it, 20-nov doesn't; the switch at 00:00 Madrid).
- **Forgetting the traces (PRO-132)**: leaving the waitlist (`/lista/baja`) or deleting the account deletes every `newsletter_sends` row with that email's hash (`forgetNewsletterSends`, in the same transaction); the newsletter-only opt-out keeps them, so a re-run of the same issue still skips that address.
- **Opt-out tokens**: `w.<the entry's waitlist opt-out token>` for waitlist entries; `u.<userId>.<HMAC-SHA256(JWT_SECRET), 22 chars>` for accounts (`newsletterTokenForUser`; a forged or changed id fails). `POST /newsletter/unsubscribe` takes `{ token }` (from the page) or `?t=<token>` with the form body `List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (from the inbox); it sets `newsletter_opt_in = false` on the account and the waitlist entry with that email, nothing else → `200 { ok, alreadyUnsubscribed }`, 404 `NEWSLETTER_TOKEN_NOT_FOUND`. Limiter 30/hour per IP.
- Headers on every issue: `List-Unsubscribe: <API one-click URL>, <web opt-out page>` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. The one-click URL uses the origin the admin call came in on (the API's own public URL).
- Template: tables + inline styles, 600 px column, brand tokens, Google Fonts link (Apple Mail shows Fraunces/Cormorant/Inter; Gmail falls back to Georgia/Helvetica), `color-scheme: light`, every string escaped (the headline's `*accent*` too), text version with the same content and links.
- Brand images are served by the web from `apps/web/public/email/` (`mimoia-logo.png`, `mimoia-simbolo.png`): the logo and symbol only show once that web build is live.
- `/newsletter/baja` is a public route (`PUBLIC_ROUTES` in `app/layout.tsx`).
- No health claims in any issue (constitution §6); the wine idea waits for Legal.

## Known limitations

- Only accounts get a recipe and a tip of their own (PRO-1020); the waitlist gets the same issue for everyone apart from the per-recipient links and its letter (PRO-1123: its name, household size and «improviso»).
- Accounts have no first-name field: the letter's name comes from the waitlist entry with the same email or a name-like username; otherwise «Hola:». The `{porque}` sentence is by rules, not written by the model per person (no cost; the API in batch with the cache stays possible if Miguel wants more variety). The waitlist rotation of general tips (39, 21, 40, 7, `trucos.md` §5) is the admin's `tip`, written per issue.
- Tips whose use isn't measured yet (the rest of `trucos.md`, e.g. 7, 9, 12, 23, 35, 40) wait for their metric (§6.2); the calendar moments of §6.3 (13 from the 25th to the 5th, 6 and 16 before a long weekend) come with them.
- `newsletter_tip_log.clicked_at` is never filled: a click doesn't know who clicked (PRO-191).
- The accounts' `/i/<code>` link needs the web route to accept household codes (PRO-1016, D-132); until then `app/i/[code]/route.ts` only knows campaign codes.
- The waitlist's WhatsApp text (`waitlistShareText`, shared with the app) still says «entramos juntos y antes» after 16-nov; only the block's title follows PRO-311.
- No opens: only clicks per link (PRO-191) and opt-outs per issue. Scanners not in the user-agent list still count as clicks.
- Unsubscribing from the newsletter has no "subscribe again" button: write to the contact in `/privacidad`.
