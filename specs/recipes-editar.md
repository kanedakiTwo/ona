# Recipes · Crear y editar recetas, la API y el escritorio

> Parte de [Recipes](./recipes.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): ingredientes en una línea y pasos pegados, lo que pueden editar los admins, cómo se ve en pantalla (también en escritorio) y los endpoints.

## User Capabilities: crear y editar

- **Ingredients in one line, steps pasted** (PRO-1115, `/recipes/new` and `/recipes/[id]/edit`): the ingredients are **one box, one ingredient per line**, as on paper — «200 g de lentejas», «1 cebolla», «2 cucharadas de aceite», «una pizca de sal», «sal», «200 g de bacalao, desalado (opcional)». A row written back as a line counts in plural when it is more than one («2 patatas», «3 dientes de ajo»; a noun with its adjective, «2 pimiento rojo», is left as it is — `countedIngredientName`, 2026-11-24: a photo import wrote «2 patata»). The save-time notices (`recipeLint.ts`) don't say «"sal" no se menciona en ningún paso» when a step says «salpimentar» (it names the salt and the pepper; «sazonar», the salt), nor a per-serving range for an ingredient with no amount («mantequilla al gusto», 0 g). Each line is read without AI (`parseIngredientLine`, `@ona/shared`): amount (decimals with comma, fractions «1/2», «1 1/2», «½», words «un/una/medio/dos…», «un par de», ranges keep the first), unit (g/kg/ml/l…, and the kitchen vocabulary of `units/`: cucharada, cucharadita, pizca, puñado, diente, vaso, chorrito, loncha…, plurals and abbreviations «cdas.», «gr.»; containers «lata», «bote» count as units), name, note (after the comma or between brackets) and «(opcional)»; no amount, or «al gusto», is «al gusto». A line ending in «:» («Para la masa:») is a section heading. Stored as the recipe rows always were: g/ml/ud/cda/cdita/pizca/al gusto, with the written amount kept in `displayQuantity`/`displayUnit` when it converts («1,5 kg» → 1500 g, «2 dientes» → 10 g, «un puñado» → 30 g). **Under each line, how it was understood**: «200 g · lentejas ✓» when the library has it (same name without case, accents or plural, or the imports' token matcher: «cebolla picada» → cebolla); **«¿cuál?»** with up to 3 buttons when it doubts (more generic than several entries — «pimentón» → dulce / picante; unanswered, the first one — or not there but sharing words — «pechuga de pollo» → pollo; unanswered, a new one) plus «Otro: "X"»; and **«nuevo, se crea al guardar»** when it isn't there (`POST /ingredients/match-lines`, a moment after typing; `matchIngredientName`). The **new ones are created when saving**, like in the imports (`POST /ingredients/ensure`: USDA, else BEDCA, else without nutrition — «nutrición incompleta» until Cocina fills it, [Nutrition](./nutrition.md); allergens from the name; no AI translation), instead of the old «Hay ingredientes sin asociar a la base» block. The **steps** are another box: written or pasted whole, split into steps by line breaks and numbering («1.», «2)», «Paso 3:», also inside one paragraph; a numbered step's unnumbered next line continues it; «Preparación:» headings drop) with `splitSteps` (`@ona/shared`), shown numbered underneath. **In the editor** the saved rows are shown as text generated from them (`formatIngredientLine`: «2 cucharadas de aceite de oliva virgen», «1,5 kg de patatas», «sal al gusto», sections as headings) and the steps numbered; a line left as it was keeps its row exactly (and untouched steps go back as they were), so opening the editor and saving without touching anything doesn't change the recipe. No AI cost. Tests: `ingredientLine.test.ts`, `splitSteps.test.ts`, `ingredientLineMatch.test.ts` (unit), `e2e/recipe-quick-lines.spec.ts` (390×844: 5 lines and 4 pasted steps, one ingredient new in the library; editor saved untouched), `e2e/recipe-add-ingredient.spec.ts` (390×844, a new ingredient)
- Authors can edit their own recipes via `/recipes/[id]/edit` (a "Editar receta" link sits at the end of the detail's Notas tab / section; hidden for non-author non-admin viewers). The edit form supports name, servings, prep/cook times, difficulty, meals, seasons, tags, the ingredients box and the steps box (above), a **unified multi-entry Notas list** (shows `recipes.notes` and then `recipes.tips` — the trucos — one entry per paragraph; on save the tips paragraphs left as they were go back to `tips`, which Mimo reads as «Trucos», and new or edited ones go to `notes`, PRO-1259), the «Tipo de plato» (`course`), and a "Guardar igualmente" path that re-submits with `?force=1` when the lint validator returns soft warnings (the button lives in the "Avisos" card above the sticky "Guardar cambios" pill; same on `/recipes/new` with "Crear receta"). "Guardar cambios" stays disabled until the recipe has a name, servings, a meal, a season and at least one ingredient line, and while it's disabled a line above the pill says which one is missing (testid `save-hint`, e.g. «Para guardar, añade al menos un ingrediente, uno por línea.»; `e2e/recipe-edit-save-hint.spec.ts`). Voice users can edit metadata fields (name, prepTime, cookTime, difficulty, notes) via the `edit_recipe` skill and ask "abre el editor" to navigate to the form for ingredient/step changes
- **Admins can edit and delete any recipe** — system catalogue rows (`authorId IS NULL`) included — so the curation workflow lives in the same form as authoring. The "Editar receta" link surfaces on the detail page whenever `user.role === 'admin'`; a small "Admin" badge is shown next to it when the admin is editing someone else's recipe (or a system one). PUT/DELETE on the API short-circuit the author check when `req.user.role === 'admin'`, and the persisted row preserves the original `authorId` (admin edits on a system recipe stay system; admin edits on another user's recipe stay under that user). The "Añadir a mis recetas" copy affordance is hidden for admins (they have direct edit access instead)
- Authors can delete their own recipes (not system recipes; admins can delete any)
- Users can extract a recipe from a photo (image upload → AI extraction); a big camera photo is made smaller first (≤ 1568 px, JPEG — `fitImageForClaude`, as the school menu), so it doesn't hit Claude's 5 MB per image
- Users can share a recipe via the native share sheet (Web Share API) from a Share2 button in the detail hero (mobile) or action row (desktop) — see [PWA](./pwa.md). **"Pásalo"**: a catalogue recipe shares **only a title and the link** `/recipes/:id?ref=receta` (the page opens without an account, with the waitlist block; the link preview says what Mimoia is). No `text` goes with the `url`: some apps glue url + " " + text into one broken link (`…?ref=receta%20Pollo…`). A private recipe is shared as text (name + ingredients) with a link to Mimoia, since its page is private (`recipeSharePayload` in `@ona/shared`). **With the household's invitation (PRO-1016, D-132):** signed in, the share carries the account's own code (`GET /referrals/me`, PRO-162): a catalogue recipe's link becomes `/recipes/:id?ref=receta&invita=<code>` (still no text), and a private recipe's text ends with «Hecho con Mimo · mimoia.com/i/<code>» instead of the generic line. The page opened signed out with a valid `?invita=` remembers it for the sign-up (`rememberSignupAttribution`, like the landing; it counts as a referral, D-121), the waitlist block's link keeps it (`/?ref=receta&invita=<code>#lista-de-espera`; with open registration `/register` carries it already) and the block ends, small and faint, with «Hecho con Mimo · mimoia.com/i/<code>» (`data-testid="recipe-made-with"`). Code not loaded yet → the share as before. e2e `share-invite-foot.spec.ts` (390×844), `sharePayloads.test.ts`
- Users can start "Cooking mode" from the detail view ("Empezar a cocinar" — one entry point: the sticky bottom action bar on mobile, the action row on desktop). It navigates to `/recipes/[id]/cook?servings=N`, which holds the screen-wake Wake Lock for the duration of the cook session — see [PWA](./pwa.md) and [Cooking Mode](./cooking-mode.md)
- Users can apply **structured ingredient overrides** to any recipe (Mimoia, theirs, or another user's) from the detail view. Toggling "Editar ingredientes" (under the ingredient list) unlocks three actions per row — quitar, modificar cantidad/unidad/nota, and a "+ Añadir ingrediente" at the bottom of the section. In read-mode the recipe renders with the overrides baked in: removed rows are struck-through and faded, modified rows show the original quantity struck-through alongside the new value in terracotta, added rows live in a forest-green block underneath the original list. Overrides are per-household (one row per `(household, recipe)` in `recipe_notes.ingredient_overrides`), persist across navigation, and apply every time the household sees the recipe. The **shopping-list aggregator consumes these overrides** before scaling: removed lines drop out of the basket entirely, modified lines use the override's quantity/unit, and added lines (when the free-form `label` resolves against the ingredient catalog) get added to the basket. Adds whose name doesn't match the catalog stay visible on the recipe but are skipped by the shopping list. The recipe matcher itself still scores against the original ingredient list, so a "sin cebolla" override doesn't change which recipes Mimoia picks for the menu — it only changes what ends up in your basket and on the recipe view
- Users can mark a recipe as **"siempre la cocino para al menos N"** (1–24 raciones; guardan o congelan lo que sobra) in *Notas → Tus notas → Raciones mínimas* on the detail. It's per household (`recipe_notes.min_servings`, migration 0035, `PUT /recipes/:id/notes { minServings }`, `null` clears). Each time the recipe is cooked, the shopping list buys for at least N; a planned leftover belongs to the same batch (see [Shopping](./shopping.md)). The detail's servings scaler starts at N until the user moves it — unless the URL carries `?servings=N` (links from the menu, PRO-75: the batch the menu cooks), which wins. The assistant sets it too (`update_recipe_notes`, "las lentejas siempre las hago para 6").
- Signed-in users see this month's **«Menú de <mes>»** card on `/recipes` (nothing filtered), which opens the month's week and copies it into theirs — see [Menus](./menus.md) → «Menú del mes» (PRO-378)
- Users import recipes (photo, URL, WhatsApp) and auto-create missing ingredients — see [Recipe Import](./recipe-import.md). Recipes get a generated house photo — see [Recipe Images](./recipe-images.md)

## Display Constraints

- A `prepTime` of `0` (or any falsy value) is **not** rendered on cards or detail meta — instead, the field is omitted entirely (fixes the prior "0" leak). Cards show `recipeMinutes` (total time, else prep time). **Season rule**: "now" is `detectSeason(date)` — meteorological seasons for Spain (Sep–Nov otoño, Dec–Feb invierno…), browser-local month, the same rule as the menu generator. A season is never derived from the order of `recipe.seasons` (until 2026-10-08 cards showed `seasons[0]`: "Primavera" on every all-year recipe in October). Catalogue cards (signed in and out) show no season. Guarded by `catalogSeason.test.ts`
- All meal/season labels go through `MEAL_LABELS` / `SEASON_LABELS` before render (no raw `lunch`/`dinner` in the UI)
- Spanish copy uses correct accents (Otoño, Año, Añadir, Preparación) — no mojibake fallbacks
- The detail view groups ingredients by `section` if any ingredient has one set; otherwise renders a flat list
- The "Para X" caption next to the ingredients title reflects the live scaler value, not a hardcoded number

### Desktop layout (lg+)

At `lg+` (≥1024 px) `/recipes` keeps the desktop sidebar and caps at `max-w-[1180px]`: row 1 = h1 "Recetas" (40 px) + search (max 560 px) + ink "Nueva receta" pill on the right; row 2 = every chip wrapped on one line + "Más filtros" (no filter column); then the two hero cards and a 4-column grid (220 px photos, row-major order). Below `lg` the grid is a 2-column masonry (tall / short photos alternate). State lives in the page; `CatalogFilters.tsx` exports `CatalogSearch`, `CatalogChips` and `CatalogFiltersSheet`; `CatalogGrid.tsx` renders `RecipeCard`; `FeaturedRecipeCard.tsx` is the hero.

`/cookbooks/[id]` is **not** a catalogue page — it's a single-cookbook detail (see [Cookbooks](./cookbooks.md)). Since PRO-43 it reuses `<CatalogGrid>` (same cards, masonry below `lg`, 4 columns at `lg+`, page capped at `max-w-[1180px]`) and passes `renderAction` to lay a "Quitar del recetario" button over each photo; it does not use `<CatalogFilters>`. Without `renderAction` the grid renders exactly as on `/recipes`.

### Recipe detail / Create / Edit desktop layouts (lg+)

`/recipes/[id]` switches at `lg+` (`useIsDesktop`, `hooks/useMediaQuery.ts`) to the two-column split described above: a `max-w-[1280px]` 50/50 grid (signed out, on the public grid `public-container`, so the photo starts under the PublicNavbar logo), the photo `sticky top-7` at `100dvh − 56px` (signed out, below the PublicNavbar: `top-[104px]` at `100dvh − 132px`), the title above the fold at 1440×900. Both layouts render the same section blocks; only the chrome differs.

`/recipes/new` and `/recipes/[id]/edit` ("D · Luz y foto", PRO-42) cap at 1180 px and split into two columns at `lg+`: data (and, on edit, the photo) on the left, ingredients and steps (and notes) on the right; the photo/URL import cards sit side by side above the manual form. Below `lg` everything stacks in one column. See [Design System](./design-system.md).

## API Endpoints

- `GET /recipes?search=&meal=&season=&maxTime=&perPage=&page=&fitsMe=1&mine=1&dishType=` — list with filters (`dishType`, PRO-1175: a `DISH_TYPES` key, `recipes.dish_types` contains it — the menu picker's type chips; an unknown key is ignored); returns the lightweight card shape (since PRO-376 it carries `course` and `allergenDoubt`; never `internalTags`). `mine=1` (PRO-299) keeps only the caller's own recipes (anonymous: none). `fitsMe=1` (authenticated only; ignored anonymously) hides recipes that break the caller's allergies or diet (profile + memory, same rules as the menu generator; dislikes not applied) — used by the menu's «Elegir otra receta» picker (PRO-61). **Optional auth:** with a valid Bearer token the response is the system catalogue + the caller's own recipes (other users' recipes are never listed; a personal copy hides the system original it was copied from); without a token only system recipes (`authorId IS NULL`) are returned, so the same endpoint backs `/recipes` both as the public catalogue (anonymous) and as the app list (authenticated).
- `GET /recipes?ingredient=<nombre>` (PRO-375) — on top of the other filters: only recipes with an ingredient of that name (normalised, whole word, plurals; a product of the calendar takes its terms, `findProduce` → `ingredientMatchesProduce`); an unknown name lists nothing. `GET /recipes/seasonal-produce?month=1-12` (public; Madrid's month by default) — `[{ name, emoji, recipeCount }]`: the month's calendar products used by ≥3 published, listed catalogue recipes, most used first. Service: `services/seasonalProduce.ts`
- `GET /recipes/with-what-i-have?ingredient=<id>[&ingredient=<id>…]` (auth) — «Con lo que tengo»: 1–3 catalogue ingredient ids (400 with none or more than 3) → card shape + `uses` (chosen names the recipe uses) + `missing` (required non-basic ingredients not chosen), ranked; at most 100. Service: `services/withWhatIHave.ts`
- `GET /recipes/:id?servings=N` — single recipe; if `servings` is provided and differs from `recipe.servings`, quantities are scaled server-side and a `scaledFrom` field is included. Carries `sourceCredit` (string or `null`, D-067) and never `catalog_source`. For its author or an admin it also carries `notes`, `tips`, `substitutions`, `storage` (when set) and `course` (PRO-1259, what «Editar receta» loads; the PUT answers with the same); for anyone else none of them. Anonymous callers can only fetch system recipes; requesting a user-authored recipe without a token returns 404 (same shape as "not found" to avoid leaking which IDs exist privately). **An id that isn't a uuid** (`/recipes/no-es-un-id`, a cut link) is the same 404 `Recipe not found` on every `/recipes/:id...` route of `routes/recipes.ts` (GET, PUT, DELETE, copy, regenerate-image, upload-image, favorite), answered by `requireRecipeId` (`middleware/recipeId.ts`) before the database, so nothing is logged as an error (PRO-181; before, Postgres rejected the uuid cast → 500). Notes, photos and cookbook routes already answered 400/404 for it. Test: `recipeIdGuard.test.ts`.
- `GET /catalog-collections` — the «Colecciones» row: `{ collections: [{ slug, title, subtitle, season, recipeCount, imageUrl }] }`, published ones with a recipe to show. `GET /catalog-collections/:slug` (optional auth, for the hearts) — `{ collection, recipes }` with the list's card shape, in order; 404 when unknown, unpublished or empty. Admin routes in [Catalogue import](./catalog-import.md).
- `POST /recipes` (auth) — create user recipe; runs lint validator
- `PUT /recipes/:id` (auth, author only; admins any) — update; runs lint validator and recomputes `nutritionPerServing` and `allergens`. Requires `name`, `servings`, `meals`, `ingredients`, `steps`. **What the body leaves out is kept** (PRO-1259, `KEPT_ON_UPDATE` in `routes/recipes.ts`): `notes`, `tips`, `substitutions`, `storage`, `course`, `yieldText`, `prepTime`, `cookTime`, `activeTime`, `servingsConfidence`, `difficulty`, `seasons`, `equipment`, `tags`, `internalTags`, `frequency`, `sourceUrl`, `sourceType` (and `advancePrep`, `imageUrl`); a field sent wins — `null` or `''` clears it (empty texts are stored as NULL). `mealFit` / `seasonFit` are derived from `meals` / `seasons` when absent. Each ingredient row accepts its `id` (from the GET): a row sent with it keeps it, so `step.ingredientRefs` pointing at it survive the save; a row without one gets a new id. A sent id that is repeated or belongs to another recipe's row is replaced by a new one and the steps' refs to it follow (`assignIngredientRowIds` in `services/recipePersistence.ts`). Until 2026-11-17 every save replaced all of it: the edit form and Cocina's fixes lost the trucos, the course and the ingredients under each step. Tests: `recipeUpdateKeeps.test.ts`, `recipeUpdateKeeps.smoke.ts`, `e2e/recipe-edit-keeps.spec.ts`
- `DELETE /recipes/:id` (auth, author only)
- `GET /user/:id/recipes` (auth) — user's own + favorited recipes
- `POST /user/:id/recipes/:recipeId/favorite` (auth) — toggle favorite. **PR 1B:** each user toggles their own `user_favorites` row, but `GET /user/:id/recipes` returns the household-wide union of favorites when `SHARED_HOUSEHOLD_SCOPE` is on. See [Household](./household.md)
- `POST /recipes/:id/copy` (auth) — clone a recipe into the caller's catalog. Only a recipe the caller can open (`canViewRecipe`: the catalogue or their household's); another household's private recipe is a 404, like `GET /recipes/:id` (PRO-1273, 2026-11-20). Refuses with 409 if the user already owns the source. Returns the new recipe
