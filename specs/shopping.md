# Shopping

Weekly shopping list and pantry stock management. Aggregates ingredients across the menu's recipes, scales them for the household, and respects real units.

## Partes

Esta spec está partida por temas (tope de 5.000 palabras por spec, PRO-405); cada parte lleva su texto entero:

- [Cómo se suma la lista, la API y lo que se añade a mano](./shopping-suma.md) — la agregación (bases, platos nota), los endpoints, lo añadido a mano con precios, las comidas fijas y los básicos que se repiten.

## User Capabilities

- Users can see a shopping list auto-generated from the current week's menu
- The list aggregates ingredients across all recipes in the week, summing quantities **per product** with proper unit handling: lines that are the same product join into one even when the catalogue has them under different names («Huevo 4 u» + «Huevos 6 u» + «Yema de huevo» → «Huevos 10 u»; the two olive oils; «Jamón cocido» + «Jamón de york»; «Limón» / «Limon»; «Salmón» + «Lomo de salmón sin piel») — see Aggregation (PRO-177)
- Tap water («agua», «agua del grifo», «agua caliente»…) and salt never show up as something to buy; «agua de coco» or «agua con gas» do
- **Coeliac household** (PRO-196, Miguel 2026-10-18): when the user's restrictions (profile + memory) include gluten, a menu row that is a cured meat whose gluten depends on the brand (chorizo, chistorra, morcilla, butifarra, salchicha, salchichón, fuet, longaniza, sobrasada, mortadela, salami, foie, paté, jamón de york/cocido, fiambre, embutido; not the cocido leftovers nor a stock) is asked for as «<name> sin gluten» («Chorizo oreado sin gluten»). Done in `GET /shopping-list` (and the legacy `/:menuId`) after the aggregation, so Mimo (`get_shopping_list`) and shop orders see the same name; staples and manual items keep the user's words, and the bought/«en casa» overlay is unaffected (`services/glutenFreeCharcuterie.ts`, `glutenFreeCharcuterie.test.ts`). The catalogue no longer tags those cured meats `gluten` (migration `0072_embutidos_sin_gluten.sql`), so a coeliac household gets lentejas con chorizo, fabada or cocido again; the «Sobras del cocido» keep the tag. **Blended spices and stocks** go the same way (PRO-1063, Dani y Sara 2026-10-27): chili/chile en polvo, curry (en polvo or paste), ras el hanout and any stock (caldo, cubito, pastilla de caldo, fumet; not «huesos para caldo») become «Caldo de verduras sin gluten», «Chili en polvo sin gluten». Cocina adds the blends that are missing to `BRAND_DEPENDENT_BLENDS`
- Users can check items off as they buy them (visual strikethrough, persisted)
- Users can mark items as already in their pantry (stock manager)
- **Change or leave out any line** (PRO-175, hogares de prueba 2026-10-15/17): tapping a line's name opens its sheet. A line that comes from the menu can get the household's own amount («Cantidad a comprar», in the line's unit; the row then reads «… · cambiada (menú: X)» and «Volver a lo del menú» undoes it) or be left out with **«No lo compro esta semana»**: it moves to a «Quitado de esta compra» section under the list with «Volver a añadir», and counts for nothing (not to buy, not in progress, total, shared text, WhatsApp summary, print or shop orders). Both survive reloads and other ranges (see Constraints). The same sheet asks **«¿Cuánto os queda en casa?»** (PRO-182, Dani y Sara 2026-10-16: «nos queda una cebolla y medio pimiento» marked the week's 7 onions «en casa»): «Comprar solo lo que falta» leaves the line pending for the rest («Te faltan 6 u · ya tienes 1»), as much as the line marks it «en casa», empty/0 clears it; Mimo does the same with `mark_in_stock` + `quantity`/`unit` (converted with the ingredient's density / unit weight). Amounts round as the partial state of PRO-199 (half a pepper doesn't save a whole one). A manual line takes any amount («2», or «2 paquetes» as written — no new unit) or «Quitar de la lista»; a staple points to Mis básicos. Mimo does the same (`remove_shopping_items`, `change_shopping_item_amount`, see [Advisor](./advisor.md))
- **Edit each line where it is** (PRO-292, Miguel 2026-10-20): on «Por comprar» every line has, under its name and without opening anything, **− / +** and the amount as a field you can type in (steps: 1 u; 25 / 50 / 100 g or ml as the amount grows; half a spoon; taps are sent together after 0,6 s), the **unit as a selector** with the units that make sense for that product (`unitOptions`: the menu's unit first, then g / ml / u when the ingredient converts — density g↔ml, unit weight g↔u, or the shop's piece weight from the buy rules when the catalogue has none: cebolla 150 g; one unit only → plain text), the € field and **×** «Quitar». Quitar on a menu line is «No lo compro esta semana» (it goes to «Quitado de esta compra»); on a manual line it deletes it; either way a bar «Quitado: X · Deshacer» shows for 7 s and Deshacer puts it back (a manual line is added again with the same name, amount, aisle and price). A menu line switched to another unit reads «cambiada (menú: 700 g)» and the selector back on the menu's unit returns to the menu's amount. Manual lines take any unit (no conversion) and a «2 paquetes» line steps its number. Staples show their amount read-only. On top of the list, **«Añadir a la lista»** is always visible (a name + Enter; aisle from the buy rules; the «···» → «Añadir a mano» form stays for amount, aisle and price). Mimo: `change_shopping_item_unit` (see [Advisor](./advisor.md)). Components in `components/shopping/LineControls.tsx`; e2e `shopping-line-controls.spec.ts` (390×844)
- **«Habitual» from the list** (PRO-293, Miguel 2026-10-20): each line to buy has a ☆ («Habitual: X», `aria-pressed`) and its sheet a «Habitual» switch. On creates — or switches on, if it exists paused — the household's staple with that name, amount, unit, aisle and price (`PUT …/item/:itemId/edit` `{ habitual }`, `services/shoppingHabitual.ts`), so it comes back by itself on every new list; off pauses it (`active = false`, never deleted: «Mis básicos» in the profile still lists it). Lines that are staples read «Habitual» (tag, filled star): staple lines by kind, and menu lines whose name is an active staple (`habitual: true`, set by `GET /shopping-list`; a staple with the same name as a menu line doesn't add a second line). A manual line marked habitual turns into the staple line; a staple line switched off leaves the list. Mimo: `set_shopping_habitual` («pon la leche como habitual», «quita el pan de habituales»; see [Advisor](./advisor.md)). e2e `shopping-habitual.spec.ts` (390×844), unit `shoppingHabitual.test.ts`
- Items in stock are excluded from the active shopping list
- Users can switch between two tabs: "Por comprar" (shopping list) and "Ya en casa" (pantry)
- Users can see items grouped by aisle (produce, carnicería, lácteos, panadería, despensa…) for faster shopping; a single chip row under the header narrows the current tab to one aisle ("Todo" shows them all; only aisles with rows in that tab get a chip)
- The date range (today → end of next week by default) shows as the page eyebrow and as the first chip of that row; tapping it (or "···" → "Cambiar fechas") opens a sheet with "Desde" / "Hasta" and the «Dos semanas» reset. **‹ ›** on either side of that chip ("Semana anterior" / "Semana siguiente", mobile and desktop) move the whole range 7 days; "anterior" never goes before today and is disabled while the range starts today
- **Atajos de fecha** (PRO-33, PRO-269): right after the ‹ › arrows, five chips set the range in one tap, and each shows what it says — **«Hoy»** (from = to = today: only today's dishes; the date chip then reads «14 oct»), **«Mañana»** (from = to = tomorrow), **«Esta semana»** (today → this Sunday), **«La que viene»** (next Monday → next Sunday) and **«Dos semanas»** (today → end of next week, the default range the page opens with). Before PRO-269 «Esta semana» was the two weeks: Laura tapped it on Monday 19 and saw «19 oct – 1 nov». The chip that matches the current range is marked (`aria-pressed`; on a Sunday «Hoy» and «Esta semana» are the same day and the one tapped is marked); after ‹ › or a custom range in the sheet none is. On a phone (390 px) the row doesn't fit, so it slides sideways: the last chip always keeps a visible margin before the right edge, and the shortcut you tap slides fully into view (PRO-69). The € total card's title follows the shortcut: «Total de hoy estimado», «Total de mañana estimado», «Total de esta semana estimado», «Total de la semana que viene estimado», «Total de las dos semanas estimado», and «Total semanal estimado» for any other range (PRO-69, `totalTitle`). Logic in `apps/web/src/lib/shoppingRange.ts` (unit `shoppingQuickRange.test.ts`, e2e `shopping-quick-ranges.spec.ts` and `shopping-two-weeks.spec.ts` at 390×844)
- **Each line says its week** (PRO-269, Familia Ruiz 2026-10-19: Mimo said «dale un vistazo a la lista para ver qué es de qué semana» and the list didn't say): when the range covers more than one week («Dos semanas», or the arrows), every menu line carries a small tag under its name — «Esta semana», «La que viene», «Las dos semanas» or «Semana del 2 nov» — from its `weeks` (PRO-270). With one week on screen there is no tag. Manual items and staples have none (`LineWeek`, `lineWeekLabel`)
- **«Mandar la lista»** (PRO-380, D-158) is the list's main button (under the progress card; full width on a phone). It opens a sheet with what's left to buy as text by aisle — `*Frutas y verduras*` then `- Tomate: 1 kg`… in the walk-through order, only the rest when part is at home (`shoppingListShareText` in `@ona/shared`) — and, at the foot, the household's own invitation, «Hecho con Mimo · mimoia.com/i/<code>» (PRO-1016, D-132: the account's code from `GET /referrals/me`, PRO-162; `madeWithMimoLine`), so a sign-up through it counts as a referral (D-121, the K factor); only while the code hasn't loaded, the generic Mimoia line with `/?ref=lista` («Por WhatsApp» and «Copiar» wait for it). Options: on a phone with the system share sheet, **«Compartir»** first (Web Share API); **«Por WhatsApp»** (`wa.me/?text=…`, new tab); **«Copiar»**; **«Enviar a {súper}»** when the household has a súper with a web that the browser extension fills (→ `/compra`, see [shop-orders.md](./shop-orders.md)); **«Pedir a mi tienda»** when it has shops (→ `/compra`). Disabled with nothing left to buy. «Compartir», «Por WhatsApp» and «Copiar» count as `shopping_list_shared` (`POST /usage/list-shared`, `channel: web`, `props.via`), and so does Mimo handing out the whole list in the chat or on WhatsApp («hazme la lista de la compra», `get_shopping_list` with something left, `channel` its own). Sending the list or an order resolves the week ([metrics.md](./metrics.md) → Resolved week). e2e `shopping-send-list.spec.ts`, `share-invite-foot.spec.ts` (390×844), unit `sharePayloads.test.ts`, `businessMetrics.test.ts`, `appSkills.test.ts`
- Check-item and stock-toggle mutations are queued offline (IndexedDB) and replay automatically when the device reconnects; an inline "Pendiente de sincronizar" Clock icon marks items still in the queue — see [PWA](./pwa.md)
- The page shows progress: "X/Y completados", "N% listo", "X comprados · Z en casa" with an ink progress bar. Each row counts once: a row bought and then marked "en casa" counts as en casa, so X never exceeds Y nor the % 100 (`shoppingProgress` in `@ona/shared`; it showed "72/56 · 129 %" before 2026-10-10)
- If no menu exists for the week, the page shows an empty state with a CTA to generate one
- **«Compártela con quien compra contigo»** (PRO-261, D-088): the first time the list is on screen, once and never again, a card under the header offers to prepare the invitation to this household («Te preparo el mensaje» → «Enviar por WhatsApp» / «Copiar»). Only for a one-account household with 2 adults or more — see [Household](./household.md) → «Ofrecer la invitación».
- Items carry their recipe notes (`notes`: "picada", "morada en juliana") and tiny buyable amounts no longer vanish (two cloves of garlic stay as a line when a shop sells garlic; pantry staples still drop). Items typed by hand with no amount are stored as 1 u with `quantitySource: 'default'` (the form starts empty) and get their aisle from the buy rules ("calabacín" → frutas y verduras). See [shop-orders.md](./shop-orders.md) → Buy rules.
- **Pedir a mis tiendas** ("···" sheet; also an outlined pill in the header at `lg+`) links to `/compra`, where Mimoia turns what's left for the next 7 days into one order per shop (frutería, carnicería, pescadería, súper) that the user sends from their own WhatsApp — see [Compra en mis tiendas](./shop-orders.md). Closing a shop order ticks its items as bought on this list.

## Item Model

Each item in `shopping_lists.items` (JSONB array) has:
- `id` — UUID
- `ingredientId` — links back to the global ingredient catalog (drives aisle grouping and unit conversions)
- `name` — display name
- `quantity` — aggregated number, in `unit`
- `unit` — `g | ml | u | cda | cdita`
- `aisle` — `produce | proteinas | lacteos | panaderia | despensa | congelados | otros`
- `mergedIngredientIds` — (menu items, optional) the other catalogue ingredients joined into this line because they are the same product (PRO-177)
- `bases` — (menu items, optional) names of the bases whose ingredients are in this line (PRO-373)
- `checked` — boolean, user has bought this
- `inStock` — boolean, user already had this in pantry
- `haveQuantity` / `haveSource` — (menu items, optional) part already bought / at home (PRO-199)
- `weeks` — (menu items, rolling list) how much of the line each week's dishes ask for, `{ <Monday>: amount }` (PRO-270)
- `mark` — (menu items, internal) the remembered mark carried into this line: `{ source, quantity, weeks? }` (PRO-270)
- `skipped` — (menu items, optional) «No lo compro esta semana» (PRO-175)
- `menuQuantity` — (menu items, optional) what the menu asked when the household set its own amount in `quantity` (PRO-175)
- `menuUnit` — (menu items, optional) the menu's unit when the household switched the line to another one; `quantity` / `unit` are then theirs (PRO-292)
- `unitOptions` — (menu items) the units the line can switch to, the menu's first; filled by `GET /shopping-list` (PRO-292)
- `habitual` — (menu / manual items, optional) its name is one of the household's active staples (PRO-293)
- `amountText` — (manual items, optional) the amount in the household's words, «2 paquetes»; shown instead of quantity + unit, sent to shops as «Pañales (2 paquetes)» (PRO-175)

## Constraints

- Field names are camelCase (`inStock`, `checked`, `ingredientId`) end-to-end — frontend, types, and DB JSONB
- While the list is being fetched again (another date range, or after a menu change) the row buttons (✓ and "En casa") are disabled and the previous range stays on screen: every GET rebuilds the row with a new id, so a tick sent with the old id used to be lost (404) when coming back to a range already seen (2026-10-10). Now the page always refetches a range (`staleTime: 0`), and the write routes (check, stock, add, edit, delete item) that get the id of a list rebuilt since apply the change to the caller's current list (item ids are deterministic, `menu:<ingredientId>:<unit>`; `loadListForCaller` in `routes/shopping.ts`, `shoppingRoute.smoke.ts`). A state mutation's response is written only into the cached rolling list with the same id — never into other ranges or the `totals` query.
- The list is regenerated on every GET — there is no longer a manual "Regenerar" button on `/shopping`. The cache invalidates automatically when any menu mutation succeeds, so editing the menu, swapping slots, or skipping a day reflects in the basket on the next render.
- **What the user marked is remembered across ranges** (2026-10-10): each rebuild merges the previous rows' `checked` / `inStock` / `pricePerUnit` into the new rows by `(ingredientId|unit)` **and** keeps them in `shopping_lists.item_state` (migration 0043); a line that joined several ingredients (PRO-177) gathers the state of all of them (marked on any old line → marked on the joined line; price only from the same unit), so a product absent from the range on screen gets its state back when it reappears (mark onions "en casa", look at next week, come back → still "en casa"). The newest rows win over the older memory; up to 400 products, oldest dropped; manual rows travel as before (`services/shoppingItemState.ts`, `shoppingItemState.test.ts`, `e2e/week-arrows.spec.ts`).
- **What was marked follows the ingredient, not the unit** (PRO-199, Miguel 2026-10-18): changing a dish can move a line to another unit («Ajo · 8 u» → «Ajo · 25 g»); the mark is looked up by ingredient (own unit first, else the latest marked line of the same ingredient in another unit; the previous rows win over older memory) and the remembered state now stores the amount marked. If the new menu asks for **more** and the two amounts compare (same unit, or the ingredient's density / unit weight), the line goes back to pending with `haveQuantity` / `haveSource` set: the row reads «Te faltan X u · ya tienes Y», Mimo's `get_shopping_list` says «Ajo (faltan 15 g; ya tienes 25 g)» and the shop order asks only for the rest; gaps under 5 g/ml or a tenth of a unit count as covered. If they don't compare, the line stays marked. Ticking the line (or «En casa») clears the partial amount. Marks saved before this change have no amount and cover any new amount (`carryItemState`, `shoppingItemState.test.ts`).
- **What was bought covers the weeks it was bought for** (PRO-270, Familia Ruiz 2026-10-19: «Merluza · 750 g» of Monday 26 came out bought because the last merluza was bought for Saturday 17). The rolling route aggregates the range once and each week of it apart, and every menu line carries `weeks` (`{ <Monday>: amount }`, each as that week's own list asks, rounded on its own; `withWeekShares`, `services/shoppingWeeks.ts`). A tick (check route, Mimo, closing a shop order: `toggleChecked`) remembers the amounts per week; on a rebuild a bought mark only covers the weeks it was bought for, so a dish of another week that asks for the product is pending (or «Te faltan X» for that week's part). Ticking the same product on «La que viene» and then on «Esta semana» keeps both weeks; unticking forgets it; weeks already gone are forgotten. «En casa» is the pantry and still covers any week. Bought marks saved before this change count for the current week only.
- **What was marked never shrinks by itself** (PRO-270, Familia Ruiz 2026-10-20: «ya tienes 15» huevos became «ya tienes 4» without touching anything). A shorter range (shop orders rebuild the next 7 days; «Hoy») asks for less, and the line used to save its own amount as the remembered one. Now a carried mark travels on the row (`mark`: source, amount, weeks, in the line's unit, internal) and is saved as it was until the household touches the line; «tengo una docena» on a line of 4 is remembered as 12. A flag changed by a path that doesn't know about `mark` wins over it. Tests: `shoppingBoughtByWeek.test.ts`.
- **«No lo compro» and the household's amount (PRO-175)** live in `item_state` under the line's `(ingredientId|unit)` as `edit: { skipped?, quantity?, until }`, written by `PUT …/edit` (`editMenuLine`) and applied on every rebuild (`applyEdit` in `carryItemState`) before the bought / «en casa» marks. A unit switch (PRO-292) is stored there too (`edit.unit` + `edit.quantity` in that unit) under the **menu's** key (`menuLineKey`); the bought / «en casa» marks stay on the row and are remembered under its current unit, so switching g → u → g never loses them (`shoppingLineUnit.test.ts`). `until` is the last day of the list it was set on: the change applies to lists that end on or before it («Hoy», «Mañana», the same range); a list reaching past it (next week) shows the line as the menu asks, and coming back shows it changed again; after that day it is forgotten. An amount equal to the menu's clears it. Tests: `shoppingLineEdit.test.ts`, `appSkills.test.ts`, `e2e/shopping-line-edit.spec.ts`
- The progress bar uses `shoppingProgress(items).ratio` = (bought-and-not-at-home + at-home) / rows, capped at 100 % by construction
- Export format is plain text suitable for paste into messaging apps; it preserves aisle grouping and ends with a "Hecho con Mimoia" line + link (the shared title is "Lista de compra · Mimoia") (`/?ref=lista`, `withOnaFooter`), so a shared list can bring another household. The list's own share is «Mandar la lista» (above), which ends with the household's invitation (PRO-1016).
- **Ingredient names on screen (detail, shopping list, pantry, staples)** read as a sentence — «Aceite de oliva virgen», «Pimentón dulce» — via `ingredientDisplayName` (`packages/shared/src/utils/shopFormat.ts`): only the first letter is raised and catalogue accents are restored; brands/acronyms the user typed («Kerrygold», «AOVE») stay. Display-only; stored names are untouched (PRO-02).
- Aisle assignment falls back to `otros` when `ingredient.aisle` is unset; curators are nudged to fill the column
- Check / stock mutations work offline; the request is held in the PWA queue until reconnect. The local UI updates optimistically and a "Pendiente de sincronizar" indicator shows while pending
- **Household scope (PR 1B):** shopping lists carry both `user_id` and `household_id`. Reads + access checks on `POST /shopping-list/:listId/regenerate` honour the env flag `SHARED_HOUSEHOLD_SCOPE`; with the flag on, any household member can regenerate the household's list. See [Household](./household.md)

- **Shopping events for the household ficha (PRO-151):** each item mutation that succeeds — tick (`…/check`), «En casa» (`…/stock`), manual add (`POST …/items`) and manual remove (`DELETE …/item/:itemId`) — leaves an `activity_events` row (`shopping_check` / `shopping_stock` / `shopping_add` / `shopping_remove`) with the product's name (`item`, ≤ 80 chars) and, for the toggles, the new state (`toggled_on`). Mimo's «marcar» and «en casa» skills do the same. They are signals of the [Ficha del hogar](./household-profile.md) (Daily signals); deleting the account deletes them
