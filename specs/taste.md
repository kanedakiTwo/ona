# Afina tu menú (taste cards)

«Afina tu menú» (PRO-146, idea and design by Miguel, 2026-10-16): Mimoia shows catalogue recipes one at a time and the user says whether they fancy each one. Every answer is a **verdict** the weekly menu generator reads (D-061, smart menu). Design: artboards «Afina tu menú · prototipo» and «Cómo se ofrece tras el primer menú» (claude.ai artifact Ekcdm6AvCXNTk1vfWkNjk9).

## User Capabilities

- **`/afinar`** (full screen, no tab bar / Mimo button / «Opinión» pill; centred 480 px column on desktop): intro «Dime qué te *apetece*.» with the three gestures explained and «Empezar» («Puedes parar cuando quieras: lo guardo todo.»); `?empezar=1` skips the intro. **Its own way out** (2026-10-21, a friend of Miguel's in the installed app on Windows: «no sale menú de navegación para ir atrás o al menú principal»): a «‹ Perfil» link on top of every screen back to the page it was opened from («‹ Menú» from the menu or on a fresh load; `lib/navHistory.ts`), and from `md` the screen starts after the desktop sidebar, which stays usable. A recipe without a photo yet (or whose photo doesn't load) shows a bone page with the pot (`taste-card-placeholder`), never an empty box. Mouse clicks on the buttons and mouse drags of the card work as on the phone (`e2e/desktop-windows.spec.ts` at 1440 and 800 px)
- Cards fill the screen: photo, «tipo de plato · tiempo» (`Cuchara · 53 min`) and name, with the next card peeking behind. **Gestures**: drag right «Me apetece», left «No para mí», up «Me encanta»; the matching stamp (ME APETECE / NO PARA MÍ / ME ENCANTA) fades in while dragging (thresholds 100 px sideways, 110 px up). **Buttons** do the same, each with its word under it (PRO-200: ✕ «No», ♥ «Me encanta», ✓ «Me apetece»; `aria-label` «No para mí» / «Me encanta» / «Me apetece», keyboard reachable) plus «No la conozco · saltar». Top: «Ya está por hoy» and the counter of the **batch** «N de 10» (PRO-200; before, «1 de 101» over the catalogue and it never stopped) with a terracotta progress bar. After 10 answers (skips included) the summary comes up
- **«¿Qué es lo que no?»** opens with the first «No para mí» and then every 2 (1st, 3rd, 5th…): «No me gusta el cilantro» / «No me gustan las lentejas» (agreement and accents restored, `dislikeChipText` in `lib/tasteText.ts`) for the recipe's 3 main ingredients (by weight; basics such as salt, oil, garlic, onion, pepper, water, flour are never offered), «El plato en sí», «Es muy largo», a free field «¿Es otra cosa? Cuéntamelo» + «Enviar», and «Prefiero no decirlo». An ingredient goes to the memory's `dislikes` (so other recipes with it are avoided too); the free text is saved with the verdict and read like Mimo's `update_memory`: an ingredient → `dislikes`, «no tengo horno» → `equipment` (the list of what the user HAS: the stored list without it, or the usual kit without it when there was none), a home habit («a mediodía como fuera») → `prep_habits`, anything else → `notes` («Sobre <receta>: …»). Health data is never stored from here
- **Summary** «Tu menú ya te *conoce* un poco más.», at the end of each batch of 10 or on «Ya está por hoy», with what was rated **in this session**: «Saldrán más a menudo» (Me encanta), «Te apetecen», «No volverán a salir» (plus «todo lo que lleve X» for each ingredient picked), «Lo que me has contado» (the free texts); only the rows with something (PRO-200: no more three «Ninguna por ahora»), and «En esta vuelta no has valorado ninguna receta. Lo que valoraste otros días sigue guardado.» when the session has none. «Ver mi menú» and «Seguir con 10 más» (the counter starts again at «1 de 10»)
- **Which recipes**: the first 10 are a **starter deck** chosen to learn the most with few cards — cuchara, pescado, carne, verdura protagonista, horno, rápido, frito, largo, algo de fuera, algo de niños (`TASTE_STARTER_DECK` in `@ona/shared`; each row lists catalogue names, first one found wins, missing rows filled by variety). After that each batch of 10 is **chosen by Claude Haiku** from the answers so far: what we still don't know or doubt («¿pescado al horno sí y frito no?»), avoiding what is already clear. The page asks for it **in the background at card 5** of each batch, so there's no wait. If Claude fails, is slow (8 s) or has no key, the **variety order**: one recipe at a time, the most different from the previous card and from what was already seen, alternating kind of dish, protein and speed. A rated recipe (skips included) never comes back
- **Only what the household can eat** (PRO-197, beta 2026-10-17: a coeliac was shown pizza, ramen and espaguetis, a vegetarian cocido, bacalao and pollo, Carmen potato with «patata» vetoed): before any deck is built, the catalogue goes through the menu generator's own filter (`violatesRestrictions`, `services/dietaryRestrictions.ts`, see [Menus → Restrictions & allergies](./menus.md)) with the allergies, diets and free-text restrictions (profile + memory, `mergeRestrictions`) and the ingredient vetoes (`dislikes`) of **every member** of the household. The card also checks the recipe's **name** as one more ingredient (`breaksTasteRules`): «Bacalao con tomate» whose stored ingredients lost the cod is still fish to a vegetarian. Starter deck, Claude's pick, the variety order, the counter's total and the onboarding cards all read this filtered catalogue. A «No me gusta X» from «¿Qué es lo que no?» therefore also takes the other recipes with X out of the next cards. «Soy celíaco» / «soy vegetariana» told to Mimo now read as the diet («soy »/«somos »/«es » stripped in `restrictionKey`)
- **Session adaptation**: after 3 «No para mí» to meat (or fish) — a «No para mí» answered «Es muy largo» doesn't count (PRO-200, `nextStreak`) — — not necessarily adjacent cards — the session stops showing that group, drops it from the queue (refilling it) and flags it; «Lo que recuerdo de ti» then asks «¿No coméis carne? Si es así, añádelo en Restricciones o cuéntaselo a Mimo.»
- **In the sign-up** (`OnboardingFlow.tsx`): right after creating the account and **before** choosing WhatsApp or the web, so everyone does it, also whoever goes on through WhatsApp. Same intro, saying «Son 10 platos: dime si te apetecen. Así tu menú empieza a parecerse a tu casa, y después te hago unas preguntas.» (PRO-200: a newcomer didn't know whether these were already the questions), with «Saltar» under «Empezar»; 10 cards with «Saltar» in place of «Ya está por hoy» and a «N de 10» counter; then the channel choice / questions. Never blocks: «Saltar» goes straight on. Shown once (`POST /taste/offers {event:'onboarding'}`), a reload continues with the questions. The **three favourite dishes question is gone** (the web questions are now 5, with PRO-138's weekday one); `favorite_dishes` is no longer filled from the web and «Me encanta» carries the favourites' weight
- **Accounts that finished the sign-up before** (criterion: onboarded and no verdict at all): (a) once, before the menu, a full screen «Novedad · Afina tu menú en un *minuto*.» with «Empezar» (→ `/afinar?empezar=1`) and «Ahora no»; (b) after «Ahora no», the card «Afina tu menú en un minuto» above the menu (3 photos of the next cards, «Empezar» / «Ahora no») until the user rates a recipe or closes it with its «Ahora no», which removes it for good. Both stamped on `users.taste_state`, never repeated, on any device (`components/taste/TasteOffers.tsx`). While «Novedad» is up, the «Primeros pasos» guide waits and starts after it is answered, never on top of it (`app/menu/page.tsx`, e2e `afina-tu-menu.spec.ts`)
- Always in **Perfil → «Afina tu menú»** («N de 78 valoradas», over the catalogue that fits the household) and in **«Lo que recuerdo de ti»** (`/profile/memoria`): every verdict with a selector to change it and «Olvidar», the protein flags, and «Empezar»/«Seguir» to `/afinar`

## Aviso a los hogares afectados (PRO-205)

Before PRO-197 the cards ignored allergies and diets. Miguel (2026-10-18): an automatic notice, by WhatsApp to those who use it and in the app to the rest; nothing manual.
- **Who**: households with a verdict given before the fix and allergies, intolerances or a diet on some member (profile or memory `restrictions`; `dislikes` don't count). Computed on the server the first time this version runs against a database (`services/afinaNotice.ts`, scan under an advisory lock; the marker row's time is the cutoff), so it ships with the fix: staging gets it when the version reaches staging (where it can be checked), production when it's published, each **once**. Only a deployed API runs it (`isDeployedRuntime`), never CI or a laptop; `AFINA_NOTICE=off` stops it
- **WhatsApp**: Mimo sends Legal's text to a member with WhatsApp connected who accepts messages (the ones who rated first, then the owner), only inside the 24 h window (there's no approved template for it): with the window closed it goes the next time they write (checked every 10 minutes). Claimed before sending, so never twice; a Meta error or a closed window isn't counted as sent
- **App**: without WhatsApp, straight away; with WhatsApp, also from day 3 if it hasn't gone out. A card above the menu (`AfinaNoticeCard`, «Aviso · Un fallo en «Afina tu menú», ya arreglado», Legal's text, «Ir a mi perfil» and «Entendido») until anyone in the household closes it; once closed, no WhatsApp either
- Text (Legal, 2026-10-18; not to be changed without Legal) in `AFINA_NOTICE_TEXT`. The «sin cerdo» add-on isn't there: PRO-198 ships in the same version
- `GET /notices/afina` (`{ show, text }`), `POST /notices/afina/dismiss`, `GET /notices/afina/stats` (admin or metrics token: households, WhatsApp sent / pending, app only, in the app, closed; aggregates only) in `routes/notices.ts`; the API log prints the same line after each pass that changes something
- Doesn't go through PRO-215's weekly cap of Mimo's messages (built in parallel); to check when both land

## Menu generator

- A recipe with «No para mí» from **anyone in the household** never comes out in the automatic menu (weekly generator, «Cambiar al azar», added meal, regenerated dish: everything that reads `loadHouseholdRatingSets`); it can still be picked by hand. It beats a 5-star rating and another member's «Me encanta»
- «Me encanta» weighs like a favourite (2×); «Me apetece» gets a small extra (×1.5 on the recipe's rounded weight, weekly generator only); «No la conozco» changes nothing
- Mimo's `generate_weekly_menu` goes through the same generator, so it respects them; Mimo's single-dish swap (`skills.ts`) does not yet (see Known limitations)

## API

- `GET /taste/next?limit=10&exclude=<ids>&avoid=carne,pescado` → `{ cards: TasteCard[], source: 'starter'|'ai'|'variety', rated, total }`. `exclude` = cards already in the user's hands; `avoid` = groups dropped this session
- `POST /taste` `{ recipeId, verdict: 'love'|'like'|'nope'|'skip', reason?: { kind: 'ingredient'|'dish'|'long'|'other'|'none', text? } }` → upsert, idempotent per recipe (the last verdict wins; a reason is kept only on «nope»). Catalogue recipes only (404 otherwise). Returns the memory facts written. When the user's verdict count reaches a multiple of 10 (a round ended, `tasteRoundEnded`), a pass of the ficha's refiner at the moment runs in the background (PRO-320, at most one every 20 minutes per household; see [Ficha del hogar → At the moment and every week](./household-profile.md))
- `DELETE /taste/:recipeId` · `GET /taste/verdicts` (name, photo, verdict, reason) · `GET /taste/status` (`rated`, `total`, `showIntro`, `showCard`, `offeredInOnboarding`, `flags`) · `POST /taste/offers { event: 'intro_start'|'intro_later'|'card_dismiss'|'onboarding' }` · `POST /taste/flags { group }`
- All per-route auth (`routes/taste.ts`)

## Data

- `recipe_verdicts` (migration `0060_recipe_verdicts`): `user_id` (cascade), `recipe_id` (cascade), `verdict` (check `love|like|nope|skip`), `reason` jsonb, `created_at`, `updated_at`; unique `(user_id, recipe_id)`
- `household_notices` (migration `0074_household_notices`, PRO-205): `key`, `household_id` (cascade; null = the key's scan marker), `user_id` (WhatsApp recipient, set null), `created_at`, `whatsapp_sent_at`, `app_since`, `dismissed_at`; unique `(key, household_id)`
- `users.taste_state` jsonb (same migration, default `{}`): `introAt`, `introChoice`, `cardDismissedAt`, `onboardingAt`, `whatsappOfferedAt` (reserved), `flags`
- Cost: both Claude calls (`taste_next`, `taste_reason`, Claude Opus 5.5 at effort low, `CLAUDE_MODEL`, 15 s limit) are billed to the household in `cost_events` like every paid call

## Constraints

- Only catalogue recipes (author null, not retired) that fit the household (above) are shown; any catalogue recipe is accepted by `POST /taste`. The counter's total is that filtered count (78 with no restriction), and only verdicts on recipes still in it count as «valoradas»
- Claude only orders recipes from the candidate list it's given (numbers out of range or repeated are dropped, the batch is filled by variety); its free-text reading keeps only `dislikes`, `equipment`, `prep_habits`, `notes`, never health keys
- Writes to memory take confidence 1 (the user said it) and keep a fact's `manual` source
- The e2e helpers mark the sign-up step as shown (`skipTasteOffer`), which also turns «Novedad» off; `{ keepTaste: true }` keeps both

## Known limitations

- In the **sign-up**, the 10 cards come right after creating the account and **before** the allergies question, so a new user's first deck can't know their restrictions yet (only what Mimo or a household member already stored). Their verdicts never put a forbidden dish on the menu (the generator filters it), but they can see it
- Verdicts given **before** PRO-197 to recipes that break a restriction stay in `recipe_verdicts` (they're hidden from the deck and the counter but still listed in «Lo que recuerdo de ti»); the menu never serves them anyway

- «A Vera no»: a verdict is per account, not per person of the household; that waits for the per-person ficha (D-064)
- A «¿No coméis pescado?» flag stays in «Lo que recuerdo de ti» even after the household says it does eat fish; it can't be removed there yet
- Mimo doesn't mention it on WhatsApp yet (the one-time mention with the `/afinar` link to WhatsApp-first users, `whatsappOfferedAt`), nor reads the verdicts in its single-dish swap, (its WhatsApp sign-up message dropped «tres platos que os gusten mucho» with PRO-245, 2026-10-19): those live in `assistant/skills.ts`, `systemPrompt.ts` and `whatsapp/inbound.ts`, which were closed for this change
- «Me apetece» weighs only in the weekly generator, not in the single-slot pickers
- «Deseos del catálogo» (PRO-154) took its `alta` source from the sign-up's favourite dishes: with the question gone on the web and on WhatsApp (PRO-245), only dishes a person tells Mimo unprompted during the WhatsApp sign-up still reach it. A «Me encanta» is always on a catalogue recipe, so it adds no wish
- The starter deck was drafted by Producto from the catalogue; Cocina should review it (`TASTE_STARTER_DECK`)
