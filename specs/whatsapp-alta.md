# WhatsApp · Empezar por WhatsApp

> Parte de [WhatsApp](./whatsapp.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): escribir al número sin cuenta, los primeros pasos en el chat, quién hace la compra, el código de enlace, emojis y «gracias» sin respuesta, «hazme la compra», el único recordatorio y el QR.

## User Capabilities

- **WhatsApp-first:** anyone can just write to our number. An unlinked number gets "Hola, soy Mimo, el asistente de cocina de Mimoia… conecta tu cuenta: <web>/whatsapp/conectar · Entra (o crea tu cuenta) y te daré un código para enviarme desde este chat", at most once an hour per number. The page asks to log in or register (with `?next=` back). Then it mints a one-time code and shows "Último paso: envía este mensaje a Mimo desde tu WhatsApp" (eyebrow "Mimo en WhatsApp") with an "Enviar desde WhatsApp" `wa.me` button (message prefilled; on iPhone/iPad every WhatsApp button — here, in onboarding «Por WhatsApp» and in Profile — opens the app by its `whatsapp://send` scheme and goes to the `wa.me` page only if the app didn't open within 1.5 s, because outside Safari — another app's browser, Mimoia on the home screen — `wa.me` lands on WhatsApp's «Download» page, 2026-11-23; `WhatsAppLink`, `lib/whatsappOpen.ts`), polling until linked → "¡Listo!" + "Volver a WhatsApp". The link carries **no token**: the phone that gets linked is always the one that sends the code, so forwarding the link to someone else can't attach a phone to the wrong account. A first version used a phone-bound token and a "¿Conectar este número?" confirm page; the 2026-10-06 review showed a forwarded link plus a spoofed profile name could hijack an account, so it was replaced before shipping.
- **First steps in the chat (WhatsApp-first sign-up, 2026-10-10; four questions since 2026-11-19).** An account that links **before** finishing onboarding (from `/onboarding` → «Por WhatsApp», or from `/whatsapp/conectar`) gets, in Miguel's words (2026-10-15), «¡Hola, X! Ya estás conectado: soy Mimo, de Mimoia. Soy un asistente de inteligencia artificial (IA) especializado en cocina: puedo equivocarme y no sustituyo a un profesional sanitario, pero puedo encargarme de las tareas rutinarias para que cocinar vuelva a ser divertido. Puedes escribirme o mandarme notas de voz, como te sea más cómodo. Para entrar en calor te propongo preparar tu primer menú en un momento: solo necesito saber cuatro cosas. *1.* ¿Quiénes sois en casa? Dime adultos y niños de 2 a 10 años. *2.* ¿Qué comidas cocináis en casa a la semana? Por ejemplo: «6 cenas y 1 comida». *3.* Tres platos que hacéis a menudo. *4.* ¿Hay algo que no entre en casa? Un alimento o un plato que no os guste.» (outbound kind `onboarding`; the disclosure keeps its three parts, AI Act art. 50; the message lists exactly `WHATSAPP_ONBOARDING_QUESTIONS` and announces as many, PRO-245). While `users.onboardingDone` is false every turn runs with `WHATSAPP_ONBOARDING_PROMPT`: Mimo asks, one at a time and without buttons, only what's missing of the first two (the dishes and what doesn't go in are optional, never insisted on); never the priority nor who does the shopping (a `shopper` said unprompted is still kept, PRO-383); never health data (the link comes later). **Everything goes where the menu reads it from day one** (`complete_onboarding`, `services/signupFirstMenu.ts`): who they are → `saveOnboarding` like the web; which meals they cook → `lunches` / `dinners` (0–7 a week) → the household's «semana tipo» (`off` on the meals they don't cook: N lunches from the weekend back, N dinners from Monday on with Saturday last; «6 cenas y 1 comida» = Sunday lunch and every dinner but Saturday) and how often they cook (`cookingStyle` / `cookingFreq` only if that's what they said); the dishes they make often → `favoriteDishes` **and into the first menu for sure**: the recipe whose name has every word of the dish (theirs first, then the catalogue's) goes in a main dish of a lunch or dinner (one a day, dinner first, from the first meal), and a dish with no recipe goes in as a note that Mimo turns into the household's recipe in the same turn (`swap_meal` with `newRecipe`; `swap_meal` is in the sign-up's tools); a dish whose recipe has what someone at home can't eat is left out and Mimo says so (PRO-1041, Constraints); what doesn't go in → the `dislikes` (families included). **The first menu starts with the next meal that hasn't gone by** (`firstMenuWeeks(now, { fromNextMeal: true })`, Madrid: from 21:00 it starts tomorrow; before, today's meals past their cut-off — breakfast 10:00, lunch 15:00, merienda 18:00 — stay empty, so a 15:00 sign-up starts with tonight's dinner), Friday to Sunday plus the whole next week (PRO-65/244); the tool tells Mimo where it starts and not to talk about meals already gone. **One message to close the sign-up (2026-11-19):** the week's picture with Mimo's one line (one or two of their dishes) and at most one question, without buttons (plus one line with the link when they told an allergy, PRO-1041); no «¿Cambio algo?», no snowball line, no «Hecho con Mimo» in that caption; the reply never says the menu «se parece poco», «no me he acercado», «genérico» or «acertaré menos» (no fit line on the first menu; `signupReply.ts` drops such a sentence and any question but the last). With no dish said, Mimo's question is «¿Qué soléis cenar un martes normal?». **~10 minutes later, one at a time** (`signupFollowUps.ts`, from the scheduler tick every 5 min under its lock, read back from the outbound kinds so a restart loses nothing): the web link for allergies/intolerances of anyone at home («Si en casa hay alergias, intolerancias o algo que no podáis o queráis comer, añádelo aquí con tu permiso… Por WhatsApp no me dejan guardar datos de salud.», `/profile`, kind `health_link`) and, after 5 quiet minutes, the opt-in question («¿Quieres que también te escriba yo? No escribo mucho, solo lo importante: un aviso antes de cocinar (congelados, tiempos) y la lista de la compra los sábados. Si quieres el menú cada mañana, pídemelo. Puedes cambiarlo cuando quieras simplemente pidiéndomelo.», kind `optin_prompt`, two buttons). Never while the conversation is going (a message in or out in the last 5 min), never 22:30–08:00, never after BAJA or ALTA, only within 20 h of the sign-up. **Last message (PRO-07):** once the person answers that question («Sí, avísame»/ALTA or «No, gracias»), Mimo sends — right after the confirmation of the answer (PRO-248: «Hecho: te enviaré avisos…» or «Vale, no te escribiré…») — «Te cuento ejemplos de cosas que puedes pedirme ya: 📸 Mándame una foto o un enlace de una receta, la proceso y la guardo en tus recetas. 🍽️ «¿Qué cenamos hoy?» 🛒 «Hazme la lista de la compra»» and «Y cuando quieras, vuelve a Mimoia en la web: allí tienes tu menú de la semana y un recorrido rápido por todo lo demás. <web>/menu» (`COPY.firstSteps(url)`, written from its guide since PRO-156; outbound kind `first_steps`). Only for numbers that did the first steps in the chat (an `onboarding` message went out) and got the opt-in question, and only once per number; accounts onboarded on the web already get these ideas in «¡Listo!». If the person never answers the question, the suggestions aren't sent. Legacy accounts (`onboardingDone` null) are treated as onboarded. Tests: `whatsappOnboarding.test.ts`, `firstMenuWhatsApp.test.ts`, `signupFirstMenu.test.ts`, `signupFollowUps.test.ts`, `firstMenuWeeks.test.ts`, `whatsappInbound.test.ts`; live case `alta-primer-menu-con-sus-platos` (`primer-menu`) in `mimoEvalCases.ts`.
- **Buttons are not repeated (PRO-141, 2026-10-15).** Every outbound body records the buttons it carried (`⟦botones: A | B | C⟧`, `outboundBody` in `render.ts`; the model sees it in the history). A reply whose buttons are exactly the last ones Mimo sent goes out as plain text (`lastSentButtons` + `dropRepeatedButtons`): in the sign-up the dishes question came with the «Casi a diario / 3-4 días / Poco» buttons of the previous one. The sign-up prompt asks its follow-up questions without buttons; only «¿Quién hace la compra?» («Yo · Otra persona de casa · Nos turnamos», PRO-383) and the optional favourite-dishes question («Mejor luego») have them.
- **«¿Quién hace la compra?» in the WhatsApp sign-up (PRO-383, D-158, D-051).** Only when the household said 2 adults or more: once Mimo has how many they are and how they cook (and before the favourite dishes, when those are asked), it asks «¿Quién hace la compra?» with the buttons «Yo» · «Otra persona de casa» · «Nos turnamos» (`WHATSAPP_ONBOARDING_PROMPT`) and passes the answer to `complete_onboarding` as `shopper` (`me` · `other` · `shared`; optional, never required; with 1 adult it isn't asked and the tool drops it). `saveOnboarding` keeps it as the household's `shopper` fact (origin onboarding, evidence `whatsapp`) and records `shopper_answered` with channel `whatsapp`. With «Otra persona de casa» or «Nos turnamos» the tool also creates the household invitation from the sign-up (`services/shopperInvite.ts`: `source: alta`, sent at once, so it counts in `familyInvites.bySource.alta`; the `alta` offer is stamped) and its summary carries the web's message, «Te he metido en nuestra casa de Mimoia: ahí tendrás el menú y la lista de la compra de cada semana. Entra aquí: <enlace>», which Mimo puts in its reply as is, in a block of its own, to forward. Nothing is created when the account can't invite (not the owner, or 2 accounts already); a failure leaves the sign-up done without it. Tests: `firstMenuWhatsApp.test.ts` (the tool), `whatsappOnboarding.test.ts` (the prompt and the parameter), `onboardingShopper.smoke.ts` (the invitation in Postgres).
- **A number that is already linked sends a link code** (2026-10-15; before, the code went to Mimo, who answered «No puedo vincular cuentas desde el chat»). Only a clear link message counts (`isLinkAttempt`: «Vincular Mimoia: …» or just the code); anything else goes to Mimo. Its own account's code → «Este WhatsApp ya está conectado a tu cuenta de Mimoia: no hace falta ningún código» (the link, its avisos and prefs untouched). Another account's valid code → the number **moves** to that account («Este WhatsApp estaba conectado a otra cuenta de Mimoia. Desde ahora está conectado a la cuenta de la que has sacado el código.»), then the usual first steps or «¡Listo!» + opt-in; sending the code from the phone proves both ends. An invalid or expired code → «Ese código no es válido o ha caducado (duran 10 minutos). Este WhatsApp sigue conectado a tu cuenta de siempre…». No model call in any of these.
- Users can also connect from Ajustes (`/profile/ajustes`, behind the gear of Perfil, PRO-1202) → card "WhatsApp" (status line "Conectado" / "Sin conectar") → "Conectar WhatsApp". This mints a one-time code, shown as "Vincular Mimoia: 4F7K2A" (only the code matters: a message prefilled before the rename, with the old name instead of Mimoia, still links), and an "Abrir WhatsApp" `wa.me` link with that message prefilled. When the user sends it from their phone, the account is linked; the card polls every 3 s and flips to "WhatsApp conectado".
- Linked users see their masked number (`+34 ••• ••• 222`), an "Abrir chat con Mimo" link, an "Avisos por WhatsApp" toggle (see Proactive messages) and "Desconectar", which asks for confirmation first.

- **Emoji reactions and messages that are only emoji get no answer** (Miguel, 2026-11-18). This covers a ❤️ or 👍 reaction to one of Mimo's messages, or a text such as «👍», «😂😂» or «🙏🏻» with no letter or digit. They are kept as ignored inbound rows (body «[reacción ❤️]», error `emoji`), so the daily review still sees them, but they never reach Mimo or the chat history and get no «escribiendo…». Before this, a reaction got «todavía no entiendo ese tipo de mensaje» (6 times from 20-oct to 17-nov). Text with an emoji in it («Ok 👍») is answered as usual. **Except when a typed emoji is an answer** (2026-11-20, reviewer: a 👍🏻 to «¿qué tal la cena?» was dropped): a typed 👍 / 👎 (any skin tone, repeated or with «!») while the dinner check-in is Mimo's last message, in the last 30 h and not yet answered, counts as that button and is recorded without the model (`checkinReplyIdOfEmoji`, `dinnerCheckinPending` in `checkinButtons.ts`); any other emoji right after Mimo asked something or sent buttons (≤ 30 min, `awaitsAnswer`) goes to Mimo, like a «vale». A reaction never answers (`isSilentEmoji` in `inbound.ts`; tests in `whatsappInbound.test.ts` and `whatsappWebhook.test.ts`).
- **Thanks and acknowledgements get no answer either** (2026-11-18): a message that is only «gracias», «muchas gracias», «vale», «ok», «genial», «perfecto», «muy bien», «de acuerdo»… (any mix, with emoji or punctuation; `isCourtesyOnly`) never reaches the model and costs nothing: ignored row (error `courtesy`), no reply, but it still opens Meta's 24 h window. **Except** when Mimo's last message, in the last 30 min, ends in a question or has buttons waiting (`awaitsAnswer`): then «vale» / «ok» is a «sí» and goes to Mimo as usual. When a thanks does reach Mimo (with more words), the WhatsApp prompt says to answer in a few words and not open another offer («si quieres…, pídemelo») nor ask anything new.
- **"Hazme la compra"** → one short link per shop (`<WEB_PUBLIC_URL>/c/<token>`) that opens the user's own WhatsApp chat with that shop and the order already written; the user sends it. Forwarding (or pasting) the shop's answer back to Mimo gets a line-by-line check and reply buttons to approve, then a second link with the confirmation. Our number never writes to a shop. See [Compra en mis tiendas](./shop-orders.md). These `/c/` links are the only URLs the model may write in a reply, and only the ones returned **this turn**: asking for the shopping again re-runs `prepare_shop_orders` (2026-10-08: "dame la compra otra vez" re-sent the morning's links from history, so shops got the pre-v1.1 message).
- **The one reminder (PRO-107, Miguel 2026-10-15: «solo dos veces en total»).** Whoever didn't connect WhatsApp is offered it in the sign-up and **once** more: a card on `/menu` (above the week), «¿Te lo mando por WhatsApp? Le escribes a Mimo «¿qué cenamos?» y te contesta con tu menú.» → «Conectar WhatsApp» (`/whatsapp/conectar`) / «Ahora no». It shows when WhatsApp is available, not connected, and the household has generated a menu and opened the shopping list (its first week resolved, from `usage_events`). Showing it stamps `users.whatsapp_nudge_at`, so it never comes back — after «Ahora no», a reload or on another device. Nothing in «Primeros pasos» and no other reminders; the Perfil card stays as it is.
- **QR on a computer (PRO-123, D-052).** Where the code is already offered — `/onboarding` «Por WhatsApp» and Perfil → WhatsApp → «Conectar WhatsApp» — screens `lg+` also show a QR of the **same `wa.me` link** as the button (code already written), «¿Estás en el ordenador? Escanea este código con la cámara del móvil…» (`components/whatsapp/WhatsAppQr.tsx`, drawn locally with `qrcode`). Not on phones. It is not another offer: it only goes where WhatsApp is already offered. e2e `whatsapp-qr.spec.ts` (1440 and 390).
