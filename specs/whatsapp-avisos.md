**Written from a guide, not a fixed text** (PRO-156, D-065; `services/whatsapp/guided.ts`). The texts in the table above are the **safety net**; normally each of those messages (all but `prep_alerts`) and the end of the WhatsApp sign-up (`first_steps`, see *First steps in the chat* above) is written each time by a writer, Claude Haiku 4.5:
- **Guide per kind** (`MESSAGE_GUIDES`): what it is for, what it must carry (dishes, hours, links; buttons stay in code), tone and maximum length; plus fixed rules (`COMMON_RULES`): only cooking, menu and shopping; nothing about health or nutrition; never «no puedo»; invent nothing; don't repeat earlier messages; Spanish from Spain (no «¿te late?», «ahorita»… — PRO-274), no filler openers («¡Ey!», «¡Al lío!») and articles, adjectives and verbs that agree with each dish. The planner adds the facts and the literal strings each message must contain (`dailyBriefGuide`, `cookingReminderGuide`…), and for every dish its gender and number (`whatsapp/dishAgreement.ts`, a heuristic on the head noun: «Costillas a la riojana» → femenino plural, «Sándwich vegetal» → masculino singular; unknown → nothing, the writer talks about «la cena»).
- **Context:** the household's memory (`user_memories` without health keys or meal times; the household's ficha is not in these guided messages yet — the conversation with Mimo does read it, PRO-253), the last 12 h of chat, the last 5 messages of the same kind to that number and (PRO-274) the openings of the last 3 avisos of any kind to that number.
- **Checked in code before sending** (`validateGuided`): every required string present, within the length, no banned word (salud, sano, calorías, dieta, proteínas, médico, alergias, «no puedo»…), no §6 health claim or body-effect talk, no link that isn't in the fixed text, a question where the buttons answer one, no sentence (4+ words) already sent in those last messages of the kind, no Latin-American expression (PRO-274), not the same opening (first clause, two words) as the last aviso to that number (`openingOf`, reason `same_opening`), and no article or «listo/lista…» before a dish that doesn't agree with it («la Costillas…», reason `agreement`). An AI disclosure (`AI_DISCLOSURE*`) in the fixed text must appear literally, and no other «IA» mention is allowed.
- **With the monthly cap used up** (`guidedOverBudget`), the avisos whose buttons only Mimo can answer — the Sunday nudge and the freezer review (`BUTTONS_NEED_MIMO`) — don't go (a tap used to get «Has alcanzado tu límite»); the rest go, the check-in with its buttons (they work without Mimo).
- **Fixed text** when there is no API key, the writer fails or takes more than 8 s, the text fails validation, or the user is over the chat's € budget or the monthly AI cap (`guidedOverBudget`). Each outbound row stores `meta.guided` = `guide` | `fallback` (+ `guidedReason`).
- Outside the 24 h window the Meta template is fixed, but its `{{1}}` is this same written text, flattened.
- **Cost:** one Claude Opus 5.5 call per message at effort low (`CLAUDE_MODEL`, PRO-232; time limit 15 s), billed to the user as `whatsapp_guided` in the cost ledger, so it counts against the monthly cap. Replay: `scripts/replayGuidedBrief.ts` writes five mornings of the same household with the real model and fails if any sentence repeats.

- **Unanswered avisos pause themselves — all at once (PRO-1303, `whatsapp/fatigue.ts`).** One count for every aviso (cooking, dinner, shopping, Sunday, morning menu, freezer review and the prep alerts, stored as `alert`): when **3 in a row** went out with no sign of life after them — no WhatsApp message, no button, no use of the app (any web usage event) — the next moment doesn't go; instead Mimo asks once «He dejado en pausa mis avisos: los últimos se quedaron sin respuesta. ¿Te sigo avisando?» [Sí] [No] (outbound kind `pausa:todos`; outside the 24 h window by the template of the aviso it replaces, with the buttons as text). From then on **every aviso waits**, prep alerts too (their rows become `cancelled`, «cancelado al enviar: avisos en pausa», no push fallback), until the person answers or writes anything on WhatsApp: any inbound message lifts the pause, and the count starts again from it. The buttons are answered without the model: «Sí» → «Hecho, te sigo avisando.»; «No» also switches every aviso off in Perfil → Avisos («Vale, no te mando más esos avisos. Si cambias de idea, los activas en Perfil → Avisos.»). Opening the app resets the count but doesn't lift a pause already asked. The question doesn't spend the weekly budget. Before PRO-1303 (19-nov) it was 5 of one kind and only that kind paused, so a silent household got 15–20 messages; the buttons of those old questions (`avisos:si|no:<kind>`) still switch their kind. **Households not cooking from the menu:** no «hora de cocinar» nor «¿qué tal la cena?» when nobody in the household opened this week's menu in the app (`screen_viewed` menu or `autoplan_viewed` since Monday) and nobody wrote to Mimo in 14 days (WhatsApp or the app's chat; `isDormant`). Tests: `avisosPausa.test.ts`, `whatsappInbound.test.ts`.
- **In the person's language (2026-11-19, `whatsapp/language.ts`).** A household that writes to Mimo in English gets its avisos in English. Until users have a language of their own (D-111, not merged yet), it is read from the person's last 15 messages to Mimo (only those of 3+ words; English needs a clear lead, else Spanish: `detectLanguage`). The check-in, «hora de cocinar», the shopping reminder, the pause question, the BAJA question and the fixed confirmations have an English text; every aviso's buttons get English titles (`EN_BUTTON_TITLES`); the guided writer writes in English (`guideInLanguage`: it keeps only the data it must carry — links, numbers, dish names). An aviso without an English text that falls back to its fixed text (no writer, over the cap) goes in Spanish, and outside the 24 h window the template's own words stay Spanish.
- **Hours (PRO-236):** nothing goes out before 08:00 or after 22:30 (Madrid); the shopping reminder and the Sunday nudge go to one person per household (owner first). See [Notifications](./notifications.md) → «Horario y avisos del hogar».
- **Weekly budget (PRO-215):** at most 4 messages per household and week, with priorities and room reserved for prep alerts and cooking reminders; the avisos of one tick go out as one message. See [Notifications](./notifications.md) → «Tope semanal de avisos».
- **Cooldowns:** 20 h per kind; 3 days for the Sunday nudge and the shopping reminder. They are stored as outbound rows (and, since PRO-215, `notice_candidates` rows, so a merged or dropped aviso is in its cooldown too), so the 5-minute scheduler tick is idempotent. Proactive messages land in chat history, so a short reply ("sí") makes sense to the assistant.
- **Switches:**
  - **Master:** `whatsapp_links.notify`, the profile toggle. **Opt-in**: a new link starts with `notify = false`, and right after "¡Listo!" ("Tu WhatsApp ya está conectado con Mimoia…") Mimo asks "¿Quieres que también te escriba yo?…" with [Sí, avísame] [No, gracias] (outbound kind `optin_prompt`). Links made before 2026-10-07 kept their setting.
    **The answer (PRO-248, 2026-10-19).** The confirmation always goes out, also at the end of the WhatsApp sign-up (before the three suggestions): the first «sí» says «Hecho: te enviaré avisos…» (`COPY.startedFirst`: no `optin` or `optout` sent before), a later ALTA «Hecho: vuelvo a enviarte avisos…»; «No, gracias» gets «Vale, no te escribiré por mi cuenta…». A «No, gracias» or a BAJA is recorded in `whatsapp_links.opt_in_declined_at` (migration `0082_whatsapp_opt_in_answer`; older answers are read from the messages, `optInDeclinedSql`). **One extra offer:** whoever was asked and never answered — never whoever said no — hears it one more time, in one line, once their first menu is at least 7 days old (`REOFFER_AFTER_DAYS`) and the household has opened the shopping list: on WhatsApp after Mimo's reply to their next message (`COPY.optInReoffer`, same two buttons, kind `optin_prompt`), or in the web chat as the last line of Mimo's reply (`REOFFER_WEB`; «sí, avísame» → `set_whatsapp_notifications`), whichever comes first. `claimOptInReoffer` (`services/whatsapp/optInOffer.ts`) checks and records it in one UPDATE (`opt_in_reoffered_at`), so it happens once across both channels; never during the sign-up. Tests: `whatsappInbound.test.ts`, `optInOffer.test.ts`, `optInOffer.smoke.ts`.
  - **BAJA / ALTA** (see *Control words*) flip the master switch from the chat.
  - **Per kind:** `whatsapp_links.prefs` (missing key = on, except `daily_brief`: missing = off), migration `0031_whatsapp_prefs.sql`.
  - Users change them in **Perfil → Avisos** or **by chat** via the `set_alerts` skill (PRO-237; `get_alerts` lists them) or `set_whatsapp_notifications`, e.g. "no me mandes el resumen de la mañana ni el recordatorio de la compra" — see [Notifications → Avisos](./notifications.md#avisos-perfil--avisos). The switch also lives in `users.alert_prefs`; a kind is off if either says off. `PATCH /whatsapp/link` accepts `{ notify?, prefs? }` (prefs through the same code), and `GET /whatsapp/status` returns `prefs`.
- Everything runs inside the existing 5-minute `notificationScheduler` tick (`runProactiveTick`). It's a no-op when WhatsApp isn't configured, and DB work only happens while one of the time windows is open.

Delivery follows Meta's 24 h customer-service window, measured from `last_inbound_at` with a 30-min safety margin:
- **Inside the window:** free-form messages with buttons.
- **Outside the window:** the approved template `WHATSAPP_TEMPLATE_NAME` (language `WHATSAPP_TEMPLATE_LANG`, default `es`), whose single `{{1}}` body variable carries the message flattened to one line. Buttons are folded in as "Responde: Sí / No." ("Reply: Yes / No." for a household that writes in English, since 2026-11-21), because Meta rejects newlines in template parameters.
- **Outside the window with no template, or a Meta error (PRO-234, D-080):** the same message goes out once as a **phone notification** (Web Push, title «Mimo», links and bold removed, opens `/menu` or `/shopping`) if the person turned those on; recorded in `notification_schedule` as `fallback:<kind>:<userId>:<day>`, so it counts as that kind's send for the day. With no phone notification either, it stays as not sent (`failed`) and isn't retried that day. See [Notifications → Channels](./notifications.md#channels-one-alert-one-channel).

## Who cooks today («hoy cocina Pablo»)

PRO-230 (D-069), only when asked — nothing new in the app. In the web chat or on WhatsApp, «hoy cocina Pablo, mándale la receta» (or «el jueves cocina Ana») → Mimo's `set_cook_today { name, day?, meal? }` → `POST /households/me/cook-today` (`services/cookToday.ts`):
- finds that person among the household's adults (first name, accents and case ignored); refuses when alone at home, for an unknown name (lists who is there), for oneself, or when that meal has no recipe;
- **with WhatsApp linked and avisos on**, sends them (outbound kind `cook_today`, 24 h window or template like any proactive message) «Hoy cocinas tú la cena (te lo pide <quien>): → <receta>: <enlace> · Con antelación: - <preparativo> (a las HH:MM) · Te aviso yo a esa hora.»; records the cook in `cook_of_day` (migration `0080_cook_of_day`, one per household and Madrid day) and moves that day's pending prep alerts to them. Alerts enqueued later for that day are theirs too (`cooksOfWeek` in `enqueuePrepAlertsForMenu`). The requester no longer gets them;
- **without WhatsApp** (or avisos off, or the message can't go out), nothing is sent or moved: Mimo says why and gives a `https://wa.me/?text=…` link with the same text to forward it.
- Meal: the one asked, else dinner, else lunch. Tests: `cookToday.test.ts` (two-person household, with and without WhatsApp, the skill), `cookToday.smoke.ts` (alerts moved and re-enqueued to the cook).

## Pregunta única por WhatsApp (Admin)

PRO-1091 (CS-500; Miguel, 2026-11-02: option A, «CS lo volverá a necesitar»). Customer Success asks a group of households **one** question by WhatsApp, from Admin → «Pregunta por WhatsApp» (`/admin/pregunta-whatsapp`, see [Admin Dashboard](./admin-dashboard.md)):
- **The question** is the `{{1}}` of the approved template of kind **`pregunta_unica`** in `WHATSAPP_TEMPLATES` (one `{{1}}`, flattened to one line, 10–900 characters). Always by that template, inside or outside the 24 h window; never the generic `WHATSAPP_TEMPLATE_NAME`. Without it (or without WhatsApp configured) the screen says so and nothing goes out. The template is registered in Miguel's WhatsApp Manager as *Utility* (if Meta wants it as *Marketing*, ask before).
- **The group:** households created between two Madrid days (both included, `households.created_at`) and with no activity for N days (0 = any; activity = something the household did: its `usage_events` except the passive ones (`PASSIVE_USAGE_EVENTS`: the week Mimo makes by itself, opening a screen…) or any member's last WhatsApp message; never active counts as inactive. Until 24-nov the autopilot's weekly menu counted as activity, so almost nobody looked asleep).
- **At most one survey a week (2026-11-19):** a household that got another survey in the last 7 days (the leftovers question, the Sean Ellis survey or another «pregunta única», `surveyCap.ts`) is left out and counted as «recibió otra encuesta esta semana» (`encuesta_semana`).
- **One person per household:** the owner, else the member who joined first, with WhatsApp linked — not suspended, not on the Gratis plan, inside `WHATSAPP_ALLOWED_EMAILS`, and who didn't ask **BAJA** or answer «No, gracias» to the avisos (`notify = false` and `optInDeclinedSql`; whoever said ALTA later is in). If every linked member said BAJA, the household is out.
- **Out:** households with an **internal account** (role `admin` or an email in `ADMIN_EMAILS`) or a **test household** (any member's email in a reserved test domain — `example.com/.org/.net`, `*.test`, `*.example`, `*.invalid`, `*.localhost`, `*.local` — or in `EMAIL_SINK_DOMAINS`; the test households of D-037 use `example.com`).
- **The count before sending:** «Contar hogares» → `POST /admin/whatsapp-question/preview` → «N hogares la recibirán» and why the rest are out («Fuera: 2 pidieron BAJA · 1 cuenta interna · 5 hogares de prueba · 4 sin WhatsApp · 3 ya la recibieron»). Editing the question or the group asks for a new count. «Enviar a N hogares» → a browser confirmation → `POST /admin/whatsapp-question/send` with `expectedCount`: if the count is no longer N, **409** and nothing is sent. Audited `whatsapp_question.send`.
- **Once per household:** `whatsapp_question_sends` has one row per question and household (unique index), claimed **before** each send, so a second press, the same text again or two admins at once never send it twice. A failed send stays `failed` and is not retried. Another question (another text) can go to the same households. At most 200 per press (Meta: 250 business-initiated conversations a day, avisos included); the rest go with the next press.
- Each send is stored as an outbound message (kind `pregunta_unica`, in the chat history so Mimo knows what was asked) and billed as a template (`meta_whatsapp/utility_template`).
- **The answers → Opiniones:** the person's next WhatsApp message after it (within 14 days, with nothing else from Mimo in between; BAJA never counts, it's a control word) is saved verbatim in `user_feedback` (channel `whatsapp`, tag `pregunta_unica`), once per send, and still reaches Mimo as usual (`recordQuestionAnswer`, called from the engine like the leftovers answer). Admin → Opiniones labels it «Pregunta única por WhatsApp»; the screen lists the questions sent with households, failures and answers.
- Code: `services/whatsapp/oneOffQuestion.ts` (pure `planQuestion` + DB store), `routes/whatsappQuestion.ts`; migration `0102_whatsapp_questions` (two new tables). Tests: `oneOffQuestion.test.ts` (the count, never twice, BAJA/internal/test out, count changed, no template), `oneOffQuestion.smoke.ts` (the real SQL, the unique index, the answer in Opiniones), e2e `admin-whatsapp-question.spec.ts`.
