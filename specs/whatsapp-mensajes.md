# WhatsApp · Cómo se tratan los mensajes y el revisor de conversaciones

> Parte de [WhatsApp](./whatsapp.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): el manejo de cada mensaje que llega y el agente que revisa las conversaciones cada día.

## Message handling

1. `POST /whatsapp/webhook` verifies `X-Hub-Signature-256`, an HMAC-SHA256 of the **raw** body keyed with `WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET`. That's why the router is mounted before `express.json()`. A bad or missing signature gets **401**.
2. Every message is inserted into `whatsapp_messages` with `ON CONFLICT (wamid) DO NOTHING`, so Meta's retries are deduplicated. Each new message is queued **right after its insert**. If one insert fails, the response is **500**: Meta retries the batch, the already-stored messages dedupe, and they are already queued. Otherwise **200**. The assistant runs after the response.
3. Messages are processed **in order per phone**, using an in-process promise chain (Railway runs one API instance). **Messages sent one after another are one turn (2026-11-19, `coalesce.ts`):** a plain text waits 5 s (20 s at most from the first of a burst; «escribiendo…» shows right away); the texts that arrive meanwhile from the same number go to Mimo together, in order, as one message («2 adultos» + «Casi a diario»). The joined ones are marked `ignored` / `merged` (history reads the joined text from the last one's row). A control word (BAJA, ALTA, «humano»), a link code, a tapped button, a photo, a voice note or a document is never joined: the texts waiting before it go first as their own turn, then it alone. In process like the queue: a message waiting when the process dies stays `received` (as one in the queue did before). **What arrives while a turn of that number is running waits for it (PRO-1295, 2026-11-24):** the texts keep joining past the 5 s and the 20 s cap, and go as one turn once Mimo has answered (`busy` / `idle` in `coalesce.ts`, from the per-phone queue), so they never cross its reply and a «sí» / «no» among them is read against what Mimo just asked (see «Each answer goes to its own question»); a text still in its 5 s when the turn ends keeps its own wait; a button, photo or voice note that comes meanwhile lets the held texts go first.
4. Each message is marked read with a "typing…" indicator (best effort).
5. Gates, in order:
   - **Control words** run first, before every other gate, and never reach the model (`commands.ts`, whole-message match only, so "para la cena pon lentejas" is a request). **BAJA / STOP / PARAR / "no quiero más avisos"** → `notify = false` + "Hecho: no te enviaré más avisos…". **The first BAJA of a linked number also asks why** (2026-11-19, `whatsapp/optOutReason.ts`; a household had left after «quedan 85 cosas» and a 138-minute aviso): in the same message, «¿Me dices por qué? Me ayuda a mejorar.» [Demasiados mensajes] [No me sirven] [Otra cosa]; a tap is answered without the model («Gracias, lo apunto.») and saved in `user_feedback` (channel `whatsapp`, tag `baja_avisos`, the option in `answer`: `demasiados` | `no_sirven` | `otra`). Later BAJAs get only the confirmation. A late (stale), suspended, not-allowed or over-budget sender is honoured too. An unlinked number that wrote BAJA never gets the connect hint again until ALTA. **ALTA / "Sí, avísame"** → `notify = true`. **HUMANO / "hablar con una persona"** → how to reach the team (`SUPPORT_EMAIL`; unset → hola@mimoia.com, `BRAND_SUPPORT_EMAIL`, PRO-179). "No, gracias" is handled here only when it answers the opt-in question; otherwise it goes to the model.
   - A message older than 2 h (a late Meta retry) is dropped silently.
   - An unlinked phone goes to the linking flow.
   - A suspended account is refused (the copy includes the human contact).
   - A household on the **Gratis** plan (trial over without a plan, only from launch day; PRO-385, D-158, [Billing](./billing.md)) never gets Mimo: once a day, its first message gets «En el plan Gratis, Mimo descansa. Con Esencial vuelve a haceros la semana él solo.» with the link to Suscripción (kind `gratis`), and the rest of that day nothing; it gets no proactive messages or prep alerts either.
   - The **founder-price offer** (PRO-136, at the first resolved week or day 10, PRO-384; [Billing](./billing.md)) goes once to the household owner as a Mimo message (kind `founder_offer`), only inside the 24 h window and with proactive messages on; never as a template.
   - An account not in `WHATSAPP_ALLOWED_EMAILS` is refused.
   - A «¿qué tal la cena?» button is recorded here, before the budget (see the table above); so are «Lo hago», «¿Te sigo avisando…?» Sí / No and the BAJA reasons (`answerAvisoButton`). «Cambia a algo rápido» goes on to Mimo as «Cámbiame la cena de hoy por algo rápido».
   - **The menu, read by the code** (PRO-1292, see [Advisor](./advisor.md) → What the code answers): a text that is only «¿qué hay hoy?», «¿qué cenamos mañana?», «el menú del jueves» or «mándame el menú» (and the like, closed phrases, whole message only) is answered here, before the budget, without the model: one day as one line per meal (no recipe links nobody asked for), the week as the usual picture with its caption and link. No cost, also with the cap used up; the inbound row keeps `meta = { tools, toolTrace, codeReply: 'ver_menu' }`, so the next turns see `⟦hecho: get_todays_menu … → …⟧`; events `mimo_message` and `mimo_code_reply`. Not during the WhatsApp sign-up, nor for a number that writes in English (`deps.codeReply`, `inbound.ts` step 1e). Anything more («¿qué hay hoy? y cámbiame el jueves»), or no menu stored, goes to Mimo as before.
   - **Surveys go as a message of their own** (2026-11-19): the leftovers question (PRO-285) and the Sean Ellis survey (PRO-334) come back from the engine apart from the reply (`AssistantResponse.survey`) and go right after it as a plain message (outbound kind `survey`), never stuck to the reply nor under another question's buttons (one had gone out like that). At most one survey a week per household (`surveyCap.ts`: the leftovers question, the Sean Ellis survey and the «pregunta única» of the last 7 days, anyone in the household).
   - Once the monthly budget is spent, the user gets the same copy as the web's 429. This check runs **before any paid work**: no transcription, no photo extraction, no model call. The WhatsApp sign-up (every turn until `complete_onboarding`, first menu included) doesn't add to the budget, only to the cost ledger. The turn that takes the month past 80 % of the budget ends its reply with «Te queda poco de Mimo este mes; se renueva el día 1.» (once a month, see [Advisor](./advisor.md) → Cost guardrail).
6. Text and button replies go through `chat(userId, text, history, db, { mode: 'whatsapp' })`. **About food, broadly:** everything around food and cooking is in scope (menu, recipes, techniques, ingredients, kitchen utensils and appliances, shopping, pantry, nutrition, recipe links and videos, the user's Mimoia settings, and any question about Mimoia itself: what it is, what it does, plans and prices, privacy, mission (D-053); "ante la duda, es de comida") and gets a real answer, including how-to questions from Mimo's own cooking knowledge. Only topics with nothing to do with food get exactly `WHATSAPP_OFF_TOPIC_REPLY` ("Solo te puedo ayudar con tu comida: menú, recetas, lista de la compra, despensa y nutrición. ¿Te ayudo con algo de eso?"). Until 2026-10-12 the prompt said "resolutivo, no para conversar" and that sentence also hit cooking questions (a barbecue fish, "¿tienes el vídeo?"). Since PRO-1297 (2026-11-24) the prompt says that a cooking doubt or a question about their own recipe (even one not in Mimoia) is never off-topic, and that the sentence never answers a thank-you, a greeting or a joke (one kind line instead; see [advisor.md](./advisor.md)). A mixed message gets the food part done and the rest omitted. The system prompt's WhatsApp mode allows `*negrita*` and dash lists, forbids "pulsa/abajo" screen language, sends cooking timers and steps to the app's cooking mode, and asks the model to end short-choice questions with `[[opciones: Sí | No]]`. The renderer turns that line into reply buttons; with more than 3 options, they are folded back into the text.
7. Replies are cut to WhatsApp's 4096-character limit, splitting on paragraph boundaries first.
8. Each reply is stored as an outbound row (`kind='reply'`). The inbound row becomes `processed`, with its final text. Images (the week picture) go in two Graph calls: `POST /{phone-number-id}/media` (multipart upload of the JPG) and a `type: 'image'` message with that media id and the caption (`client.ts` `uploadMedia`).
9. If anything fails, the user gets "Vaya, algo ha fallado…" and the inbound row becomes `failed` with the error.

## Conversation reviewer agent

Every day after **01:00 Madrid** (`REVIEW_HOUR`; 07:00 until D-189, 2026-11-19, so the HQ snapshot of the night already carries it), the scheduler tick runs `runDailyReviewIfDue` (`services/whatsapp/reviewer.ts`) once for **yesterday's** conversations with Mimo: WhatsApp (`whatsapp_messages`) **and the app's chat** (`chat_messages`, the web turns kept since PRO-148 — [advisor.md](./advisor.md) «Web chat kept 90 days»; `appTurnRows` moves what Mimo did from the assistant row to the user's turn, as WhatsApp keeps it). Each transcript says its channel (`· canal whatsapp` / `· canal app`); `stats.appTurns` counts the app's turns and a day with only app turns is reviewed too:
- **Objective signals** (`computeStats`): turns, failed turns, slow turns (> 20 s), engine corrective rounds, frustration cues ("eso no", "ya te he dicho"…), replies ending in a question, proactive sends, send failures, and (PRO-156) messages written from their guide vs. sent as the fixed text (`guidedWritten`, `guidedFallback`). The transcript marks them «redactado con guía» / «texto fijo: <motivo>», runs the health detectors on the written ones, and the reviewer judges them too (natural, varied, nothing invented, no health).
- **Transcripts** (`buildTranscripts`): one per user and channel, Madrid times, with the engine trace per turn. Each inbound turn stores `whatsapp_messages.meta = { tools, corrections, ms, toolTrace, photo? }`; since 2026-10-12 `toolTrace` lists each tool's input and result (bounded) and the transcript shows them under the turn, so "dijo Hecho pero no cambió nada" is judged from facts. `corrections` now holds signals only (`claim_retry` —the engine gave one more round to a claim without a changing tool, PRO-239—, `unverified_claim`, `truncated`, `refusal`). Older turns read "sin registro" and are not judged on tools.
- **LLM review:** `claude-opus-5-5`, effort `high`, JSON-schema output, against the product rules: decisive, truthful, transparent about substitutions, brief "Hecho:" without being curt, **useful** (a cooking question answered with knowledge; categories `respuesta_poco_util` and `rechazo_indebido` since 2026-10-12), explicit request beats stored data, fast, useful proactivity, transcription quality. Each finding has severity (alta/media/baja), category, the turn, what happened, what was expected and a concrete suggested fix (prompt, skill, or missing capability) — the text for Admin. **Structured part (D-189, `services/reviewFindings.ts`)**, written without user data (no names, quotes, phones, emails, links or anyone's health): `canal` (whatsapp | app), `clave` (stable key, lowercase with dots/underscores, e.g. `checkin.varias_preguntas`), `patron` (one sentence about what Mimo does), `donde` (`prompt`, `guia_aviso:<tipo>`, `herramienta:<nombre>`, `generador`, `webhook`, `plantilla`, `tope` or `otro`; anything else → `otro`), `arreglo` (ONE fix, the recommended one), `prueba` (the test that would fail if it came back), `tipo` (`arreglo` | `decision` — a business decision for Miguel, with `arreglo` as the recommended option). **Memory:** the model gets the keys and patterns of its `ok` reviews of the 14 days before (`formatMemory`) and must reuse the key for the same problem; then `annotateFindings` adds `dias` (days the key showed up in those 14 days, plus today) and `regresion` (the key had been away 3 days or more and is back). The prompt also knows the company rules: Opus 5.5 at least (D-070, never a cheaper model as a fix), the spend cap is a business decision (`tipo: decision`), and outside WhatsApp's 24 h window notices go out as templates with their buttons as text (not a finding). It never touches user data.
- **Output:** a row in `assistant_reviews` (one per Madrid day; `ok` / `empty` / `failed`; the structured fields live in the `findings` JSONB, no migration), shown at `GET /admin/assistant-reviews` (admin). Since D-189 the daily run sends **no** WhatsApp summary (it runs at 01:00 and Miguel wants nothing before 08:00; it only reached him inside the 24 h window anyway): the findings reach Mimoia HQ through the flags route below, where the Producto run turns them into tasks and only the `decision` ones reach Miguel.
- **On-demand runs:** `POST /admin/assistant-reviews/run { day?, notify? }`; with `notify: true`, the WhatsApp summary (outbound `kind='review'`, excluded from the recipient's chat history) to `WHATSAPP_REVIEW_EMAILS` (else `ADMIN_EMAILS`) when they have a linked phone.
- **Detectores deterministas** (`services/assistantFlags.ts`, desde 2026-10-13): sobre cada respuesta de Mimo, `health_talk` (habla de efectos en el cuerpo, `talksHealthEffects`, o el motor la marcó `health_talk`), `health_claim` (un reclamo de la lista §6, `HEALTH_CLAIM_PATTERNS`, la misma que `publicHealthClaims.test.ts`), `unverified_done` («Hecho:» / «he cambiado…» sin ninguna herramienta que cambiara algo en la traza del turno —las de solo lectura, las que dieron error o las que dijeron «no he encontrado…» no cuentan—, o el motor la marcó `unverified_claim`; los turnos sin `toolTrace` no se juzgan) y `error_refusal` (turno fallido, respuesta no entregada, rechazo o respuesta cortada). Entran en `stats` (`healthTalk`, `healthClaims`, `unverifiedDone`) y en la transcripción como «⚑ señales automáticas» bajo la respuesta: el revisor las comprueba, no las da por buenas.
- **Para los agentes de Mimoia HQ:** `GET /admin/assistant-reviews/flags?days=7` (JWT de admin o `x-metrics-token`; `days` 1–31). Con el JWT de admin, agregados y textos anonimizados: `{ generatedAt, windowDays, from, to, channels: ['whatsapp'], turns, reviews { ok, empty, failed }, counts { byCategory { health_talk, health_claim, unverified_done, error_refusal, other }, reviewerByCategory, reviewerBySeverity }, items[] { date, channel, category, source (detector | reviewer | both), reviewerCategory, severity, reply, note } }`. Los detectores se calculan al pedirlo sobre `whatsapp_messages`; los hallazgos del revisor salen de `assistant_reviews` (afirmacion_falsa / accion_no_hecha / promesa_incumplida → `unverified_done`; error_tecnico / rechazo_indebido → `error_refusal`; el resto → `other`) y se funden con el aviso del detector del mismo día, categoría y ±3 min. `reply` (máx. 600 caracteres) y `note` pasan por `anonymise()` (`services/anonymise.ts`): emails, enlaces, teléfonos, DNI/NIE, IBAN, direcciones y códigos postales enmascarados; nombres de usuario, del perfil de WhatsApp, del hogar y de los otros miembros, y los nombres con mayúscula tras «soy», «para», «a», «con», «mi marido»… → `[nombre]`. Nunca salen el mensaje del usuario, ids, teléfonos ni la hora exacta. Los detectores (`items[]` de origen `detector`) miran solo WhatsApp; el chat de la app (`chat_messages`, que sí se guarda desde PRO-148) entra en la revisión diaria y sus hallazgos llegan aquí como los demás, y los nombres de sus usuarios y los que citan en sus mensajes también se enmascaran. **`hallazgos[]` (D-189, `buildHallazgos`):** la parte estructurada de cada hallazgo con clave, el día más reciente primero: `{ date, canal, clave, patron, donde, arreglo, prueba, tipo, gravedad, categoria, dias, regresion }`; los hallazgos anteriores a D-189 (sin clave) no salen. `patron`, `arreglo` y `prueba` (máx. 300 caracteres) pasan sin comillas (todo lo citado → `[cita]`) y por `anonymise()` con los nombres conocidos; un nombre dentro de la clave se cambia por `x`. Con `x-metrics-token`, una lista explícita de campos y cada valor de su enum (`services/agentView.ts`): `items[]` solo trae `{ date, channel, category, source, reviewerCategory, severity }`, ni `reply` ni `note`; una categoría o gravedad del revisor fuera de `REVIEW_SCHEMA` sale como `otro` / `null`, y los recuentos solo con esas claves. `hallazgos[]` sale también con el token (es lo que leen los agentes, D-189): cada campo vuelve a comprobarse (`hallazgoForAgents`: forma de la clave, `donde` de la lista, `canal`/`tipo`/`gravedad`/`categoria` de su enum, `dias` 1–15) y las tres frases pasan otra vez sin comillas ni correos, teléfonos o enlaces. Límite: que no hablen de la salud de nadie depende del prompt del revisor; no hay un detector que lo compruebe.
- **Cost:** one Opus call per day with conversations; about 20 s.
