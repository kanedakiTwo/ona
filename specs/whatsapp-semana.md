# WhatsApp · La semana en el chat

> Parte de [WhatsApp](./whatsapp.md) (la portada lista todas las partes): «tu menú está listo», la semana en una imagen, el acierto, la invitación en la semana reenviada, la lista en el chat, varias peticiones en un mensaje, cada respuesta a su pregunta y los «me pongo con ello».

## User Capabilities: la semana en el chat

- **«Tu menú está listo» in the web tab (PRO-161, 2026-10-18).** The `/onboarding` tab that follows the chat no longer jumps to `/menu` when `complete_onboarding` finishes: it shows «Tu menú está *listo*» («Antes de verlo: ¿hay algo que no comáis en casa? Alergias, intolerancias o cosas que no os gustan. Las alergias e intolerancias se guardan en tu perfil solo si nos das permiso, y el menú las tiene en cuenta. Si añades algo, rehacemos el menú sin ello.»), the separate, unticked health-data box and the same chips and free field as the web sign-up's step 4 (`RestrictionsPicker`). «Ver mi menú» with nothing added goes straight to `/menu` (Mimo's menu untouched). With something added it records the consent (`POST /user/:id/health-consent`), saves the restrictions (`PUT /user/:id`), rebuilds the weeks `complete_onboarding` built (`POST /menu/generate` with `fromDate` = today, next week too Friday to Sunday; the old rows stay as history) and then opens `/menu` («Rehaciendo tu menú…»; on an error, «No hemos podido guardarlo. Inténtalo otra vez o añádelo luego en tu perfil.» and it stays). No extra WhatsApp message (D-052). Who opens the web later from the chat's `/menu` link, without that tab, doesn't see this screen: the chat's `/profile` link stays the way to add allergies. On `/menu` an account with WhatsApp linked gets the «Primeros pasos» tour in **two** steps (the dish's «···» and the shopping list; not «Este es Mimo», they talk to Mimo by WhatsApp) — see [Auth](./auth.md). Health-data line as Legal wrote it (PRO-204); texts pending Customer Success review (`components/onboarding/OnboardingMenuReady.tsx`, e2e `onboarding-whatsapp-allergies.spec.ts` at 390×844) When the allergies were already asked at the start of the sign-up (2026-10-22), it doesn't ask again and opens `/menu`.
- Linked users can text the assistant anything they'd ask Mimo in the app: what's on today's menu, swap a meal, the shopping list, mark items bought, nutrition questions, create recipes, etc. Replies are short WhatsApp-style messages. When something is visual, the reply ends with a deep link into the app: `Ver menú: …/menu`, `Ver lista de la compra: …/shopping`, `Ver receta: …/recipes/:id` (also when the recipe was asked by name, `get_recipe_details`), `Modo cocina: …/recipes/:id/cook`. **Recipes are linked (PRO-85, 2026-10-15):** when Mimo shows a day's menu (`get_todays_menu`: today, tomorrow, «el jueves»…), before «Ver menú» comes one line per recipe of that day with its short link, «→ Lentejas estofadas: mimoia.com/recipes/<id>» (`WEB_PUBLIC_URL` without scheme or `www.`); notes («cenamos fuera») get no link, and a link already in the text isn't repeated, also when it's there in its short form (`mimoia.com/menu` stops a second `Ver menú: https://mimoia.com/menu`) (`appLinkFor` / `renderAssistantReply` in `render.ts`). Per-recipe links are for one-day messages only: the week message has none, and «pásame la receta del pollo» answers with `Ver receta: …` (`get_recipe_details`).
- **The generated week arrives as one picture** (2026-10-15, Miguel). After `generate_weekly_menu` / `complete_onboarding` (also the first menu of the WhatsApp sign-up) Mimo sends **one image message**: a JPG of the whole week (1080 px wide) with a short caption — the opener, the menu link and the invite of the text digest below, e.g. «Listo: tu menú hasta el domingo 🍽️ / 📖 Recetas y lista de la compra: mimoia.com/menu / ¿Cambio algo? Dímelo, p. ej. «el sábado, pescado».» (`weekMenuCaption`, built from the same parts as `weekMenuDigest`, so they never disagree). The picture: cream page, «Tu *semana*» + «Del 12 al 18 de octubre · comida y cena», the Mimoia logo, one column per meal the week uses (☕ Desayuno, ☀️ Comida, 🍎 Merienda, 🌙 Cena; at most 4), one row per day of the week (past days faded to ~42 %; a past day with nothing on it is left out, so a first menu made mid-week starts today — PRO-244; today in a white card with a terracotta border and a «HOY» pill), each dish with its recipe photo and name, and «¿Cambio algo? **Díselo a Mimo** por aquí · **mimoia.com**/menu» at the foot. **Format A** (≤ 2 meal columns and ≤ 1 dish per slot): square photo left of the name. **Format B** (anything else): photo on top of the cell, name below, the second dish as «+ Nombre» and «+N más» beyond two. Free-text notes and recipes without a photo get a neutral cream tile with the meal's icon; «(sobras)» / «(del congelador)» as in the text; a skipped day reads «Sin cocinar», a day with nothing «Nada previsto», a missing meal «—»; emoji in names are dropped (names cut with «…» after 3 lines in A, 2 in B). The model's other words (minus its «menú listo» line, see below) go in the caption above the opener. **One message (PRO-277):** if they and the caption pass 1,024 characters, the caption's fixed lines go first — the invite («¿Cambio algo?…»), then the menu link (`captionVariants` in `menuText.ts`); the opener, the snowball and the holiday lines always stay. Only if it still doesn't fit, or the words carry buttons, or a shopping list came in the same turn, they follow the picture as a normal message, without a second menu link. The WhatsApp prompt keeps Mimo's part to one short answer (about 600 characters at most) that doesn't repeat the week and answers in it whatever else was asked («dime qué dejo hecho el domingo»: what to leave ready, with the new menu's dishes; eval case `menu-y-que-dejo-hecho-domingo`, check `menu-un-mensaje`). **Fallback:** if the picture can't be drawn (or the week has nothing to draw), or its upload/send to Meta fails, exactly the text digest below goes out instead — the reply is never lost. One-day messages (today's menu, the daily brief) stay text. History stores the caption after the mark «⟦imagen enviada: el menú de la semana, día a día⟧» so Mimo knows the picture went out (the engine strips ⟦…⟧ if the model copies it); a picture that failed and fell back is stored as outbound kind `image_failed` and kept out of history (`replyMessages` in `inbound.ts`, `services/weekImage/`).
- **Asking to see the week sends the same picture** (2026-10-15, Miguel). «Dame el menú de la semana» (or «…de la semana que viene») runs `get_weekly_menu` (one call; the prompt says not to read it day by day with `get_todays_menu` and, on WhatsApp, not to write the week nor links) and Mimo sends the week picture with a **viewing caption**: «Tu menú de esta semana 🍽️» (the week contains today) or «Tu menú de la semana del 19 al 25 de octubre 🍽️», then the menu link and the invite — never «Listo:» nor the snowball line. The model's own day-by-day list and the one-day recipe link lines don't go out; its words stay above the caption only when the turn also changed something (e.g. «Hecho: - Martes 20, comida: Merluza»), not when every tool of the turn was a menu read. Fallback: drawing or sending the picture fails → the full text digest with the same «Tu menú…» opener. A week with no menu or nothing planned answers in text as before (the tool returns no menu data; a picture with nothing to draw also keeps the model's words). One-day reads (`get_todays_menu`: today, «el jueves») stay text with their recipe links (`weekViewOf` / `replyMessages` in `inbound.ts`, `view: 'viewing'` in `menuText.ts`). **A generation that changed nothing is a read too** (2026-11-21, reviewer `menu.tarjeta_listo_sin_generar`): when `generate_weekly_menu` leaves the week as it was (it already had a menu, `replaced: false` with the kept `menuId`) the picture goes with the viewing caption «Tu menú de la semana…» and Mimo's question («¿lo rehago entero o cambio algunos platos?») in one message, never «Listo:». Only a call that did generate (not failed, not `unchanged` in the turn's trace) gets «Listo:»; a stop with no menu (the fridge allergy question) sends Mimo's words alone.
- **How close the week came (PRO-155).** With a generated week, Mimo's words above the picture add one or two sentences on how close it is to what the household likes (from the menu evaluator, [Menus](./menus.md) → Acierto): sure and brief when high, what it couldn't fit when medium, humble and inviting to send a photo, a link or a written recipe when low (see [Advisor](./advisor.md) → `generate_weekly_menu`). No verdict → nothing is said.
- **«Efecto bola de nieve» line on the first generated weeks** (2026-10-15, Miguel's idea; wording: Marketing's version A, approved 2026-10-17, PRO-170; one constant `SNOWBALL_LINE` in `menuText.ts`): the caption of a GENERATED week — and its text fallback — starts with «Las primeras semanas acertaré menos con los gustos de tu casa. Dime qué cambiarías y lo apunto: así iré acertando cada vez más. Es el efecto bola de nieve ⛄» above «Listo: …», for the household's first `SNOWBALL_WEEKS` = 2 generated weeks. Counted after the turn as the number of distinct `week_start` among the household's menus (scoped like every menu read) that have a `menu_logs` row — written only when a week is generated (`generate_weekly_menu`, `complete_onboarding`, `POST /menu/generate`), never for the empty row the web creates by opening a week — so this week counts, and regenerating the same week doesn't count again. A count that fails → no line. Not on viewing, not on one-day messages (`generatedWeeksOf` in `services/menuWeek.ts`, `showsSnowball`). The web chat shows the same line in the same weeks (see [advisor.md](./advisor.md)).
- **The forwarded week carries the household's invitation** (PRO-1016, D-132): the week picture ends, under its «¿Cambio algo?…» foot, with a small faint line «Hecho con Mimo · mimoia.com/i/<code>» (above a hairline, never over a dish; `madeWith` in `weekImage/model.ts`, drawn in `render.ts`), and the caption — and its text fallback, under the invite — end with the same line (`inviteCode` in `WeekReplyOptions`, `madeWithMimoLine` in `@ona/shared`). `<code>` is the writing account's own code (`ensureReferralCode`, PRO-162; `weekReplyOptions` in `whatsapp/wiring.ts`), on generated and viewed weeks; a code that can't be read → no line, the week still goes out. `mimoia.com/i/<code>` opens the landing with `?invita=` and a sign-up from it is stored as a referral (`users.invited_by_code`, D-121; see [Auth](./auth.md)). When Mimo's words don't fit with the caption (PRO-277), this line is the first to go (the picture keeps it), then the invite, then the menu link (`captionVariants`). Tests: `weekImageModel.test.ts`.
- **Holiday line** (PRO-226, see [menus.md → Festivos](./menus-semana-tipo.md)): a generated week with a national or regional holiday of the household's comunidad (memory `region`) among the days shown carries «El jueves es festivo: te he puesto algo con más calma.» under the opener, in the caption and its text fallback (`holidayLine` in `menuText.ts`; region and time limits loaded in `weekReplyOptions`, `whatsapp/wiring.ts`).
- **Generating the week or asking for the list shows it in the chat** (2026-10-08), so the user can review and iterate without opening the app. The week's **text digest** (now the fallback of the picture above, and what a deploy without it sends) is the week read back from the DB after the whole turn, in the format Miguel approved on 2026-10-15 after the first real feedback («Overwhelming… too much info»): «Listo: tu menú hasta el domingo 🍽️», then one block per day separated by blank lines — «*Jueves 15 · hoy*» (full day name + day of month; «· hoy» / «· mañana» on the Europe/Madrid clock) and one line per meal with a fixed marker, ☕ desayuno, ☀️ comida, 🍎 merienda, 🌙 cena (no other emoji in those lines; dishes of one meal joined with « + », notes as written, «(sobras)» / «(del congelador)»; a meal the day doesn't have gets no line), «Sin cocinar» for a skipped day, a day with nothing left out — then «📖 Recetas y lista de la compra: mimoia.com/menu» (the only link: **no per-recipe links in the week message**) and «¿Cambio algo? Dímelo, p. ej. «el sábado, pescado».» (the example names the second day still to come in the message, the only one if there's one, «hoy» if today is all that's left). When the menu's week contains today only today and later days show; on Sunday the opener is «Listo: tu menú de hoy 🍽️». A week that hasn't started (or a mid-week menu with nothing left from today on) shows its seven days and opens «Listo: tu menú de la semana del 19 al 25 de octubre 🍽️». The model's own «menú listo» line is dropped so it isn't said twice, in all its shapes (matched without accents, case or `*bold*`): «(Tu primer) menú de (la|esta) semana (que viene|del 19 de octubre…) (está) listo/generado», «para la (próxima) semana…», a trailing «(semana del 19 de octubre)», bullets «- »/«• », «Hecho: …» on the same line, «Listo: tu menú de la semana que viene»; a «Hecho:» left with nothing under it goes too. Anything else it wrote (another change of the turn under its «Hecho:», a warning) stays above the week (`withoutMenuReadyLines`, table-tested in `whatsappWeekReply.test.ts`); the trailing `Ver menú` link is dropped because the week already carries it. If reading the week fails, the model's text and `Ver menú` go out unchanged. After `get_shopping_list` the reply carries the pending items grouped by aisle with quantities ("*Frutas y verduras:* Cebolla (300 g), Calabaza (1.2 kg)"), ticked items left out, "Ya tienes en casa: …", and "¿Añado o quito algo?…", before the app link. The model writes only "Hecho: - Menú de la semana listo" / a short line (`withDigests` in `inbound.ts`, `weekMenuDigest` in `menuText.ts`, `shoppingListDigest` in `shoppingText.ts`). The list is read from the tool's own `data` (`{ listId, items }`, `shoppingItemsOf`); until 2026-11-18 only a bare array counted, so the list never came («la tienes justo debajo» and nothing below). Test: the real `get_shopping_list` output through `processInbound` (`whatsappInbound.test.ts`).
- **Decisive by design.** Several requests in one message ("el jueves pon filete, el sábado cenamos fuera, apunta leche") all get done in that turn. The reply is short: "Hecho:" plus one line per change, with no unrequested advice and no closing question unless one is really needed. Reversible changes need no confirmation; only destructive or bulk ones (delete a recipe, replace an existing week) are confirmed. A missing recipe gets the closest catalogue match, and the reply says which. Explicit requests beat stored dislikes. Anything the app can do is a skill; see [Advisor](./advisor.md) → UI-parity skills.
- Yes/no and short-choice questions arrive as native WhatsApp reply buttons (max 3). Tapping one is the same as typing its label — read against the question it belongs to (below) — **except that a button never makes a whole week nobody asked for** (PRO-1298; the review of Mimo's history, 23-nov: a button generated a whole week): Mimo gets the button's title and the question it answered (the question its ref points at when that isn't Mimo's last message, PRO-1295; else the last line of Mimo's message before it, without the ⟦…⟧ marks and links), and `generate_weekly_menu` only runs when one of them asks for a week («¿Te preparo el menú de la semana que viene?» → «Sí»; `buttonAsksForWeek`, `services/menuRequestGuard.ts`). Otherwise the tool answers «Menu NO generado: solo ha pulsado el boton…» and Mimo does what the button asks (a swap is `swap_meal`) or asks. The sign-up's own first menu (`complete_onboarding`) is not affected.
- **One request, one menu** (PRO-1298): the turns of a number go one after the other, so «¿hola? el menú» sent while the menu was being made waits and runs after it. Mimo gets when each message was **sent** (Meta's timestamp, `sentAt`); if the week's menu was made after that, `generate_weekly_menu` makes no second one, even with `replaceExisting` («No he generado otro…»; the week is shown again as a read). Before, the household got two different menus. A request asked after the menu (and confirmed) does remake the week as before.
- **Each answer goes to its own question (PRO-1295, 2026-11-24; examen del histórico, letra E).** Until then a tap or a written «sí» / «no» was read against Mimo's *last* message: a «No» to a question about the household's ficha was taken as «no cambies nada», a «No» typed while Mimo was making the menu answered the menu's «¿Cambio algo?», a «me gusta» to a proposal was logged as a cooked dinner (`answerTarget.ts`, `answerTurn.ts`, step 1c' of `inbound.ts`).
  - **The question rides in the button id.** Every button Mimo sends (its own `[[opciones]]`, the check-in, «hora de cocinar», the Sunday menu, the freezer, the avisos question, the pause and BAJA questions) goes out as `<id>~<ref>`, `ref` = the second it was sent, in base 36 (`tagButtons`, added in `client.sendMessage`); the parser hands every reader the old id (`replyId`) and the ref apart (`replyRef`). Buttons sent before 24-nov carry none and work as before. A tap is resolved against its question: the check-in button records **that day's** dinner (up to a week later, `CHECKIN_REF_WINDOW_MS`; older → Mimo; without a ref, the last check-in of the last 30 h, as before) and `recordDinnerCheckinAnswer` counts a dinner as already logged only by that recipe's cook log; «Cambia a algo rápido» under the reminder of a dinner gone by gets «Ese aviso era de la cena del lunes 23 de noviembre, que ya pasó. Si quieres cambiar la de hoy, dímelo.» (no model, nothing swapped); the closed «Ahora no» of the Sunday menu and the freezer review are answered by code («Vale, no lo preparo. Cuando quieras el menú de la semana que viene, pídemelo.» / «Vale, lo dejo en el congelador.»); «No, gracias» tapped answers the avisos question even when Mimo sent something after it. Any other tap goes to Mimo; when its question isn't Mimo's last message, the turn carries a note quoting it («[Nota del sistema…] «No» contesta a tu mensaje de hoy a las 19:10: «¿Tienes horno en casa?»…», never stored) and history keeps «No (a «¿Tienes horno en casa?»)».
  - **A written «sí» / «no» that could answer more than one question runs nothing.** A bare yes or no (`bareYesNo`: «No», «no, gracias», «sí, vale»; «No, ponme pescado» says more and goes on as usual) when Mimo left **two or more questions open** — sent after the household's last answered turn, in the last 12 h, the rows of one reply counted once (`openQuestions`; a message asks when it has buttons or its last line, links aside, has «?») — **or the last one went out after the household wrote it** (Meta's timestamp: it hadn't seen it) gets, without the model, «Para no equivocarme: ¿a qué contestas «No»?» with each question in full and one button per question (`cual:<si|no>:<ref>`, titles ≤ 20 characters), plus «Lo de antes» when the last one wasn't seen (`…:antes`; outbound kind `clarify`, in history; the «No» row is kept processed with `meta.ambiguous`). The tap sends the household's own «No» to Mimo with the note of that question, or — «Lo de antes» — with one saying it doesn't answer the question it hadn't seen. One open question, seen: the «sí» / «no» is its answer, as before. Not in the WhatsApp sign-up. English households get it in English.
  - **An opinion is no cooked meal.** «Me gusta», «qué rico», «buena pinta», «suena bien»… without a word saying it was cooked or eaten («la hice», «nos gustó», «estaba rica»; `isOpinion`), when Mimo's last message isn't the dinner check-in, reaches Mimo with a note («es una opinión… no lo apuntes como hecho») and a turn that can't use `log_cooked` nor `mark_meal_eaten` (`ChatOptions.withoutTools`: out of the tools and of the skills that can run). «Qué rico» to the check-in still records the dinner.
  - Tests: `whatsappAnswerTarget.test.ts`.
- **"Me pongo con ello" acks:** a turn never sits silent for long.
  - When the assistant starts a slow skill, the user gets a specific note right away: "Vale, preparo el menú. Dame unos segundos…" for `generate_weekly_menu`, and similar ones for `import_recipe_from_url`, `recipe_variation` and `create_recipe`.
  - A recipe photo gets "Recibida la foto. Voy a leer la receta…" immediately.
  - Anything else still silent after 8 s gets "Un momento, me pongo con ello…".
  - At most one ack per turn, always before the answer and never after it (`createAcker` + `ChatOptions.onToolStart`). Acks are stored as outbound `kind='ack'` and kept out of chat history.
  - Added after the first real use (2026-10-06), when "genera menú para la semana" took 25 s with no feedback.
  - **How long it takes** (PRO-1298, goal: a menu in under a minute): every tool's own time is kept in the turn's trace (`toolTrace[].ms`), next to the whole turn's (`meta.ms`), and `GET /admin/usage` → `menuWait` gives p50 and p95 by week of `generate_weekly_menu` and of the sign-up's last step (see [Metrics](./metrics.md)). The sign-up's first menu no longer waits for the «acierto» evaluation (up to 12 s a week, which the sign-up never shows; it is still saved on the menu). Moving the generation out of the turn («te lo mando en un minuto») waits for that figure: it only pays if the tool, not the model's own rounds, is what takes the time.
- Conversation context carries across messages: the server rebuilds the last 20 messages from the last 12 h, so "y el jueves?" works after "¿qué ceno el miércoles?". Since 2026-10-12 each reply in that history starts with a **⟦hecho: …⟧ line** (`traceLine`) listing what the tools of that turn got and returned (bounded to ~1 200 chars), so Mimo knows what it already did; the marks never reach the user (`runToolLoop` strips them). History is filtered by phone **and** user, so a phone that moves to another account never carries the previous owner's chat into the new one. Assistant replies count even if their delivery failed: the actions behind them ran, and a request left "unanswered" would be redone by the decisive model.
- A message from an unlinked number that looks like a profile code but isn't valid gets "Ese código no es válido o ha caducado".
- Users can send **voice notes**: they are transcribed by the same chain as Mimo's voice in the app (2026-10-25; detail in [Voice (Mimo)](./voice-mode.md) → «How a spoken turn works»): Groq Whisper (`GROQ_API_KEY`, free plan), then OpenAI (`OPENAI_TRANSCRIBE_MODEL`, `gpt-4o-transcribe`); never AiKit (PRO-1122, no processor contract), all with language `es` and the Spanish cooking vocabulary; the ogg/opus notes go as they come. The note is handled exactly like typed text; the transcript is what lands in history. A failed or empty transcription gets "No he podido entender el audio…"; with neither key the reply is "Ahora mismo no puedo escuchar audios. ¿Me lo escribes?".
- Users can **send the school's monthly menu** (PRO-224, see [Menus](./menus.md) → Menú del comedor escolar): a photo with «menú del cole / del colegio / del comedor / comedor escolar» in the caption, or right after a message saying so (30 min), or any **PDF** (WhatsApp `document` messages with `application/pdf`; other files stay unsupported), gets «Recibido. Voy a leer el menú del cole…», is read (Claude Opus 5.5) and saved for the household, and the reply lists what was read with «Si algo no está bien, dímelo». Not a school menu → «No he encontrado el menú del cole en ese archivo…». History stores «[Menú del cole] …» with the days in the tool trace, so a correction («el martes era merluza») goes to `set_school_menu_days` (`handleSchoolMenu` in `inbound.ts`, `readAndSaveSchoolMenu` in `wiring.ts`).
- Users can **share a recipe link** (YouTube, a recipe article, or an Instagram/TikTok post), bare or with text ("guárdame esta"): the assistant calls `import_recipe_from_url`, saves it to their recipes and replies with `Ver receta: …/recipes/:id`, offering to put it on the menu. Links that aren't recipes, or pages the extractor can't read, get an honest "no he podido leerla" answer. An Instagram/TikTok post whose caption doesn't carry the recipe gets exactly «No he podido leer la receta de este vídeo; mándame una captura»; the screenshot is then imported like any recipe photo (see [Recipe Import](./recipe-import.md)).
- Users can **send a photo of a recipe** (cookbook page, handwritten card, screenshot). It is extracted with the existing photo extractor and **saved directly** (soft lint, tags `auto-extracted`/`from-photo`; caption kept as context in history). Reply: "He guardado *<nombre>* en tus recetas" + link, plus a "revisa los ingredientes" nudge when some weren't matched to the catalogue. History records `[Foto de una receta: <caption>]`, so "ponla el jueves para cenar" works next. **Link to the original (PRO-66):** an Instagram/TikTok link or a recipe page that got «mándame una captura» in the previous 30 minutes becomes the source of the screenshot's recipe («Le he puesto como fuente el enlace que me mandaste.»), without asking. Otherwise, when the extractor says the photo looks like a screenshot of a web page or a social network (`looksLikeScreenshot`, same vision call), the reply ends with «¿Tienes el enlace del original? Así lo guardo como fuente.» instead of the menu question, and a message with a link in the next 30 minutes is saved as that recipe's `sourceUrl` (no chat turn, no new recipe): «Hecho: he guardado el enlace como fuente de *X*. Lo verás en «Ver fuente».» + «Ver receta». A cookbook page or a handwritten note gets no question. The photo's inbound row keeps `meta = { photoRecipeId, photoRecipeName, photoSourceUrl?, sourceAsked? }` and the answering link's row `meta.sourceAttachedTo`, so a question is answered once and a newer photo closes it (`whatsapp/photoSource.ts`).
- Users can **ask about a photo** (a pan, an ingredient, a dish): a caption that is a question (`photoIntent`: contains "?" or starts with cómo/qué/cuánto/cuál/puedo/se puede/sirve/dime/explica…) goes straight to Mimo, who sees the image (`chat(…, { images })`) and answers. A photo without a question goes to recipe import, and if it isn't a recipe Mimo looks at it and answers instead of "No he encontrado ninguna receta" (2026-10-11: "¿cómo cambia la receta con esta plancha?" got exactly that). History records `[Foto] <caption>` and `meta.photo = true`.
- One message can trigger several actions ("genérame el menú y dime qué ceno hoy") — the engine runs up to 4 tool rounds per turn (see [Advisor](./advisor.md)).
- Sending a sticker, location or other unsupported type gets a polite "todavía no entiendo ese tipo de mensaje".
