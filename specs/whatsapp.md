# WhatsApp

Talk to **Mimo**, Mimoia's assistant, from WhatsApp (the bot speaks as Mimo / Mimoia since the 2026-10-08 rename; the old project name stays only in identifiers: env var names and Meta template names such as `ona_aviso`, which are registered in Meta and don't change). WhatsApp is another way to reach the same assistant as Mimo in the app, with the same skills, user memory and monthly € budget, through Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API. Since 2026-10-14 Mimo answers from a real number, **+34 626 68 55 11** (display name «mimoia», the spoon-and-heart logo as profile photo), on Mimoia's own Meta app; who can use it is still limited by `WHATSAPP_ALLOWED_EMAILS`. Before that, v1 ran on Meta's test number (+1 555 183 2642, at most 5 verified recipients, shown as «Test Number») under a Meta app that wasn't Mimoia's.

## User Capabilities

- **WhatsApp-first:** anyone can just write to our number. An unlinked number gets "Hola, soy Mimo, el asistente de cocina de Mimoia… conecta tu cuenta: <web>/whatsapp/conectar · Entra (o crea tu cuenta) y te daré un código para enviarme desde este chat", at most once an hour per number. The page asks to log in or register (with `?next=` back). Then it mints a one-time code and shows "Último paso: envía este mensaje a Mimo desde tu WhatsApp" (eyebrow "Mimo en WhatsApp") with an "Enviar desde WhatsApp" `wa.me` button (message prefilled; on iPhone/iPad every WhatsApp button — here, in onboarding «Por WhatsApp» and in Profile — opens the app by its `whatsapp://send` scheme and goes to the `wa.me` page only if the app didn't open within 1.5 s, because outside Safari — another app's browser, Mimoia on the home screen — `wa.me` lands on WhatsApp's «Download» page, 2026-11-23; `WhatsAppLink`, `lib/whatsappOpen.ts`), polling until linked → "¡Listo!" + "Volver a WhatsApp". The link carries **no token**: the phone that gets linked is always the one that sends the code, so forwarding the link to someone else can't attach a phone to the wrong account. A first version used a phone-bound token and a "¿Conectar este número?" confirm page; the 2026-10-06 review showed a forwarded link plus a spoofed profile name could hijack an account, so it was replaced before shipping.
- **First steps in the chat (WhatsApp-first sign-up, 2026-10-10; four questions since 2026-11-19).** An account that links **before** finishing onboarding (from `/onboarding` → «Por WhatsApp», or from `/whatsapp/conectar`) gets, in Miguel's words (2026-10-15), «¡Hola, X! Ya estás conectado: soy Mimo, de Mimoia. Soy un asistente de inteligencia artificial (IA) especializado en cocina: puedo equivocarme y no sustituyo a un profesional sanitario, pero puedo encargarme de las tareas rutinarias para que cocinar vuelva a ser divertido. Puedes escribirme o mandarme notas de voz, como te sea más cómodo. Para entrar en calor te propongo preparar tu primer menú en un momento: solo necesito saber cuatro cosas. *1.* ¿Quiénes sois en casa? Dime adultos y niños de 2 a 10 años. *2.* ¿Qué comidas cocináis en casa a la semana? Por ejemplo: «6 cenas y 1 comida». *3.* Tres platos que hacéis a menudo. *4.* ¿Hay algo que no entre en casa? Un alimento o un plato que no os guste.» (outbound kind `onboarding`; the disclosure keeps its three parts, AI Act art. 50; the message lists exactly `WHATSAPP_ONBOARDING_QUESTIONS` and announces as many, PRO-245). While `users.onboardingDone` is false every turn runs with `WHATSAPP_ONBOARDING_PROMPT`: Mimo asks, one at a time and without buttons, only what's missing of the first two (the dishes and what doesn't go in are optional, never insisted on); never the priority nor who does the shopping (a `shopper` said unprompted is still kept, PRO-383); never health data (the link comes later). **Everything goes where the menu reads it from day one** (`complete_onboarding`, `services/signupFirstMenu.ts`): who they are → `saveOnboarding` like the web; which meals they cook → `lunches` / `dinners` (0–7 a week) → the household's «semana tipo» (`off` on the meals they don't cook: N lunches from the weekend back, N dinners from Monday on with Saturday last; «6 cenas y 1 comida» = Sunday lunch and every dinner but Saturday) and how often they cook (`cookingStyle` / `cookingFreq` only if that's what they said); the dishes they make often → `favoriteDishes` **and into the first menu for sure**: the recipe whose name has every word of the dish (theirs first, then the catalogue's) goes in a main dish of a lunch or dinner (one a day, dinner first, from the first meal), and a dish with no recipe goes in as a note that Mimo turns into the household's recipe in the same turn (`swap_meal` with `newRecipe`; `swap_meal` is in the sign-up's tools); a dish whose recipe has what someone at home can't eat is left out and Mimo says so (PRO-1041, Constraints); what doesn't go in → the `dislikes` (families included). **The first menu starts with the next meal that hasn't gone by** (`firstMenuWeeks(now, { fromNextMeal: true })`, Madrid: from 21:00 it starts tomorrow; before, today's meals past their cut-off — breakfast 10:00, lunch 15:00, merienda 18:00 — stay empty, so a 15:00 sign-up starts with tonight's dinner), Friday to Sunday plus the whole next week (PRO-65/244); the tool tells Mimo where it starts and not to talk about meals already gone. **One message to close the sign-up (2026-11-19):** the week's picture with Mimo's one line (one or two of their dishes) and at most one question, without buttons (plus one line with the link when they told an allergy, PRO-1041); no «¿Cambio algo?», no snowball line, no «Hecho con Mimo» in that caption; the reply never says the menu «se parece poco», «no me he acercado», «genérico» or «acertaré menos» (no fit line on the first menu; `signupReply.ts` drops such a sentence and any question but the last). With no dish said, Mimo's question is «¿Qué soléis cenar un martes normal?». **~10 minutes later, one at a time** (`signupFollowUps.ts`, from the scheduler tick every 5 min under its lock, read back from the outbound kinds so a restart loses nothing): the web link for allergies/intolerances of anyone at home («Si en casa hay alergias, intolerancias o algo que no podáis o queráis comer, añádelo aquí con tu permiso… Por WhatsApp no me dejan guardar datos de salud.», `/profile`, kind `health_link`) and, after 5 quiet minutes, the opt-in question («¿Quieres que también te escriba yo? No escribo mucho, solo lo importante: un aviso antes de cocinar (congelados, tiempos) y la lista de la compra los sábados. Si quieres el menú cada mañana, pídemelo. Puedes cambiarlo cuando quieras simplemente pidiéndomelo.», kind `optin_prompt`, two buttons). Never while the conversation is going (a message in or out in the last 5 min), never 22:30–08:00, never after BAJA or ALTA, only within 20 h of the sign-up. **Last message (PRO-07):** once the person answers that question («Sí, avísame»/ALTA or «No, gracias»), Mimo sends — right after the confirmation of the answer (PRO-248: «Hecho: te enviaré avisos…» or «Vale, no te escribiré…») — «Te cuento ejemplos de cosas que puedes pedirme ya: 📸 Mándame una foto o un enlace de una receta, la proceso y la guardo en tus recetas. 🍽️ «¿Qué cenamos hoy?» 🛒 «Hazme la lista de la compra»» and «Y cuando quieras, vuelve a Mimoia en la web: allí tienes tu menú de la semana y un recorrido rápido por todo lo demás. <web>/menu» (`COPY.firstSteps(url)`, written from its guide since PRO-156; outbound kind `first_steps`). Only for numbers that did the first steps in the chat (an `onboarding` message went out) and got the opt-in question, and only once per number; accounts onboarded on the web already get these ideas in «¡Listo!». If the person never answers the question, the suggestions aren't sent. Legacy accounts (`onboardingDone` null) are treated as onboarded. Tests: `whatsappOnboarding.test.ts`, `firstMenuWhatsApp.test.ts`, `signupFirstMenu.test.ts`, `signupFollowUps.test.ts`, `firstMenuWeeks.test.ts`, `whatsappInbound.test.ts`; live case `alta-primer-menu-con-sus-platos` (`primer-menu`) in `mimoEvalCases.ts`.
- **Buttons are not repeated (PRO-141, 2026-10-15).** Every outbound body records the buttons it carried (`⟦botones: A | B | C⟧`, `outboundBody` in `render.ts`; the model sees it in the history). A reply whose buttons are exactly the last ones Mimo sent goes out as plain text (`lastSentButtons` + `dropRepeatedButtons`): in the sign-up the dishes question came with the «Casi a diario / 3-4 días / Poco» buttons of the previous one. The sign-up prompt asks its follow-up questions without buttons; only «¿Quién hace la compra?» («Yo · Otra persona de casa · Nos turnamos», PRO-383) and the optional favourite-dishes question («Mejor luego») have them.
- **«¿Quién hace la compra?» in the WhatsApp sign-up (PRO-383, D-158, D-051).** Only when the household said 2 adults or more: once Mimo has how many they are and how they cook (and before the favourite dishes, when those are asked), it asks «¿Quién hace la compra?» with the buttons «Yo» · «Otra persona de casa» · «Nos turnamos» (`WHATSAPP_ONBOARDING_PROMPT`) and passes the answer to `complete_onboarding` as `shopper` (`me` · `other` · `shared`; optional, never required; with 1 adult it isn't asked and the tool drops it). `saveOnboarding` keeps it as the household's `shopper` fact (origin onboarding, evidence `whatsapp`) and records `shopper_answered` with channel `whatsapp`. With «Otra persona de casa» or «Nos turnamos» the tool also creates the household invitation from the sign-up (`services/shopperInvite.ts`: `source: alta`, sent at once, so it counts in `familyInvites.bySource.alta`; the `alta` offer is stamped) and its summary carries the web's message, «Te he metido en nuestra casa de Mimoia: ahí tendrás el menú y la lista de la compra de cada semana. Entra aquí: <enlace>», which Mimo puts in its reply as is, in a block of its own, to forward. Nothing is created when the account can't invite (not the owner, or 2 accounts already); a failure leaves the sign-up done without it. Tests: `firstMenuWhatsApp.test.ts` (the tool), `whatsappOnboarding.test.ts` (the prompt and the parameter), `onboardingShopper.smoke.ts` (the invitation in Postgres).
- **A number that is already linked sends a link code** (2026-10-15; before, the code went to Mimo, who answered «No puedo vincular cuentas desde el chat»). Only a clear link message counts (`isLinkAttempt`: «Vincular Mimoia: …» or just the code); anything else goes to Mimo. Its own account's code → «Este WhatsApp ya está conectado a tu cuenta de Mimoia: no hace falta ningún código» (the link, its avisos and prefs untouched). Another account's valid code → the number **moves** to that account («Este WhatsApp estaba conectado a otra cuenta de Mimoia. Desde ahora está conectado a la cuenta de la que has sacado el código.»), then the usual first steps or «¡Listo!» + opt-in; sending the code from the phone proves both ends. An invalid or expired code → «Ese código no es válido o ha caducado (duran 10 minutos). Este WhatsApp sigue conectado a tu cuenta de siempre…». No model call in any of these.

- Linked users can text the assistant anything they'd ask Mimo in the app: what's on today's menu, swap a meal, the shopping list, mark items bought, nutrition questions, create recipes, etc. Replies are short WhatsApp-style messages. Dímelo, p. ej. «el sábado, pescado».» (the example names the second day still to come in the message, the only one if there's one, «hoy» if today is all that's left). - **Decisive by design.** Several requests in one message ("el jueves pon filete, el sábado cenamos fuera, apunta leche") all get done in that turn. The reply is short: "Hecho:" plus one line per change, with no unrequested advice and no closing question unless one is really needed. Reversible changes need no confirmation; only destructive or bulk ones (delete a recipe, replace an existing week) are confirmed. A missing recipe gets the closest catalogue match, and the reply says which. Explicit requests beat stored dislikes. Anything the app can do is a skill; see [Advisor](./advisor.md) → UI-parity skills.

**Written from a guide, not a fixed text** (PRO-156, D-065; `services/whatsapp/guided.ts`). - **Guide per kind** (`MESSAGE_GUIDES`): what it is for, what it must carry (dishes, hours, links; buttons stay in code), tone and maximum length; plus fixed rules (`COMMON_RULES`): only cooking, menu and shopping; nothing about health or nutrition; never «no puedo»; invent nothing; don't repeat earlier messages; Spanish from Spain (no «¿te late?», «ahorita»… — PRO-274), no filler openers («¡Ey!», «¡Al lío!») and articles, adjectives and verbs that agree with each dish. - **Context:** the household's memory (`user_memories` without health keys or meal times; the household's ficha is not in these guided messages yet — the conversation with Mimo does read it, PRO-253), the last 12 h of chat, the last 5 messages of the same kind to that number and (PRO-274) the openings of the last 3 avisos of any kind to that number.

## Conversation reviewer agent

Every day after **01:00 Madrid** (`REVIEW_HOUR`; 07:00 until D-189, 2026-11-19, so the HQ snapshot of the night already carries it), the scheduler tick runs `runDailyReviewIfDue` (`services/whatsapp/reviewer.ts`) once for **yesterday's** conversations with Mimo: WhatsApp (`whatsapp_messages`) **and the app's chat** (`chat_messages`, the web turns kept since PRO-148 — [advisor.md](./advisor.md) «Web chat kept 90 days»; `appTurnRows` moves what Mimo did from the assistant row to the user's turn, as WhatsApp keeps it). - **Objective signals** (`computeStats`): turns, failed turns, slow turns (> 20 s), engine corrective rounds, frustration cues ("eso no", "ya te he dicho"…), replies ending in a question, proactive sends, send failures, and (PRO-156) messages written from their guide vs. sent as the fixed text (`guidedWritten`, `guidedFallback`). The transcript marks them «redactado con guía» / «texto fijo: <motivo>», runs the health detectors on the written ones, and the reviewer judges them too (natural, varied, nothing invented, no health).

Each inbound turn stores `whatsapp_messages.meta = { tools, corrections, ms, toolTrace, photo? }`; since 2026-10-12 `toolTrace` lists each tool's input and result (bounded) and the transcript shows them under the turn, so "dijo Hecho pero no cambió nada" is judged from facts. `corrections` now holds signals only (`claim_retry` —the engine gave one more round to a claim without a changing tool, PRO-239—, `unverified_claim`, `truncated`, `refusal`). ## Linking

- The code has 6 characters from `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789` (no 0/O/1/I) and always contains at least one digit, so an ordinary 6-letter word is never mistaken for a code. It expires after 10 minutes and works once. Generating a new code invalidates the user's previous unused one.
- Sending a valid code links `phone ↔ user` one-to-one:
  - If the phone was linked to another account, the link moves to the new owner.
  - If the user had another phone linked, the old phone is replaced.
  - Messages the phone sent before linking are assigned to the new owner.
- Phones are stored as Meta's `wa_id`: digits only, with the country code.

## Message handling

1. `POST /whatsapp/webhook` verifies `X-Hub-Signature-256`, an HMAC-SHA256 of the **raw** body keyed with `WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET`. That's why the router is mounted before `express.json()`. A bad or missing signature gets **401**.
2. Every message is inserted into `whatsapp_messages` with `ON CONFLICT (wamid) DO NOTHING`, so Meta's retries are deduplicated. Each new message is queued **right after its insert**. If one insert fails, the response is **500**: Meta retries the batch, the already-stored messages dedupe, and they are already queued. Otherwise **200**. The assistant runs after the response.
3. Messages are processed **in order per phone**, using an in-process promise chain (Railway runs one API instance). **Messages sent one after another are one turn (2026-11-19, `coalesce.ts`):** a plain text waits 5 s (20 s at most from the first of a burst; «escribiendo…» shows right away); the texts that arrive meanwhile from the same number go to Mimo together, in order, as one message («2 adultos» + «Casi a diario»). The joined ones are marked `ignored` / `merged` (history reads the joined text from the last one's row). A control word (BAJA, ALTA, «humano»), a link code, a tapped button, a photo, a voice note or a document is never joined: the texts waiting before it go first as their own turn, then it alone. In process like the queue: a message waiting when the process dies stays `received` (as one in the queue did before). **What arrives while a turn of that number is running waits for it (PRO-1295, 2026-11-24):** the texts keep joining past the 5 s and the 20 s cap, and go as one turn once Mimo has answered (`busy` / `idle` in `coalesce.ts`, from the per-phone queue), so they never cross its reply and a «sí» / «no» among them is read against what Mimo just asked (see «Each answer goes to its own question»); a text still in its 5 s when the turn ends keeps its own wait; a button, photo or voice note that comes meanwhile lets the held texts go first.
4. Each message is marked read with a "typing…" indicator (best effort).
5. Gates, in order:
   - **Control words** run first, before every other gate, and never reach the model (`commands.ts`, whole-message match only, so "para la cena pon lentejas" is a request). **BAJA / STOP / PARAR / "no quiero más avisos"** → `notify = false` + "Hecho: no te enviaré más avisos…". **The first BAJA of a linked number also asks why** (2026-11-19, `whatsapp/optOutReason.ts`; a household had left after «quedan 85 cosas» and a 138-minute aviso): in the same message, «¿Me dices por qué? Me ayuda a mejorar.» [Demasiados mensajes] [No me sirven] [Otra cosa]; a tap is answered without the model («Gracias, lo apunto.») and saved in `user_feedback` (channel `whatsapp`, tag `baja_avisos`, the option in `answer`: `demasiados` | `no_sirven` | `otra`). Later BAJAs get only the confirmation. A late (stale), suspended, not-allowed or over-budget sender is honoured too. An unlinked number that wrote BAJA never gets the connect hint again until ALTA. **ALTA / "Sí, avísame"** → `notify = true`. **HUMANO / "hablar con una persona"** → how to reach the team (`SUPPORT_EMAIL`; unset → hola@mimoia.com, `BRAND_SUPPORT_EMAIL`, PRO-179). "No, gracias" is handled here only when it answers the opt-in question; otherwise it goes to the model.
   - A message older than 2 h (a late Meta retry) is dropped silently.
   - An unlinked phone goes to the linking flow.
   - A suspended account is refused (the copy includes the human contact).

Text and button replies go through `chat(userId, text, history, db, { mode: 'whatsapp' })`. **About food, broadly:** everything around food and cooking is in scope (menu, recipes, techniques, ingredients, kitchen utensils and appliances, shopping, pantry, nutrition, recipe links and videos, the user's Mimoia settings, and any question about Mimoia itself: what it is, what it does, plans and prices, privacy, mission (D-053); "ante la duda, es de comida") and gets a real answer, including how-to questions from Mimo's own cooking knowledge. Only topics with nothing to do with food get exactly `WHATSAPP_OFF_TOPIC_REPLY` ("Solo te puedo ayudar con tu comida: menú, recetas, lista de la compra, despensa y nutrición. ¿Te ayudo con algo de eso?"). Until 2026-10-12 the prompt said "resolutivo, no para conversar" and that sentence also hit cooking questions (a barbecue fish, "¿tienes el vídeo?"). Since PRO-1297 (2026-11-24) the prompt says that a cooking doubt or a question about their own recipe (even one not in Mimoia) is never off-topic, and that the sentence never answers a thank-you, a greeting or a joke (one kind line instead; see [advisor.md](./advisor.md)). A mixed message gets the food part done and the rest omitted. The system prompt's WhatsApp mode allows `*negrita*` and dash lists, forbids "pulsa/abajo" screen language, sends cooking timers and steps to the app's cooking mode, and asks the model to end short-choice questions with `[[opciones: Sí | No]]`. The renderer turns that line into reply buttons; with more than 3 options, they are folded back into the text.
7. Replies are cut to WhatsApp's 4096-character limit, splitting on paragraph boundaries first.
8. Each reply is stored as an outbound row (`kind='reply'`). The inbound row becomes `processed`, with its final text. Images (the week picture) go in two Graph calls: `POST /{phone-number-id}/media` (multipart upload of the JPG) and a `type: 'image'` message with that media id and the caption (`client.ts` `uploadMedia`).
9. If anything fails, the user gets "Vaya, algo ha fallado…" and the inbound row becomes `failed` with the error.

## Data model

- `whatsapp_links(user_id UNIQUE, phone UNIQUE, profile_name, notify, prefs jsonb, linked_at, last_inbound_at)`. `last_inbound_at` marks the start of Meta's 24 h customer-service window.
- `whatsapp_link_codes(code PK, user_id, expires_at, used_at)`: used by both the profile card and `/whatsapp/conectar`.
- `whatsapp_messages(wamid UNIQUE NULL, phone, user_id, direction in|out, kind, body, status, error_message)`.
  - Inbound statuses: `received` / `processed` / `failed` / `ignored`.
  - Outbound statuses: `sent` / `failed`.
  - Outbound kinds `system` (budget, linked, errors), `link` (how to connect) and `ack` ("me pongo con ello") are left out of chat history. `image_failed`: a week picture whose send failed and whose text went out instead (status `failed`, also out of history).
- `whatsapp_questions(id, text UNIQUE, created_by, created_at)` and `whatsapp_question_sends(question_id, household_id, user_id, phone, status sent|failed, error_message, sent_at, answered_at; UNIQUE(question_id, household_id))`: the Admin «pregunta única» (PRO-1091, migration `0102_whatsapp_questions`, additive).
- Migration `0030_whatsapp.sql`, which only creates new tables and is idempotent. `users.whatsapp_nudge_at` (migration `0052_whatsapp_nudge.sql`, additive): when the reminder card was shown.

## API

- `GET /whatsapp/webhook` (public): Meta's verification handshake. It echoes `hub.challenge` when `hub.verify_token` matches `WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN`; otherwise **403**.
- `POST /whatsapp/webhook` (public, signed): **503** when the channel isn't configured.
- `GET /whatsapp/status` (auth): `{ available, linked, nudge, phone, notify, prefs, chatLink }`. `nudge` = show the one reminder card on `/menu` (PRO-107, rule in `services/whatsapp/nudge.ts`). `available` = configured AND email allowed. The profile card is hidden unless `available || linked`, so a user can always disconnect.
- `POST /whatsapp/nudge/seen` (auth): the reminder card was shown; stamps `users.whatsapp_nudge_at` once (keeps the first date). **204**.
- `POST /whatsapp/link-code` (auth): `{ code, expiresAt, message, waLink }`. **403** `WHATSAPP_UNAVAILABLE` when not available.
- `PATCH /whatsapp/link` (auth) `{ notify?: boolean, prefs?: { <kind>: boolean } }`: **404** when not linked.
- `DELETE /whatsapp/link` (auth): **204**.

## Constraints

- **Health data over WhatsApp** (LEG-03, Meta's terms 4.2; PRO-24, changed by PRO-1305 / D-1025): the physical data and a diet for religious reasons are never stored from a WhatsApp conversation (`update_profile` / `update_memory` drop them and reply «Por WhatsApp no guardo datos de salud físicos (edad, sexo, peso, altura, actividad) ni una dieta por motivos religiosos. Añádelos en tu perfil de la web» (`WHATSAPP_PHYSICAL_DATA_REPLY`; Telegram keeps `WHATSAPP_HEALTH_DATA_REPLY`)). **Allergies, intolerances and food not eaten for health are**, under the one-time permission, with the code's «Apuntado…» notice; the permission question, its buttons, deleting and withdrawing from the chat and the 30 days without it are in [privacy.md](./privacy.md) «Alergias dichas en el chat». The buttons (`hc:`, `hcp:`, `hcw:` ids) are answered by the code before any other gate but BAJA, without the model, also with the cap used up. Telegram turns keep the old rule: nothing is saved there ([Telegram](./telegram.md)). Tests `whatsappHealthData.test.ts`, `chatAllergiesInbound.test.ts`
- **An allergy said and not saved still shapes the menu** (PRO-1041, Seguridad; also in the web chat without the consent; PRO-1305: and every menu of the household for 30 days, with no link from Mimo — the permission question goes instead). Test households 2026-10-24 → 31: «soy celíaca», «alérgico al marisco», «alergia a frutos secos» in the first message; the first menus brought them and only Mimo's manual check took them out (on 2026-10-28 it failed: «caldo de pollo» for a vegetarian). Now: when the allergy is the reason something isn't saved, `update_profile` and `update_memory` reply with the link to «Tus alergias y tu dieta» with it marked (`saidAllergyReply`, [Household](./household.md)), and `generate_weekly_menu` and `complete_onboarding` apply, **for that menu only and without storing it**, what Mimo passes in `restrictions_said` plus the allergens named in the person's own messages of the last 30 days (WhatsApp inbound by the linked phone; earlier web-chat turns), by the fridge reader's keyword rule plus «sin gluten» / «sin lactosa» (`services/saidRestrictions.ts`), minus what anyone in the household has saved. They ride on the generation's async call (`withMenuOnlyRestrictions`, `services/menuOnlyRestrictions.ts`) and `withHouseholdRestrictions` adds them to every pick inside it. The tool tells Mimo «Aplicado solo a este menu (no esta guardado): sin gluten…» with the link to send; the prompts tell it to pass them and never to refuse the menu over it. **In the WhatsApp sign-up**, with its dishes of 2026-11-19: a dish they make often never comes in as a recipe with what someone at home can't eat, said to Mimo or saved (`signupDishes`, `assistant/skills.ts`, the generator's own list). It is left out, not even as a note (`swap_meal` by name would bring that same recipe back), and the tool tells Mimo «No he puesto «macarrones con tomate»: su receta lleva algo que en casa no pueden comer…» to say so in a line; when a dish the catalogue doesn't have goes in as a note, the tool adds that the household's recipe Mimo writes for it can't have it either. The sign-up's one closing message carries one more line, the link (`signupReply.ts` doesn't count the «?» of a link as a question, so it isn't dropped with the extra questions; `WHATSAPP_ONBOARDING_PROMPT` says where it goes). Diets that aren't health data (vegetarian) are not covered here (PRO-1053, PRO-1082). Tests `saidRestrictions.test.ts`, `assistant-skills.test.ts`, `firstMenuWhatsApp.test.ts` and `whatsappInbound.test.ts` (the sign-up), smoke `saidAllergies.smoke.ts` («soy celíaca» by WhatsApp, no consent: first menu without gluten, `restrictions: []`, `healthConsentAt: null`)
- **AI disclosure (EU AI Act art. 50):** both first messages Mimo can send in a conversation — the unlinked "Hola, soy Mimo, el asistente de cocina de Mimoia…" hint and the "¡Listo!" after linking — include "Soy un asistente de inteligencia artificial (IA): puedo equivocarme y no sustituyo a un profesional sanitario." (`AI_DISCLOSURE_FIRST_PERSON` in `@ona/shared`). Pinned by `whatsappInbound.test.ts`.
  - **Messages Mimo sends on its own (PRO-1169, LEG-34):** inside the 24 h window every proactive message (`sendProactive`: avisos, prep alerts, «hoy cocina X», the founder offer, the daily review) ends with the fixed line «— Mimo, asistente con IA» (`AI_SIGNATURE`), added by the code after the text is written (`withAiSignature`, `services/whatsapp/aiNotice.ts`; as its own message when a button body or caption would go over Meta's limit). It is stripped from the history the model and the guide writer see (`stripAiSignature`), so they never copy it. Outside the window the template carries it in its footer, «Mimo, asistente con IA. Responde BAJA para no recibir avisos» (60 characters, `AI_TEMPLATE_FOOTER`), registered by hand in WhatsApp Manager (MIG-05), not by the code.
  - **Back after 30 days (PRO-1169):** when a linked number writes after 30 days or more without writing (`whatsapp_links.last_inbound_at`, read before it's updated), Mimo's first message of that turn starts with `AI_DISCLOSURE_FIRST_PERSON` (`withReturnDisclosure`). Tests: `aiDisclosureMimo.test.ts`.
- No WhatsApp groups. Signing up happens on the web (the connect link offers "Crear cuenta"), not inside the chat.
- The business isn't verified (D-004): the display name may show as the number until Meta approves it, and business-initiated conversations are capped at 250 a day. Business verification lifts the cap and is needed before opening the channel widely.
- In-process queue: if the API restarts mid-turn, that message stays `received` and gets no answer (no sweeper; messages older than 2 h are dropped as stale anyway).
- Every turn checks the chat's € budget **and** the monthly cap on all paid AI work (`USER_MONTHLY_SPEND_CAP_EUR`, see [Advisor](./advisor.md)) before any paid step. Every paid call is recorded in the cost ledger ([Metrics](./metrics.md)), including each template message (`meta_whatsapp/utility_template`) and, since 2026-10-15, every free-form message Mimo sends (`meta_whatsapp/service_message`, feature `whatsapp_service`): Meta bills these from the 1,001st of the month per number, so the first 1,000 are recorded at 0 € and the rest at €0.0166 (`services/whatsapp/serviceCost.ts`).
- "Today" and "this week" use the Europe/Madrid clock (`services/madridTime.ts`), for the assistant's `get_todays_menu` / `generate_weekly_menu` and for the daily brief.
- **Week picture rendering** runs in the API with no browser: `satori` (layout → SVG, flexbox only) + `@resvg/resvg-js` (SVG → PNG) + `sharp` (photo crops, JPG q85). Fonts (Fraunces, Cormorant Garamond italic, Inter; SIL OFL) and the meal icons (Noto Emoji PNGs, Apache-2.0) and logo are bundled in `apps/api/assets/` (licences in `assets/NOTICE.txt`); nothing is cached. Recipe photos are fetched per send (`image_url`; relative catalogue paths resolved against `WEB_PUBLIC_URL`), 2.5 s timeout and 6 MB cap each, 8 at a time; a photo that fails becomes a tile, never a failed picture. About 1 s to draw (measured locally) plus the photo downloads.
- The model sees text only: photos are never shown to the chat model, they go straight to recipe import (a photo of the fridge is not understood yet). Cooking-mode hints (`set_timer`, `cooking_step`) have no effect on WhatsApp; the reply points to the app instead.
