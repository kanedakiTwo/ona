# WhatsApp

Talk to **Mimo**, Mimoia's assistant, from WhatsApp (the bot speaks as Mimo / Mimoia since the 2026-10-08 rename; the old project name stays only in identifiers: env var names and Meta template names such as `ona_aviso`, which are registered in Meta and don't change). WhatsApp is another way to reach the same assistant as Mimo in the app, with the same skills, user memory and monthly € budget, through Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API. Since 2026-10-14 Mimo answers from a real number, **+34 626 68 55 11** (display name «mimoia», the spoon-and-heart logo as profile photo), on Mimoia's own Meta app; who can use it is still limited by `WHATSAPP_ALLOWED_EMAILS`. Before that, v1 ran on Meta's test number (+1 555 183 2642, at most 5 verified recipients, shown as «Test Number») under a Meta app that wasn't Mimoia's.

## User Capabilities

- **WhatsApp-first:** anyone can just write to our number. An unlinked number gets "Hola, soy Mimo, el asistente de cocina de Mimoia… conecta tu cuenta: <web>/whatsapp/conectar · Entra (o crea tu cuenta) y te daré un código para enviarme desde este chat", at most once an hour per number. The page asks to log in or register (with `?next=` back). Then it mints a one-time code and shows "Último paso: envía este mensaje a Mimo desde tu WhatsApp" (eyebrow "Mimo en WhatsApp") with an "Enviar desde WhatsApp" `wa.me` button (message prefilled), polling until linked → "¡Listo!" + "Volver a WhatsApp". The link carries **no token**: the phone that gets linked is always the one that sends the code, so forwarding the link to someone else can't attach a phone to the wrong account. A first version used a phone-bound token and a "¿Conectar este número?" confirm page; the 2026-10-06 review showed a forwarded link plus a spoofed profile name could hijack an account, so it was replaced before shipping.
- **First steps in the chat (WhatsApp-first sign-up, 2026-10-10).** An account that links **before** finishing onboarding (from `/onboarding` → «Por WhatsApp», or from `/whatsapp/conectar`) gets, in Miguel's words (2026-10-15), «¡Hola, X! Ya estás conectado: soy Mimo, de Mimoia. Soy un asistente de inteligencia artificial (IA) especializado en cocina: puedo equivocarme y no sustituyo a un profesional sanitario, pero puedo encargarme de las tareas rutinarias para que cocinar vuelva a ser divertido. Puedes escribirme o mandarme notas de voz, como te sea más cómodo. Para entrar en calor te propongo preparar tu primer menú en un momento: solo necesito saber tres cosas. *1.* ¿Cuántos sois en casa? Dime adultos y niños de 2 a 10 años. *2.* Dime tres platos que os gusten mucho.» (the disclosure keeps its three parts, AI Act art. 50; two questions in one message to save one) (outbound kind `onboarding`) instead of the usual «¡Listo!» + opt-in. While `users.onboardingDone` is false, every turn runs with the onboarding block of the WhatsApp prompt (`WHATSAPP_ONBOARDING_PROMPT`): the household and three dishes were asked in the first message; Mimo asks, one at a time, only what's missing: how often they cook ([[opciones: Casi a diario | 3-4 días | Poco]]) and whatever wasn't answered — then `complete_onboarding` saves them exactly like the web onboarding (`services/onboarding.ts` `saveOnboarding`, no restrictions or consent) and generates the first week; the reply carries the week digest. Right after, Mimo sends the web link for allergies/intolerances («Si en casa hay alergias, intolerancias o algo que no podáis o queráis comer, añádelo aquí con tu permiso… Por WhatsApp no me dejan guardar datos de salud.», `/profile`) and then the opt-in question («¿Quieres que también te escriba yo? No escribo mucho, solo te mandaría el menú del día, un aviso antes de cocinar (congelados, tiempos) y la lista de la compra los sábados. Puedes cambiarlo cuando quieras simplemente pidiéndomelo.» — true: Mimo has the tool that switches the avisos, and BAJA/ALTA still work). **Last message (PRO-07):** once the person answers that question («Sí, avísame»/ALTA or «No, gracias»), Mimo sends — with no confirmation line before it during the sign-up (2026-10-15; outside the sign-up the usual «Hecho: vuelvo a enviarte avisos…» / «Vale, no te escribiré…» stay) — «Te cuento ejemplos de cosas que puedes pedirme ya: 📸 Mándame una foto o un enlace de una receta, la proceso y la guardo en tus recetas. 🍽️ «¿Qué cenamos hoy?» 🛒 «Hazme la lista de la compra»» and «Y cuando quieras, vuelve a Mimoia en la web: allí tienes tu menú de la semana y un recorrido rápido por todo lo demás. <web>/menu» (`COPY.firstSteps(url)`, the fixed text: since PRO-156 it's written from its guide, see *Proactive messages*; outbound kind `first_steps`; the web tab that started it has already moved on, see «Tu menú está listo» below). Only for numbers that did the first steps in the chat (an `onboarding` message went out) and got the opt-in question, and only once per number (no `first_steps` sent before); accounts onboarded on the web already get these ideas in «¡Listo!». If the person never answers the question, the suggestions aren't sent. Legacy accounts (`onboardingDone` null) are treated as onboarded.
- **Buttons are not repeated (PRO-141, 2026-10-15).** Every outbound body records the buttons it carried (`⟦botones: A | B | C⟧`, `outboundBody` in `render.ts`; the model sees it in the history). A reply whose buttons are exactly the last ones Mimo sent goes out as plain text (`lastSentButtons` + `dropRepeatedButtons`): in the sign-up the dishes question came with the «Casi a diario / 3-4 días / Poco» buttons of the previous one. The sign-up prompt also says only the cooking-frequency question has buttons.
- **A number that is already linked sends a link code** (2026-10-15; before, the code went to Mimo, who answered «No puedo vincular cuentas desde el chat»). Only a clear link message counts (`isLinkAttempt`: «Vincular Mimoia: …» or just the code); anything else goes to Mimo. Its own account's code → «Este WhatsApp ya está conectado a tu cuenta de Mimoia: no hace falta ningún código» (the link, its avisos and prefs untouched). Another account's valid code → the number **moves** to that account («Este WhatsApp estaba conectado a otra cuenta de Mimoia. Desde ahora está conectado a la cuenta de la que has sacado el código.»), then the usual first steps or «¡Listo!» + opt-in; sending the code from the phone proves both ends. An invalid or expired code → «Ese código no es válido o ha caducado (duran 10 minutos). Este WhatsApp sigue conectado a tu cuenta de siempre…». No model call in any of these.
- Users can also connect from `/profile` → card "WhatsApp" (status line "Conectado" / "Sin conectar") → "Conectar WhatsApp". This mints a one-time code, shown as "Vincular Mimoia: 4F7K2A" (only the code matters: a message prefilled before the rename, with the old name instead of Mimoia, still links), and an "Abrir WhatsApp" `wa.me` link with that message prefilled. When the user sends it from their phone, the account is linked; the card polls every 3 s and flips to "WhatsApp conectado".
- Linked users see their masked number (`+34 ••• ••• 222`), an "Abrir chat con Mimo" link, an "Avisos por WhatsApp" toggle (see Proactive messages) and "Desconectar", which asks for confirmation first.
- **«Tu menú está listo» in the web tab (PRO-161, 2026-10-18).** The `/onboarding` tab that follows the chat no longer jumps to `/menu` when `complete_onboarding` finishes: it shows «Tu menú está *listo*» («Antes de verlo: ¿hay algo que no comáis en casa? Alergias, intolerancias o cosas que no os gustan. Por WhatsApp no guardamos datos de salud; aquí sí, si nos das permiso. Si añades algo, rehacemos el menú sin ello.»), the separate, unticked health-data box and the same chips and free field as the web sign-up's step 4 (`RestrictionsPicker`). «Ver mi menú» with nothing added goes straight to `/menu` (Mimo's menu untouched). With something added it records the consent (`POST /user/:id/health-consent`), saves the restrictions (`PUT /user/:id`), rebuilds this week (`POST /menu/generate`, the week `complete_onboarding` built; the old row stays as history) and then opens `/menu` («Rehaciendo tu menú…»; on an error, «No hemos podido guardarlo. Inténtalo otra vez o añádelo luego en tu perfil.» and it stays). No extra WhatsApp message (D-052). Who opens the web later from the chat's `/menu` link, without that tab, doesn't see this screen: the chat's `/profile` link stays the way to add allergies. On `/menu` an account with WhatsApp linked gets the «Primeros pasos» tour in **two** steps (the dish's «···» and the shopping list; not «Este es Mimo», they talk to Mimo by WhatsApp) — see [Auth](./auth.md). Texts pending Customer Success review (`components/onboarding/OnboardingMenuReady.tsx`, e2e `onboarding-whatsapp-allergies.spec.ts` at 390×844)
- Linked users can text the assistant anything they'd ask Mimo in the app: what's on today's menu, swap a meal, the shopping list, mark items bought, nutrition questions, create recipes, etc. Replies are short WhatsApp-style messages. When something is visual, the reply ends with a deep link into the app: `Ver menú: …/menu`, `Ver lista de la compra: …/shopping`, `Ver receta: …/recipes/:id` (also when the recipe was asked by name, `get_recipe_details`), `Modo cocina: …/recipes/:id/cook`. **Recipes are linked (PRO-85, 2026-10-15):** when Mimo shows a day's menu (`get_todays_menu`: today, tomorrow, «el jueves»…), before «Ver menú» comes one line per recipe of that day with its short link, «→ Lentejas estofadas: mimoia.com/recipes/<id>» (`WEB_PUBLIC_URL` without scheme or `www.`); notes («cenamos fuera») get no link, and a link already in the text isn't repeated, also when it's there in its short form (`mimoia.com/menu` stops a second `Ver menú: https://mimoia.com/menu`) (`appLinkFor` / `renderAssistantReply` in `render.ts`). Per-recipe links are for one-day messages only: the week message has none, and «pásame la receta del pollo» answers with `Ver receta: …` (`get_recipe_details`).
- **The generated week arrives as one picture** (2026-10-15, Miguel). After `generate_weekly_menu` / `complete_onboarding` (also the first menu of the WhatsApp sign-up) Mimo sends **one image message**: a JPG of the whole week (1080 px wide) with a short caption — the opener, the menu link and the invite of the text digest below, e.g. «Listo: tu menú hasta el domingo 🍽️ / 📖 Recetas y lista de la compra: mimoia.com/menu / ¿Cambio algo? Dímelo, p. ej. «el sábado, pescado».» (`weekMenuCaption`, built from the same parts as `weekMenuDigest`, so they never disagree). The picture: cream page, «Tu *semana*» + «Del 12 al 18 de octubre · comida y cena», the Mimoia logo, one column per meal the week uses (☕ Desayuno, ☀️ Comida, 🍎 Merienda, 🌙 Cena; at most 4), one row per day of the week (all seven: past days faded to ~42 %, today in a white card with a terracotta border and a «HOY» pill), each dish with its recipe photo and name, and «¿Cambio algo? **Díselo a Mimo** por aquí · **mimoia.com**/menu» at the foot. **Format A** (≤ 2 meal columns and ≤ 1 dish per slot): square photo left of the name. **Format B** (anything else): photo on top of the cell, name below, the second dish as «+ Nombre» and «+N más» beyond two. Free-text notes and recipes without a photo get a neutral cream tile with the meal's icon; «(sobras)» / «(del congelador)» as in the text; a skipped day reads «Sin cocinar», a day with nothing «Nada previsto», a missing meal «—»; emoji in names are dropped (names cut with «…» after 3 lines in A, 2 in B). The model's other words (minus its «menú listo» line, see below) go in the caption above the opener; if they carry buttons (or a shopping list came in the same turn, or it all passes 1,024 characters) they follow the picture as a normal message, without a second menu link. **Fallback:** if the picture can't be drawn (or the week has nothing to draw), or its upload/send to Meta fails, exactly the text digest below goes out instead — the reply is never lost. One-day messages (today's menu, the daily brief) stay text. History stores the caption after the mark «⟦imagen enviada: el menú de la semana, día a día⟧» so Mimo knows the picture went out (the engine strips ⟦…⟧ if the model copies it); a picture that failed and fell back is stored as outbound kind `image_failed` and kept out of history (`replyMessages` in `inbound.ts`, `services/weekImage/`).
- **Asking to see the week sends the same picture** (2026-10-15, Miguel). «Dame el menú de la semana» (or «…de la semana que viene») runs `get_weekly_menu` (one call; the prompt says not to read it day by day with `get_todays_menu` and, on WhatsApp, not to write the week nor links) and Mimo sends the week picture with a **viewing caption**: «Tu menú de esta semana 🍽️» (the week contains today) or «Tu menú de la semana del 19 al 25 de octubre 🍽️», then the menu link and the invite — never «Listo:» nor the snowball line. The model's own day-by-day list and the one-day recipe link lines don't go out; its words stay above the caption only when the turn also changed something (e.g. «Hecho: - Martes 20, comida: Merluza»), not when every tool of the turn was a menu read. Fallback: drawing or sending the picture fails → the full text digest with the same «Tu menú…» opener. A week with no menu or nothing planned answers in text as before (the tool returns no menu data; a picture with nothing to draw also keeps the model's words). One-day reads (`get_todays_menu`: today, «el jueves») stay text with their recipe links (`weekViewOf` / `replyMessages` in `inbound.ts`, `view: 'viewing'` in `menuText.ts`).
- **How close the week came (PRO-155).** With a generated week, Mimo's words above the picture add one or two sentences on how close it is to what the household likes (from the menu evaluator, [Menus](./menus.md) → Acierto): sure and brief when high, what it couldn't fit when medium, humble and inviting to send a photo, a link or a written recipe when low (see [Advisor](./advisor.md) → `generate_weekly_menu`). No verdict → nothing is said.
- **«Efecto bola de nieve» line on the first generated weeks** (2026-10-15, Miguel's idea; wording: Marketing's version A, approved 2026-10-17, PRO-170; one constant `SNOWBALL_LINE` in `menuText.ts`): the caption of a GENERATED week — and its text fallback — starts with «Las primeras semanas acertaré menos con los gustos de tu casa. Dime qué cambiarías y lo apunto: así iré acertando cada vez más. Es el efecto bola de nieve ⛄» above «Listo: …», for the household's first `SNOWBALL_WEEKS` = 2 generated weeks. Counted after the turn as the number of distinct `week_start` among the household's menus (scoped like every menu read) that have a `menu_logs` row — written only when a week is generated (`generate_weekly_menu`, `complete_onboarding`, `POST /menu/generate`), never for the empty row the web creates by opening a week — so this week counts, and regenerating the same week doesn't count again. A count that fails → no line. Not on viewing, not on one-day messages (`generatedWeeksOf` in `services/menuWeek.ts`, `showsSnowball`). The web chat shows the same line in the same weeks (see [advisor.md](./advisor.md)).
- **Generating the week or asking for the list shows it in the chat** (2026-10-08), so the user can review and iterate without opening the app. The week's **text digest** (now the fallback of the picture above, and what a deploy without it sends) is the week read back from the DB after the whole turn, in the format Miguel approved on 2026-10-15 after the first real feedback («Overwhelming… too much info»): «Listo: tu menú hasta el domingo 🍽️», then one block per day separated by blank lines — «*Jueves 15 · hoy*» (full day name + day of month; «· hoy» / «· mañana» on the Europe/Madrid clock) and one line per meal with a fixed marker, ☕ desayuno, ☀️ comida, 🍎 merienda, 🌙 cena (no other emoji in those lines; dishes of one meal joined with « + », notes as written, «(sobras)» / «(del congelador)»; a meal the day doesn't have gets no line), «Sin cocinar» for a skipped day, a day with nothing left out — then «📖 Recetas y lista de la compra: mimoia.com/menu» (the only link: **no per-recipe links in the week message**) and «¿Cambio algo? Dímelo, p. ej. «el sábado, pescado».» (the example names the second day still to come in the message, the only one if there's one, «hoy» if today is all that's left). When the menu's week contains today only today and later days show; on Sunday the opener is «Listo: tu menú de hoy 🍽️». A week that hasn't started (or a mid-week menu with nothing left from today on) shows its seven days and opens «Listo: tu menú de la semana del 19 al 25 de octubre 🍽️». The model's own «menú listo» line is dropped so it isn't said twice, in all its shapes (matched without accents, case or `*bold*`): «(Tu primer) menú de (la|esta) semana (que viene|del 19 de octubre…) (está) listo/generado», «para la (próxima) semana…», a trailing «(semana del 19 de octubre)», bullets «- »/«• », «Hecho: …» on the same line, «Listo: tu menú de la semana que viene»; a «Hecho:» left with nothing under it goes too. Anything else it wrote (another change of the turn under its «Hecho:», a warning) stays above the week (`withoutMenuReadyLines`, table-tested in `whatsappWeekReply.test.ts`); the trailing `Ver menú` link is dropped because the week already carries it. If reading the week fails, the model's text and `Ver menú` go out unchanged. After `get_shopping_list` the reply carries the pending items grouped by aisle with quantities ("*Frutas y verduras:* Cebolla (300 g), Calabaza (1.2 kg)"), ticked items left out, "Ya tienes en casa: …", and "¿Añado o quito algo?…", before the app link. The model writes only "Hecho: - Menú de la semana listo" / a short line (`withDigests` in `inbound.ts`, `weekMenuDigest` in `menuText.ts`, `shoppingListDigest` in `shoppingText.ts`).
- **Decisive by design.** Several requests in one message ("el jueves pon filete, el sábado cenamos fuera, apunta leche") all get done in that turn. The reply is short: "Hecho:" plus one line per change, with no unrequested advice and no closing question unless one is really needed. Reversible changes need no confirmation; only destructive or bulk ones (delete a recipe, replace an existing week) are confirmed. A missing recipe gets the closest catalogue match, and the reply says which. Explicit requests beat stored dislikes. Anything the app can do is a skill; see [Advisor](./advisor.md) → UI-parity skills.
- Yes/no and short-choice questions arrive as native WhatsApp reply buttons (max 3). Tapping one is the same as typing its label.
- **"Me pongo con ello" acks:** a turn never sits silent for long.
  - When the assistant starts a slow skill, the user gets a specific note right away: "Vale, preparo el menú. Dame unos segundos…" for `generate_weekly_menu`, and similar ones for `import_recipe_from_url`, `recipe_variation` and `create_recipe`.
  - A recipe photo gets "Recibida la foto. Voy a leer la receta…" immediately.
  - Anything else still silent after 8 s gets "Un momento, me pongo con ello…".
  - At most one ack per turn, always before the answer and never after it (`createAcker` + `ChatOptions.onToolStart`). Acks are stored as outbound `kind='ack'` and kept out of chat history.
  - Added after the first real use (2026-10-06), when "genera menú para la semana" took 25 s with no feedback.
- Conversation context carries across messages: the server rebuilds the last 20 messages from the last 12 h, so "y el jueves?" works after "¿qué ceno el miércoles?". Since 2026-10-12 each reply in that history starts with a **⟦hecho: …⟧ line** (`traceLine`) listing what the tools of that turn got and returned (bounded to ~1 200 chars), so Mimo knows what it already did; the marks never reach the user (`runToolLoop` strips them). History is filtered by phone **and** user, so a phone that moves to another account never carries the previous owner's chat into the new one. Assistant replies count even if their delivery failed: the actions behind them ran, and a request left "unanswered" would be redone by the decisive model.
- A message from an unlinked number that looks like a profile code but isn't valid gets "Ese código no es válido o ha caducado".
- Users can send **voice notes**: they are transcribed with OpenAI (`OPENAI_TRANSCRIBE_MODEL`, default `gpt-4o-transcribe` (the mini model mis-heard short Spanish notes as Galician), language `es`, plus a Spanish cooking-vocabulary `prompt`) and handled exactly like typed text; the transcript is what lands in history. A failed or empty transcription gets "No he podido entender el audio…"; without `OPENAI_API_KEY` the reply is "Ahora mismo no puedo escuchar audios. ¿Me lo escribes?".
- Users can **share a recipe link** (YouTube, a recipe article, or an Instagram/TikTok post), bare or with text ("guárdame esta"): the assistant calls `import_recipe_from_url`, saves it to their recipes and replies with `Ver receta: …/recipes/:id`, offering to put it on the menu. Links that aren't recipes, or pages the extractor can't read, get an honest "no he podido leerla" answer. An Instagram/TikTok post whose caption doesn't carry the recipe gets exactly «No he podido leer la receta de este vídeo; mándame una captura»; the screenshot is then imported like any recipe photo (see [Recipe Import](./recipe-import.md)).
- Users can **send a photo of a recipe** (cookbook page, handwritten card, screenshot). It is extracted with the existing photo extractor and **saved directly** (soft lint, tags `auto-extracted`/`from-photo`; caption kept as context in history). Reply: "He guardado *<nombre>* en tus recetas" + link, plus a "revisa los ingredientes" nudge when some weren't matched to the catalogue. History records `[Foto de una receta: <caption>]`, so "ponla el jueves para cenar" works next. **Link to the original (PRO-66):** an Instagram/TikTok link that got «mándame una captura» in the previous 30 minutes becomes the source of the screenshot's recipe («Le he puesto como fuente el enlace que me mandaste.»), without asking. Otherwise, when the extractor says the photo looks like a screenshot of a web page or a social network (`looksLikeScreenshot`, same vision call), the reply ends with «¿Tienes el enlace del original? Así lo guardo como fuente.» instead of the menu question, and a message with a link in the next 30 minutes is saved as that recipe's `sourceUrl` (no chat turn, no new recipe): «Hecho: he guardado el enlace como fuente de *X*. Lo verás en «Ver fuente».» + «Ver receta». A cookbook page or a handwritten note gets no question. The photo's inbound row keeps `meta = { photoRecipeId, photoRecipeName, photoSourceUrl?, sourceAsked? }` and the answering link's row `meta.sourceAttachedTo`, so a question is answered once and a newer photo closes it (`whatsapp/photoSource.ts`).
- Users can **ask about a photo** (a pan, an ingredient, a dish): a caption that is a question (`photoIntent`: contains "?" or starts with cómo/qué/cuánto/cuál/puedo/se puede/sirve/dime/explica…) goes straight to Mimo, who sees the image (`chat(…, { images })`) and answers. A photo without a question goes to recipe import, and if it isn't a recipe Mimo looks at it and answers instead of "No he encontrado ninguna receta" (2026-10-11: "¿cómo cambia la receta con esta plancha?" got exactly that). History records `[Foto] <caption>` and `meta.photo = true`.
- One message can trigger several actions ("genérame el menú y dime qué ceno hoy") — the engine runs up to 4 tool rounds per turn (see [Advisor](./advisor.md)).
- Sending a sticker, location or other unsupported type gets a polite "todavía no entiendo ese tipo de mensaje".
- **"Hazme la compra"** → one short link per shop (`<WEB_PUBLIC_URL>/c/<token>`) that opens the user's own WhatsApp chat with that shop and the order already written; the user sends it. Forwarding (or pasting) the shop's answer back to Mimo gets a line-by-line check and reply buttons to approve, then a second link with the confirmation. Our number never writes to a shop. See [Compra en mis tiendas](./shop-orders.md). These `/c/` links are the only URLs the model may write in a reply, and only the ones returned **this turn**: asking for the shopping again re-runs `prepare_shop_orders` (2026-10-08: "dame la compra otra vez" re-sent the morning's links from history, so shops got the pre-v1.1 message).
- **The one reminder (PRO-107, Miguel 2026-10-15: «solo dos veces en total»).** Whoever didn't connect WhatsApp is offered it in the sign-up and **once** more: a card on `/menu` (above the week), «¿Te lo mando por WhatsApp? Le escribes a Mimo «¿qué cenamos?» y te contesta con tu menú.» → «Conectar WhatsApp» (`/whatsapp/conectar`) / «Ahora no». It shows when WhatsApp is available, not connected, and the household has generated a menu and opened the shopping list (its first week resolved, from `usage_events`). Showing it stamps `users.whatsapp_nudge_at`, so it never comes back — after «Ahora no», a reload or on another device. Nothing in «Primeros pasos» and no other reminders; the Perfil card stays as it is.
- **QR on a computer (PRO-123, D-052).** Where the code is already offered — `/onboarding` «Por WhatsApp» and Perfil → WhatsApp → «Conectar WhatsApp» — screens `lg+` also show a QR of the **same `wa.me` link** as the button (code already written), «¿Estás en el ordenador? Escanea este código con la cámara del móvil…» (`components/whatsapp/WhatsAppQr.tsx`, drawn locally with `qrcode`). Not on phones. It is not another offer: it only goes where WhatsApp is already offered. e2e `whatsapp-qr.spec.ts` (1440 and 390).

## Proactive messages ("Avisos por WhatsApp")

When the master toggle is on (default after linking), Mimo writes first. All times are Europe/Madrid. Breakfast and dinner times come from `user_memories.meal_times`, defaulting to 09:00 and 21:00.

| Kind (`prefs` key) | When | Message |
|---|---|---|
| `daily_brief` | breakfast time, for 90 min | "Buenos días. Hoy toca: - Comida: … - Cena: … ¿Quieres cambiar algo?" + menu link; under each meal, one "→ <receta>: mimoia.com/recipes/<id>" line per recipe (PRO-85; notes get none); skipped if the day is empty |
| `cooking_reminder` | only if today's dinner takes ≥ 40 min (`recipes.total_time`, else prep+cook): around dinner time − total − 10 min | "Si quieres cenar a las 20:00, toca empezar con *Carrilleras* (unos 90 min)." |
| `dinner_checkin` | 2–3 h after dinner time, if dinner has a recipe and nothing was cooked-logged today | "¿Hiciste hoy la cena (*X*)? Así lo apunto." [Sí, la hice] [No]. "Sí, la hice" → the assistant calls `log_cooked` (cook log + pantry decrement) |
| `shopping_reminder` | Saturday 10:00–12:59, if the current list has ≥ 3 unchecked items | "¿Toca compra? Te faltan N cosas: …" + list link |
| `weekly_nudge` | Sunday 18:00–21:59, if next week has no menu | "Domingo de planificar: ¿te preparo el menú de la semana que viene?" [Sí, prepáralo] [Ahora no] → `generate_weekly_menu(nextWeek: true)` |
| `prep_alerts` | from `notification_schedule` | "Acuérdate: Merluza — sácalo del congelador…" (also sent by Web Push; the row is `sent` if either channel delivers) |

**Written from a guide, not a fixed text** (PRO-156, D-065; `services/whatsapp/guided.ts`). The texts in the table above are the **safety net**; normally each of those messages (all but `prep_alerts`) and the end of the WhatsApp sign-up (`first_steps`, see *First steps in the chat* above) is written each time by a writer, Claude Haiku 4.5:
- **Guide per kind** (`MESSAGE_GUIDES`): what it is for, what it must carry (dishes, hours, links; buttons stay in code), tone and maximum length; plus fixed rules (`COMMON_RULES`): only cooking, menu and shopping; nothing about health or nutrition; never «no puedo»; invent nothing; don't repeat earlier messages. The planner adds the facts and the literal strings each message must contain (`dailyBriefGuide`, `cookingReminderGuide`…).
- **Context:** the household's memory (`user_memories` without health keys or meal times; the household's ficha is not in these guided messages yet — the conversation with Mimo does read it, PRO-253), the last 12 h of chat and the last 5 messages of the same kind to that number.
- **Checked in code before sending** (`validateGuided`): every required string present, within the length, no banned word (salud, sano, calorías, dieta, proteínas, médico, alergias, «no puedo»…), no §6 health claim or body-effect talk, no link that isn't in the fixed text, a question where the buttons answer one, and no sentence (4+ words) already sent in those last messages of the kind. An AI disclosure (`AI_DISCLOSURE*`) in the fixed text must appear literally, and no other «IA» mention is allowed.
- **Fixed text** when there is no API key, the writer fails or takes more than 8 s, the text fails validation, or the user is over the chat's € budget or the monthly AI cap (`guidedOverBudget`). Each outbound row stores `meta.guided` = `guide` | `fallback` (+ `guidedReason`).
- Outside the 24 h window the Meta template is fixed, but its `{{1}}` is this same written text, flattened.
- **Cost:** one Haiku call per message (~1,500 input + 200 output tokens ≈ $0.0025), billed to the user as `whatsapp_guided` in the cost ledger, so it counts against the monthly cap. Replay: `scripts/replayGuidedBrief.ts` writes five mornings of the same household with the real model and fails if any sentence repeats.

- **Cooldowns:** 20 h per kind; 3 days for the Sunday nudge and the shopping reminder. They are stored as outbound rows, so the 5-minute scheduler tick is idempotent. Proactive messages land in chat history, so a short reply ("sí") makes sense to the assistant.
- **Switches:**
  - **Master:** `whatsapp_links.notify`, the profile toggle. **Opt-in**: a new link starts with `notify = false`, and right after "¡Listo!" ("Tu WhatsApp ya está conectado con Mimoia…") Mimo asks "¿Quieres que también te escriba yo?…" with [Sí, avísame] [No, gracias] (outbound kind `optin_prompt`). Links made before 2026-10-07 kept their setting.
  - **BAJA / ALTA** (see *Control words*) flip the master switch from the chat.
  - **Per kind:** `whatsapp_links.prefs` (missing key = on), migration `0031_whatsapp_prefs.sql`.
  - Users change them **by chat** via the `set_whatsapp_notifications` skill, e.g. "no me mandes el resumen de la mañana ni el recordatorio de la compra". `PATCH /whatsapp/link` accepts `{ notify?, prefs? }`, and `GET /whatsapp/status` returns `prefs`.
- Everything runs inside the existing 5-minute `notificationScheduler` tick (`runProactiveTick`). It's a no-op when WhatsApp isn't configured, and DB work only happens while one of the time windows is open.

Delivery follows Meta's 24 h customer-service window, measured from `last_inbound_at` with a 30-min safety margin:
- **Inside the window:** free-form messages with buttons.
- **Outside the window:** the approved template `WHATSAPP_TEMPLATE_NAME` (language `WHATSAPP_TEMPLATE_LANG`, default `es`), whose single `{{1}}` body variable carries the message flattened to one line. Buttons are folded in as "Responde: Sí / No.", because Meta rejects newlines in template parameters.
- **Outside the window with no template:** skipped silently and retried on the next tick while the time window is still open.

## Conversation reviewer agent

Every day after 07:00 Madrid, the scheduler tick runs `runDailyReviewIfDue` (`services/whatsapp/reviewer.ts`) once for **yesterday's** WhatsApp conversations:
- **Objective signals** (`computeStats`): turns, failed turns, slow turns (> 20 s), engine corrective rounds, frustration cues ("eso no", "ya te he dicho"…), replies ending in a question, proactive sends, send failures, and (PRO-156) messages written from their guide vs. sent as the fixed text (`guidedWritten`, `guidedFallback`). The transcript marks them «redactado con guía» / «texto fijo: <motivo>», runs the health detectors on the written ones, and the reviewer judges them too (natural, varied, nothing invented, no health).
- **Transcripts** (`buildTranscripts`): one per user, Madrid times, with the engine trace per turn. Each inbound turn stores `whatsapp_messages.meta = { tools, corrections, ms, toolTrace, photo? }`; since 2026-10-12 `toolTrace` lists each tool's input and result (bounded) and the transcript shows them under the turn, so "dijo Hecho pero no cambió nada" is judged from facts. `corrections` now holds signals only (`unverified_claim`, `truncated`, `refusal`). Older turns read "sin registro" and are not judged on tools.
- **LLM review:** `claude-opus-5-5`, effort `high`, JSON-schema output, against the product rules: decisive, truthful, transparent about substitutions, brief "Hecho:" without being curt, **useful** (a cooking question answered with knowledge; categories `respuesta_poco_util` and `rechazo_indebido` since 2026-10-12), explicit request beats stored data, fast, useful proactivity, transcription quality. Each finding has severity (alta/media/baja), category, the turn, what happened, what was expected and a concrete suggested fix (prompt, skill, or missing capability). It never touches user data.
- **Output:** a row in `assistant_reviews` (one per Madrid day; `ok` / `empty` / `failed`), shown at `GET /admin/assistant-reviews` (admin), plus a WhatsApp summary (outbound `kind='review'`, excluded from the recipient's chat history) sent to `WHATSAPP_REVIEW_EMAILS` (else `ADMIN_EMAILS`) when they have a linked phone.
- **On-demand runs:** `POST /admin/assistant-reviews/run { day?, notify? }`.
- **Detectores deterministas** (`services/assistantFlags.ts`, desde 2026-10-13): sobre cada respuesta de Mimo, `health_talk` (habla de efectos en el cuerpo, `talksHealthEffects`, o el motor la marcó `health_talk`), `health_claim` (un reclamo de la lista §6, `HEALTH_CLAIM_PATTERNS`, la misma que `publicHealthClaims.test.ts`), `unverified_done` («Hecho:» / «he cambiado…» sin ninguna herramienta que cambiara algo en la traza del turno —las de solo lectura, las que dieron error o las que dijeron «no he encontrado…» no cuentan—, o el motor la marcó `unverified_claim`; los turnos sin `toolTrace` no se juzgan) y `error_refusal` (turno fallido, respuesta no entregada, rechazo o respuesta cortada). Entran en `stats` (`healthTalk`, `healthClaims`, `unverifiedDone`) y en la transcripción como «⚑ señales automáticas» bajo la respuesta: el revisor las comprueba, no las da por buenas.
- **Para los agentes de Mimoia HQ:** `GET /admin/assistant-reviews/flags?days=7` (JWT de admin o `x-metrics-token`; `days` 1–31), solo agregados y textos anonimizados: `{ generatedAt, windowDays, from, to, channels: ['whatsapp'], turns, reviews { ok, empty, failed }, counts { byCategory { health_talk, health_claim, unverified_done, error_refusal, other }, reviewerByCategory, reviewerBySeverity }, items[] { date, channel, category, source (detector | reviewer | both), reviewerCategory, severity, reply, note } }`. Los detectores se calculan al pedirlo sobre `whatsapp_messages`; los hallazgos del revisor salen de `assistant_reviews` (afirmacion_falsa / accion_no_hecha / promesa_incumplida → `unverified_done`; error_tecnico / rechazo_indebido → `error_refusal`; el resto → `other`) y se funden con el aviso del detector del mismo día, categoría y ±3 min. `reply` (máx. 600 caracteres) y `note` pasan por `anonymise()` (`services/anonymise.ts`): emails, enlaces, teléfonos, DNI/NIE, IBAN, direcciones y códigos postales enmascarados; nombres de usuario, del perfil de WhatsApp, del hogar y de los otros miembros, y los nombres con mayúscula tras «soy», «para», «a», «con», «mi marido»… → `[nombre]`. Nunca salen el mensaje del usuario, ids, teléfonos ni la hora exacta. Solo WhatsApp: las conversaciones de Mimo en la web no se guardan.
- **Cost:** one Opus call per day with conversations; about 20 s.

## Linking

- The code has 6 characters from `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789` (no 0/O/1/I) and always contains at least one digit, so an ordinary 6-letter word is never mistaken for a code. It expires after 10 minutes and works once. Generating a new code invalidates the user's previous unused one.
- Sending a valid code links `phone ↔ user` one-to-one:
  - If the phone was linked to another account, the link moves to the new owner.
  - If the user had another phone linked, the old phone is replaced.
  - Messages the phone sent before linking are assigned to the new owner.
- Phones are stored as Meta's `wa_id`: digits only, with the country code.

## Message handling

1. `POST /whatsapp/webhook` verifies `X-Hub-Signature-256`, an HMAC-SHA256 of the **raw** body keyed with `WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET`. That's why the router is mounted before `express.json()`. A bad or missing signature gets **401**.
2. Every message is inserted into `whatsapp_messages` with `ON CONFLICT (wamid) DO NOTHING`, so Meta's retries are deduplicated. Each new message is queued **right after its insert**. If one insert fails, the response is **500**: Meta retries the batch, the already-stored messages dedupe, and they are already queued. Otherwise **200**. The assistant runs after the response.
3. Messages are processed **in order per phone**, using an in-process promise chain (Railway runs one API instance).
4. Each message is marked read with a "typing…" indicator (best effort).
5. Gates, in order:
   - **Control words** run first, before every other gate, and never reach the model (`commands.ts`, whole-message match only, so "para la cena pon lentejas" is a request). **BAJA / STOP / PARAR / "no quiero más avisos"** → `notify = false` + "Hecho: no te enviaré más avisos…". A late (stale), suspended, not-allowed or over-budget sender is honoured too. An unlinked number that wrote BAJA never gets the connect hint again until ALTA. **ALTA / "Sí, avísame"** → `notify = true`. **HUMANO / "hablar con una persona"** → how to reach the team (`SUPPORT_EMAIL`; unset → "la persona de Mimoia que te invitó a la beta"). "No, gracias" is handled here only when it answers the opt-in question; otherwise it goes to the model.
   - A message older than 2 h (a late Meta retry) is dropped silently.
   - An unlinked phone goes to the linking flow.
   - A suspended account is refused (the copy includes the human contact).
   - A **paused** account (trial over without a plan, only from launch day; PRO-135, [Billing](./billing.md)) gets only «Tu prueba de Mimoia ha terminado y tu cuenta está en pausa…» with the link to choose a plan (kind `paused`), never Mimo; it gets no proactive messages or prep alerts either.
   - The **day-7 founder-price offer** (PRO-136, [Billing](./billing.md)) goes once to the household owner as a Mimo message (kind `founder_offer`), only inside the 24 h window and with proactive messages on; never as a template.
   - An account not in `WHATSAPP_ALLOWED_EMAILS` is refused.
   - Once the monthly budget is spent, the user gets the same copy as the web's 429. This check runs **before any paid work**: no transcription, no photo extraction, no model call.
6. Text and button replies go through `chat(userId, text, history, db, { mode: 'whatsapp' })`. **About food, broadly:** everything around food and cooking is in scope (menu, recipes, techniques, ingredients, kitchen utensils and appliances, shopping, pantry, nutrition, recipe links and videos, the user's Mimoia settings, and any question about Mimoia itself: what it is, what it does, plans and prices, privacy, mission (D-053); "ante la duda, es de comida") and gets a real answer, including how-to questions from Mimo's own cooking knowledge. Only topics with nothing to do with food get exactly `WHATSAPP_OFF_TOPIC_REPLY` ("Solo te puedo ayudar con tu comida: menú, recetas, lista de la compra, despensa y nutrición. ¿Te ayudo con algo de eso?"). Until 2026-10-12 the prompt said "resolutivo, no para conversar" and that sentence also hit cooking questions (a barbecue fish, "¿tienes el vídeo?"). A mixed message gets the food part done and the rest omitted. The system prompt's WhatsApp mode allows `*negrita*` and dash lists, forbids "pulsa/abajo" screen language, sends cooking timers and steps to the app's cooking mode, and asks the model to end short-choice questions with `[[opciones: Sí | No]]`. The renderer turns that line into reply buttons; with more than 3 options, they are folded back into the text.
7. Replies are cut to WhatsApp's 4096-character limit, splitting on paragraph boundaries first.
8. Each reply is stored as an outbound row (`kind='reply'`). The inbound row becomes `processed`, with its final text. Images (the week picture) go in two Graph calls: `POST /{phone-number-id}/media` (multipart upload of the JPG) and a `type: 'image'` message with that media id and the caption (`client.ts` `uploadMedia`).
9. If anything fails, the user gets "Vaya, algo ha fallado…" and the inbound row becomes `failed` with the error.

## Data model

- `whatsapp_links(user_id UNIQUE, phone UNIQUE, profile_name, notify, prefs jsonb, linked_at, last_inbound_at)`. `last_inbound_at` marks the start of Meta's 24 h customer-service window.
- `whatsapp_link_codes(code PK, user_id, expires_at, used_at)`: used by both the profile card and `/whatsapp/conectar`.
- `whatsapp_messages(wamid UNIQUE NULL, phone, user_id, direction in|out, kind, body, status, error_message)`.
  - Inbound statuses: `received` / `processed` / `failed` / `ignored`.
  - Outbound statuses: `sent` / `failed`.
  - Outbound kinds `system` (budget, linked, errors), `link` (how to connect) and `ack` ("me pongo con ello") are left out of chat history. `image_failed`: a week picture whose send failed and whose text went out instead (status `failed`, also out of history).
- Migration `0030_whatsapp.sql`, which only creates new tables and is idempotent. `users.whatsapp_nudge_at` (migration `0052_whatsapp_nudge.sql`, additive): when the reminder card was shown.

## API

- `GET /whatsapp/webhook` (public): Meta's verification handshake. It echoes `hub.challenge` when `hub.verify_token` matches `WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN`; otherwise **403**.
- `POST /whatsapp/webhook` (public, signed): **503** when the channel isn't configured.
- `GET /whatsapp/status` (auth): `{ available, linked, nudge, phone, notify, prefs, chatLink }`. `nudge` = show the one reminder card on `/menu` (PRO-107, rule in `services/whatsapp/nudge.ts`). `available` = configured AND email allowed. The profile card is hidden unless `available || linked`, so a user can always disconnect.
- `POST /whatsapp/nudge/seen` (auth): the reminder card was shown; stamps `users.whatsapp_nudge_at` once (keeps the first date). **204**.
- `POST /whatsapp/link-code` (auth): `{ code, expiresAt, message, waLink }`. **403** `WHATSAPP_UNAVAILABLE` when not available.
- `PATCH /whatsapp/link` (auth) `{ notify?: boolean, prefs?: { <kind>: boolean } }`: **404** when not linked.
- `DELETE /whatsapp/link` (auth): **204**.

## Constraints

- **No health data over WhatsApp** (LEG-03, Meta's terms 4.2; PRO-24): allergies, intolerances, restrictions and physical data are never stored from a WhatsApp conversation, with or without the health-data consent. The engine passes `channel: 'whatsapp'` in the skill context; `update_profile` and `update_memory` drop those fields and reply «Por WhatsApp no guardo datos de salud… Añádelos en tu perfil de la web: <WEB_PUBLIC_URL>/profile» (other changes in the same message still apply), and the WhatsApp prompt tells the model so. Restrictions already saved from the web are still respected. Test `whatsappHealthData.test.ts`
- **AI disclosure (EU AI Act art. 50):** both first messages Mimo can send in a conversation — the unlinked "Hola, soy Mimo, el asistente de cocina de Mimoia…" hint and the "¡Listo!" after linking — include "Soy un asistente de inteligencia artificial (IA): puedo equivocarme y no sustituyo a un profesional sanitario." (`AI_DISCLOSURE_FIRST_PERSON` in `@ona/shared`). Pinned by `whatsappInbound.test.ts`.
- No WhatsApp groups. Signing up happens on the web (the connect link offers "Crear cuenta"), not inside the chat.
- The business isn't verified (D-004): the display name may show as the number until Meta approves it, and business-initiated conversations are capped at 250 a day. Business verification lifts the cap and is needed before opening the channel widely.
- In-process queue: if the API restarts mid-turn, that message stays `received` and gets no answer (no sweeper; messages older than 2 h are dropped as stale anyway).
- Every turn checks the chat's € budget **and** the monthly cap on all paid AI work (`USER_MONTHLY_SPEND_CAP_EUR`, see [Advisor](./advisor.md)) before any paid step. Every paid call is recorded in the cost ledger ([Metrics](./metrics.md)), including each template message (`meta_whatsapp/utility_template`) and, since 2026-10-15, every free-form message Mimo sends (`meta_whatsapp/service_message`, feature `whatsapp_service`): Meta bills these from the 1,001st of the month per number, so the first 1,000 are recorded at 0 € and the rest at €0.0166 (`services/whatsapp/serviceCost.ts`).
- "Today" and "this week" use the Europe/Madrid clock (`services/madridTime.ts`), for the assistant's `get_todays_menu` / `generate_weekly_menu` and for the daily brief.
- **Week picture rendering** runs in the API with no browser: `satori` (layout → SVG, flexbox only) + `@resvg/resvg-js` (SVG → PNG) + `sharp` (photo crops, JPG q85). Fonts (Fraunces, Cormorant Garamond italic, Inter; SIL OFL) and the meal icons (Noto Emoji PNGs, Apache-2.0) and logo are bundled in `apps/api/assets/` (licences in `assets/NOTICE.txt`); nothing is cached. Recipe photos are fetched per send (`image_url`; relative catalogue paths resolved against `WEB_PUBLIC_URL`), 2.5 s timeout and 6 MB cap each, 8 at a time; a photo that fails becomes a tile, never a failed picture. About 1 s to draw (measured locally) plus the photo downloads.
- The model sees text only: photos are never shown to the chat model, they go straight to recipe import (a photo of the fridge is not understood yet). Cooking-mode hints (`set_timer`, `cooking_step`) have no effect on WhatsApp; the reply points to the app instead.
